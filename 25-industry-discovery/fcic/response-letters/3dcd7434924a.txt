                                      Re: Commission Letter to Louis Lebedin re: Potential Use of Quote

                      Dear Gary:

                                   On behalf of JPMorgan Chase & Co., in response to the Commission's
                     December 3, 2010 letter to Louis Lebedin, I write to object to the Commission's use of a
                     purported quote from Mr. Lebedin's interview with Commission staff (the "Interview").

                                    First, as the Commission is aware, there was no tape-recording made of
                     the Interview, and we therefore cannot confirm with certainty the accuracy of any
                     statements made by the witness.

                                   Second, the purported quote lacks necessary context. The quote suggests
                     that Mr. Lebedin was speaking about more than one customer withdrawing business from
                     Bear Steams; however, in the context of the Interview, it was clear that Mr. Lebedin was
                     speaking about a single, specific prime brokerage customer of Bear Stearns at a specific
