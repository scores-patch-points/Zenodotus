         Re:       Use of Potentiallv Confidential Interview Material

Dear Mr. Cohen:

        As counsel for Mr. Ludwig, I wish to convey his appreciation for the Financial Crisis
Inquiry Commission's recognition of potential sensitivities regarding possible inclusion in the
public record of Mr. Ludwig's interview with Commission staff and for this opportunity to
apprise the Commission of portions of the interview that Mr. Ludwig considers confidential or
otherwise inappropriate for public release.

        Mr. Ludwig has a strong respect for the Commission's mission, and accordingly he
endeavored during his interview to be responsive and direct in answering questions from
Commission staff that covered a broad range of complex and variegated issues concerning the
systemic causes of the economic crisis. As noted previously, Mr. Ludwig sought to avoid
specific comment about individuals or entities with which he has worked previously or is
working now.

        Having reviewed Mr. Ludwig's interview audio file with a presumption in favor of
release, we identified only a handful of statements that Mr. Ludwig requests be maintained in
confidence. They are identified below by reference to the time stamp of the audio file, per the
instruction of Ms. Waskowicz.
