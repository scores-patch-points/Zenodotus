                       Impact of GDN not participating in AdX auctions
                                              Nirmal Jayaram
                                                 Jan 2014

      Auction simulations were performed to understand the impact of GDN not participating in AdX
      auctions. The impact is a 70% reduction in Google revenue and a 65% reduction in publisher
      payout. Details are provided below. The auction simulation is run on the Bemanke holdback
      data.

      AdX buyers obviously continue to win these queries when GDN stops submitted ads. They,

      AdX buyers now win some of these queries, but a large number (70%) go unmatched (which is
      interesting). Also interesting is that the RPM on queries won now by AdX buyers is only a third
      of the RPM on queries won by GDN earlier.
