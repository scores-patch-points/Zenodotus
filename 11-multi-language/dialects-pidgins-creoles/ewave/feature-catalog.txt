eWAVE (Electronic World Atlas of Varieties of English)
The 235 morphosyntactic features -- full catalog
======================================================================

Source: Kortmann, Bernd & Lunkenheimer, Kerstin & Ehret, Katharina
(eds.) 2020. The Electronic World Atlas of Varieties of English.
Leipzig: Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology.
Available online at https://ewave-atlas.org/
License: CC BY 3.0 (Attribution 3.0 Unported)

This document lists every one of the 235 morphosyntactic features
rated across eWAVE's 77 varieties of English (including its 26
English-based pidgins and creoles), organized by the atlas's own
12 feature categories (Pronouns, Noun Phrase, Tense & Aspect, Modal
Verbs, Verb Morphology, Negation, Agreement, Relativization,
Complementation, Adverbial Subordination, Adverbs & Prepositions,
Discourse & Word Order). Each entry gives the feature's atlas ID,
its name, a short illustrative gloss where the atlas provides one,
the source cited for that gloss, and two summary statistics computed
across all 77 rated varieties: Attestation (the proportion of
varieties in which the feature is attested at all -- ratings A, B,
or C) and Pervasiveness (the proportion of attested cases in which
the feature is pervasive or obligatory -- rating A). Ratings
themselves follow a fixed six-point scale used for every
feature/variety pair in the atlas:

  A  feature is pervasive or obligatory
  B  feature is neither pervasive nor extremely rare
  C  feature exists, but is extremely rare
  D  attested absence of the feature
  X  feature is not applicable given the variety's structural make-up
  ?  no information on the feature is available for this variety
