Tlhaloso
nna wa Moaforika Borwa o bo o laolwe ke Moaforika Borwa a le mongwe kgotsa go feta kgotsa nna wa setheo sa kgwebo, baagi, setšhaba kgotsa mokgatlho wa batho ba ba ipopileng ka fa tlase ga melao ya Repaboliki ya Aforika borwa mme e bile bontsi jwa dišere le ditshwanelo tsa go vouta di laolwa ke Maaforika borwa.
Mokoro wa go thaya ditlhapi wa selegae ka gale o diretswe go thaya ditlhapi le go dira ditiro dingwe tse di tsamaelanang le se. Mokoro o akaretsa lootwana la kere, ditlamelo, borekisetso, morwalo, le lookwane le le pegilweng mo mokorong. Mokoro wa go thaya ditlhapi wa selegae e ka nna mokoro mongwe le mongwe o o thusang mokoro o le mongwe kgotsa e mentsi mo lewatleng go dira tiro nngwe le nngwe e e tsamaisanang le go thaya ditlhapi. Yona e ka akaretsa ditiro tse di latelang, le tse dingwe gape:
ipaakanyo thelesa polokelo tsidifatsa go pagamisa (transportation), kgotsa go rulaganya go dira ditiro dingwe tsa go thaya ditlhapi mo Lefelong le le kgethegileng la Ekonomi (Exclusive Economic Zone) (EEZ) kgotsa mo metsing a boditšhabatšhaba ka fa tlase ga folaga ya Aforika Borwa.
Ga go na motho yo o tshwanetseng go dirisa mokoro wa go thaya ditlhapi kgotsa mokoro ofe kapa ofe a dirisa tshwanelo nngwe le nngwe fa e fela fa a abetswe laesense,le phemiti e e nyalanang le yona fa mong mokoro a na le tshwanelo ya go thaya ditlhapi.Fa mong wa mokoro a se na tshwanelo ya go thaya ditlhapi, o tshwanetse a bo a na le konteraka ya go dirisa mokoro go thaya ditlhapi.

Dikgato tse di latelwang
Iponele foromo ya kopo go tswa mo ofising e e gaufi ya Lefapha la Merero ya Tikologo le Bojanala (DEAT).
O tshwanetse wa bo o na le tshwanelo ya go thaya ditlhapi ( o dirile kopo mme o abetswe tshwanelo ya go thaya ditlhapi). Fa o se na tshwanelo ya go thaya ditlhapi yo o nang le mokoro, o tshwanetse go tlisa konteraka, e o e dirileng le mong wa mokoro go dirisa mokoro wa gagwe, fa o tlisa kopo ya laesense ya mokoro.
Isa foromo kwa ofising e e gaufi ya Lefapha la Merero ya Tikologo le Bojanala le na le tshedimosetso e e latelang:
o leina la mokoro wa go thaya ditlhapi o leina le dintlha ka botlalo tsa mong wa mokoro (go akaretsa dišere tsa mokoro fa e le gore ke mokoro o o tlhakanetsweng le moagi wa RSA)
o mofuta wa enjine e e tsamaisang mokoro o boleele ga mmogo le bogolo jwa mokoro o nomoro kwadiso ya mokoro o leina la folaga ya puso e mokoro o kwadisitsweng ka fa tlase ga ona (fa o hirilwe mo Aforika borwa kgotsa o tlhakanetswe le moagi wa Aforika borwa)
Supa lefelo le mokoro o tlileng go dirisiwa mo go lona ga mmogo le sebaka se se tlileng go tsewa.
Tlatsa foromo ka go e gatisa o ditlhakakgolo fela mme o kwale lekwalo le le tshegetsang kopo ya gago fa go tlhokagala.
