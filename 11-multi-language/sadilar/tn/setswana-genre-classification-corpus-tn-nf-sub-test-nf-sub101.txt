Dintlha dingwe tse di kgatlhang
SATI e ntshitse kgololo ya pegodikgang go tshegetsa tshwetso ya Mopitlwe 16, 2010 e e dirilweng ke Kgotlatshekelo e Kgolo e mo go yone puso e neng ya laelwa gore e dire tumalanong le maikarabelo a yone go ya ka molaotheo wa yone malebana le tiro ya dipuo tsa semmuso mo dingwageng tse pedi tse di tlang. SATI e ne ya gatelela gore e ikemiseditse go tshegetsa puso ka botlalo malebana le go dirisa pholisi ya dipuo-dintsi gore dipuo tsa semmuso di dirisiwe ka go lekalekana. Maloko a bontshitse fa a itumeletse taolo eo mme ba ne ba itumelela go bo SATI e lemotshitse batho ka ntlha eno.
Leloko la SATI e leng Wilna Liebenberg o tlile go emela SATI ka go dira pontsho  kwa London Book Fair (Moranang 19-21, 2010, Earls Court). Pontsho ya, “South African Translators’ Institute: Unlocking South Africa's Multilingual Treasure Trove”, e tla nna ka 20 Moranang mo Market Focus Pavilion. 
SOS Talk ke tsamaiso ya boditšhabatšhaba ya go toloka dipuo ka mogala, e e  dirang gore badirelwa ba tiro eno ba kgone go dirisa batoloki ka mogala kwa dikopanong tsa kgwebo, mo malatsing a boikhutso kgotsa gope fela. Batoloki ba ka ikwadisa mo webosaeteng ya, www.sostalk.com. 
Serweboleta le Diwebosaete
Langmates ke kgola di le ditheo tsa boranodi mo badirisi ba kgonang go buisana mmogo teng ka inthanete, ba tlhabana botlhale ka tiro ya go ranola le go ranolela dirweboleta mo dipuong tsa dikgaolo tsa rona le go gapa dintlha tse di ka dirisiwang mo webosaeteng. Leba http://www.langmates.com/home.php 
Lingoproz.co.za ke lenaneo la webosaete e ntšha la batho bao ba tlamelang ka dipuo tsa Aforika. Thulaganyo eno e dira gore badira-ka-dipuo ba kgone go phasalatsa ditirelo tsa bone le go akaretsa mo phasalatsong eno lefelo le maloko a ka tshwarang dipuisano mo go lone ka mareo le dikgang tse dingwe tse di amanang le dipuo, le makasine wa mo inthaneteng. Leba http://www.lingoproz.co.za.
