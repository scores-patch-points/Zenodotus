Letsatsi la Thanolo la Boditšhabatšhaba la 2010
Setlhogo sa ITD monongwaga e ne e le "Boleng jwa Thanolo ya Dipuo ka go Farologana". Kgololo ya boradikgang e e ntshitsweng ke FIT e ka bonwa mo webosaeteng ya SATI (www.translators.org.za>Int. Translation Day).
SATI e ne ya keteka le go tsenya seatla mo go tlhatloseng porofaele ya porofešene eno ka ditsela di le dintsi.
Tšhapotara ya kwa Boland e ne ya keteka letsatsikgopotso la yone la bo 20 ka Lwetse a le 18 kwa Welgemeend mo Cape Town o mo go one go neng go na le dijo tsa motshegare tsa monate wa go itoma monwana le seminara e e neng ya ipelelwa ke botlhe ba ba neng ba le gone mo go yone e e neng e le malebana le setlhogo sa ITD sa 2010. Dibui e ne e le maloko a  SATI eleng Sharon Tabraham, Barbara Bohle, Alta van Rensburg, Johan Blaauw le Anne-Marie Beukes. Dikhopi tsa dipuopontsho tsa seminara eo di ka bonwa mo webosaeteng ya SATI. Ikgolaganye le Wilna Liebenberg (wilna@lieben.co.za kgotsa leletsa 082 824 8910) go bona tshedimosetso ya tsela ya go tsena mo tšhapotareng eno.
Leloko la Khansele e leng Manzo Khulu o ne a ema baithuti ka lefoko mo Letsatsing la Thanolo kwa Tshwane University of Technology e e kwa khamphaseng e e ka fa Borwa jwa Soshanguve ka Lwetse a le 22. Dibui tse dingwe di ne di akaretsa leloko la SATI Mashite Mogale wa GCIS le Dr Rolse Masebulele wa UNISA. Tiragalo eno e ne e le karolo ya ditlapele tsa Letsatsi la Boswa la Lwetse a le 24 mme setlhogo sa konokono e ne e le gore go botlhokwa thata gore Ba Aforika Borwa ba dirise le go rotloetsa tiriso ya dipuo tsa kwa magaeng a bone ka go farologana.
Modulasetulo wa SATI Anne-Marie Beukes o ne a botsolotsiwa mo SAfm ka Lwetse a le 30 kaga thanolo le botoloki mo Aforika Borwa, mo bareetsi ba neng ba bolela fa ba tshwenyegile segolo thata ka boleng kwa go toloka mo dikgotlatshekelong.
SATI le Van Schaik Publishers e ne ya nna baamogedi kwa go gololweng ga buka ya Veeltalige Vertaalterminologie/Multilingual Translation Terminology e e kwadilweng ke  Anne-Marie Beukes le Marné Pienaar (yo le ene e leng leloko la SATI) ka Lwetse a le 30 kwa moletlong wa dino kwa Yunibesithing ya Johannesburg. 
Motlatsa-Modulasetulo wa SATI, ebong Johan Blaauw, o ne a naya pontsho kaga SATI kwa moletong wa ITD ya North-West Provincial Language Service ka Lwetse a le 30. Dibui tse dingwe di ne di akaretsa Moporofesara S. Sebate yo o neng a naya kakaretso ya se tota se kaiwang ke thuto ya thanolo mabapi le go ranolela mo dipuong tsa Aforika mme Edwin de Klerk ene o ne a dira pontsho kaga Trados.
Johan o ne a tshwaragane le meletlo ya ITD ya PanSALB North West le Yunibesiti ya Bokone-Bophirima kwa Potchefstroom ka Diphalane a le 9. Karolo ya ntlha ya porokeramo e ne e le ka dikgang tsa semolao tsa go tlhoma lekgotla la baranodi le batoloki kwa Porofenseng ya Bokone-Bophirima (mo nakong eno le tla bidiwa North West Language Practitioners’ Association). Karolo ya bobedi ya porokeramo e ne ya remelela mo setlhogong sa ITD 2010, segolobogolo mabapi le go ntlafatsa boleng ka go dirisa ditsompelo tse di ka bonwang ke baranodi ba ba iperekang. Senthara ya Thekenoloji ya Mokwalo (Centre for Text Technology [CtexT]) ya YBB le National Language Body for Afrikaans ba ne ba naya dipuo-pontsho tsa dipuisano kaga dinetefatsa-mopeleto tsa dipuo tsa Se-Aforika le porokeramo ya serweboleta se se sa duelelweng sa thanolo, Autshumato.
Leloko la SATI ebong Tammy Baikie o kwadile athikele kaga thanolo mo go Mail & Guardian (e e tlileng go tswa ka Diphalane a le 15). 
Go Tsalana ga Puo le Badiri ba Bobegadikgang (LAMP)
SATI e kopanetse le PEG (Professional Editors’ Group), Safrea (Southern African Freelancers Association) le WGSA (Writers Guild of SA) go tlhoma tirisanommogo e e sa gagamalang ya puo le badiri ba bobegadikgang. Fa nako e ntse e tsweletse, go tla buisanngwa le ditheo tse dingwe tse di tshwanang gore di tsene mo tirisanongmmogo eno. Boikaelelo jwa seno ke go kopanya ditsompelo tsa go tlhotlheletsa mafapha a bobegadikgang, go tlhatlosa diporofaele tsa morafe le go abelana boitseanape, mme go ntse go bolokilwe boemanosi jwa setheo sengwe le sengwe. Go sa ntse go kwarinngwa memorantamo wa tumalano mme maloko a tla itsisiwe kafa o tsweletseng ka teng fa nako e ntse e tsweletse. 
Go newa tetlelelo-semmuso ya go toloka ga go sala sebui morago
Re leboga maloko otlhe a rona a a neng a tsaya kgato mo patlisisong e e seng kafa molaong malebana le go newa tetlelelosemmuso ya go toloka ga go sala sebui morago. Re amogetse ditsibogelo di feta 100 mme go lebega go na le palo e e lekaneng ya batho ba ba batlang gore re akanyetse go simolola mofuta ono wa go newa tetlelelo-semmunso.  Kgato e e latelang ke go bona gore re ka dira jang go tlhatlhoba ba ba batlang go newa tetlelelo-semmuso eo.
	Gakologelwa gore SATI e setse e ntse e naya tetlelelo-semmuso ya go tolokela gongwe le sebui, thanolo ka kakaretso, thanolo ya baranodi ba ba ikanisitsweng, mareo le tseleganyo ya puo, gammogo le go newa tetlelelosemmuso ya kgwebo ya diofisi tsa puo le baemedi. Ikgolaganye le ofisi ya go naya tetleelosemmuso o atereseng ya (exams@translators.org.za) go bona tshedimosetso e nngwe gape.
