Ditiragalo tsa go itsanye tsa SATI 
Leloko la Lekgotla la SATI Michelle Rabie o ile go tshwara kokoano-puisano kwa Cape Town ka 5 Phatwe 2010. Setlhogo ke “Tsela ya go Ipapatsa Jaaka Modiri ka Puo yo o Iperekang” (“How to Market Yourself as a Freelance Language Practitioner”), kokoano eno e tla itshupa go nna tshono e ntle ya go itsanye le maloko a mangwe a tiro ya boranodi mo maemong a mantle. Ikgolaganye le Michelle mo atereseng ya michelle@translators.org.za.  Ikwadiso e tswalwa ka 28 Phukwi 2010, o se ka wa diega – ikwadise gompieno!
Eno e tshwana le kokoano-puisano eo e neng e tshwerwe kwa Pretoria ka Moranang, mme go rulaganyediwa gore e tshwarwe gape kwa Durban le Bloemfontein.
Tsweetswe itsise ofisi ya SATI gore o eletsa gore go dirwe dithulaganyo dife tsa katiso.
Pontsho ya Dibuka ya Boditšhabatšhaba kwa Cape Town 
Pontsho ya Dibuka e tla tshwarwa go simolola ka 30 Phukwi go ya go 2 Phatwe 2010 mme lekgotla la SATI le tla bo le tshwaraganetse dipontsho tseno le PEG le McGillivray Linnegar Associates. Fa e le gore o tla bo o le teng kwa Pontshong eno, re etele mo lefelong le re tla bong re direla dipontsho tseno mo go lone le go ema nokeng baithaopi bao ba tla bong ba re emetse.
SATI e tla dira dipontsho tseno mo letsatsing la kgwebo fela (Labotlhano, 30 Phukwi): “Se Bagatisi ba Tshwanetseng go se Itse ka Thanolo” (“What Publishers Should Know about Translation.”) Maloko a letleletswe go nna teng – mme rotloetsa ditlelaente tsa gago go nna teng le tsone–. Puisano e tla tshwarwa ka 15:00  Room 1.62 kwa Cape Town International Convention Centre.  
Gape re tla bo re tsholetse baeng ba ba tlhophilweng dikgetsana tsa dimpho bao ba tla bong ba etetse lefelo la SATI mme maloko a ka tsenya dikarata-kgwebo tsa one kana dipampitshana-papatso (tse di sa feteng A5) mo teng ga dikgetsana tseno – a bo eno e tlile go nna tshono e ntle jang  ne ya go itsanye le ba bangwe! Go bona tshedimosetso e e oketsegileng, ikgolaganye le Marion  mo atereseng ya (office@translators.org.za).
Sejana sa Lefatshe sa  2010
A o ne o le gone? Fa e le gore o ne o golagane le dikabelo tsa boranodi mabapi le tiragalo eno (pele ga tiragalo eno, fa e ntse e tsweletse kgotsa morago ga yone), tsweetswe re itsise. Re eletsa go kwala pego ka boripana mabapi le mofuta wa tiro ya puo oo Sejana sa Lefatshe se e diretseng Baagi ba Aforika Borwa.  Romelela Marion tshedimosetso kwa atereseng ya office@translators.org.za. 
Dikgang go tswa go FIT
FIT e golotse kgatiso ya dikgang e e tlhalosang setlhogo se se tlhophetsweng Letsatsi la Boditšhabatšhaba la Thanolo la 2010: “Boleng Jwa Thanolelo mo Dipuong Tse di Farologaneng.” (“Translation Quality for a Variety of Voices”).  Leba aterese ya  http://www.fit-ift.org./download/en/pr-itd-2010.pdf.
Tokololo ya bosheng ya lekwalo-dikgang  ya  FIT  ebong,  Translatio, e gone mo atereseng ya http://www.fit-ift.org./en/translatio-e.php mme e akaretsa pego ka mekete ya SATI ya 2009 ITD.
Patlisiso ya Dituediso ya 2010 
Dipholo tsa patlisiso ya dituediso ya 2010 di bontshitse gore ga go na diphetogo tse di kalo fa di bapisiwa le tsa patlisiso ya bofelo ya 2008 (gongwe ka ntlha ya maemo a itsholelo a a gone mo nakong ya ga jaanong), mme ka jalo ga go kitla go gololwa pego monogwaga. SATI e tla dira patlisiso e nngwe mo tshimologong ya ngwagwa wa 2011 go lekeletsa maemo. Re ikuelela mo malokong otlhe go tsaya karolo fa go dirwa dipatlisiso: go boima thata go bona tshedimosetso e e ikanyegang fa e le maloko a le mmalwa fela a a nnang le seabe.
