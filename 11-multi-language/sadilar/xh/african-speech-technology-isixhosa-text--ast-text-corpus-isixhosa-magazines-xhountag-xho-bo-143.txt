Kuba iintsuku zisiya ziphela ukuya kwiwaka lesibini leminyaka (2000), akunakuvumela ukunonophela iinwele zakho nako kuphaphatheke kuba kusebusika.

KUBA sizilungiselela iwaka lesibini leminyaka (2000)
sekusele nje phantse ikhulu elinemivo leentsuku ukususela ngoku,abanye abaziingcali ekunonopheleni iinwele baya kuphikisa ukuba iinwele zethu kule mihla nobudala abusenampilo njengoko kwakunjalo ekuqaleni kwenkulungwane. Abanye bathi noko zintle kuba kukho iimveliso ezininzi onokuzikhetha kwanezimbo ezifumaneka kule mihla kuthi esingazange sikhe sicinge nokucinga ngazo.

Nakuba iimveliso ezininzi esizifumanayo kule mihla zisivulela kumathuba amatsha, zibangele iinwele zethu zibe nezimbo esasingazazi ngaphambili, kubalulekile xa sizisebenzisa sisoloko sifunda size silandele imiyalelo yenkampani ezivelisayo. Kukwabalulekile ukutyelela rhoqo kwindawo elungisa iinwele apho kugxininiswe kakhulu ekuhlumiseni nasekukhondishineni iinwele.

nokutshintsha kwamaxesha enyakeni. Iinwele zakho
unokuzikhathalela njani ebusika?
