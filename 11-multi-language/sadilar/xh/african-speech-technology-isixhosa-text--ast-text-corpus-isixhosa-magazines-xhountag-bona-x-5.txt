XA uMatiwane Manana wayeqalisa iphulo lePeoples Educational theatre ngo-1985 wayezimisele ukufundisa uluntu, ingakumbi ulutsha.
Oko ndakwenza emva kwexesha localucalulo olwalugquba kweli. Iingqondo zabantu zazitshatyalalisiwe. Nkqu nezithethe zethu bezicekiswa ngabany'abantu.
Ngeli phulo ndabona ukuba kulungile ukuqalisa beselula abantwana ndibafundise ukuba kuthetha ntoni ukuba ngumntu oNtsundu. Sibuyela kwimvelaphi yethu, sibenza bazithande izithethe zethu phambi kokuba okusezingqondweni zabo kufunxwe ziimfundiso zezinye iintlanga, utshilo uManana.
UMatiwane ubone ukuba ukusebenzisa amajelo eendaba abaNtsundu yeyona ndlela yokufikelela ebantwini- njengoko kube njalo nangaphambili - kuba neminye imiba yentlalo ifikelele ebantwini ngaloo majelo. Ukufunda ngale ndlela kuyahlonelwa kwaye kuxatyisiwe.
UMatiwane ufumanise ukuba abantu bafunda kakuhle ngale ndlela, zimbalwa neempazamo




ezenziwayo xa kufundwa.
Xa ndandiseUnited States ndacinga ngokuyisebenzisa le ndlela ndakubuyela ekhaya. Endikubone kulutsha lwamaSwazi kukuba aluzithandi izithethe zabaNtsundu.
Lucekisa izithethe namasiko alo. Ngamafutshane, lufuna ukuba ngabantu abamhlophe. Oku kuyandimangalisa kuba kwangaxeshanye lufuna ukuba ngamaSwazi.
Ulutsha luzingqondi. Lufunda kwizikolo zangasese, inkoliso yalo iya ecaweni. Luyayazi imidlalo yeqonga, kodwa aluyazi injongo yalo ebomini.
UMatiwane ubhenela kulutsha ukuba lube neqhayiya ngokuba ngabantu abantsundu. Uthi lufanele luxabise okuphembelela ubomi balo kodwa malungalinganisi ezinye iintlanga okanye lube zizikhwenene.
Njengoko singabantu abantsundu siyakuthanda ukuba ngumzekelo oza kulandelwa zezinye iintlanga - ingakumbi emculweni wejazz .
Umdlalo othi My Destiny nothi Asiyeni Lapho yimidlalo yeqonga emibini esetyenziswa yiP.E.T ukufikelela elutsheni nelizwe. I-P.E.T ikwasebenzisa umxhentso weSibhaca, umxhentso, ukubonga nomculo ukucacisa umyalezo wayo.
Ufundiswe nguIan Barnard eDorkay House . Ukwangomnye weqela lokuqala elalidlala kumdlalo othi Sponono, okhe wakhenketha eUSA . Xa uqala lo mdlalo nguye ocula kwigwijo elimnandi lesiZulu.
Kuthe xa lo mdlalo uyekwa, uMatiwane wahlala phesheya kunye nabanye. Ucule noMirriam Makeba noLetta Mbuli, ube negalelo kunye nabo ekuqanjweni kweLumumba.
Kwizinto zakhe azixabisileyo kukho iileta ezimbini zombulelo enye ivela kuRobert F. Kennedy ongasekhoyo nowayenguMongameli waseMerika , nakuCorreta Scott King inkosikazi engasekho kaGqr. Martin Luther King .
Umyalezo kaMatiwane kulutsha kukuba likho ithemba xa kumanyene. Ikamva lisezandleni zabantu.
Xa ukholelwa kwinkululeko, unokuthetha ngezinto zaseAfrika okanye ezakwaNtu, utsho uManana.

IINDABA ZOLUTSHA

Lo ngumhlathi wesihlanu kungcelele lwamanqaku
athunyelwa lulutsha lwalo lonke eli luthetha nangayiphi na ingongoma enomdla kwinkqubela yalo. Kule nyanga
umxholo uthi: UKUMODELA .


Osithumelele umhlathi wale nyanga nguZintathu Mqhikiza waseMtata. Ungumfundi weBanga le-10 ezingisa High School , uye ebalasela ekumodeleni . Sephumelele kabini kukhuphiswano lokumodela njengokuba engumfundi eLadibo School of Modelling eMtata. UZintathu unconywa ngokuba nesithomo esihle esingenza abantu abanjengoNaomi Campbell bammonele.


 ukumodela kuyonwabisa, kodwa nako kunamahla-ndinyuka. Xa ufuna ukuphumelela kufuneka uzimisele, ukhuthale. Phofu ke, ungadimazeki, lo ngumsebenzi okugcina ukhenketha kwaye okwenza ube nenkathalo kwinto yonke. Ukwakufundisa indlela yokuziphatha xa uphakathi kwabantu.
Enye inzuzo yokuModela kukudibana nabantu abaninzi ababalulekileyo - kanti ufunda nendlela yokusingatha lula ezo ndiba No. Okokugqibela, ufunda ngoku lungelelanisa impahla yefashoni kwanezinye iinkcukacha ezibandakanyeke ekumodeleni . Inkoliso yabantu imodela ukuze ifumane udumo lokuba yimodeli  ithuba lokuvela kwiimagazini naku mabonakude.
Esinye isizathu sokuba abanye abantu bamodele ngamathuba okuya kusebenza kwiindawo ezikude, apho bathi besebenza babe bonwabile. Njengoko ndiyimodeli ndifumanise ukuba uzuza amathuba amaninzi okwaziwa. Oku kuthetha ukuba usethubeni lokuzuza imali eninzi qho xa unikwe umsebenzi. Phofu ke, xa ufuna ukwenza umsebenzi wokumodela kukho izinto ekufuneka uzikhumbule. Okokuqala, kufuneka ube nesithomo esincinane yaye uphile qete emzimbeni. Kwakhona, xa unemali yakho ikunceda ekuthengeni izixhobo zalo msebenzi. Kanti ke, ukumodela kunokukuxhobisa ngeendlela zokonga imali ukuze ungaxhomekeki mntwini. Khumbula, indlela oziphethe ngayo yiyo ebonisa ukuba izinto ezingakuhambeli kakuhle ziyingxaki enkulu na okanye zizinto onokuzoyisa. Imodeli enempumelelo izisombulula iingxaki ngobulumko nangendlela eyanelisayo xa zisithi gqi. Ukuze wenjenjalo kufuneka uzinike ixesba kwinto nganye oyenzayo noyithethayo ukuze ungathi ethubeni ufumanise ukuthi okuthile ukwenze ngokungxama uze uzisole.
Imodeli efanelekileyo kufuneka izame ukuba ngumzekelo omhle apho ihlala khona. Kufuneka ukhuthale, ubakhuthaze abadimazekileyo, ubancede abantu benze inkqubela. Lilonke ukumodela kufuna izinto yinto. Ubuhle buyafuneka kumntu omodelayo , kodwa ubuhle bulilize xa ungazithembi, ungenabuntu. Enye into ebalulekileyo omawuyikhumbule kukuba unokuba yimodeli ixesha elide ngokuthanda kwakho. Akukho budala bumiselwe ukuba yimodeli . Unina wemodeli ebalaseleyo ehlabathini, uNaomi Campbell , usandul'ukuqala ukumodela . Ndiva enconywa gqitha ngokwenjenjalo ulandela ekhondweni lentombi yakhe.

UKUVAVANYWA KOBUHLOBO
BOKWENYANI


Kwakukho amakhwenkwana amabini, uBongani noNdumiso ayengabahlobo abasenyongweni. Eneneni babevana nangaphezu kwabantwana abazalwa kunye, bebaliselana ngezinto ezimbi ezibehleleyo, iminqweno, izinto ezibonwabisayo, izinto abazoyikayo neemfihlo zabo.
Athe akufikelela ebudaleni, izinto zatshintsha. Abazali bakaBongani babezizinhanha, wathi akupasa imatriki bamthumela eyunivesithi . UNdumiso yena kwanyanzeleka ukuba axhome esikolweni eneBanga lesiBhozo waza wazifumanela umsebenzi ukuze ondle oodadewabo nonina ongumhlolokazi.
UBongani waba ngusomashishini onempumelelo, okulusizi kukuba okukhona esiba sisinhanha kokukhona waya ephelelwa ngumdla kumhlobo wakhe wasebuntwaneni, uNdumiso.
Wafikelwa yingcingane ethi UNdumiso lo akandifanele. Unxiba iimpahla zexabiso eliphantsi, uhlala emathyolweni. Abantu baza kundijonga njani xa ndinxulumana nabantu abafana naye?
UBongani uye eziphakamisa, akamhoya umhlobo wakhe omdala. Kodwa ilishwa laliza kumfundisa isifundo uBongani. Kuthe ngaminazana ithile emva kwetheko nabahlobo bakhe abazizinhanha, uBongani wenzakala kwingozi yemoto , wangxwelerheka.
Loo ngozi yamenza wangabinako ukusebenza, kungekho nomntu onokumondla kuba nabazali bakhe basweleka.
Ngequbuliso uBongani owayedumile wayehlupheka engenamncedi, elilolo, Abahlobo bakhe abazizinhanha babengafuni ukumbona kuba wayengekho kumgangatho abakuwo.
Kodwa kwakukho umhlobo amlibala kudala nowathi gqi engalindele.
Bongani, nguwe nyhani lo mhlobo wam? Kwenzeke ntoni kuwe? uNdumiso wambuza exhalabile.
Uthe uNdumiso akuva ibali elibuhlungu lomhlobo wakhe wamcela ukuba ayokuhlala naye kwityotyombe lakhe.
Ungathini ukundiphatha kanje emva kokuba mna ndingazange ndikuhoye? uBongani wambuza ekhwankqisiwe.
Ubuhlobo bokwenyani buvavanywa xa
izinto zingakuhambeli kakuhle. Mna uya kuhlala ungumhlobo wam nokuba sekumnyam entla, watsho uNdumiso encumile.
