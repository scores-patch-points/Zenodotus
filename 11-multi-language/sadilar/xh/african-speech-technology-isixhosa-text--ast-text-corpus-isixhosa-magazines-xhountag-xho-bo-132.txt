KWABAPHATH l
ABATSHA
Inkqubo yokuthathwa ngabaphathi abatsha kwenkampani yakwaPerskor ebi ngaphantsi kwayo IBONA nezinye iimagazini elishumi, ndingatsho ukuba ide yafikelela esiphelweni. lbe yinkqubo ende nebhidisayo etsale iinyanga ezilishumi zonke. lnto enifanele ukuba niye nayiqaphela kukuba bezingekho izibhengezo zerediyo ebezibakho zonke iinyanga zinixelela ukuba ngawaphi amanqaku akwiBONA. Kuye kwanyanzeleka ukuba sikhe sizimise ezi zibhengezo ngoxa kusaqhubeka iingxoxo phakathi kwabaphathi abadala nabaphathi  abatsha bakwaCaxton Magazines.

Kubalulekile ukuba sinixelele obekuqhubeka ukuze nazi ukuba kuqhubeka ntoni kwimagazini yenu. Kufanele nazi bafundi ukuba ngoku sisebenzisana nenye yeemagazini ezaziwayo eMzantsi Afrika, iPace. Siyavuya ngokuba phantsi kwabaphathi abanye nemagazini efana nePace eyaziwayo ngokuba namanqaku anomdla.

Njengoko unyaka-mali wethu uqalisa kule nyanga silindele ukuba siqalise ukwenza zonk izinto ebesiziyekile. Senza amacebo okuba nisoloko niziva izinto eniziphathelwe yiBONA ngokubhengeza kunomathotholo, kumabonakude nangezinye iindlela. Abaphathi bethu abatsha bakhe bawuxhasa ngokupheleleyo umbono wethu wokungena kule nkulungwane yeminyaka siseyimagazini ephambili eMzantsi Afrika. Nina bafundi sinithembisa ngokuba siza kuniphathela amabhaso nezipho eziza kuhambelana namanqaku anomdla esiwabhalayo. Kwakhona zisavuliwe iingcango zokuba nisinike amacebo enu namanqaku angaba negalelo kwimbono yethu. Njengoko lugqithile unyulo, lixesha elihle lokuba sisebenzise amacebiso amatsha.

Siyakholwa ukuba amanqaku akolu shicilelo anomdla ongathethekiyo. Kudla ngokuba lula ukubalula inqaku elithile njengelona linomdla, kodwa alu shicilelo lunamanqaku amaninzi anomdla. Siniphathele ibali elingoAlyce Chavunduka esimqhele kumabonakude kunye nebali elingeqela laMaZulu kwezemidlalo nekungamanqaku esizimisele ukuba sisoloko siniphathela amanye anomdla njengawo. Nangona kunjalo uya kuqaphela ukuba asinalo ibali lemifanekiso nomhlathi ohlekisayo kule nyanga. Sibone ukuba masingazikhuphi kule nyanga.ukuze sifake amanqaku neefoto esibone ukuba ziza kude ziphelelwe Iixesha.

SISIBHEDLELE OKANYE IHOKO YEEHAGU?
Ngokwento endiyibone ngala wam amehlo nebonwe nangabanye isiBhedlele saseMamelodi sifana nqwa nehoko yeehagu. Singcolile, bambalwa oogqirha, amayeza awekho nabongikazi ababaphathi kakuhle abaguli. Babaphatha gadalala bengakhathali nokuba umntu ubhubha ekhwele kwinqwelo yokuqhuba izigulani. Mhla ndandibeleka izibulo lam, ndandingomnye wabaguli ababekwiwadi yokubeleka ababebethwa. Omnye umguli kwafuneka azale umntwana wakhe elele phantsi, ngokutsho kwabongikazi. Kwakungafuneki besebhedini kuba kwakufuneka benze imithambo! Baphi abahloli abanokubona ezi zimanga? Noko kukho ekumele kwenziwe malunga nale miba.
nguM.M., eMamelodi.

UCALUCALULO LUNGCONO
KUNEDEMOKHRASI
Nyakenye ndaya eLouis Trichardt ndiyokufuna umsebenzi. Sasibasi-8 apho: abathathu ngabaseMzantsi Afrika, abahlanu ingabaseZimbabwe. Bonke abaseZimbabwe baqeshwa akukho namnye waseMzantsi Afrilca owaqeshwa kuba umqeshi esithi thina sikumbutho olwela amalungelo abasebenzi. Ngaba kunokulindelwa ukuba sivotele iANC? Baqesha abantu bakwamanye amazwe endaweni yethu. Mihla le kumaphepha-ndaba esiwafundayo nakumabonakude sibona ulwaphulo-mthetho. Ulwaphulo-mthetho lungaphela njani? Asiphangeli, ukanti abantu bakwamanye arnazwe koomaZimbabwe, Ghana, Mozambique, Tanzania naseMalawi bafumana imivuzo emihle ukanti thina iphango lisiphethe. Bobuphi obu bomi bungcono kuthethwa ngabo?
Ngeemini zocalucalulo sasiphangela nolwaphulo-mthetho lwalungaxhomis amehlo kuba abantu babephangela. nguRobert eMutale.

ISITHANDWA ASINGOMATSHINI
OKHUPH 1MALI
Ndiwathele nqa amanenekazi athe phithi ngabafo abanemali eninzi nabaqhuba iimoto zexabiso. Uthando olu alusekelwe emalini. Uthando kukuthemba, ukukhathala nokuqonda. Kwiivenkile ezithengisa ukutya la mabhinqa aoda ukutya abangakwazi nkqu nokuba kuxabisa malini. Amadoda aphathwa oku kweematshini ezikhupha imali nto Ieyo endicaphukisayo. Andiqondi ukuba la mabhinqa afuna ntoni kanye, ngaba emba igolide. Kwelinye icala, amadoda kufuneka alumke - imali ayinakuluthenga uthando. Yakuphela imali nothando lutsha okomlambo mini kuqatsele imbalela.
nguPhilile K. Shabalala, eLadysmith.

MAKAHLONITSHWE NAWO

Abanye abafazi bawuthatha wonke umrholo wabayeni babo babashiye bengenayo nesenti. Banikwe imali yokuya emsebenzini ukuze bayokusebenzela imali abazakuyizisa ekhaya. lngaba ayikokuxhatshazwa kwamadoda oku?
