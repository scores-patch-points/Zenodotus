Ukuthengisa ngemizimba akukhange kube negalelo kakhulu kuqoqosho lweli kuba akugunyazwanga ngumthetho. Ukuthengisa ngomzimba yinto eyenziwayo neza kuqhubeka nokwenziwa sithanda singathandi, nokuba igunyazisiwe na ngumthetho okanye ayigunyaziswanga. Uqoqosho lweli lunokuhlomla kwimali yerhafu xa kunokugunyazwa ngumthetho ukuthengisa ngomzimba. Urhulumente ufanele aqinisekise ukuba iikhondom ziyasetyenziswa, ukuthi amabhinqa aya ayoxilongwa rhoqo nokuthi oonongogo ababhalisiweyo bafumana umvuzo obakhusela bangaxhatshazwa ngabaxumi babo. Amaphepha-mvume okuba ngunongogo afanele angawanikwa amantombazana aneminyaka eli-1 6 nangaphantsi, abanezifo ezisulelwa ngesondo nabaneAids, kanti bafanele babanjwe oonongogo abangenawo amaphepha-mvume. La manyathelo aya kunciphisa ukusasazeka kwezifo ezisulelwa ngesondo, kwaye oko kuya kunceda abantu ababhenela ekudlwenguleni kuba befuna ukuzanelisa.
