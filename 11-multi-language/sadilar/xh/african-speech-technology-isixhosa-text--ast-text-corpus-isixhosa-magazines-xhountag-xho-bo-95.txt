BAKHETHISISE ABAHLOBO


 Molweni Bantwana! Kumnandi ukudibana nani kwakhona!
Kudala kwakukho nkwenkwana ithile egama linguSenzo Zuma. Wonwaba kakhulu uSenzo bakuthi abazali bakhe baza kuyohlala edolophini enkulu. Kaloku wayesele ewavile amabali amnandi amalunga nentlalo yasedolophini kwaye naye, njengoko eyinkwenkwana yasezilalini eneminyaka elishumi elinambini ubudala, wayesele eza kuzivela obo buncwane kusoloko kuthethwa ngabo ngabantu.
Kodwa kuthe kusiba yinyanga yesibini ukuya phambili babe ubomi bungasekho mnandi njengakuqala kuSenzo. Abantwana esikolweni babemphatha kakubi kwaye kungekho mntu ufuna ukuba ngumhlobo wakhe.
Ngenye imini wahlangana neqela lamakhwenkwe amahlanu. Ubonakala ulilolo, siXhosanat Awufuni ukuba lilungu leli qela lethu7 ubuze watsho uMandla, nowayengoyena umdala kweli qela.
Ewe, ndingathanda ukuba ngomnye wenu! waphendula ngokungxama okukhulu uSenzo.
Ngaphambi kokuba ukwenze oko, kufuneka uzibonakalise ukuba ungumhlobo wenene, watsho uMandla, ahleka amanye amakhwenkwe. Kulungile! watsho ukuphendula uSenzo. Ndiyakukwenza konke enifuna ndikwenze!
Jonga ke! uyalibona elaa nenekazi linxibe ilokhwe ebomvu lihla ngendlela7 Ndifuna uyokuxhwila isipaji salo uze ubaleke naso kangangoko unako. Siyakudibana nawe evenkileni kwaJabula ngentsimbi yesibini sahlule imali ekuso.
Akazakhange awuthande lo mbono uSenzo kodwa kunjalo akafunanga kuba ngathi uligwala kuloo rnakhwenkwe.
Heyi! Kutheni ungxame kangaka kwedini? Khona isipaji senenekazi sifuna ntoni kuwe? labuza ipolisa limbamba ngengalo uSenzo.
Ngenxa yokothuka akazange abe naqhinga lakuphendula wasuka walibalisela ipolisa elo ibali njengokuba linjalo.
Akulunganga ukufuna ukukholisa abahlobo bakho. Kwaye ngelishwa umsebenzi wam kukubamba, latsho ipolisa.
USenzo walala eziseleni zamapolisa ngobo busuku. Ngemini elandelayo wavela phambi kwenkundla yamatyala esoyika. Waxelela ijaji isizathu sokuxhwila kwakhe isipaji senenekazi wathembisa kananjalo ukuba akasayi kuphinda.
Kuba eli ilityala lakho lokuqala, ndiyakulumkisa ukuba ungaze ube kwakhona. Ukuba uphinde wabanjwelwa kwa esi senzo sinye andiyi kwenza nto yimbi ngaphandle kokukuthumela kwintolongo ekuvalelwa kuyo ulutsha olungevayo, yatsho ijaji ibukeka ingqongqo ebusweni.
De kube lixesha elizayo bantwana nisale kakuhle!
