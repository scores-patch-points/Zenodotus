UNtombi
UNtombi Gasa oneminyaka engama-21 waqala ngo-1993 ukudanisa yaye uyakuthanda. Wafunda ukudanisa emva kokufunda kwinkampani ekufutshane yokudanisa nasemva kokuvavanywa ngempumelelo ngabe Jazzart.

'Ndiyakuthanda ukudanisa. Ndiyayithanda imidlalo yeqonga enomculo njengothi' Evita 'nothi 'The Wiz'. Ndiqale kunyaka ophelileyo ukudanisa kule midlalo. Sikhe satyelela naseJamani. Apho besidanisa nabantwana abagogekileyo. Bekuchukumisa gqitha ukudanisa nabo.'

Akusoloko kumnandi yaye kulula ukudanisa, kukho neminye imisebenzi. Abadanisi abaninzi basebenza nzima. Ukudanisa kufuna amandla nokusebenzisa ingqondo. Baninzi nabantu abafuna ukudanisa. Maxa wambi kuza abantu abangama-200 kuvavanyo kube kufuneka abantu abathathu okanye abane.

UNtombi uthi abafundi mabangayeki isikolo kuba befuna ukudanisa. Endaweni yoko, ngoxa besafunda banokuya kufunda ngeempela-veki okanye sakuphuma isikolo.

Ukudanisa ngumsebenzi kodwa onzima. Abantu abaninzi abafumani mali ininzi, ngoko kufuneka ucingisise ngaphambi kokuba ube ngumdanisi. Xa ubucinga ngokwenza omnye umsebenzi kufuneka umdaniso kube yinto oyithandayo. Ungatshintshi umsebenzi onqwenela ukuwenza ngenxa yokudanisa.

Ukuphathwa kakubi emzimbeni

Ukophuka nokwenzakala kwengqondo
Olu lolona hlobo luxhaphakileyo lokuphathwa kakubi emzimbeni. Baninzi abantwana abafayo ngenxa yoku beseneminyaka engaphantsi kweminyaka emibini. Phofu ke amaxesha amaninzi abazali badla ngokuthi umntwana uwile okanye uzenzakalise yena. Kunzima ukufumana inyaniso xa umntwana esoyika ukuthetha.
Ukwenzakala ngaphakathi
Umntwana udla ngokwenzakala ngaphakathi ngenxa yokubethwa kakhulu. Ugqirha nomongikazi banokutsho ukuba wenzakale njani.

Ukutsha
Abantwana badla ngokutsha ngengozi. Kukho iimeko apho abazali bamtshisa ngabom umntwana kuba bemohlwaya. Badla ngokutshiswa ngecuba. Kodwa kunzima ukubona ukuba utshisiwe kuba izilonda zokutshiswa licuba ziyafana nezilonda ezizivelelayo.
Ukutyiswa ityhefu
Abanye abazali banika abantwana utywala namayeza ukuze bangakhali. Kwakhona kunzima ukufumana ukuba umntwana utyiswe ityhefu kuba abazali badla ngokuzihlangula ngokuthi abantwana bazithathele iyeza okanye utywala.
