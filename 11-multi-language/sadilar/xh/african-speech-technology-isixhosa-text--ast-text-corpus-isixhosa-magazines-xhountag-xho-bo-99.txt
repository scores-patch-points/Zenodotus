IMPUMELELO YEQELA LEBHOLA
LABASE1YHINI KWAMANYE AMAZWE
ISoweto Ladies Football Club ibe nempumelelo emangalisayo kumnyhadala wemidlalo obuseSvenborg, eDenmark. Eli qela lebhola lasesoweto liphumelele kuyo yonke imidlalo esibhozo eliyidlalileyo! Apha sibona ikapteni yalo, uNelly Jomo Mthembu, ephakamise indebe eliyizuze eDenmark.



UGQWESILE EKUNCIPHISENI
UMZIMBA
Umhlali waseSunward Park nohlohla eEast Rand College of Education, uThokozile Magudulela onama-39 eminyaka, kutshanje ukhethwe njenge-1995 Weigh-Less Slimmer of the Year. Unciphise umzimba wakhe nge-31kg, esuka kubunzima be-99,4kg waya kutsho ku-68,4kg yaye uwufezile umnqweno wakhe wokuzinciphisa iinyanga ezisibhozo ngenkqubo yeweigh-Less. Uwuphumezile umnqweno wakhe yaye akasenangxaki kwicala lempilo nolonwabo. Ngaphambi kokuba ndinciphise umzimba wam, ndandiwuquma ngokuthetha gqitha nangokuthetha izinto ezihlekisayo ngokuba mkhulu. Kodwa babundililisa xa ndindedwa ubukhulu bam yaye ndikhathzwa ziingxaki ezininzi endandijamelana nazo ngenxa yobukhulu bam, utshilo.



IIMBALEKI ZIFUMENE INKXASO-MALI UMnu. Peter Atcock, uMlawuli weNgingqi wakwaTastic Rice kutshanje ubhengeze inkxaso-mali eza kunikwa iNatal Athletics Development Programme waphinda waqalisa ne-1995 Tastic Rice Cross Country Projects. Kulo nyaka bazimisele ukuqhuba 1-25 Rural Cross Country Events eza kuba neembaleki ezingama-310 000 eziza kuba nenxaxheba kuyo. Kuza kubandakanywa izikolo neeklabhu zeembaleki ekuququzeleleni olu gqgitso. Njengoko kudityaniswe iNatal Athletics neKwaZulu-Natal School Sport Forum, kuza kwenziwa ukhuphiswano olune lweengingqi.
INATAL PARKS BOARD IZUZE
IBHASO
INatal Parks Board izuze iASTA/Smithsonian Conservation Award yanyakenye. Eli bhaso lehlabathi jikelele linikwe uMlawuli oyiNtloko weNatal Parks Board, uGqr. G.R. Hughes, kwitheko ebeliselisbon. Ngeli bhaso bekunconywa igalelo layo kulondolozo lwendalo kwilizwe elisakhasayo.


Olu khuphiswano lweengingqi luza kuba seMntla-Natal (eNewcastle), lube seMbindini (ePietermarilzburg), lube kuNxweme oluseMantla (eShowe) nakuNxweme oluseMazantsi (eThekwini). UMnu. Alcock uthe balindele ukuba olu khuphiswano lube yintsebenziswano ephakathi kwezikolo nemibutho yamaphondo. Olu khuphiswano luya kuqukunjelwa ngokhuphiswano lweTastic Rice Cross Country Championships apho amaqela eengingqi aya kukhuphisana namanye amaqela. Uhambise wathi ukhuphiswano lweTastic Rice Cross Country luza kuba nomdla kakhulu kulo nyaka.

Sazi-mthetho,
Ndingumsebenzi wasefama owamkela ama-R250 ngenyanga. NgoSeptemba nyokenye umqeshi wam wathi ufumene amaphepha achaza imivuzo yabosebenzi basefama. Uthe la maphepha achaza ukuba abasebenzi kufuneka banyuselwe nge-30%, msebenzi ngamnye axhuzelelwe i-R6 kumvuzo wakhe. Ngaba ikho imithelho echaza imivuzo neendlela zokusebenza kwabasebenzi basezifama?
NdinguLM.S., eRoedtan.
Kuba usinike inkcazelo emfutshane kunz!ma ukukuphendula. Phofu ke, singasebenzisa imithetho emibini. nantsi:
a) Kuba iLabour Relations Act yolulelwe nakubasebenzi basezifama, unokuba lilungu lombutho wabasebenzi basezifama, xa ulilo kukho imali exhuzulwayo yamalungu alo mbutho. Lo mthetho ulawula imithetho enento yokwenza nokubhaliswa kwemibutho yabasebenzi nomqeshi, ithintela, isombulule iimbambano phakathi komqeshi nabaqeshwa, njl. b) I-unemployment lnsurance Act isetyenziswa ukuze incede xa obenlkela imali egula, ekhulelwe, ephelelwe ngumsebenzi. Okubalulekileyo kulo Mthetho yindlela ecaciswe ngayo ngayo imivuzo, ukuba wamkela i-R100 iza kunyuswa nge-30%. Iimall ezixhuzulwa emvuzweni zize zifakwe kwiUnemployment Insurance Fund zixhomekeka kumvuzo ofunyanwa ngumqeshwa.
Omnye umthetho wamalungelo abasebenzi basefama yiBasic Conditions of Employment Act, 10 mthetho uchaza iimeko asebenza kuzo umsebenzi, ngokomzekelo, iiyure azisebenza ngemini nezeveki, ixesha elongeziweyo nemali oya kuyifumana, iholide yonyaka neyaxa ugula. Phofu ke ayikabikho imithetho echaza umvuzo wabasebenzi basezifama. Ukuze ufumane amacebiso nenkcazelo ebhekele phaya, yiya kwiDepanment of Manpower ekufutshane nawe.

Sazi-mthetho,
Ndacela ukubolekwa imali eli R1 000 kwinkampani ebolekisa ngemali. Bathi mandikhuphe idipozithi eyi-R150, ndayihlawula. Ndithe ndakubuza ukuthi le mali ndiyibolekileyo ndiza kuyifumana nini, bathi ndiya kuyifumana ndakubathumela inombolo yepolisi yam. Kuba ke kungekho polisi ndiyithathileyo, abanakundinceda, ndifuna bondibuyisele i-R150 yam. Ndithini ukuze ndifumane imali yam? NdinguM.N. eMaqume.

Xa inkampani owacela imali-mboleko kuyo ibhaliswe njengebolekisa ngemali, banelungelo lokufuna idipozithi ukuze bakunike imali oyibolekayo. Phofu ke khawukhangele kuxwebhu lwesicelo semali-mboleko ukuze ubone ukuba sikho na isivakalisi esichaza ukuthi idipozithi ekhutshiweyo iyabuyiswa kusini na, xa usayine olo xwebhu akunandlela yakubacela babuyise imali yakho. Ukuba asikho eso sivakalisi, loo nkampani yimangalele kwiSmall Claims Court (unokuyifumana kwiiofisi zakwaMantyi,) ukuze bakuncede kulo mba. Kubalulekile ukuba ube nedilesi yesakhiwo seeofisi zale nkampani ukuze bakhutshelwe iisamani.
