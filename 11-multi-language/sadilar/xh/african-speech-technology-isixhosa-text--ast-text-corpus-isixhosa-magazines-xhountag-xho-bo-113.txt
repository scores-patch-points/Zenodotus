8 BONA XHOSA - JuIy 1995

AMAKHASI ABAFUNDI ABATSHA

UKhathazile Madlala usibalisela ibali lokudibana nokutshata kwakhe noGasa. Unqwenela ukulibalisela abanye abantu ibali lakhe ukuz'iNatal Worker History Project imbhalele lona. IConcertina. Ukhathazile ulandela intliziyo yakhe ibhalelwe bonke abafundi ukubakhuthaza ekubhaleni nasekufundeni.


Mhla ndaphinyiswa ngumfana othile


Ngenye imini emtshatweni ndaphinyiswa ngumfana uthile. Igama lakhe yayinguGasa waseMgodi, ekuyilali ekufutshane neyethu.
Ukususela ngalo mini yomtshato uGasa wayedla ngokundilinda apho ndikha khona amanzi okanye kwihlathi enditheza kulo. Endixelela ukuba akalali buhlayo ngenxa yokucinga ngam. Nokuthi xa ndinokwamkela uthando lwakhe ndiya kuba ndimenzele into enkulu emhlabeni. Wayedla ngokudlala iconcertina yakhe xa eze kundibona.

Kungekudala ndaqalisa ukuchitha amanzi ngabom kuba ndifuna ukuyokha amanye ukuze ndifumane nethuba lokumbona. Ndandiqonda ngelo xesha ukuba sendizomvuma. Kodwa ndandibona kungalunganga ukukhawuleza ndivume, ngoko andizange ndikhawuleze ndimvume.
UGasa wayengafuni ukuba ndibe yenye yeentombi ancuma nazo. Wathi, Ndifuna uye kuphekela umama, Wayengxamele ukuba ndibe ngumkakhe. Wathi kufuneka singachithi xesha.
Wathi ukuyondicela kutata, ukulobola nokwenza ezinye izinto kuya kuchitha ixesha. Wathi kubhetele sigcagce ekuyindlela emfutshane yokutshata.
Ngelo xesha ndasendimthanda ngokungazenzisiyo, ndaza ndavuma. Sagqiba ngomhla aza kundithatha ngayo. Andizange ndiyixelele mntu loo nto, nkqu nomakazi okanye umsakwethu omdala.

Ndandonwabe gqitha ngobusuku owayeza kundilanda ngabo kanga-
ngokuba abuzange buhle ubuthongo. Ndachwechwa ngaphuma ekhaya.

Ngentsasa elandelayo, uGasa wathumela abantu ekhaya bayoxela ukuba bangothuki kukulahleka kwam, njengoko isiko lisitsho. Bafika bathi:

Sizonazisa ngentombi yenu esiyithathileyo ngethokazi elimnyama elinamanqina amhlophe, nethokazi elimhlophe elineempondo ezitsolo. Babexoka bona, kodwa utata wabamkela ngezandla ezifudumeleyo.

Ndahlala kuloGasa ndaba yinxalenye yosapho lwakowabo. Oodadewabo bakaGasa, uNomaningi, uNomajoni, uNomsobo, uGwingci noMjaku, babephantse babe ziintanga zam.

Weza wazondilanda utata emva kweenyanga ezimbalwa. Ndandingazi ukuba uza kufika, ndothuka kuba sendibona intloko yehashe lakhe ikrobe endlwini yam. Wayenomsindo uMadlala. Wandithatha wahamba nam engakhange athethe nelimdaka.

Umxholo 19:
Ukutya nesondlo

Abantwana kufuneka batye ukutya okunezakha-mzimba ukuze bakhule bephilile yaye bomelele. Ukutya ukutya okufanelekileyo asikokutya kakhulu.
Kukho iindidi ngeendidi zokutya ofanele umtyise zona umntwana wakho  kwaye azibizi imali eninzi zonke ezo ndidi zokutya. Kufuneka uyazi indlela yokuqinisekisa ukuba uzifumana zonke izakha-mzimba ekutyeni akutyayo!

Kule nyanga sikunika amacebo okondla abantwana bakho ukuze babe sempilweni. Fundisa abantwana bakho ngokutya okunik'impilo!

Bafundise ukulima eyabo imifuno! Bafundise ukukuncedisa xa upheka ukutya okulula ukukupheka! Baya kufunda lukhulu ngokutya xa besenza ezi zinto!

Zintathu iindidi zokutya:
1. Ukutya okunik' amandla Ukutya kusinik'amandla. Rhoqo xa usitya umzimba wakho ufumana amandla athe kratya. Ezinye iindidi zokutya zikunika amandla ngaphezu kwezinye. Oko kukutya okuneswekile nokunesitatshi nokunamafutha neeoyile njengepapa, isonka, umngqusho, ibhotolo nejam.


2. Ukutya okwakha umzimba
Iindidi zokutya ezinceda ekwakheni umzimba ziquka ubisi, inyama, intlanzi, amaqanda, itshizi, amandongo-mane neembotyi ezomileyo.

3. Ukutya okusikhuselayo
Iziqhamo nemifuno ikhusela umzimba kwizifo. Imifuno enebala eliluhlaza kakhulu okanye enebala eliqanda, neziqhamo eziqanda zezona zikhusela umzimba. Imizekelo yazo iquka iminqathe, ikhaphetshu, ispinatshi, iiorenji nepopo.

Abantwana ekhitshini


Abantwana bayakuthanda ukupheka! Unokubafundisa izinto ezininzi ngokutya xa ubavumelana bakuncedise ekuphekeni ukutya okulula. Baxelele ngokubaluleka kokuhlamba izandla ngaphambi kokuba baphathe ukutya.

Khetha ukutya okukhawulezayo nokumnandi ukukupheka. Unako nokubaphekisa iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya ukuze bakuqhele ukuzipheka nokuzitya ezo ndidi zokutya! Nazi ezinye izidlo onokuzipheka nomntwana wakho!

1.Iisandiwitshi:
Yitsho abantwana bakho bakhethe into abafuna zenziwe ngayo iisandiwitshi zabo. Basenokukhetha phakathi kwepeanut butter, ijam, itshizi, nebhanana... Bavumele balinge iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya.

2.Isaladi yeziqhamo:
Abantwana bayathanda ukwenza izinto ezilula njengokunqunqa iziqhamo.

mnike isiqhamo aza kusinqunqa.

Zidibanise ezo ziqhamo zakunqunqwa uze wenze isaladi yeziqhamo enokutyiwa ngumntu wonke.

3. Ukupheka imifuno:

Vumela umntwana wakho akhethe kwimifuno eyahlukeneyo anqwenela ukuwulungisa nokuwupheka. Mncedise ekuyixobuleni imifuno. Ukuba mdala mbonise indlela obuza kuyipheka ngayo imifuno. Ngoxa ubafundisa ukupheka abantwana, unokubaxelela iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya. Kulindeleke ukuba uyitye nawe imifuno oyipheke nabo! Ngokupheka ukutya nabo, unako nokubafundisa izinto ezahlukeneyo. Banokufunda ngencasa nangevumba lokutya okwahlukeneyo.

Umsebenzi 2
Kwikhitshi lakho yenza iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya uzigalele ezityeni ezincinane. Kusenokuba ziziqhamo ezahlukeneyo, iflawa, iswekile, iqanda, ijam, izinongo, njalo-njalo. Yitsho abantwana bakho benze oku kulandelayo:

1. Ukuva ukutya
Azivakali ngokufanayo iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya. Yithi
abantwana mabakuphathe ukutya ukuze bakuxelele ukuba kuvala njani Ngokomzekelo iflawa icolekile kunetyuwa. Oku kuya kwenza bakwazi ukwahlula izinto ngokuziphatha.

2. Ivumba
Unako ukwenza abantwana bakwazi ukwahlukanisa amavumba ngokubheka banukise iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya baze baxele ukuba kutya kuni.
3. Incasa
Abantwana bayathanda ukungcamla iindidi ezahlukeneyo zokutya nokuxela indlela okunencasa engafaniyo ngayo. Ngokomzekelo bangeva ukuba ilamuni limuncu gqitha kuneorenji.







Kuvela phi ukutya?



Abantwana abaninzi bayamangaliswa bakufumana apho kuvela khona ukutya. Baye bangakholwa ukuba iminqathe ilinywa emhlabeni nokuthi iiapile ziphuma emithini. Abakholwa xa ubaxelela ukuba ubisi lusengwa kwiimazi zeenkomo.

Indlela yokufundisa abantwana ngokutya nangendawo okuvela kuyo kukuba ubafundiswa indlela yokukuvelisa.

Ukulima iimbotyi okanye iierntyisi:
1. Thatha iimbotyi okanye iierntyisi ezintsha.
2. Zifake emanzini iiyure ezingama-24.

3. Yahlulu inkonkxa yejam ngephepha lokuya ngasese elifumileyo.

4. Galela intwana yamanzi kwinkonkxa yejam.

5. Faka iimbotyi okanye iierntyisi phakathi kwephepha nebhotile yejam.
6. Beka ibhotile kwindawo ekhanyayo.

7. Bukela iingcambu namagqabi ephuma.
Xa unesitiya onokutyala kuso imifuno unokuzityala ezo zityalo zembotyi okanye iierntyisi kuso. Qinisekisa ukuba ziyankcen-kceshelwa yaye abantwana banokumana beyozikroba.

Siyathemba unako ukuyisebenzisa loo mbotyi xa upheka kwixesha elizayo. Phofu ke uya kube uphumelele ukubabonisa indlela okulinywa ngayo ukutya nokuthi ukutya esikutyayo kusinika amandla.
