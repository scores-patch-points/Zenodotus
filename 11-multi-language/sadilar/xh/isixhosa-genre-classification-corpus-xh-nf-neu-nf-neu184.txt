Umthi
Wazalelwa phantsi komthi. Ngelo xesha ke kwakungekabikho neeselfowuni kwaye unina walunywa engekho uyise kwaza kwafuneka alinde de kubuye ubawokazi wakhe ukuze amse esibhedlele.
Kodwa uThapelo wayengxamele ukuza emhlabeni kwaza kwanyanzeleka ukuba uyisekazi amise endleleni.
Uninakhulu kaThapelo uCarol kunye nomhlobo wakhe uMa Flansie bamhlisa emotweni unina baza bambelekisela khona apho phantsi komthi womkhamba.
"Umama wandibalisela eli bali xa ndandineminyaka engama-22 ndaza ndayowubona loo mthi. Ndandifuna nje ukuwubona, ndiwuphathe, ndiwufote. Ndafika ikukuphela komthi oseleyo emva kweenguqu eziliqela ezenzekileyo.
"Ukhe ubone ukuba abantu bawaphatha njani amatye abo ethamsanqa" Laa mthi ke uyinto yam yethamsanqa - womelele kwaye uyandomeleza nam. Ndiza kwenza umzobo wawo emlenzeni wam," utsho uThapelo.
