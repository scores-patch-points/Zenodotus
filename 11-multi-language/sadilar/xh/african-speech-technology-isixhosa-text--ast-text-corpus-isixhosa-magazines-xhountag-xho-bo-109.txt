Umbane
iSiqendu 1
Njengoko ngoku kuza kufakelwa umbane emizini emininziebingenawo ngaphambili, kubalulekile ukuba wonk'umntu azi indlela oveliswa ngayo umbane, uncedo lwawo, iingozi zawo kunye neenkcukacha zokhuseleko nokuwonga. Siza kukuchazela kangangoko sinako ukuze niwonwabele umbane nosapho lwakho.

INDAWO OVELA KUYO UMBANE
Umbane uveliswa kwipower stations kusetyenziswa amalahle okanye ioli, amanzi (hydroelectric) okanye iuranium (nuclear power).

Injongo kukwenza umphunga onamandla okanye amanzi ehla phezulu. La mandla enza iibhleyidi zeturbine zijikele okwefenize. Njengoko iturbine ineshalt ende ejikelezayo eqhuba ijenereyitha. Amandla aveliswa yiturbine ejikelezayo ajikwa abe ngumbane yijenereyitha.

Kusetyenziswa iuranium namalahle okanye ioli xa kuveliswa umbane womphunga kanti kusetyenziswa amanzi ukwenza umbane wehydroelectric. Izikhululo zombane wamanzi aziyivelisi inkunkuma ebonakalisayo ubume bemeko yendawo.
INDLELA OTHUNYELWA NGAYO
UMBANE
Iintambo ezinkul ziwuthwala umbane ukusuka kwijenereyitha unamandla amakhulu zize ziwuse kwiitransformer eziwunciphisa amandla awo ukuze ube nokusetyenziswa emakhaya, eziofisini nakwimizi-mveliso.

Iintambo ezithwala umbane zineengcingo ezininzi ngaphakathiezigqunywe ngeplastikhi okanye irabha ukuze zingabi nabungozi. Xa uphatha intambo ethwala umbane inokukukhaba ikubulale.

Umzimba womntu unjengezi ngcingo kuba uyawuthwala umbane Xa umbane ufumene ithuba lokuphuma entanjeno uze ungene nakwintoni ethwala umbane njengamanzi, intsimbi, umzimba womntu kodwa awuhambi elukhunini, kumoya owomileyo okanye iphepha elomileyo.

Iintambo zombane ezisuka epower station zifika kwakho zihamba phantsi komhlaba okanye zihamba kwizibonda ezikhulu esidla ngokuzibona. Zifika ekhaya zingena kwibhodi ethumela umbane kumagumbi
sewulapha umbane kufuneka ukhuseleke ebantwini abawusebenzisayo.

Uya kuqaphel;a ukuba loo bhodi inamaqhotsha amhlophe azicima ngokwawo xa esetyenziswa ngokugqithisileyo. Oku kwenza abe shushu yaye kusenokwenza kutshe nokutsha. La maqhotsha abizwa ngokuba ziiHy-Mag Circuit Breakers yaye xa ezicimile awugqithi umbane kuwo. Zisenokuba shushu kwakhona iintambo zombane ngenxa yesixhobo sombane esingalunganga. Wakusiqaphela isixhobo esingalunganga nesiwisa iqhotsha sicime uze uphinde ulinyuse kwakhona. Kwenzeka oko nakumaqhotsha aluhlaza, phofu ke wona akhusela ubomi bomntu.



Njengoko sithe umzimba womntu uthwala umbane la maqhotsha kuthiwa ziiEarth Leakage Protector abonisa apho kukho undonakele yaye ayazicima ngokwawo ukuze asindise ubomi. Ungaghathwa ngabantu bathi ungawafaki la maqhotsha ukuze wonge imali. Ayinakukunceda nganto imali xa ahlukeneyo edla ngokuba sekhitshini. Xa seuwphulekena nobomi bakho.
