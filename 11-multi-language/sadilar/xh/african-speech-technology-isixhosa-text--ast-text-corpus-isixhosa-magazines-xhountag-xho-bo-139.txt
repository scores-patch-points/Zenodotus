iBONA. Naku akutshiloyo:
Mandiqale ndithi yandicaphukisa into eyathethwa nguNdou . Bekungekho mfuneko yokuba athethe ngolo hlobo. Uyazi ukuba ufanele athini xa efuna nyhani ukulwa nam. Isizathu sokuba ndivalu mlomo nangona endinyola kungenxa yokuba ndiyimbethi-manqindi evuthiweyo - kunjalonje oko akuphelele eqongeni kuphela. Kukwathetha indlela ubani aziphatha ngayo xa engekho eqongeni lamanqindi.
Utata owandazisa lo mdlalo undicebise ukuba njengembethi-manqindi evuthiweyo ndingakuvumeli kudicaphukise okutshiwo nguNdou . Okwenziwa nguNdou akufuneki kwaye. kubonisa ukuba akavuthwa. Mna ndibona ukuba xa iimbethi-manqindi ezimbini zidelene kufuneka zikhwele eqongeni lamanqindi zingagibiselani ngamazwi kumajel endaba. Ligwala elenjenjalo.

Time Bomb Ndou ungahle uphele ejongile xa unokuphulaphula okuthethwa nguTshabalala. Unenkolo yokuba UNdou yimbethi-manqindi eselula nesetyenziswa ngabaqeqeshi bayo ukuba ikhuphe izixhiba ezidala.
Utshabalala, uBaloyi nezinye iimbethi-manqindi ezininzi bashiye inkampu kaNick Durandt , umqeqeshi kaNdou malunga neminyaka emibini edlulileyo emva kokuba uDurandt wangena engxakini ngenxa yeentetho zobuhlanga ekuthiwa wazitsho.
Ndiza kumyeka athethe yonk into afuna ukuyitsho, andizuthoba isidima sam ngokuxambulisana naye. Kodwa kufuneka azi ukuba ikratshi landulela isiwo.
Umqeqeshi kaBaloyi, uElias Tshabalala, akamphikisi. Uyavuma ukuba uya kuba uchitha ixesha namandla akhe ngokulwa noNdou kuba abakho kwinqanaba elinye noBaloyi.
Ukuba uPhillip ubeyintshatsheli yehlabathi besiya kuluqwalasela ucelo-mngeni lwakhe. Okwangoku asinamdla kuye kuba akanto ukanti uCassius uligugu lethu.
	Hayi asinamdla kuNdou kuba asinakutya nto kanti uCassius ubalulekile, utshilo umqeqeshi kaBaloyi uElias Tshabalala.

Uqalise ukudlala eSoweto apho azalelwa khona kodwa kuthe ngo-1988 bafudukele eTembisa apho afumanise ukuba akukho nebala eli leqakamba.
Emva kokuhlala ndingadlali unyaka wonke ndidjbene noCrossby Tladiyane eyokuqhelisela ihockey , ukhumbula oko uDaniel onama-23 eminyaka. Siqale sangabahlobo sithetha ngalo mdlalo kwanocelo-mngeni olulo. Emva
kweentsuku ezintathu ndihambe noCrossby saya kuprekthiza kwibala laseTemong Section kufutshane neRoyal Beechnut Stadium .
uDaniel ukhawuleze wazibonakalisa ukuba ungumdlali othembisayo waza waya kwiModderfontein Development Clinic . Kungekudala ngasekupheleni ko-1992, uDaniel wenza inkqubela de wafumana indawo kwiTembisa Hockey club.
Emva kokudlala ihockey iminyaka emithathu, eyona mpumelelo yam iqalise ngo-1994. Kuthe ngo-1996 uDaniel wakhethwa njengomdlali wonyaka kwaza oku kwamvulela amathuba amaninzi -kwakuloo nyaka ube ngumdlali wokuqala omnyama kwiqela leHockey laseMzantsi Afrika le-Under 21 elalidlala kwitumente eyayiphakathi kweBotswana, iNamibia neSwaziland
