Utsho UNana Moloi 
ongumsasazi kamabonakude uthi ubomi bakhe bunokuguquka kunyaka ozayo - xa aya kuba egqiba amashumi amane eminyaka ubudala. Uthi okwangoku uxaxekile, akanalo ixesha lokuhoya indoda.

kubalisa Uwillie BOKALA

UNANA MOLOI linenekazi elisebenza nzima gqitha. Ungumsasazi kumabonakude, uphinde abe ngumchongi wabasasazi abaza kuvela kwinkqubo, kanti ukwangumama ongatshatanga wentombi ene-12 leminyaka, ngoko usoloko exakeke kangangokuba akanalo ixesha lendoda ebomini bakhe.
Indoda ifana nomntwana. Ifuna ukuhoywa kangangoko unako, mna ke andinalo tu elo xesha. Ukukhathalela intombi yam nomsebenzi wam
undixakekisa kangangokuba andinako ukongeza enye into. Ndikubona kungalunganga ukuba ndibe nendoda, ndijike ndingayihoyi ngendlela efuna ngayo, utshilo ngomzimba ongenaxhala.
Uvuka kwakusasa -alungisele intombi yakhe, uPaballo , aze amse esikolweni - yaye uphuma ebusuku emsebenzini akugqiba umsebenzi omninzi waseSABC . Akanalo nkqu ixesha lokuqhubeka nokucula nangona eselilungu lekwayala yakwaSABC .
Mininzi imisebenzi owayeyinqwenela xa wayesemncinane, kodwa yiSABC eyamnika umsebenzi athi ngowona msebenzi uyibetha yonke. Umsebenzi wakhe ulucelo-mngeni, yaye uyaziphucula ngokwenza izifundo ezinxulumene nomsebenzi wakhe.
UNana unxiba kakuhle yaye ukrele-krele. Ukususela mhla wanikwa umsebenzi wokuchonga abasasazi
abaza kuvela kuSABC2 , uyaqinisekisa ukuba bonke abasasazi bonwabile ngamaxesha abangena ngawo. Yiloo nto lo msebenzi ulucelo-mngeni kuye.
Njengoko eneminyaka engumsasazi kamabonakude -ubonakala engumntu onolwazi. Phofu ke uthi ukunyuselwa kwakhe ekubeni ngumchongi wabasasazi kwamvula amehlo. Kuninzi okwenziwa kulo msebenzi. Ndim endiceba ixesha abaza kungena ngalo abasasazi abahlukeneyo, neelwimi ekufuneka zisetyenziswe ngaloo maxesha.
Kwakhona kufuneka ndicebe amaxesha okusasaza kwabo kumabonakude ukuze angangqubani neminye imisebenzi abayenzayo ngaphandle kwaseSABC . Oko kufuneka ndikwenze ngendlela yokuba kwaneliseke wonk' umntu.
Nentombi yakhe, uPaballo , ngathi izimisele ukulandela ekhondweni likaNana.

Ngokokutsho kwakhe ungumpheki, ilasagna kokona kutya abalasele kakhulu kuko.
U-Allan ukwathe uyithanda gqitha iCapital Radio , nto leyo echaza isizathu sokuba abuyele kwesi sikhululo nokuba sehambile kwiminyaka edlulileyo. Ukwachaze nokuba iCapital Radio yeyona ndawo ibalaseleyo yokuqeqesha abasasazi abakhalisa amacwecwe, inkoliso yabo iye kwezinye izikhululo zosasazo njengeRadio 5, iRadio Good Hope , iEast Coast Radio nezinye.
U-Allan uthe iCapital iye yaneenguqu eziliqela ukususela ekusekweni kwayo ngo-1979, kwinguqu nganye ukuba bhetyebhetye nomgangatho oxhomileyo kuqhubeka kubalasela kwaye iye yaqhubeka ivelisa abasasazi ababalaseleyo kweli.
Uthe ngoku bajolise ekuzuzeni abaphulaphuli abaselula embalini yesikhululo sabo - abaneminyaka ephakathi kwe-16 nama-24. Bakwenza ngokuxuba iHip Hop , iRap , iReggae , iSoul . R&B neHouse and Acid  Jazz emculweni abawudlalayo.
