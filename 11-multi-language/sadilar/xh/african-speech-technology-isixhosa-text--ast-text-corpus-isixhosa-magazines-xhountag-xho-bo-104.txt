Umhla wesi-4 kuJulayi nguMhla weNkulilleko eUnited States of America. Lo ngumhla obalulekileyo enyekeni kumaMelika yaye lo nyaka ngowama-219 amaMelika afumana inkululeko eBrithani. AmaMelika ebesibukele, ebhiyoza nathi ngomhla wama-27 kuApirili ekungumhla wenkululeko kweli, nathi masibhiyoze nawo kule mini yawo inkulu. Njengathi, amaMelika ayathanda ukubhiyozela le mini ibalulekileyo ngokwenza iminyhadala, ukuba neentsapho nabahlobo, nangokutya okumnandi.

Irayisi esemgangathweni ilinywa eMerika.

Ngaphezu kweminyaka engama-90 phambi kokuba amaMelika afumane inkululeko kwiBrithani, irayisi ibinxalenye yokutya kwawo. Ingaphezu kwama-300 iminyaka iUnited States of America ilima yaye ithumela irayisi esemgangathweni kumazwe 



ngamazwe. Kuyacaca ke ukuba ngomhla we-4 kuJulayi irayisi ingundoqo kwiminyhadala yamaMelika. EMzantsi-Afrika yonke irayisi ivela kwamanye amazwe, kodwa nathi sinokonwabela ukutya okuthandwa ngamaMelika. Iindidi zerayisi eziphambili eMzantsi-Afrika zisebenzisa irayisi esemgangathweni elinywa eMelika.
Ukuze ungcamle umbhiyozo waseMelika zama le resipi iluncuthu usebenzisa irayisi esemgangathweni nelinywa eMelika.
Ezinye iiresipi zerayisi neziluncuthu ziza kuboniswa kumabonakude ngomhla woku-1 nowe-8 kujulayi ka-1995, ngeentsasa zangomGqibelo ngeye-9:00 kusasa kwinkqubo ethi Opposites And Equals. Abaphumelele kukhuphiswano lweRice Grown in America/Kelvinator baya kwaziswa kwiOppositesAndEquals ngomhla wesi-8 kujulayi ka-1995.
