AMABHINQA
MAKALI NGANE NABANYE ABANTU
Impumelelo nenkqubela yalo naliphi na ilizwe ixhomekeke ekubeni bonke abantu balo bafumane amathuba alinganayo ukuze abe negalelo kubutyebi balo. Kwiminyaka edlulileyo amabhinqa ebejongelwa phantsi yaye engahoywa. Nangona ngoku amathuba evuleleke kumntu wonke, amabhinqa aya kusoloko esala ngasemva xa engemi ngeenyawo alwele ilungelo lawo. Kufuneka azame ukuzithemba wona ngokwawo. Amabhinqa akasajongananga nocelo-mngeni lokukhetha phakathi kokuba libhinqa elisebenzayo okanye elihlala ekhaya, kodwa amele azenze zombini ezi zinto ngokulinganayo. Amabhinqa azimeleyo aluncedo kwiintsapho zawo nakuqoqosho lwelizwe. Ngaphandle kokuba amabhinqa azinike ngokwawo amagunya yaye afunde nokuzimela, iinzame zawo zokuphumelela ebomini ziya kutshabalala.
NguM.M., eWitsieshoek

INGXAKI YOKUTSHAYA

Asifanelanga silindele ukuba abantwana bangatshayi babe abazali neetitshala zabo zimpakuza icuba yonk'imihla phambi kwabo. Abantu mabayeke ukutshaya ukuze bahlale bephilile kunye nabantu abakufutshane nabo. NguMX, eSambo

UBOMI bunzima kuVusi Phiri
waseNazareth kufuphi nasepinetown, owenzakala ngokuhambis umzimba emva kokuba amapolisa ezame ukumbulala nge- necklace. Eyona nto ibuhlungu kukuba amapolisa ayezama ukumbulala akazange avalelwe nangona afunyanwa enetyala.
Uvusi oneminyaka engama-20 ngoku
ubudala, usayikhumbula into ebuhlungu eyamehlela ngomhla we-13 kuJanuwari ka-1992, xa wavuswa ngamapolisa amathandathu ezinzulwini zobusuku esithi makawanike umpu wakhe oyi-9mm.
Ndathi ndakuwaxelela ukuba andinampu, afuna endlwini kodwa akafumana nto. Emva koko athi aza kuhamba nam ayondincina ngeminye imibuzo.
La mapolisa ayengawePinetown Crime Prevention Unit, yaye ahamba naye uVusi afika ehlathini athi makaphume emotweni. Ahamba naye ngeenyawo angena phakathi ehlathini, afika apho athi makahlale phantsi.
Aqalisa ukundibetha, endikhaba enditshisa nangomatshini wokulumeka icuba entloko, ezinyaweni nasebusweni. Ayendigrogrisa nangokuba aza kundibulala xa ndingawaxeleli apho ndifihle khona umpu.
Kusenjalo indoda entsundu eyayihamba namapolisa, engazange indibethe xa ndandibethwa
ngamapolisa, yathi iza kundiyekela ezandleni zawo xa ndingatsho ukuba umpu uphi.
Yathi yakuhamba loo ndoda, ipolisa elinguMark Allan Lucy laya emotweni labuya nevili lafika lalifaka emqaleni kuVusi.
Kwathi kusenjalo uVusi wabona ipolisa elinguSimon Coetzee lisiya kwindawo enenjini yemoto lafika lafunxa ipetroli layigalela enkonkxeni.
UCoetzee wabuya wafika wagalela uVusi ngepetroli emalunga neelitha ezimbini entloko nasemzimbeni. Emva koko elinye lamapolisa elinguBradtord Brown, lalumeka uVusi ngomatshini wokulumeka icuba kwavutha amadangatye.
Abuya umva amapolisa xa sendivutha ebaleka ubushushu bamadangatye ndaza ndafumana ithuba lokubaleka ndingene phakathi ehlathini. Lonke elo xesha ndandizama ukucima umlilo owawunditshisa umzimba wonke. Ekugqibeleni ndalikhupha ivili emqaleni. utshilo uPhiri,
