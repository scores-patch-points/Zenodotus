 NANGONA isekude nokuphela imidlalo yeligi, abathandi bebhola nabalandeli bamaqela sebethetha ngendebe yeligi nangeqela elingayizuza.
Nakubeni kunjalo, kukho iimpawu zokuba iqela eliza kugqwesa kwiligi ekupheleni konyaka liza kuphuma phakathi kweOrlando Pirates, ekuyiyo eyikhuselayo le ndebe, Iwisa Kaizer Chiefs neSundowns.
Kutshanje ndithethe nomsasazi wezemidlalo kwiRadio Metro, uJoe Nkosi, kunye nentatheli yezemidlalo yeSowetan, uSbusiso Mseleku, ukuze ndifumane izimvo zabo nam ndibaxelele ngolwam uluvo ngabo mba.
Nazi izimvo zabo...
UMike Mtakati - iNtatheli Yezemidla/o yeBona: Omathathu la maqela akhe ayizuza indebe yeligi ngaphantsi kweNational Soccer League, kwaye iChiefs neSundowns ziyizuze ngaphezu kwesihlandlo esinye kanti iPirates yona ibiqala ukuyizuza nyakenye.
Ngokuqinisekileyo abantu abaninzi bajonge iPirates ukuba ibonise ukuba ukuzuza kwayo le ndebe nyakenye akuzange kube lithamsanqa nokuthi inabo abadlali enoyikukhusela.
Kwelinye icala, abaphathi nabadlali beChiefs bafuna ukubonisa abalandeli babo ukuba iqela labo lisengaMakhosi ngokuzuza le ndebe.
Nangona benqwenela ukukwenza oko, andikholwa ukuba eli qela lingayizuza indebe yeligi kulo nyaka, kuba lisafuna ukwakhiwa ngokutsha eli qela nokuphucula abadlali babo babe namava okudlala kwifirst division. IChiefs inabadlali abaselula endikholwa kukuba baza kuba yingozi kwamanye amaqela bakuba namava.
iSundowns ngathi inengxaki efanayo neyeChiefs, nangona inabadlali abambalwa abanamava abanjengolnnocent Buthelezi, uSizwe Motaung, noBennet Masinga
Eyona nto iza kulichana eli qela ngumqeqeshi walo omtsha, uClemens Westerhoff, osenengxaki yokukhetha abona badiali baliphumelelisayo eli qela. Ngathi unomkhwa wokuthembela kubadlali abadala ayeke ukusebenzisa abadlali abaselula nabanesiphiwo.
Ngoko kucacile ukuba yiPirates esethubeni lokuyikhusela ngempumelelo le ndebe yeligi, xa ujonga indlela abadlali bayo abadlala ngayo namava abo. Umqeqeshi wabo, uMike Makaab, ubenze badlala ngokuzimisela abadlali yaye oko kuyaliphumelelisa iqela lakhe.
UJoe Nkosi - Umsasazi Wemidlalo kwiRadio Metro: Andithandabuzi ukuba yiOrlando Pirates eza kuba� ziintshatsheli zeligi. Eli qela linayo yonke into efunekayo kwiqela lebhola ukuze lizuze indebe yeligi!
IBucs idlalisa abadlali abanamava nabayingozi kanti nangaphandle isala inabadlali abanamava.
Mna iPirates ndingayifanisa namaqela amanye azwe anjengeBlackburn Rovers neMachester United, ekungamaqela anabadlali abomeleleyo nto leyo iwenza aqhube kakuhle kwimidlalo yawo yeligi.
