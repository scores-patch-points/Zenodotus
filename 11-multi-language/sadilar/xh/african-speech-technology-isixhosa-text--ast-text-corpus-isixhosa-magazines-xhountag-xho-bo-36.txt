5.Phonononga amaphepha akho emali
Phambi kokuba ulahle nawaphi amaphepha , qiniseka ukuba iyahambelana nento obuyilindele ukuyibona - unalo ufifile ndleko zakho xa ububhala phantsi konke .okucetyisiweyo ekuqaleni.
Thelekisa amaphepha ebhanki neetshekhi ozibhalileyo,  imali oyikhuphileyo nemali oyibhatala rhoqo. Ukuba awusiboni isizathu saso nasiphi isiphene okanye kukho into ubona kungabalulekanaga ukubhatala enye into, qinisekisa ukuba ibhanki yakho ayenzi zimpazamo.
Ngokufanayo, jonga amaphepha emali omatshini wemali uwathelekise namaphepha owafumana ebhankini uthelekise namaphepha ecredit - card nendlela obhatale ngayo icounterfoil oyifumana xa ubhatala . Kwakhona buza nantoni na oyibona ingalunganga njengoko kusenokwenzeka ukuba kukho umntu okhupha imali kwi-akhawunti yakho, kwimeko enjalo inkampani inyanzelekile ukuba kubhatale imali yakho.


6.Zuzi iinombolo zakho
Ukugqibezela indlela ogcina ngayo iimali zakho, bhala uluhlu lwazo zonke iinombolo esizisebenzisayo yonke imihla - eyesazisi, eyelayisensi yokuqhuba, eyasebhankini , eyecredit cards ,  i-inshorensi ,  inombolo yomthengi yombane, yamatyala neyamatyala eempahla nokunye. Beka ikopi yolu luhlu kwifayile yakho, yenza ikopi yeqabane lakho wenze nenye oza kuyigcina, xa kukho imfuneko, kwifayile eyimfihlo emsebenzini ukuze ilalisele xa kunokugqekezwa okanye kutshe.
Landela ezindlela zintandathu uza kuyazi into okufuneka uyenze - lo nto ithetha ukuba uza kukhawuleza uzibone sele uliphumelela idabi okuphatha imali kunyaka ka-2000.
