Iindleko zokuthenga umzi
Amaxesha amaninzi abantu abafuna ukuboleka imali yokuthenga umzi kunzima ukwazi ukuba kufuneka babe namalini. Ngaba bafanele babe ne-R50 000 okanye i-R1 50 000? Ngaba baya kukwazi ukuhlawula izavenge ngenyanga nokuhlawula ezinye iindleko zomzi?

Iibhanki
Abantu abaninzi bafumana imali kwiibhanki. iibhanki zibolekisa imali ukuze kuthengwe imizi. Oko kuthetha ukuba xa umzi ungahlawulelwa, ibhanki iya kuwuthathela kuyo umzi.

Zininzi izinto eziluncedo ngokuboleka imali ebhanki kunokuyiboleka emntwini nje. Nazi izizathu zoko:

lmbali oyibolekwayo ayinaxesha limiselweyo lokuba uyihlawule (20, 25, 30 eminyaka).

Umntu unokuba ngowakhe umzi nokuba akakagqibi ukuwuhlawula umzi.

lmali yenzalo iyafana ezibhankini ngenxa yokukhuphisana kwazo.
