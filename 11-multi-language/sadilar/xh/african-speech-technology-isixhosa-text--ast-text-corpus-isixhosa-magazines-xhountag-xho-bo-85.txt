NDlSOLOKO ndizibuza ngendlela l abazaIi abaziva ngayo bakulahla abantwana babo. Oko ndikutsho ngenxa yabantwana abaninzi abayabula kwizitrato zaseJohannesburg bengenandawo yokulala ngokuhlwa.
Kunjalo, ndifuna ukuthetha kakhulu ngootata abalahla abantwana babo. Aba tata bayathanda ukuphika iintsana ezingekazalwa. Nokuba sele luzelwe usana utata uyakuluphika ukuba lolwakhe, nditsho nokuba lufana kangakanani na naye.
Kuluntu olumnyama oomakhulu ubukhulu becala kuye kufuneke bebakhulise ngokwabo aba bantwana bangaziwayo ootata babo. Abanye oomama, bambi belula kakhulu ukuba babe nabantwana, bashiyeka benxunguphele kungekho nto banokuyenza kangangokuba bagqibela ngokuzilahla emigqonyeni yenkunkuma okanye eziplastikhini iintsana zabo.
Ndibothulel umnqwazi oomama abomeleleyo, abathi nokuba sebephikiwe bazithande bangavumi ukuba zithotywe izidima zabo. Ndithetha ngoomama abaxolela ukuzikhulisela abantwana ngokunokwabo ngoxa ootata babantwana babo bezonwabisa ebomini bengenaso nesincinci isazela.
Uphando olusanda kwenziwa ezindabeni ludiza ukuba uninzi lwabantwana abaphelela ezintolongweni okanye ezitalatweni sukuba ingabo baphuma kumakhaya angenatata okanye abangabaziyo ootata babo.
lsizathu soku kukuba ootata ngokwesintu ngabona bantu baqeqesha abantwana, lilonke ukungabikho kwabo kuthetha ukuba akukho mntu unokubaqeqesha.
Phofu ke, abanye babantwana abakhuliswe ngoomama kuphela baba nempumelelo ebomini, babe zizinto kwaye babe ngumzekelo omhle kuluntu lwabo, ngokuchaseneyo nenkolo yabantu abaninzi. Oku kwenzeka xa umama enemali eyaneleyo okanye efundile nto leyo emnika amandla okumenzela konke okufaneleyo umntwana wakhe eyedwa
lnto embi kukuba xa sele beliphumelele ibanga labo leshumi aba bantwana, ootata babo bolahleko bayabuya benethemba lokuzixolelanisa naba bantwana kwaye badla ngokwamkelwa ngabantwana zilityalwe kwaphela ezezolo.
Kodwa ke xa uyiqwalaseta kakuhle Ie meko inokubonakala ngathi iyamngcatsha umama osokole kakhulu ezama ukuba unyana okanye intombi yakhe ibe kwindawo ekuyo.
Mna ndibona efanele utata
omphikileyo umntwana wakhe avalelwe kwezimnyama izisele kuba kaloku xa elushiyile usapho uya kuhlala nolunye usapho okanye pmntu obhinqileyo onabakhe abantwana. Oku kubonakalisa mhlophe ukuba banakho ukuzondla nezizezabo iintsapho - qha bafuna ukunyanzeliswa ukwenza oko!
Xa sewufikile umzuzu wokugqibela wokuphila, utata uye ababize abantwana bakhe afune ukuba bamxolele. Xa abantwana bemthobela uThixo baye bamxolele aze ngaloo ndlela afe eluxolweni.
Mna ndicebisa ulutsha oluyeka isikolo kuba kufuneka londle usapho ukuba malungalahl ithemba kuba uThixo wenza imimangaliso.
Xa utata wakho efe ethwel umnqwazi sukuzikhathaza, uya kubuya ngenye imini. Kwabo mama bometeleyo ndithi, uThixo anisikelele, ngelingento eli tizwe ngaphandle kwenu!
