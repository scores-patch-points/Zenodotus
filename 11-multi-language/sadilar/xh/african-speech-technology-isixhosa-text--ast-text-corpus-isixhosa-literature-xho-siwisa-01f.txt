Sisonka sethu
Amadoda aphesheya kwenxuba ayengamadoda aziwayo, afumfleyo, aneentlanti zeenkomo nezeebhokhwe, kunjalonje engamadoda anamagama.  Ayemaninzi ke namabali abaliswayo ngokuhluma kobo butyebi, phofu engenakubaliswa emini naselubala ngenxa yokoyikisela ukuphuma komphefumlo ngokweempondo entsomini.
UNxele, inkulu kandabangayi, iqabukele kwiintlanti ezintathu kowayo, ubuhlanti ngabunye buneselwa labo.  Phofu uyisemkhulu wayesel' atshaba kwangaphambili kombathalala.  Inqwelo yayo le ndedeba yayisondezwa kakhulu kwesi sifo, nangona eyona nyariiso yayiyeyokuphelela kwayo kwizibuko elalisemazantsi enxuba kufuphi nanazizi, apho yafunyanwa khona ikhulekwe ngeminxeba esikhondweni somngcunube, seyifile.  Wakhula naye unxele esazi esi sizathu sokusutywa kukayise omkhulu sesi sifo sasinamandla nezigigaba.  Uqabuke unkabi kunya kazela  yamahashe neyeebhokhwe neyeenkomo phakathi kwamasango kayisemkhulu, uyise yena eyinkweli eyaziwayo yodumo, njengenkoliso yamadoda aphesheya kwenxuba, ngezo mini.  Wabona kumana kufika iindladiya ngeendladiya zeengcungela zeenkabi zamahashe ngobusuku, kuse sezingekho.  Waqala ukuyiqonda inyaniso mhla wakhwela noyisekazi ngobusuku kwiinkabi zamahashe ezazifika kwangobo busuku, besiya kwisihlonti esasisemantla omqhokro wasezitshanganeni.  Badubula iintsuku ezintlanu apho beziqaba ngamasi onihlontlo phezu kweempuphu, nasezikolweni, bezijika iinibala.  Ngosuku lwesithandathu baqabela kuzo besinga elukhuko, kunantla ebhofolo.  Babuya ngeenqwelo zamabhulu ezazisiya edikeni.  Uthe akuphelelwa uyisekazi lowo, wathatyathwa ngunxele ngokwakhe lo msebenzi awayeselewuqonda kakuhle neengontsi zawo.
