82 BONA XHOSA - JuI 1995


UNANA ENERIA MASANGO. 


 INDLU yoWiso-Mthetho inaMalungu ePalamente angama-400, kanti
ali- 103 kuwo ngamabhinqa. INdlu yeeNgwevu (Senate) inamalungu angama-90, kanti ali-16 angamabhinqa. Aliqela amabhinqa angamalungu ePalamente asezikhundleni eziphezulu naziwayo ngabantu. Phofu ke akhona namabhinqa anomsebenzi omkhulu kodwa angaziwa kakhulu ngabantu. Nanga amanye aloo mabhinqa:
* UNANA ENERIA MASANGO (iLungu lePalamente leNATIONAL PARTY). UNana olinenekazi elidlamkileyo nowazalwa ngaphantsi kwenkwenkwezi yeAries, wayengumongikazi nosomashishini ngaphambi kokuba angene kwipolitiki. Wayeqhuba amashishini aquka iivenkile ezimbini zotywala, idisko enenight-club, indawo yokulungisa iinwele, indawo yokusela utywala, nelokucoca izitrato. Wayewaqhuba loo mashishini akhe kunye nomsebenzi wakhe wokuba ngumongikazi.
Waxhoma emsebenzini ngoJanuwari ka 1994 ukuze abe nguMququzeleli osisigxina we NP, waza wakhethwa waliLungu lePatamente ngoAprili ka-1994. Ukwasisithethi seNP kwiPopulation Development Forum, ulilungu leSocial Study Group, nelungu elikhutheleyo leWomens Action yeNP.
Akafikelelanga lula enkcochoyini uNana, ekwanyanzeleka ukuba ashiye umzi nomtshato wakhe ayoqala ubomi obutsha. Uthe: Ukukholwa kwam nokukhula ndiqeqeshiwe kwandenza ndazithemba yaye ndakhuthala kuzo zonke izinto zobomi. Ngoko ndikhetha ukuzibandakanya

ANGAMALUNGU EPALAMENTE!

* UPHUMZILE MLAMBO-NGCUKA (iLungu lePalamente leANC). UPhumzile ulinenekazi eliphethe iindawo ezimbini. Ngaphandle kokuba lilungu leNdlu yoWiso-Mthetho, ukwangumfazi weNgwevu noSekela-sihlalo kwiANC eNtshona-Koloni. Indoda yakhe nguBulelani Ngcuka.
Uphumzile onesidanga asifumana kwiNational University of Lesotho onenkwenkwezi esiScorpio, wayefudula eyititshala, umcebisi wabaphathi nomlawuli wemibutho eliqela. Ukwalilungu leRDP Select Committee noSihlalo wepublic Service Select Committee.

*UTHENJIWE KONA (eUniversity of Cape Town)
Ubonakala rhoqo kwiziNdlu zePalamente uThenjiwe Kono -igosa lemisebenzi elingaphantsi kweDevelopment and Pubfic Affairs Department eUniversity of Cape Town.
UThenjiwe wazalelwa eZimbabwe waza wabuyela elizweni abazalelwa kulo abazali bakhe ngo-1992. Yintoni umsebenzi wakhe? Kukuphucula ubudlelane phakathi koluntu, urhulumente neyunivesithi. UThenjiwe akanalusini tu. Uthi lixesha lokuba amaziko anjengeeyunivesithi norhulumente bayeke ukubonakala njengezinto ezikude nabantu, koko afanele asondele ebantwini.

* UTHEMBEKA GAMNDANA (INGWEVU YE-ANC).
UThembeka lelinye lamanenekazi asoloko exakekile. Ngaphandle kwemisebenzi yakhe emininzi yokuba yiNgwevu, uThembeka ukhuthele gqitha kwimicimbi yeANC Womens League.
Uyititshala eqeqeshiweyo yaseMpumo-Transvali, kwaye ungumntu ongqongqo ofuna yonk into iqhutywe ngendlela efanelekileyo. Kutshanje ube ngundaba-mlonyeni ngokuchasa ukutshwankathelwa koMhobe weSizwe. Abantu abaninzi bafile befela lo Mhobe weSizwe. Kungani usenziwa mfutshane nje ngoku? utsho
