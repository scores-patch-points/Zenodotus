 MVANJE kufike ityendyana lomfo oneenwele ezizaphothe kwiiofisi zeBona, uSimphiwe Mfeka. Lo mfo. bekuliRasta, kuqala ndimamkele ndingamthembi.
UnguSihlalo wevukuzakhe Community Development Foundation eseMlazi, enjongo yayo ikukwenza amaphulo oluntu aqondwe kwiofisi YeRDP eThekwini. Nakuba leFoundation inamaRasta amane angabasunguli bayo kukho
amalungu ayo ali-1 2 akumaqela ngamaqela epolitiki, unqulo kwanecawa yakwashembe. Buyintoni ubuRasta?
Okokuqala usichazele ngemibala yobuRasta. lmibala ebomvu, eqanda noluhlaza yalo mfuziselo isekelwe kwiflegi yase-Ethiopia endala
eyayibomvu emzantsi, igolide phakathi ize ibe luhlaza emntla. Obomvu umela igazi labo bonke abantu base-Ethiopia elaphalazwa ukuze kuzuzwe inkululeko yeli lizwe lamandulo. Oqanda umela iminerali ebubutyebi beli lizwe uze oluhlaza umele uhlaza lwamasimiEthiopia.

Kungani bekhatha ilizwe lase-Ethiopia? Libaluleke ngantoni? Ndimbuzile.

IEthiopia yindawo eyayinesitiya sase-Edeni, iparadisi encinane esifunda ngayo eBhayibhileni apho uThixo wababeka khona abantu bokuqala uAdam noEva; kungenxa yoko i-Ethiopia ingazange ibe lithanga. Ingumzekelo wenkululeko, utshilo uSimphiwe.

Athi mandikhulule yonke into endandiyinxibile emzimbeni wangezantsi. Lathi ipolisa lingibetha umvumbo wesithathu, lwaba uswazi selundikrazule inyama, ndisopha. Andizange ndikwazi ukuhlala ngeempundu inyanga yonke.
