IYAHLEKISA


USean Connery udlala indawo kaPaul Armstrong, ungunjingalwazi owaziwayo kwezomthetho eHarvard ochase isigwebo sentambo. Uyavuma ukuphanda ityala lalowo otyholwa ngokubulala eminyakeni esibhozo edluleyo.
Njengoko uArmstrong ebhuduzela ukuphanda ubungqina phambi kokuba umbulali (uBlair Underwood) agwetywe, uzibhaqa exabana qho
nepolisa lokuqala (uLaurence Fishbourne) elabamba umbulali, elipolisa liqinisekile ngelityala.
UArmstrong uzibhaqa ekumnatho wenkohliso engasongeli nje ngobomi bakhe kuphela kodwa nantoni na ayimelayo.

Kudlala uTonie Colette, uBill Hunter, uRachel Griffiths, uJeanie Dynan noGennie Nevinson.
iNOMCULO

Kule filim enomculo kaP.J. Hogan, uMuriel Hesiop ulindele uPrince Charming azokumhlangula kuyise osisigantsontso, kubahlobo abangoonongogo nakwindawo eyoyikekayo
kwidolophana iPorpoise Spit.

Ndithande ukubalisa ibali elingaqhelekanga ngendlela eqhelekileyo. Ibali labantu abaminqweno yabo iphoswe kwelokulityalwa kodwa sekunjalo bakwazi ukuyigcina, utsho uP.J. Hogan. ongumlawuli kule filim.
