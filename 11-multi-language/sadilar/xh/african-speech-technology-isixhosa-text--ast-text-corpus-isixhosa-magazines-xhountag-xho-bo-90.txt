Olunye ulusu luneengxaki ezininzi kunolunye. Lufuna ukunonotshelwa okukhethekileyo ngokulucoca nokulufumisa luze kwezinye iimeko luthanjiswe ngecream eliyeza. Ungawacudisi amaqhankqa okanye amaqhakuva. Xa ulucoca sebenzisa icottonwool endaweni yelaphu. Ukuze uthintele le ngxaki ingasasazeki lucoce ngecottonwool netawuli eyodwa yobuso bakho.
Kukho ezinye iinkathazo zolusu eziqhelekileyo:
AMAQHANKQA - athanda ukuvela kulusu olunyinyithekayo. Iindawo ezithanda ukuhlaselwa, zizidlele, ibunzi nempumlo. Akwachaphazela isifuba, intamo, amagxa nomqolo, apho kufumaneka amadlala eoyile. Amaqhankqa akahlaseli nje ulutsha olufikisayo, anokubakho nasebantwini abadala -ingakumbi abasetyhini.
Zama kangangoko ukulugcina ulusu lwakho lucocekile lunganyinyitheki. Hlamba ngesepha efana nePalmolive Green Natural Skin Care Soap with Extra Cleansing, Glycerine and Herbs kabini ngemini, uzame nokuba ubuso bakho ubugqume bufumane unphunga okwisitya esinamanzi abilayo. Emva koko ubuso ubuhlambe ngamanzi abandayo ukuze kuvaleke iintunja.
Ungatyi ukutya okunamafutha amaninzi, sela amanzi amaninzi, ungazisebenzisi kakhulu izithambisa kuba zivala iintunja.
IINTUNJA EZIVULEKE KAKHULU - Le yingxaki ethanda ulusu olunyinyithekayo. Ibangelwa kukungahlambisisi, ukungahambi kakuhle kwegazi, nokungasebenzisi izikhanyisi-lusu ezifanele wena. Xa unolusu olunyinyithekayo hlamba ngesepha engebukhali njengePalmolive Green Nalural Skin Care Soap with Extra Cleansing, Glycerine and herbs ukulucocisisa ulusu lwakho.
Hlambisisa ngamanzi abandayo, thambisa iyeza elishwabanisa imithambo ukuze zivaleke iintunja. Kulusu olomileyo' hlamba ngesepha iPalmolive White Natural Skin Care Soap, inezithako ezondla ulusu. Hlamba ngamanzi abandayo, landelisa ngeyeza elishwabanisa imithambo, sebenzisa isifumisi-lusu. Soloko uliphethe iyeza elishwabanisa imithambo okunye isijiki-bala ukuze uhlaziye ulusu lwakho ebudeni bemini. Zama ukusebenzisa isikhuseli-lusu kwiintsholongwane kanye ngeveki ukuqinisa iintunja. INYEBETHU - Esi sisifo esibangelwa yintsholongwane. Le ntsholongwane ingena emzimbeni xa kukrazuke ulusu, ihlala kwiiseli zemithambo-iuvo kufutshane nalapho uye wosulelwa khona. Xa udiniwe okanye ucinezelekile, le ntsholongwane isasazeka kulusu, ijikeleze ikakhulu imilebe, ibangele amadyungudyungu.
Loo ndawo yigcine icocekile, yomile, usebenzisa icottonwool ecocekileyo, yidyobhe ngealcohol solution efumaneka ekhemisti. Xa ingapholi, yiya kugqirha.
IZIBHAXU ZAMABALA - Zibangelwa kukutshiswa

lilanga, okanye zibangelwe kukusebenzisa iipilisi zokuthintela ukukhawula. Zivela ziphinde zinyamalale, unokuzinceda kule ngxaki ngokungagcakameli ilanga, sebenzisa isikhuseli-langa esikwasisifumisi.
Ezi zibhaxu zamabala unokuzifihla ngokuthambisa kuqala isikhanyisi lusu kuloo ndawo, yenza ulusu lufane ngokuthambisa isikhanyisi-lusu esiqhelekileyo kubo bonke ubuso. Mcele ugqirha wakho atshintshe isithinteli-kukhawula osisebenzisayo ukuba singunobangela wezibhaxu zamabala.
