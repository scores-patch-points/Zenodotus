Ugqirha Sipho

Gqirha endimthandciyo, Ndikufumanisa kunzima ukuhamba kuba amathanga am ayakhuhlana nto leyo ebangela ukurhawuzela kwesikhumba. Kubuhlungu. Ndithambise iintlobo-ntlobo zezithambiso kodwa akukho mahluko.
NdinguB.M., esoshanguve. Unobangela oqhelekileyo werhashalala phakathi kwamathanga ungenxa yokukhuhlana okubangelwa kukuba nomzimba omkhulu. Le rhashalala ide ibe mbi gqitha ngenxa yemozulu eshushu nefumileyo. Ukuba irhashalala ingenxa yokukhuhlana, inokupheliswa ngokusebenzisa icream yamadlala. Phofu ke, kwezinye iimeko irhashalala ibangelwa kukungunda, xa kunjalo kufuneka icream elwa nokungunda. Kanti ke khumbula ukuthi ukuwutha kusisisombululo esingakunceda umlibe.

Gqirha endimthandayo, Ndinesisu esibuhlungu, siyaqhina. Ndiwasebenzisa qho amayeza okurhuda kodwa ndinexhala lokuba anokuba yingozi. Enye ingxaki yam, kukulibala. Ndinama-30 eminyaka. Ndingalufumana phi unyango? NdinguLR., elusuthu. Akuphazami xa ucinga ngobungozi bokuwasebenzisa umlibe amayeza okurhuda. Kubalulekile ukusebenzisa iindlela zemvelo ukuze isisu singaqhini. Sela amanzi kakhulu, ebuncinaneni iiglasi zamanzi ezisibhozo ngemini (ingakumbi xa linyibilikis'amafuth'ehagu). Yitya ukutya okuneentsinga, njengesonka sengqolowa entlaka-ntlaka, irayisi emdaka ngebala, iAlbran flakes, ijungle Oats, isiqhamo okanye umfuno osandul ukukhiwa. Yenza imithambo qho. Xa kungancedi ndikucebisa ukuba ukhe uye kugqfrha akuxilonge. Ukuze ukhumbule ziqeqeshe kanti kwabanye abantu oku kufuna umzamo ogqithiseleyo kunabanye. Abanye banenkolo yokusebenzisa amayeza okanye okuneevithamin ukuze baphucule indlela abakhumbula ngayo kodwa oku kunjalo ingakumbi kwabo batya ukutya okungenazo izondlo eziyimfuneko.
