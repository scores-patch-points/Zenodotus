Kungabanga thuba lide wabandisa abaphulaphuli besi sikhululo lo mfo unelizwi elimnandi, kwaye wazifumanela namawakawaka abaphulaphuli. abamthandayo kulo lonke eli. Ngokuqinisekileyo lo msasazi uzenzele igama njengomnye wabasasazi abaphambili kweli abanjengoBob Mabena, uLawrence Dube, uTich Mataz noAlex Jay , xa sibala nje abambalwa. Uzingomba isifuba ngelithi inkqubo yakhe ethi Bigboy Aftermoon Drive Show ezithandwa ngokulinganayo neenkqubo eziqhutywa ngaba basasazi bakhankanywe ngasentla.
Ndiyayithanda inkqubo yam yasemva kwemini kuba ilucelomngeni. Izikhululo ezininzi zoonomathotholo ziyakhuphisana ngokufumana abaphulaphuli abaninzi, utshilo.
uBigboy ufike kwiRSS wasasaza le nkqubo ebifudula iphathwa nguOlihle Seraseng phakathi kwentsimbi .yesi-3 neye-5 emva
Ukususela oko uBigboy Mothusiotsile Moagi waqala ukusasaza kwiRadio Setswana Stereo uthandwa gqitha ngabaphulaliphuli besi sikhululo

kwemini ukususela ngoMvulo kuse ngolwesihlanu. Kungabanga thuba lide waqala ukufumana iincwadi neminxeba evela kubaphulaphuli. Ndandifumana iincwadi ezingaphezulu kweshumi ngemini zivela kubaphulaphuli ababevuyisana nam ngokuqeshwa kwesi sikhululo, bendixelela nendlela abayithanda ngayo inkqubo yam, utsho. Wayengaqali ukuba ngumsasazi uBigboy xa wayeqeshwa yiRSS . Wasibonisa isiphiwo sakhe sokuba ngumsasazi xa wayesebenza kwiRadio Mmabatho, apho waqala
- uBigboy MoThusiotsile Moagi, umsasazi othandwayo ngabaphulaphuli beRadio Setswan Stereo








ukuzenzela igama ekusasazeni kwiminyaka emihlanu ayisebenze kweso sikhululo. Waqeshwa yiRadio Mmabatho emva kokuba ephumelele iMatriki eKebalepile High School eMontshiwa. Ndazibona ukuba ndinesiphiwo sokuba ngumsasazi ndiseyimveku eneminyaka emihlanu qwaba. Ndandisoloko ndiphulaphule unomathotholo yaye ndinomdla wokwazi indlela osebenza ngayo. Ngenye imini ndavula unomathotholo wasekhaya ngaphakathi ndithi ndifuna ukubona abantu abathetha kuwo. Ndamangaliswa kukungaboni mntu ngaphakathi! Ndazixeleia ukuba ngoomatshini abathethayo asingobantu. Ndazixelelci ukuba nam ngenye imini ndiza kuba ngumatshini othetha kunomathotholo, utsho.
Xa wayesebenza kwiRadio Mmabatho, wakhethwa wangoyeno Msasazi Uphambili kwiiMarang Awards ngo-1991 nango-1993. Waphinda wazuza ibhaso leBest TV Actor Award ngokudlala kakuhle indawo yomfo othetha gqitha onguPapankie emdlalweni weBop TV othi Sepelong.
Ndakwazi ndiselula ukudlala emidlalweni yeqonga. Ndiseyinkwenkwe ndadlala emidlalweni yeqonga emininzi esikolweni nangeempela-veki . Abantu ababebukela imidlalo endandidlala kuyo babendithanda Ndathi ndakuphumelela iMatriki , ndenza izifundo zokuthetha nokudlala kwimidlalo yeqonda eMmabana Cultural Centre eMmabatho ndaza ndafumana isiqinisekiso, utshilo.
uBigboy uyaziqokelelela umculo aza kuwudlala kwiinkqubo zakhe kanti uphinde asasaze nemidlalo kwiRadio Setswana. Uthi umnqweno wakhe kukuziqalela esakhe isikhululo sosasazo njengoko urhulumente eyiguzule imithetho ebingayivumeli loo nto. Ngaphambile kokuba enguMiss South Africa olawulayo kwakunye nombhexeshi wenkqubo ethi Top Billing , uBasetsane Makgalemele usengulowa wayenguye ngaphambili







UBASETSANE ukholelwa kwizinto zamandulo kuba uthi zizo ezimnceda ekubeni azikhumbule ukuba ungubani kwaye nezinto aziqwalasele ngale ndlela ziyiyo Nangona kumnandi ukuphila
kule minyaka sikuyo masingaleqi yonke into le masizame ukuhlala siyinto esiyiyo, ngaloo ndlela siya kuphumelela, ulumkisa atsho uBasetsane.
Ungumntu ofikelelekayo kangangokuba akhange abe ngumntu oqaphelekayo ngaphambi kokuba athweswe isithsaba sobuMiss South Africa . Nangethuba kuthatyathwa imifanekiso eRosebank eGoli abafoti abaninzi abazange bamqwalasele, baqwalasela. UBasetsane ngoku ubhexesha inkqubo kamabonakude ethi Top Billing esasazwa ngabakwaSABC .



abanye onobuhle.

Nangoku enguMiss South Africa kwaye ephume kwindawo yesibini kukhuphiswano lukaMiss World uBasetsane usengulowo wayenguye ngaphambili.
Ndibulela indlela eyiyo endikhuliswe ngayo ngabazali bam, Ndafundiswa ukuzixabisa izinto ezincinci ezenzekayo ebomini. Yiyo ke le nto ndiye ndithi xa ndivuka rhoqo kusasa ndicwangcise indlela yam.
Kuthi kwakuphela imini ndizingce ngako konke endibe nako ukukuphumeza. Yinto ke leyo eyenza bungatshintshi ubume bam, kwabo ndiphila nabo kwanabo ndihlangana nabo.
Bungatshintsha ubomi kodwa mna andisokuze nditshintshe . Andikatshintshi nesithandwa sam utshilo uBasetsane.
Nangona engakuthandi ukuthetha ngosenyongweni kuye imbethi-manqindi uDingaan Thobela kuninzi okubadibanisayo. Ebeyile ukuyobukela uDingaan ngethuba ezenzela igama ngokuphumelela kumjikelo wokuqala nge-KO .
UBasetsane usanda kufumana inyhweba yokucelwa ukuba abhexeshe inkqubo yeTV1 ethi Top Billing , encedisana noNeil McCarthy kunye noMichelle Gosforth .
Ukukhethwa kwam ukuba ndibhexeshe le nkqubo ithi Top Billing kubonakalisa iinguqu ezintle ezenzekayo kweli lizwe lethu. Ngaphandle kokuphanda nokukhenketha ndilungiselela le nkqubo, ndizithembile.
Ngaphezu kwako konke uBasetsane ulungile ukuba uyakwenza anako ukuqinisekisa ukuba inkqubo yolwakhiwo ngokutsha nenkqubela iyaphumelela. Yiyo ke le nto emenze ukuba athiywe igama lokuba uyikumkanikazi yale nkqubo kwiindawo ezithile. Kunjalo uBasetsane usazimisele ekufakeni igxalaba.
Ngethuba ethweswa isithsaba sobu Miss South Afrika uBasetsane ebengumfundi opheleleyo kwiDyunivesithi yaseVenda. Lilonke unethemba lokuqhubekeka nezifundo zakhe bucala eUnisa okanye eRAU ukuphela kolawulo lwakhe.�
