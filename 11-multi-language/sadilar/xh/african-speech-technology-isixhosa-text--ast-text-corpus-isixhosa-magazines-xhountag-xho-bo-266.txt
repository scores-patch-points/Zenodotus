ICWECWE elithi One ln A Million nekulicwecwe lakhe elitsha, uAaliyah uthi gqi kwisimbo esitsha sefunk, etsho ngoncuthu lweebhaladi zothando neengoma ezinesingqisho esikhawulezayo.
Eli cwecwe litsho ngocwambu lwelizwi lomlonjikazi ozithembileyo nosandul ukubhaqwa.
U-Aaliyah uthe: Kwasekuqaleni ndithande ukuyila okuphuma kum nenkululeko yokuba eli cwecwe ndilijolise phi.
Ndithande ukuzimisela emculweni. Kuqala ndithande ukuzibekela isiseko. Ngoku ndiye ndakhupha konke endinako yaye ndilinge nezinye izinto.
Ngenxa yokuzinikela kwabavelisi abaselula nabaneziphiwo ukusuka kuKay Gee ukusa kuVincent V.H. Herbert noCraig King, ukusa kuJermaine Dupri, ulRodney Jerkins, uJ. Dibbs noDarryl Simmons -
lsiphumo licwecwe leemeko eziguqu-guqukayo, izandi ezinento yokwenza nesondo neentlobo-ntlobo zomculo omnandi.
Ngenxa yolwalathiso lukaBarry Hankerson neProdyusa ezizi Ngqondi uJomo Hankerson, uMicheal Haughton nocraig Kallman, eli cwecwe liqala ngeengoma ezicwengileyo ukusa kuncuthu lwejazz.
Kwingoma ethi lf Your Girl Only Knew, neyingoma yokuqala kweli cwecwe, uAaliyah ubonisa iziqhamo ezihle zokusebenzisana neprodyusa nombhali weengoma uTim Timbaland Mosley nombhali weengoma okwangumlungelelanisi, uMissy Elliot.
Eli qela lineengoma ezisixhenxe kwicwecwe elineengoma ezili-1 7.
U-Aaliyah uchithe iminyaka yokugqibela emibini ekhenketha athi aseYurophu, athi ngcu eJapan aze abuyele ekhaya eMzantsi-Afrika.
