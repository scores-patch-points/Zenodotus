Sasingamakhwenkwe asibhozo ekhaya. Ndandingowesine ekhaya, yaye ndim kuphela owayenqwenela ukuphucula imfundo. Abanye baxhoma esikolweni kwiBanga lesi-4 baza baya kusebenza eJohannesburg ukuze bafumane imali yokuhlawula irhafu! Kwakufuneka sirhafele izinja, izindlu, izintlobo novoto . Kwakhona kwakufuneka unxibe kakuhle kanti iimpahla
zazibiza. Kwafuneka ndisebenze nzima ukuze ndifumane imali yokuthenga iimpahla.

Phantse ngo-1910- ngexesha leHalley's Comet - abefundisi baseWesile baqalisa isikolo sangasese ekhaya, baza baqesha ititshala eyayiza kusifundisa. Kungekudala abazali bam beva ngendawo ekuthiwa yiDriefontein , eyayithengwe ngabantu abaNtsundu. Yayinezikolo ezakhiweyo ukuze
zifundise abantu abaNtsundu. Abazali bam bafuduka eSpioenkop baya kuhlala eDriefontein . Xa usapho lwasekhaya lwaluhlala apho, utata waya kusebenza kwifama yobisi eJohannesburg .

Ndathi xa ndiqala ukufunda eDriefontein ndafumana ukuba abafundi kwakufuneka balime izitiya njengenxalenye yezifundo zabo. Ndandikhule ndiwuthanda umhlaba, ekhaya nasesikolweni eSpieonkop . Ngoko ndawusebenza kakuhle umhlaba. Ndikhumbula sinconywa isitiya sam yinqununu nangabahloli ababevela ePietermaritzburg . Ndafumana isiqinisekiso sokuphumelela amabanga aphakamileyo eDriefontein . Kwakulelona nqanaba liphezulu lemfundo eNatal ngoko. Emva koko ndaya eSt. Francis Teachers Training College eMarianhill ngo-1928.

AMABALI AMAFUTSHANE




Ndandifundiswa nguMfundisi Father Bernard Huss , owayehlohla izifundo zolimo. Ndavuya kukufundiswa nguye kuba wandifundisa lukhulu. Wayekholelwa ekubeni abantu bafanele bafunde -bangafundisi ingqondo kuphela koko bafundise nomzimba.
Sasifundiswa ukulungisa izitulo , iibhodi neefestile . Basifundisa ukunceda uluntu ngokulwakhela izindlu zangasese nokutyala imithi nezitiya zemifuno.

UMfundisi Huss wandifundisa nokwenza umgquba wesitiya, ekuyinto esasiyenza ekhaya kodwa singalazi igama layo. Kodwa yayisenziwa ngendlela ephucukileyo ndaza nam ndaba nomdla. UMfundisi Huss wayengakholelwa ekusebenziseni amachiza ekulimeni imifuno nezityalo. Wasifundisa ukuba ezo zinto ziyabiza yaye zenzakalisa imeko-bume yendawo. Wayekholelwa kumgquba owenziwe ngomhlanga, ingca nezinto ezimdaka zasekhitshini .

Ngentsimbi yesibini, zonke iititshala
eziqeqeshwayo zazisiya kwizitiya zazo. Umfundi ngamnye wayenesitiya esiza kubonisa ukuba uza kusebenza njani xa sesebenza. Ukufuya, ukulima ukulima imithi ndandikuthanda ndisemncinane. Ndathi oko kwakusisipho esivela ezulwini. USista Glenantia , ititshala yam kunyaka wesibini ekholeji , wakuqaphela oko waza wandibeka ukuba ndijongane nomyezo weentyatyambo eSt. Francis College . Yonke imihla kusasa nasemva kwemini ndandisusa ukhula ndize ndizinkcenkceshele.
Ukuthanda indalo kwandenza ndazithanda nangakumbi. Ndabona indlela izinambuzane ezithanda ngayo iintyatyambo, neenyosi eziza kufunxa amanzi eentyatyambo neentaka ezazifunxa iintyatyambo ezinkulu.
