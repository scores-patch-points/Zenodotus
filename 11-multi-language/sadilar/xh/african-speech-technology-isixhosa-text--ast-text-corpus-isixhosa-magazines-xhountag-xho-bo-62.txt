 -Eskia Mphahlele wazale lwa ePitoli ngo-1919 kodwa eseyinkwenkwe wakhulela eMaupaneng kufuphi nasePietersburg. Apho wayesa lusa iinkomo neebhokhwe. Wathi akuphumele iBanga kesi-3 waya kufunda eMarabastad ePitoli.
Waqala esemncinane ukuthanda ukufunda uEskia. Kodwa zazimbalwa iincwadi esikolweni sakhe. Ngoko wayezama ukufumana iimagazini namaphepha-ndaba amadala ukuze awafunde. lmfundo, ukufunda nokubhala kwakuzizinto ezibalulekileyo ebomini bakhe.
Akuphuma esikolweni uEskia waya kuqeqeshelwa ubutitshala eAdams College eThekwini. Ngo- 1952 wagxothwa ebutitshaleni kuba wayechasene neBantu Education Act. Wayesithi lo mthetho uvimba abantu abantsundu imfundo esemgangathweni.
UMphahlele waya kusebenza kwimagazini iDrum ukususela ngo-1955 ukuya ku-1957. Wayengakuthandi ukuba yintatheli ngoko akazange onwabe. Wagqiba ekubeni abhace kweli lizwe ukuze kwilizwe abhacele kulo afundise yaye abhale kwakhona.
U-Eskia wasebenza eNigeria, eFrance, eKenya, eMelika naseZambia. Kodwa wayekukhumbula eMzantsi-Afrika waza wabuya ngo-1977. Ukususela ngoko ubesebenza kwimfundo yabadala.
