MADIBA - LO UFANELE ABE
NGUNDOFA-NAYE,
Ndikhumbula umhlobo wam ongasekhoyo waseKatlehong eMpuma Randi . UJeffrey Maswanganyi wayengumfo okrele-krele, onxiba kakuhle nokuthandayo ukunconywa. Nangona wayenjalo, wayeluthanda uluvo lomnye umntu nangona wayengadli ngakuyenza into omcebise yona.
Wayekholelwa gqitha kum yaye wayedla ngokufika
ekhaya ehamba nenzwakazi entle asandul' ukuyifumana.
Unjani? wayebuza atsho. Mhle gqitha, ntangam ndandiye nditsho ukuphendula. Umbona efanele ukuba ngumkam? wayebuza enjenjalo. Ningafanelana kakhulu  Mtshate my bra' !
Ndandimtsho agigitheke yintsini uJeff , ebonakala
ukuba uyithandile indlela endimphendule ngayo.
Kwiveki elandelayo ndandiye ndimbone esiza nelinye inenekazi, aze andibuze kwalaa mibuzo ubendibuze yona ngemini engaphambili, ndize ndimphendule ngendlela ebendimphendule ngayo ngaloo mini. Eyona nto yayihlekisa kukuba wayeqinisekisa ukuba uza nenekazi elihle ngaphezu kwebeze nalo ngaphambili. Andazi ukuba mangaphi amanenekazi endandimxelela ukuba "mtshate"! qho xa efika nalo. Phofu ke UJeffrey akazange atshate nangona kwakusithiwa makatshate ngunina, abantakwabo, oodadewabo - nandim. Oko kundicingisa uMadiba nebhinqa asandul' ukuwa kulo uGraca Machel . Ngokuqinisekileyo, uNksk. Machel ulibhinqa
elinento yonke. Usemgangathweni, unegama, undilisekile, mhle yaye ukrele-krele. Esi sibini sisandul' ukuzithi pahaha iindaba zokuthandana kwaso yaye sisazinika ixesha lokwazana. Ngaphezu koko, uMadiba usandul' ukuqhawula umtshato wakhe wesibini, ngokuqinisekileyo oko kumkhathazile emoyeni. Ngaba sifanele simleqe ngokuba makatshate, nangona simva yena ukuba akakungxamelanga ukutshata? Ngabantu abayaziyo intlungu yokuqhawula umtshato abaya kundingqinela ukuba asifanelanga sibaleqe ngokuba batshate. Kaloku kwixabiso lobudala labo bafanele babe ngoondofa-naye. Ndikunqwenelel' ithamsanqa, Madiba! Ilizwe likuxhasa ngokupheleleyo. Ndiyathemba uya kwenza isigqibo esifanelekileyo.

De kube kwinyanga ezayo.


(UMHLELI OMKHULU)

LUNIKENI ITHUBA
LOKUKHUPH' IMBILINI
YALO ULUTSHA
Ulutsha lufuna ukunikwa ithuba lokukhuph'
imbilini yalo nanjengoko oku
kunokulukhuthaza ukuba luzibandakanye
ekwakheni ilizwe. Ulutsha lwelizwe lethu
luziinkokheli zangomso. Ulutsha
seluzibandakanyile kumaphulo oluntu aliqela
afana neenkqubo zophuhliso. Ukuzibandakanya
kwalo kwizinto ezininzi kuya kunceda ekuvaleni
umsantsa, ingakumbi kwezemfundo. Imfundo
eyiyo iza kuluxhobisa ngezixhobo ezizizo lube nokuvala imisantsa ekhoyo eluntwini. Abantu
abatsha kufuneka bazinikele ekumanyeni
abemi beloMzantsi-Afrika ekulweni ulwaphulo
mthetho nodushe.
NguR.N., eSiyabuswa.

UTHIXO UYAFUNEKA
KWA-ZULU/NATAL
Unyanisile uMhleli Omkhulu xa esithi iKwa-Zulu/Natal idume kakubi ngokuba ngowona mmandla kugquba kuwo udushe eMzantsi-Afrika uphela. Nanjengoko Rebecca Malope ecula esithi masifune uxolo silusebenzise. Mlisela nomthinjana, masizibeke phantsi izikhali. Masikhumbule ukuba akuyi kuphumelela naliphi icala ekugqibeleni. Ngubani oza konwabela inkululeko esiyilwele nzima kangaka xa sibulalana ngolu hlobo?
NgUB.C.K., eZambia .








ALIPHELI ITHUBA
LOKUFUNDA
Kubonakala ngathi abantu abanaxesha lemfundo. Awunakuthi ufundile xa
unesatifikethi sebanga leshumi kule mihla. Kufuneka uqhubele phambili ngemfundo ube nokufumana umsebenzi obhadlileyo. Ukuba awuzange ube nathuba lakufunda ebutsheni bakho, akhona amaziko emfundo yabadala afana neAdult Basic Education and Training .
Ndicinga ukuba imfundo isisitshixo
sekammva	eliqaqambileyo emntwini wonke. NguA.L., eRandburg .




.	UKUTHINTELA UKUKHULELWA KOLUTSHA KUYA KUSINDISA UBOMI
Ukukhulelwa kolutsha kuyingozi empilweni yalo. Ulutsha oluninzi aluziqondi iingozi olujamelene nazo kule mihla. Ulutsha kufuneka lufunde
ukuzihlonela. Kufuneka lwazi ukuba ngokusebenzisa ikhondom luzikhusela kwizifo
ezisulelwa ngesondo ezifana neAIDS . Amantombazana aselula akaqondi kubalulekile ukugqiba imfundo ngaphambi kokuba lube neentsapho. Malungazibandakanyi nendibano sondo, kodwa ke xa lungafuni ukuzibandakanya
kuzo, malube neendibano zesondo
ezikhuselekileyo.
NguT.D., eGaborone.
