Kwakukho intwazana enguNozipho owayeneentloni.
Eneneni ukuba neentloni kwakhe kwakumenza angabi nabahlobo. Wawufika ezifihlile ngoxa abanye abantwana bedlala ngexesha lekhefu esikolweni. Eklasini wayehlala athi khunubembe angenzi nelimdaka.
Abanye abantwana babemnyola ngokuba ngumnyewu kwakhe, nto eyayimenza abe neentloni nangakumbi. Wagqibela engasafuni ukuya esikolweni kangangokuba wayezigulisa yonk' imihla.
Unina kaNozipho wayekhathazekile ngengxaki yentombi yakhe. Andiyifuni le nto yokuba neentloni kangaka kwakho. Bathi abanye abantwana bezonwabisa ngokudlala wena ube ungumnyewu ohleli phantsi obukeleyo.
Abandithandi kuba andifani nabo, waphendula watsho uNozipho.
Suk' apha! Nawe ungumntwana njengabanye, qha kufuneka uziqhelanise nabanye abantwana xa ufuna bakuthande, watsho unina. Kodwa uNozipho akazange ayikholelwe loo nto.
Ngaminazana ithile wafumana isimemo sokuya kwitheko lokubhiyozela umhla wokuzalwa wenye intombazana afunda nayo. Wavuya gqitha, wasamkela nesimemo sokuya kwelo theko.
Unina wamthengela ilokhwe entle aza kuyinxiba kwelo theko nesipho aza kusinika intombazana ebhiyozela umhla wokuzalwa.
Ngemini yetheko uNozipho walungiselela ukuhamba kuselixesha. Wayeyivuyele intombi yakhe unina, kwaye enethemba lokuba uza kuzifumanela abahlobo.
Wanduluka uNozipho nonina ngentsimbi yesithathu besiya kumzi owawunetheko. Kodwa bathi xa befika esangweni laloo mzi, uNozipho wathi:
Mna andizukungena apha. Ndinesisu esibuhlungu.
Nozipho, intombazana yalapha ikulindele uya kuyiphoxa xa ungayi kwitheko layo, watsho unina. Waqonda ukuba kufuneka abe ngqwabalala ukuze ayakhe intombi yakhe, wamthi hlasi ngengalo uNozipho wamtsala wangena
naye kuloo mzi.
Abantwana beza bebaleka bamamkela ngolonwabo uNozipho. Kwaphela ukuba neentloni ebusweni bakhe watyhileka wonwaba nabanye abantwana.
UNozipho wafunda ukuba akuyongxaki ukuzenzela abahlobo ngenxa yokunyanzelwa ngunina, yaye ngaphezu koko wonwaba gqitha! Libhalwe nguTHOKO MKHIZE
