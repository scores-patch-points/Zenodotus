Sifanele simngqinele umphathi weqela leli, uMorne du Plessis , xa esithi ukuphumelela koMzantsi-Afrika kubonisa ukuba eli lizwe laliza kuyizuza le ndebe ukuba laliyingenele ngo-1987 nango-1991. Noko ke ilizwe laseMzantsi-Afrika libe nobubele kuJonah Lomu ngokumvumela agoduke ezifumanele intombi ancuma nayo.
Kuthe xa iNew Zealand ibetha iNgilani kwiisemi-fayinali , abantu bazixelela ukuba iza kuzuzwa yiyo indebe yehlabathi. Iyibethe lula iNgilani ngokungathi yi-Ivory Coast ibethwa yiFrance kwimijikelo yokuqala. Bekungekho mntu onokubheja athi uMzantsi-Afrika, obethe iFrance kwiisemi-fayinali , uya kuba nako ukunqanda abafana beAll Blacks ekuzuzeni indebe yehlabathi yombhoxo. Ngenxa yeentsikelelo zikaMandela aphumelele amaBhokobhoko . Into emangalisayo kukuba nguBurger obesandul'ukuzuza enye ikheyisi yewayini kuNozakuzaku waseUnited States , uJosiah Beeman , emva kokuba iqela laseNew Zealand lizuze iYachting Cup yaseMerika kutshanje.

KUMBINDI weJohannesburg phakathi kwezakhiwo zedolophu ezide kuqhubeka ushishino olubi kakhulu. Aba bantwana bathengisa ngemizimba yabo khon'ukuze bazuze nantoni na abanokuyifumana!
Bohluke mpela kumntwana wakho oneminyaka eli-11! Banxiba njengamantombazana amadala. Banxiba izikeyiti ezifutshane neetophu ezincinane kwaye bathi gqi okomshologu xa uhamba kwisitrato ekuthiwa yiGoud Street embindini weJoburg , ekulapho kwenziwa lo mkhwa mbi nowaziwa ngabantu abambalwa.
Indlela abatsala ngayo abaxumi nabafumaneka ngayo isetyenziswa naphi na; njengokuyilileka ezitratweni , maxa wambi ngokuveza amabele angekaphumi ngokupheleleyo. Indoda efuna uncedo ithi isathethana nentombazana kube kusenziwa izinto eziyihendayo. Bakuvumelana ubona ucango lwemoto luvuleka, ingene intombazana ihambe nendoda uluntu olunokuthi iphambene. Oku bakwenza benoncumo kobo buso babo buncinane.
Eneneni kwenzeka ntoni apha? Ndichithe iveki yonke ndisihla ndinyuka kwindawo abafunyanwa kuyo abantwana abathengisa ngemizimba kwaye ndibone ngaphezu kwama-50 amantombazana aneminyaka esusela kweli-10 ukuya kwi-17 enza lo mkhwa mbi.
