Ndafika emzini kaMprofethi uMhambi ndigula kwaye ndisecicini lokufa, wandihoya ngokukhawuleza. Ndandiphethe imbombozi yamanzi eziilitha ezingama-25. Wawasikelela loo manzi uMptofethi. Wathi aza kundikhutyula ngaphakathi, wathi ndibuye akuphela.

Siyazi ukuba zimbalwa izinto ezinokwenziwa ngumntu ongenangalo kodwa uyakuthi akufakelwa iingalo ezinguzenzele aqeqeshelwe ukuba akwazi ukuzisebenzela, utshilo uNkzn. Mamabolo.
