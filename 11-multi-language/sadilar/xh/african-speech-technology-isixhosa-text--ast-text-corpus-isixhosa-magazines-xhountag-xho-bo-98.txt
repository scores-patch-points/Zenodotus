Vumbulula eyakho imbali

Sibhalelwe ngabafundi abaninzi abafuna uTyhila Ufunda abancede emsebenzini wabo
wesikolo. Siziphulaphule zonke izicelo zenu yaye ukususela kule nyanga siza
kunikhuphela iinxalenye zezifundo zeMbali zeBanga lesi-6 nelesi-7. Qhubeka ufunde
uze usibhalele usixelele ukuba la makhasi akuncedile na!



Mhlawumbi uninzi lwenu lucinga ukuba isifundo seMbali sizele ngamagama, iminyaka neendawo ekwakuliwa kuzo iimfazwe. Kanti ke akunjalo! Isifundo seMbali sinomdla ngaphezu koko. Imbali isenokuba ngamadoda namabhinqa aqhelekileyo.
Ngaphambi kokuba sifunde ngeembali ezahlukeneyo, kufuneka sazi indlela yokufumana okwakuse-benzeka kwixesha elidlulileyo. Ezinye iindlela esikwenza ngayo oku zinokuba nomdla.
Sinokuthembela kwiincwadi zokubhala esiza kukwenza, iincwadi, imibongo, udliwanondlebe neefoto. Ukuze sibe zizazi ngembali kufuneka siyazi indlela okusebenzisa izinto ezahlukeneyo ezinokusinceda.

Indlela yokufumana ngeyakho imbali

Uke ube nayo ingxaki yokungazi apho ungena khona kwimbali? Kungani ungafumani ngokufunda imbali yosapho lwakowenu. Oku unokukwenza ngeendlela ezininzi. Enye yezo ndlela kukudlana indlebe namalungu osapho ahlukeneyo. Unokubhala imbali yosapho lwakowenu usebenzisa izinto abazikhumbulayo!

Imbali yabanye abantu
Nangona kulula ukufumana ngeyethu imbali, singayifumana njani imbali yabanye abantu? Xa wena okanye usapho lwakho ningamazi umntu, niya kuba anikwazi ukukhumbula. Iifoto ziya kuninceda, kodwa azinakunixelela nto. Masifumane imbali yabanye abantu!


Ubomi babantu
abaqhelekileyo
Sifuna ngabantu abazizinhanha nabadumileyo mihla le kwimagazini namaphepha-ndaba. Iifilim neenkuqubo zeTV zidla ngokuba ngabadlali beefilim, iimvumi nangabantu bepolitiki.

Ababhali nabaqambi abaninzi bacinga ukuba abafundi nababukeli bafuna ukufunda ngabantu abadumileyo kuphela.

Abadumanga abantu abaninzi ehlabathini. Kudla ngokuba ngabantu abafana nathi abaguqula imbali.

Kusenokubonakala ngathi yinkokeli ethile eguqule izinto kodwa kudla ngokuba yintshukumo yabantu eyenza kubekho iinguqu.

Njengoko sikholwa kukuba ubomi babantu abaphantsi bubaluleke njengobabantu abadumileyo, sigqibe ekubeni siqwalasele ubomi bukaConfidence Radebe.

Ngebali lakhe sinokufunda indlela yokufumana ngembali yabanye abantu.

Ibali likaConfidence
lgama lam ndinguConfidence Radebe. Nangona ndinalo igama lesiZulu likaNosipho, ndithanda eli IikaComfidence kakhulu kuba nam ndifuna ukuzithemba.

Ndazalelwa eMbumbulu ngo-1978. Ekhaya kwakukho izindlu ezintathu esasihlala kuzo nomama, utata, abasakwethu ababini, abantakwentu abathathu, uPeter, uMbuso nojabu. Babebaninzi abantu ababakhe kwingingqi yakowethu - mhlawumbi babengama-6OO

Ndilusizi kuba ndikhumbula izinto ezibuhlungu ikakhulu ezenzeka ngelo xesha. Kwakukho abantu abaqalisa imfazwe. Batshisa umzi wasekhaya babulala nabantu abaninzi kwingingqi yasekhaya. Kwakungo-1983 ngelo xesha yaye mna ndandineminyaka emihlanu.

Kwanyanzelaka ukuba siyohlala kwisikhululo saseMbumbulu malunga neenyanga ezintathu silinde ukuba iphele imfazwe. Zazininzi iintsapho ezazihlala esikhululweni kuba zibaleke imfazwe. Andiqinisekanga ukuba ngawaphi amaqela ayesilwa. Andisakhumbuli kakuhle.

Ngelo xesha sasingasamboni utata. Wayesebenza edolophini(eThekwini) yaye ehlala eGlebelands Hostel eMlazi. Wayetyelelwa ngumama kuphela.

Imfazwe yembambano
-Staff Reporter

Kubulewe abantu abathathu, kwenzaka abangaphaya kwama-300 emva kokuqhambuka kwemfazwe yembambano kwisithili saseMbumbulu izolo. Awucaci unobangela wale mfazwe nangona abahlali bekholwa kukuba imfazwe ingumvuka wembambano ephakathi kosapho lwakwaMkhize nolwakwaXulu.

Sithetha nje amawaka-waka amabhinqa nabantwana babalekile kule ndawo bayohlala kwisikhululo saseMbumbulu. Kubizwe aMapolisa aseMzantsi-Afrika ukuba azogcina ucwangco kule ndawo.
Ngoku ufanele ube nako ukuyongeza imbali kaConfidence. Imibhalo iya kukunceda.

Xa usebenzisa imibhalo nezinye izinto ukufumana ngezinto ezenzeka kudala, kudla ngokubakho imibuzo engaphendulekiyo. Kulapho ke apho kufuneka udlane indlebe nabantu.

Unokudlana indlebe nabantu abanolwazi ngeenkcukacha ozifunayo. Sikunikile umzekelo wemibuzo onokuyibuza koomawokhulu bakho. Ngoku cinga ngemibuzo obunokuyibuza uConfidence xa ubufuna ezinye iinkcukacha ngembali yakhe.

KuTyhila Ufunde wenyanga ezayo siza kukunika iingcombolo ezibhekele phaya ngodliwano-ndlebe nangendlela yokufumana iimpendulo zemibuzo engaphendulwanga. Siza kukwenza oko ngokufunda imbali yokufunda eMzantsi-Afrika.
