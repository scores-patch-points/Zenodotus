 Ngokweencwadi esizifumanayo kwaBONA- ingakumbi ezivela kubafundi abaselula  uninzi lwenu luyaqhubeka nokuzibek'esichengeni sokuba neendibano zesondo lungazikhuselanga nangona lungafuni bantwana.
Ezinye zezizathu esizivayo zokungasebenzisi zithinteli-kumitha ubukhulu becala zezokuba: azindilungeli iipilisi; ikhondom ayiyifumanisi ngokupheleleyo indoda ubuncwane besondo  umfundi othile ude wazekelisa ngelithi kufana nokumunca ilekese isephepheni; iluphu yona niyikhalazela nithi, impompozisa ngamandla kwaye ibangele isiluma xa usexesheni kwaye iyagudleka kwiqabane xa ninendibano yesondo.
Kuninzi anokukhetha kuko amabhinqa kule mihla ukuthintela ukumitha. Phofu ke njengoko iipilisi ezahlukeneyo zilungele amabhinqa ahlukeneyo, sukuzikhalala
kuloko qhagamshelana nogqirha wakho okanye abeKlinikhi yocwangciso-ntsapho ukuze utshintshelwe kwezinye okanye bakucebise ngezinye izithintela-kumitha,

IZITHINTELI EZIFUMANEKA NGOKU ICOMBINED PILL- ine-oestrogen neprogostogen, kwaye inqumamisa obhinqileyo ekuveliseni iqanda nyanga zonke.
Okuhle ngayo kulula ukuyisebenzisa, ithembakele kangange-99 ekhulwini, uya ngokufaneleyo exesheni kwaye iyasinqumamisa isiluma ikwenze ungayi ngamandla. Uninzi lweepilisi ezine-oestrogen zilwenza luhle ulusu. Okubi ngayo  inokuba neziphumo ezingancumisiyo ezingephi neziphelayo ethubeni. Ayiwalungelanga amabhinqa angaphezu kobudala bama-55, atshayayo angaphezu kobungama-35 nalawo akhe okanye aphuma kwiintsapho ezakhe zaphathwa sisi-stroke, intliziyo, ihigh blood pressure neswokile.

IMINI PILL- ineprogestogen qha kwaye idala iinguqu ezibangela kube nzima ukuba imbewu yendoda ingene esibelekweni, okanye isibeleko silamkele iqanda eliqanduselweyo.

ILUPHU-ifakwa esibelekweni kwaye ithintela iqanda lingaqanduseleki kuso, Okuhle ngayo ayiphozisi maseko kwaye ithembakele kangangama-96 ukuya kuma-99 ekhulwini iminyaka emibini ukuya kwemihlanu (engaphezulu xa yenziwe ngeplastikhi kuphela) Okubi ngayo unokuya ngamandla exesheni. Ayimlungelanga umntu ongazange akhulelwe, okhathazwa qho zizifo zangaphantsi nonesiluma.

IDIAPHRAGM OKANYE ICAP - ifakwa kusisi ngaphambi kwendibano ngesondo ukuze imbewu yendoda ingadibani neqanda. lmelwe kukusetyenziswa nesibulali-mbewu (ispermicide) kwaye iyekwe ihlale phakathi iiyure ezintandathu emva kwendibano yesondo. Okuhle ngayo  kumele isetyenziswe ngokufaneleyo izokuthembeka kangangama-85 ukuya kuma-97 ekhulwini. lyakhusela nasekuhlaselweni ngumhlaza womqala wesibeleko nezifo ezisulelwe ngesondo.
Okubi ngayo  kufuneka imane ihlolwa emva kweenyanga ezintandathu ukuqinisekisa ukuba isalingana kakuhle. Amanye amanenekazi ayalana nesibulali-mbewu kwaye kumelwe ibhinqa lifundiswe ukuyifaka ngendlela eyiyo.

ISPONGE-sifakwa kusisi kwisithuba seeyure ezingama-24 ngaphambi kwendibano yesondo kodwa kufuneka siyekwe phakathi iiyure ezintandathu emva koko size silahlwe emva kokusetyenziswa. Sinesibulali-mbewu, sigquma umlomo wesibeleko kwaye sithintela imbewu yendoda ekungeneni ngaphakathi.
Okuhle ngaso - isayizi inye, ilingana wonke umntu. Sithembakele kangangama-75 ukuya kuma-9l ekhulwini.
Okubi ngaso - asithembakelanga ncam ukuba singasetyenziswa rhoqo.

IKHONDOMU - inxitywa ngaphambi kwendibano yesondo de kugqitywe. ikhuseleke bhetele xa isetyenziswa nesibulali-mbewu.
Okuhle ngayo - ithembakele kangangama-85 ukuya kuma-98 ekhulwini kwaye ngaphandle nje kokuba inqanda ukukhulelwa iwakhusela omabini amaqabane kwizifo ezosulelwa ngesondo imkhusele obhinqileyo nakumhlaza womqala wesibeleko.
Okubi ngayo - ituna ukunonotshelwa nanjengoko isenokugqabhuka okanye imuncuke.

IKHONDOMU YABABHINQILEYO -yeyona ithembisa kakhulu kwezisanda kuyilwa.
Okuhle ngayo - inokufakwa kwiiyure
ezimbalwa ngaphambi kwendibano yesondo kwaye kubonakala ngathi sisisombululo kwingxaki yamadoda angafuni kusebenzisa khondomu. Kuba igquma umphandle oko kuchaza ukuba iyakhusela kwizifo ezisulelwa ngesondo.

IINALITI - zihlatywa esihlunwini ziphumele kancinci kwimithombo yegazi zithintele ukuqandusela.
Okuhle ngazo - awube ucingana nokutya ipilisi yonke imihla kwaye unokuhlala ukhuselekile ekumitheni iiveki ezisibhozo ukuya kwezili-12 kuxhomekeka ukuba usebenzisa luhlobo luni. linaliti zithembakele kangangama-99 ekhulwini.
Okubi ngazo - unokungayi ngokufaneleyo exesheni, mhlawumbi ungayi kwakuya, ngaloo ndlela ungakhawulezi ukhulelwe naxa sowuziyekile.

IMVALO-NZALO KWABABHINQILEYO
- Kuvalwa imithambo yenzala ngotyando. Okuhle ngayo - isebenza ngokukhawuleza. Njengoko ivalwa imithambo iqanda alikwazi kuqanduseleka ngaphakathi kuyo unaphakade. Ilungele amabhinqa angasafuniyo ukuzala.

IMVALO-NZALA YAMADODA
(VASECTOMY) imibhobho ethubelezisa imbewu isikwa ivalwe. Lulula utyando emadodeni kunasemabhinqeni. Okuhle ngayo - kulungele amadoda angasafuni kumithisa kwaye ahlala enjalo ngonaphakade.
Okubi ngayo - ayisebenzi ngokukhawuleza nanjengoko kufuneka kuchithwe amaxesha amaninzi ngaphambi kokuba iphele imbewu ekwimibhobho. Lilonke, kunokusetyenziswa esinye isithinteli kumitha kwiinyanga ezimbalwa. Ekukuphela kohlobo lwesithinteli-kumitha esithembakele kangange-100. olukhuselekile, olungabizi nesenti, nolungakubeki sichengeni sakufumana zifo, kukuzikhwebula kwiindibano zesondo.
