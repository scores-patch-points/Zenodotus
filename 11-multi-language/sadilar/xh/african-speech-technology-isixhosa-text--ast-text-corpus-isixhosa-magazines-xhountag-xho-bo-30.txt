nehagu.
Bekunzima ukuziyeka, bendiqhubeka lihendelwa kuzo. Ndineentloni zokuthi ndiye ndaqhubeka neziyobisi nkqu emva kokuvuma ukuhleni ukuba ndiziyekile.
uSenyaka uthethe enombulelo kwisithandwa sakhe uThembi Tshabalala oye wamnika ikxaso nenkuthazo ekuzameni ukuzicoca. Ngoku kulonyaka uzimisele njengokulwa ukulwa abathengisi beziyobisi eHillbrow .
Kudala ndilapho, ndisazi nokuba ezo ziyobisi nokwenza ntoni kwimpilo yakho, utshilo. uSenyaka uye wazenzela igama kwiqonga lomculo ngoApril 1999 xa wadibana noKamazu uDanny Malewa ukushicilela ingoma ethi Fongong - egxeka abantu abathengisa iimpahla ezixhobo ngamagama engengawo. Nkqu akuba abantu babeyithanda lengoma ithengise ngaphezu kwamacwecwe angama-60 000 - ugxekwe ngokukhuthaza ukuzondwa kwabantu bangengabo abakweli, kuba ikakhulu kuthiwa ngamaTshayina nabanye abantu abavela Namanye amazwe abathengisa ezi mpahla. Oyena ongasoze alibaleka nobethandwa butsheni beyee-1990 uBrenda Fassie oYvonne Chaka Chaka. Impumelelo ibathe hithi thwanga, noSenyaka uthi ngathi balibele apho basuka khona. Elithi Why Uzonda magents limbangele uFassie waphindisela gelithi Angizondi Amagents . USenyaka ulandelise ngelithi Brenda Ka Mpara. Phakathi kwabo bangene ngebhaqo mculweni uSenyaka uye waphumelela. Uqale njengomcoci, umhlambi weemoto kwaba xa ingumlungisi weenwele eCarlton Centre Johannesburg wabonwa nguSello Chicco Twala . Uchicco wathanda kungekuphela nje idlela azilungisa ngayo iinwele kodwa kwanobuntu bakhe nelizwi lakhe elimnandi emculweni.
Namhlanje uSenyaka akakhuphisani nolutsha ikwaito kodwa ubatsalisa nzima ukuze bazuze mali . Uza kuba ebalaseliswa kwiFNB SAMA Awards ethandwayo neza kuqhutywa ngasekupheleni kwale nyanga.
uSenyaka uceba ukubonakala kwiifilim .
