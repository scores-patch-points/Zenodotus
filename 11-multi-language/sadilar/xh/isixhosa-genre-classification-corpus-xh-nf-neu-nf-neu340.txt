Okwenzekileyo:
Kaloku amaqokobhe amaqanda anento ekuthiwa yicalcium carbonate. Yiyo ewenza aqine.
Iviniga iyi-asidi ekuthiwa yiacetic acid.
Xa kudibana icalcium carbonate (yeqokobhe leqanda) ne-acetic acid (iviniga) kulwa amachiza kuze kuphume icarbon dioxide (engumoya).
Xa ulifaka kwiviniga iqanda kuphuma amadlu ecarbon dioxide.
Ukulwa kwamachiza kuqhubeka de wonk' umoya oseqandeni uphume - oko kuthabatha malunga nosuku.
Iqanda ulikhupha lithambile kwiviniga kuba yonke icarbon dioxide ekulo iphume yangamadlu.
