INTSHAYELELO:

Ubomi bubalulekile yaye bufanele bukhuselwe ngumntu wonke. Kufa abantu abaninzi ezindleleni zaseIlzantsi-Afrika ngenxa yokungaqhubi ngenkathalo nangenxa yokungayithobeli imithetho yendlela.

lmithetho yendleta yenzetwe ukunceda abantu -ukuthintela umbhodamo ezindleleni nokwenza iindlela zikhuseleke ebantwini abazisebenzisayo. Njengomsebenzisi wendlela kufuneka uyithobele imithetho yendlela ukuze iindlela zikhuseleke.

Baninzi abantu abaphulukene nemiphefumlo yabo nezalamane zabo ngenxa yeengozi zendlela ebezinokuphetshwa. Oku kwenze abantu balahlekelwa yimivuzo yabo ngenxa yokwenzakala, abantwana abashiyeka bengenabazali baze bangabi nabantu baza kubafundisa. Ungavumeli oku kwenzeke kuwe nakwizalamane zakho.

Le ncwadana yenzelwe ukufundisa umqhubi nabahambi ngeenyawo ngeendlela zokuphepha iingozi. Ukhumbule ukuba kokwenziwa ngumntu wonke osebenzisa indlela okwenza zingabikho iingozi. Ungacingi ngesiqu sakho kuphela. Cinga nangabanye abantu abasebenzisa indlela njengawe.

Siyathemba ukuba ukufunda le ncwadi kuya kukuvula amehlo kukukhumbuze nangokhuseleko lwakho endleteni nolwabanye abantu abasebenzisa indlela. Qiqa, yiba nomonde, lumka ukhumbule nokuba imithetho yendlela ibekelwe ukuba ithotyelwe - ukuze ukhuseleke nabanye abantu abasebenzisa indlela.
