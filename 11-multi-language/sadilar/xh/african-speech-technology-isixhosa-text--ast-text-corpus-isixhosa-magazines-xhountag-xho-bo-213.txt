UTommy wayethandana nentombi eyayisebenza eHHL . Yayintle yaye ingumntu wabantu. Wayethanda ukuthetha izinto ezilula nayo, wayengazithethi nayo izinto eziba1ulekileyo kuthi sobathathu. Yayingakuthandi ukufunda iincwadi, kanti uTommy wayekuthanda ukufunda iincwadi nokuphulaphula umculo. Le ntombi yazama nje yona ukuba ifane noTommy .

Le ntombazana yayityhilekile yaye sasiyithanda sinoJonny njengodade. Oko kusenokuvakala kumangalisa, kanti akunjalo. Nkqu noTommy wayengathethi ngokuthandana okunenjongo. Wayesithi kunzima ukufumana intombi emfaneleyo kweli lizwe ngenxa yemeko zelizwe. Ngoko wayonwabile nesithandwa sakhe.

Nathi sazama ukwenjenjalo sinoJonny , kodwa amalinge ethu awa phantsi. uJohnny wayengafani tu noTommy . Sasingabantu abangafaniyo kangangokuba andiyazi into eyayisenza sivane.

uJohnny wayekuthanda ukuncokola yaye engumntu onengxolo. Wayesoloko esenza izinto ezingalunganga. Wayesoloko ezifaka ezingxakini kuze kufuneke simncede sinoTommy . Ebantwini abangamaziyo wayengumntu ongachanekiyo ukuba unjani nongakhathaliyo.

Umama wakhe wayebulewe yindlala. Wayesesikolweni yena ngelo xesha, ngoko wayiva emva kwexesha elide loo nto. Wayezibulala ngendlala kuba efuna ukufundisa unyana wakhe yaye yena wayengayazi loo nto. Emva wachitha ixesha elininzi eyedwa yaye ethe cwaka. Nkqu nathi sinoTommy sasingafikeleli kuye. Wayengangeneki tu. Kuthi wayesenguJonnyi esimaziyo qha engonwabanga. Wayetshintshile kumntu wonke. Kodwa akazange ayeke ukuphoxa abantu uJohnny , yaye wayenobuchule bokususa uchuku. Babembalwa abantu ababemqonda, uninzi lumgxeka.

Nobam ubomi babufana nobabo. Ndandilihlwempu, ndifuna intombi eza kuba liqabane lam nezinye izinto onokuzifumana emfundweni xa ungenguye uMlungu eMzantsi-Afrika.

Sathi sakugqiba ukufunda kwisikolo sethu sehlukana, yangulowo wabheka kwelakhe icala. UTommy wayefuna nathi sibe ziititshala . Wayesithi:
Kunceda ngantoni ukufunda into ungazukufumana mali ngayo? uJohnny wayenqwenela ukubona iindawo zehlabathi. Ndathi mna ndiza kuhamba-hamba ukuze ndazi abantu, ndizame nokuziphilisa ngokuba ngumbhali. Bobabini babekhathazekile yimeko yomzimba wam, kodwa mna ndandizimisele.

Savumelana ukuba siya kusoloko siqhagamshelana, saza sohlukana. uJohnny

uPeter Abrahams wazalwa ngo1919 eVrededorp , eJohannesburg .
Wahamba eMzantsi-Afrika ngo1939 waya eNgilani eyokwandisa ulwazi lwakhe lokubhala. Nangona ehlala elubhacweni ixesha elininzi, usengumbhali obalulekileyo waseMzantsi-Afrika. uPeter Abrahams ubhala ngeengxaki zaseMzantsi-Afrika. Nangona esebenzisa amagama afana noo-Coloured , u-Native no-European kungenxa wayebhala ebutsheni boo-1900. U-Abrahams uyayigxeka inkqubo yocalucalulo kodwa unomdla kwindlela abaNtsundu kwihlabathi jikelele abaziqonda ngayo imvelaphi yabo. U-Abrahams uhlala eJamaica ngoku apho afumana ubumnandi bokuhlala kwindawo engenalo ucalulo lweentlanga. U-Abrahams ubhale imibongo, iincwadi nencwadi engobomi. iiAkhawunti nendlela ezisebenza ngayo
Zalisa ifomu yokwenza isicelo. Kufuneka uphethe isazisi nemvulophu yomvuzo ( ipayslip ).

Ivenkile iya kukuxelela ukuba unokuthenga ngamalini ngetyala. Oko kuxhomekeka kwizinto ezinini. Ivenkile iya kujonga umvuzo wakho, enye imali oyifumanayo, ukuba ukhe wathenga na ngetyala ngaphambili nokuthi yimalini onako ukuyihlawula ngenyanga.

Ivenkile iya kukunika ikhadi emva koko unokukhetha izinto ofuna ukuzithenga. Ezinye iivenkile zifuna idiphozithi yempahla oyithengayo, kanti ezinye ziyakuvumela uthenge uze uhlawule izavenge ngenyanga.

Ekupheleni kwenyanga uya kufumana incwadi ekuchazela ngetyala lakho. Iya kukuxelela imali ofanele uyihlawule kuloo nyanga. Xa ungahlawuli ivenkile ifaka imali yenzalo kwityala lakho.

Unokuhlawuliswa inzala naxa uza kuhlawula ngaphezu kweenyanga ezintandathu. Buza ivenkile xa uvula iAkhawunti ukuba uya kuyihlawula inzalo xa uhlawula ngexesha elingakanani.
