LIFIKILE KWAKHONA ELO XESHA LONYAKA..

Ndithe ndizimisele ukubhala ngeengozi zendlela negalelo lotywala kwezi ntlekele, suka kwasweleka umhlobo wam ebesisebenzisana naye kwezoshishino. De kube ngoku andiyikholelwa indlela afe ngayo uSydney Chama obephile qete, yaye engumfo obephila impilo esulungekileyo nengenachaphaza. Sisuka kude sinoSydney -sobabini singabaseKatlehong; sobabini siye sazama ukusungula imisebenzi yethu yolonwabo
- yena engumdlali wemidlalo yeqonga mna ndingumququzeleli wezolonwabo. Ngokuhamba kwexesha ndawuyeka loo msebenzi ndazingela amakriwa aluhlaza kodwa uSydney waqhubeka nabekwenza, waza wasweleka xa imizamo yakhe ineziphumo ezihle njengabanye esasikhula nabo, uJacob Mpharanyana Radebe noSam Sefatsa Ntsinyi abangasekhoyo! USydney Chama ubeliyolisa. Ubeyazi intlupheko eyiqonda nentlungu.
Ukusweleka kwakhe kubeyilahleko nakuthi apha kwaBONA. Kaloku bekunguye umqambi nombhali waBafana - ikhasi lethu elihlekisayo nenqaku elithi Ubomi Belokishi. Nakuba nje engasekho siseza kuqhubeka sikupapasha oku kwiBONA. Sithanda ukuvakalisa uvelwano kwinkosikazi yakhe, uNana, nabantwana bakhe. Sithi akuhlanga lungehliyo, lalani ngenxeba.
Sisekulo mba weziphelo ezingathandekiyo, kwikhasi lama-52 kolu shicilelo sibhale ibali elibuhlungu likaPeter Terror Mathebula, imbethimanqindi yokuqala eNtsundu eyazuza iNtshinga yeHlabathi. Ukumbona ngoku uTerror engaphangeli, edimazekile eselela ukuzibulala akuxolisi. Makubekho umntu onik'uncedo kule meko!
Masithembe ukuba niza kulonwabela eli thuba leeholide, kodwa khumbulani ukuba aninedwa, kukho nabanye emasibacingele! Nibe neKrismesi emyoli.
De kube lixesha elizayo.

EZANDL.ENI ZENU UMTHETHO
Siye sothuka sakubona kumabonakude iqela lamaSilamsi iPAGAD (iPeople Against Gangsterism and Drugs) elwa nemigulukudu yeziyobisi, ethubeni ilwa namapolisa. Ngokuqinisekileyo, iziyobisi zibulala ubomi babantu, ingakumbi ulutsha, kanti nabathengisi beziyobisi babaphatha kakubi abantwana bethu ukuze batyebe. Bayaqonda na ukuba babulala abantu abaza kuba ngoogqirha namagqwetha angomso? IPAGAD kufuneka ibe liqela elisebenza namapolisa ukuze ilwe nolwaphulo-mthetho olunento yokwenza neziyobisi nemigulukudu konke ikwenza ekhusini Iomthetho. Urhulumente kufuneka abonisane nawo wonke amaqela abandakanyekileyo uze lo mba uwujonge ngeliso elibukhali. Thina bantu kufuneka sisebenzisane namapolisa kangangoko sinako. Nakuba mna ndiyixhasa imibutho esekwe luluntu elwa nolwaphulo-mthetho, andisokuze ndibaxhase abo bawuthabathela ezandleni zabo umthetho. NguE.M., eWitbank.
