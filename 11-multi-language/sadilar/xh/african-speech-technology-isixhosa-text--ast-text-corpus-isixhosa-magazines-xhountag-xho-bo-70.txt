Ukubala
Ukubala yeyona nto bayifunda nzima abantwana, phofu ngoku baya kuba benento abayaziyo ngamanani nangento ayithethayo! Qala ngokubala izinto ndawonye. Baxelele baqhwabe izandla zabo ngokwamanani obanike wona! Yitsho babethe igubu! Benzise into eza kubenza babale yonke imihla.

Bafundise ukubata igrosa, ukubala iimoto ezisesitratweni nokubala abantwana abafunda nabo. Bafundise ngendleta eyonwabisayo! Ungababalisi bagqithe kuntathu okanye kune de bawazi bonke la manani.

Nanku umzobo onezinto ezahlukahlukeneyo -iintaka, iimoto, izindlu nezinja. Yitsho abantwana benze isangqa sijikezele izinto ezifanayo. Abantwana bamatshintshane ngokudibanisa amaqela ezinto ezifanayo ngeepensile ezinemibala eyahlukeneyo.

Amakhadi amanani
Amakhadi amanani ayanceda ekufundiseni abantwana ukuba uphawu oluthile luhamba neliphi inani. Ngezantsi yingqokelela yamakhadi amanani anamanani abhaliweyo. Wasike la makhadi. Yitsho abantwana babeke inani lezinto ezikwikhadi ngatinye.
