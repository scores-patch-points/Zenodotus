wayengathandi abantu basebenzise ukuduma kwakhe. Kodwa uTandie nolrene babevana gqitha. yam imanzi. Emva koko ndeva isandi esikhulu esithi Bhuum! sento eqhushumbela ebusweni bam. UWillem noTheo bandigalela ngepetroli baza balumeka ngematshisi, ndatsha!
Ndandingakholwa ukuba ndim lowo utshiswayo. Ndazama ukuwucima umlilo, inyama isiwa esandleni sasebusweni bam. Ndandikhala ndisithi, Thixo, ndiyatsha! Ndandisiva isandi somlilo otsha emzimbeni wam. Intloko yayinzima ngathi lilitye. Ndandingayikholelwa into endiyibonayo Wayekhona umntakwabo kaWillem Waphosa ibhatyi yakhe kum wanditsalela esitratweni. Ndabetheka ngentloko elityeni ndaza ndaziluma ulwimi ngamazinyo angaphambili. Omnye umntu wabaleka wayobiza umsakwethu, uTandeka, owayesekhaya noLorraine Bobabini beza bebaleka, wafika uLorraine wazama ukucima amalangatye omlilo ayenditshisa. Watsha ezandleni nasemilenzeni. Ndeva ukuba naye wafa isiqaqaqa.

UTandie wasiwa eNatalspruit Hospital, apho wafika wafakwa emanzini abandayo. Wonk umntu wayecaphuka kukutsha kwemvumi eyaziwa ngolo hlobo. Umongikazi wabopha amanxeba okutsha kaTandie, waza wanikwa igumbi lakhe yedwa wangadityaniswa nabanye abaguli UTandie uthi:
Ndabekwa kumgangatho wesihlanu kuba ndandinevumba elibi ngokungathi ndingumntu ofileyo. Ndandidla ngokulala ndizivale ngeminwe iimpumlo zam.

Ubuso bukaTandie, umqala nezandla zazibotshwe ngeebhandeji. Akukho mntu wayesazi ukuba uya kuba njani xa sepholile amanxeba okutsha. UTandie waxelela oogqirha ukuthi Ndiza kuzibulala xa ndingabuboni ubuso baml Kodwa babengenako ukususa iibhandeji.

Amanxeba kaThandi ayerhawuzela. Wayesiva ngathi kukho izinto ezihamba-hamba emzimbeni wakhe Ubuso bakhe bakuqine okwelitye ngaphantsi kwebhandeji. Wayengathi nokuba ufuna ukuphila na
okanye ufuna ukufa UTandie uthi: Zathi zakususwa iibhandeji, oogqirha banexhala. Bebengazi nokuba ndiza kuyamkela na indlela endibonakala ngayo. Babephethe isitofu xa kunokwenzeka ndingayamkeli into endiyiyo. Wathi umongikazi akususa ibhandeji laqina ivumba. Yathi yakususwa ibhandeji yawa inyama yam. Omnye ugqirha wayezibambe ngeenkophe iinyembezi. Ndathi ndakuzibona ndasibona isizathu sokulila kwakhe. lnyama yam yayijinga okwekhandlela elinyibilikileyo. lnyama yentamo yayitshe yaphela. Kukho isihlunu esinye esasibambe isilevu sam. Wawungabonakali umlomo, yayingumngxuma nje. Ngaba ndinokuphinda ndibone? Ndacinga ndathi Thixo, kubhetele undithathe ngoku!

Funda ngokuzabalaza kwakhe ukuba acule kwakhona! Odola incwadi ethi Tandies Blues kwaViva Books, P.O. Box 2851 0, Kensington, 21 01. Uya kukhuthazwa sisibindi anaso.
