ePitoli.
UPele ongumdlali webhola omdala noyena ubalaseleyo owenza iBrazil yaphumelela indebe yehlabathi ngo 1958 nango 1970 kwaye wafaka ngaphezu kwamanqaku ali-1200 esadlala.
Namhlanje unguMphathiswo wezeMidlalo eBrazil kwaye wonyulwa nguMongameli Fernando Henrique Cardoso emva kokuba enze iSebe leMidlalo okokuqala eBrazil.
Kutyelelo lwakhe kweli ubenobubele yaye ekhuthaza xa ekudliwano-ndlebe okanye echazela umdlali osakhulayo into ethile.
Kumonwabise kakhulu ukubonana noMongameli Mandela nomhlobo wakhe wakudala uJomo Sono nabanye abadlali abaphambili kweli abanjengo Marks Maponyane.
Ubhengeze ukuba iqela lebhola lelizwe laseBrazil linokutyelela kweli loMzantsi-Afrika kunyaka ozayo kwaye kuza kwenziwa amalungiselelo okutshintshana ngabadlali nabaqeqeshi ukuncedisa kwiinkqubo
zokuphucula umdlalo webhol'ekhatywayo kweli.
UMzantsi-Afrika unabadlali abanesiphiwo nabangamachule. IAfrika iphela inabadlali abaneziphiwo. Eneneni, iziphiwo zabadlali baseAfrika ziyafana nezabadlali baseBrazil. Ndityelele eMelika nasejapan kodwa endikuqapheleyo kukuba abadlali baseAfrika banobuchule bendalo ebholeni.
Into efunekayo kubo kukuqeqeshwa nokulungiswa ngendlela eyiyo. Banazo zonke iziphiwo ezifunekayo kulo mdlalo, utshilo.
Ngaba ubona ngathi uya kuze abekho omnye uPele?
Akanakubakho, kaloku umama notata bayeka ukuzala. Lilonke asoze abekho omnye uPele.
UPele walifumana eli gama ekukhuleni kwakhe esayidlala esitratweni ibhola ekhatywayo. Akayazi eyona nto ithethwa leli gama kodwa walamkela ekukhuleni kwakhe ngenxa yokuba wonk'umntu wayembiza ngalo nangona wayebaxelela ukuba unguEdson.
Abanye abantu abakakwazi
ukundibiza ngoMphathiswa do Nascimento. Andazi ukuba baya kuqala nini ukundibiza kanjalo. Kucacile ukuba ndiza kuhlala ndibizwa ngoPele.
Uphoxeke kakhulu kukuna kwemvula okwenze akabi nako ukufundisa abadlali abaselula ibhola ekhatywayo.
lzifundo zebhola ebemele azenze eOrlando Stadium aziphumelelanga kwaye naye akakhange adlale emdlalweni we-Masters obuza kubaseFNB nobuza kugabulela izigcawu umdlalo weAfrika Champions Cup obuphakathi kweOrlando Pirates neEleven Men In Flight yaseSwazini.
Ubeza kudlalela neqela ebeliza kudlala nelikhokelwa nguJomo Sono kodwa awukhange udlalwe ngenxa yezantyala-ntyala zemvula.
Nangona kunjalo, uyile eOrlando Stadium apho abonane namakhwenkwe aseEsselen Park, awaseOrlando and Bosmond Development Centre nawase Johannesburg College of Education Super Centre. Nangona imvula ibisina la makhwenkwe achulumancile kukubonana nekumkani yebhol'ekhatywayo.
