Xa usela iincindi ezisandul' ukwenziwa, uzifumana ngokukhawuleza ziphelele izondlo zokutya zingangxengwanga.

IKHOLIFLAWA IKUGCINA UBUKEKA USEMTSHA

Ngaba ufuna ukuhlala usemtsha ixesha elide? Yitya ikholiflawa. Lo mfuno omnandi uneekhalori ezimbalwa kodwa uneentsinga ezininzi, ukwane glutathione eyienzyme elwa nokwaluphala. IGlutathione iluncedo ekuthinteleni amachiza ayityhefu eyingozi angangeni emzimbeni, utsho uJean Carper oyingcali oyingcali kwezempilo. Ezi tyhefu zinokulwenza lwaluphale ulusu namanye amalungu, zibangele izifo ezinokukubulala. Eyona ndlela intle kukuyitya ikholiflawa isandul ukuvunwa, usenokuyitya iyodwa okanye ixutywe neesaladi. IGlutathione ikho kwiidiliya, iitapile ezimhlophe, usenza, iipesika, amaqunube, iiorenji neetumato ezingaphekwanga.

IANABOLIC STERIODS
Inkoliso yabantu ngoku iyazazi iianabolic steriod maxa wambi ezisetyenziswa ekwakheni umzimba nezihlunu zeembaleki nabathanda ukuphakamisa iintsimbi.
Nakuba kusemthethweni ukuba ube neanabolic steriods ukuze uzisebenzise, ukuzisebenzisa kunokukubangela ujongwe ungafaneleki okanye ungamkelwa kwaphela kwimidlalo. Iziphumo ezitenxileyo zokuzisebenzisa ithuba elide kumadoda zinokuquka ukonakaliswa kwesibindi, ukuthotha kokuyileka kwamadlozi okanye ungazali, ukushwabana nokuqina kwamasende, ukuba lidlolo nokungakhuli kwamabele. Kwabasetyhini oku kuquka iingxaki ezinjengokuphazamiseka kokuya exesheni, iinguquko kwilungu lesini, ukuba nempandla, amaqhankqa ukuhluma uboya ebusweni nokuba sisitshotho.

UKWENZA IMITHAMBO GQITHA KUYAGULISA

Ukukhuthala ekwenzeni imithambo kunokukugulisa, kuthothisa amandla omzimba okulwa nezifo, lutsho uphando olwenziwa eOstreliya. Abaphandi kwiuniversity of Western Australia bathi bafumanise ukuba ukuthanda ukwenza umthambo kakhulu kuthothisa iiglutamine, ekuyiyo ebalulekileyo kwinkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba kwizifo.

PHELISA IVUMBA ELIBI LOMZIMBA

Ngaba uthanda ukuphelisa ivumba elibi emzimbeni? Ukusebenzisa ibicarbonate of soda endala engagalelwanga nto kuphelisa ivumba elibi emzimbeni. Amachiza enawo ibulala izinto ezenza umbilo ube nevumba elibi. Yithambise emakhwapheni xa ugqiba ukuhlamba umzimba, uya kunuka kamnandi imini yonke.

PHELISA ISITSHISA NGEGINGER TEA
Nanini na xa unesitshisa sela ikomityi yeginger tea efudumeleyo. Iginger inezithako ezilwa nokwenzeka manqaphanqapha, ithomalalisa ummandla wometyiso, utsho uGqr Kit Karson Crumb.Izinto ezibulala iiasidi zinexabiso eliphezulu kwaye zineziphumo ezitenxileyo, zitsho iingcali zempilo. Zikwenza urhude okanye kuphehl' iiasidi esiswini. Ukuze uphelise ukukhathazwa sisitshisa, sela ikomityi yeginger wakugqiba ukutya. Unokuzenzela uncuthu lwesiselo
ngokugalela iitispuni ezimbini zeginger root esandul ukugreyithwa, okanye iitispuni ezimbini zeground ginger ekomityini yamanzi abilayo. Yiyeke emanzini imizuzu elishumi, sela.

IPEANUT BUTTER IPHELISA
UKUPHUTHELWA
Ipeanut butter inesakha-mzimba ekuthiwa yitryptophan, ikubangela ube nobuthongo bendalo, iphelise ukumana ugqutshalaza, uphethuphethuka ebhedini, ikubangela uphumle kamnandi ubusuku bonke.
Iingcali zithi kufuneka utye amacephe amabini ukuze ulale. Itryptophan ngathi isebenza ngokufanayo neziyobisi eziqhelekileyo zobuthongo, utsho uGqr Sheldon Hendler okwiuniversity of California.
Inezithomalalisi ezingebukhali ezozelisayo kwaye unokulala kungekho ziphumo zitenxileyo ezidla ngokubakho xa usebenzise amanye amayeza obuthongo. Kuyanceda xa uyitya nokutya okunekhabhohidrate njengesonka phambi kokuba ulale.


IFISH OIL ITHOMALALISA IINTLUNGU
ZOMQOLO
Oku kwamafutha kwiihenjisi ezinomhlwa, ifish oil ithambisa amalungu aqaqambayo. Kufuneka nje uzithomalalise ngeepilisi ezimbini ngemini. Icod liver oil nezinye iifish oils zilwa nokudumba emalungwini zithomalalise neentlungu ingakumbi iingqaqambo zomqolo, utsho uGqr Earl Mindel, unjingalwazi wezondlo ePacific Western University eLos Angeles.
Esinye isithako kwifish oil  iomega-3 eyiasidi enamafutha ikwathomalalisa iintlungu nokuqina kwamalungu okubangelwa kukuqaqamba kwamathambo, ludiza oko uphando eAlbany Medical College eNew York. Kulula ukongeza icod liver oil ekutyeni. Unokufumana i-1 000mg yegel capsules kuzo naziphi na iivenkile ezithengisa ukutya okanye iikhemisti. Iingcali zempilo zincomela ukuba uginye iicapsules ezimbini ngemini.
