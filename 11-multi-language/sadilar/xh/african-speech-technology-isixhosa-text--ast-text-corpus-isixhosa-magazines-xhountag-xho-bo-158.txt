UMCEBISI weBONA


Mcebisi weBona, Andizalelwanga kweli, sendineminyaka engaphezu kwemine ndihleli kweli. Ndineshishini lam. Sekuyiminyaka emibini ndihlala nentokazi yakweli nendizimisele ukuyitshata. Kumazwe aseNtshona xa uMntu welinye ilizwe etshata intombi yelo uyalifumana ilungelo lokuba ngummi. Ngaba kunjalo apha eMzantsi Afrika? Enye ingxakana endinayo kukuba isithandwa sam asifuni ndibonane nabazali baso kanti abanye kowaso nezihlobo bayandazi. Isonjululwa njani ke le? Ngu A.A.A., eKostad .

LiSebe leKhaya elingasombulula ingxaki yakho yokuqala. Makhe sikhangele ekusengenzeka kube zizizathu zale ngxakiyesibini. Kusengenzeka isithandwa sakho sineentloni ngawe, okanye kungenxa yokubethabethana kwezithethe zenu kwaNtu. Abazali abantsundu abazibandakanyi kwezothando lwabantwana babo ngaphandle kokuba kwenzeka okukhulu njengokukhulelwa okanye ukutshata. Ukuba ke uzimisele ukusitshata isithandwa sakho, sixelele ukuba uthanda ukuya kubazali baso uyokusicela. Xa sekunjalo sisala siya kucaca isizathu sokwenyani.

Mcebiso WeBona, ityendyana lomfo ebendithandana nalo selithe phithi yeny' intokazi. Ndakuyibona ndiyacaphuka. Abah1obo bam bandicebise ukuba ndiyinkule kanobom, kodwa mna andiyiyo loo
ntlobo ilwa ngendoda. Ndimele ndimshiye nalo mfo ndizifumanele ongathandi mabhinqa? NguB.A.N., eBatharos .

Ndiyakuncoma ukubhadla kwakho kunabahlobo bakho. Akukho ndoda ongade uzithobe isidima ngenxa yayo. Amanenekazi akalwi ngendoda. Lowo ngumsebenzi wamaonontolotyi. Ukufumana indoda engahlab'iziduli kunzima kakhulu. Inkoliso yamadoda ingakumbi aselula ayithandi ukuzibophelela ngentokazi enye.

Mcebisi WeBona, Mide imilebe yelungu lam langasese, iyajinga. Amanye amantombazana ayandih1eka, sekuthethwa ngam kwihostela yesikolo esifunda kuso, Ndithini?
NguB.Z.S., eGcuwa.

Mhlawumbi endiza kukutsho kungakuthuthuzela, amanenekazi ezinye iintlanga angena kwenkulu inkxamleko nentlungu ezama ukufumana loo nto wena uyiphiwe yindalo kuba kusithiwa yenza isoya . Kuthi mandithi xola ngomzimba wakho, uyithande imilebe yelungu lakho langasese ozalwe nayo. Kodwa ukuba akunako, khawuyokuthetha nogqirha wezabasetyhini.

Mcebisi weBona, Ndiyintombazana ekwishumi elivisayo leminyaka. Hayi ndibuthathaka. Malunga neendibano zesondo
andikwazi ukuthi HAYI. Nakuba yona ingqondo yam isithi HAYI, umzimba wam wona uthi EWE. Ngenxa yoku ndiye ndaneendibano zesondo namakhwenkwe amaninzi, ndaza ndazenyanya. Onke aye andikhohlisa kuba emva koku akukho nanye kuwo ebuya ithande ukulala nam kwakhona. Kodwa ngenxa yokuba buthathaka kwam, ndiba neendibano zesondo kunye naye namphi na ondifunayo. NguJ.P.Z., ePinetown .

Nyhani usengxakini enkulu ingakumbi kule mihla yeAids ! Ukungakwazi ukuthi HAYI xa kuthethwa ngeendibano zesondo akukuthobi nje isidima kodwa kukwakubeka engozini enkulu yokuba uchole izifo ezidluliselwa ngeendibano zesondo, ukuba akukazicholi. Nguwe oziyekele kwelokuba kudlaliswe ngawe, IKWANGUWE onokukunqanda oko! Indoda inokuma apho uthi mayime khona. Ukuze wahlukane nalo mkhwa, hlukana nokuba nibe nodwa nenkwenkwe. Ukucetyiswa kunokukunceda. Khawuthethe nonontlalo-ntle kummandla okuwo ukuze akucebise okanye ufumane unyango.

wabadlali bayo.

INTSOMI  KAMAKAZ' UBARBARA
Molweni bantwana! Ibali endifuna ukunibalisela lona kule nyanga lithi:
ALIPHELI IXESHA LOKUFUNDA. Ndiyathemba ukuba niza kulonwabela.



Kwakukho nenekazi lithile elinguThembi. Wayehlala kwidolophu encinci nabantwana abathathu uJabu, uVusi noMandla.
Njengabo bonke oomama abalungileyo uThembi wabafundisa besebancinane ukuba kubalulekile ukunyaniseka. Xa uthatha into engeyoyakho okanye uphosisa kubakho indawo ebolayo entliziyweni yakho, wabalumkisa.
Niyayibona laa nkothovu yexhego inguvuma ihlala yodwa ngasemlanjeni -intliziyo yakhe yonke ayikho ntle kuba kaloku wayengenanyaniso yaye engalunganga. Akukho mntu umthandayo kuba akathembakalanga, watsho uThembi.
Abantwana bakaThembi babenyanisekile kwaye wayezingca ngabo. Naxa kunjalo, xa ebhake amaqebengwane e-oatmeal wayeqaphela ukuba aphela msinyane esityeni awayewagcina kuso.
Akubuza ukuba atyiwe ngubani babemthi phuhlu ngamehlo athembakeleyo baza baphendule ngaxeshanye bathi: Asindim mama.
Nyhani asindim. Wayengacaphuki kakhulu uThembi
kuphela nje wayebavela usizi. Ngokungathandabuzekiyo abantwana bakhe babesazi ukuba abanakubethwa xa benokuvuma ukuba bawatyile qha baya kungxoliswa. Kutheni ke ngoku beza kumphosisela phezu kokuba benganyanisekanga yaye bengenantobeko?
Kwafuneka uThembi aphande ukuba nguwuphi kwaba bantwana bakhe olisela waza wabhaka kodwa ke ngoku wafaka yeza lithile entlameni yakhe -lalingavakali qha lisenza isisu silume lisakutyiwa.
Njengesiqhelo wawagcina esityeni loo maqebengwana uThembi wayalela abantwana bakhe ukuba: Ningawatyi la maqebengwana. Niza kuwafumana ngomso xa niphunga iti .
Ngobo busuku untondo wakhe uJabu wavuka ekhala: Mama, mama! Ndiyafa! isisu sam sibuhlungu kakhulu!
Waqonda uThembi ukuba wehlelwe yintoni unyana wakhe. Ngaba uwatyile amaqebengwana Jabu? wambuza.
Hayi, andikhange ndiwatye mama.
Ndincede!
Ndingakunceda xa unokuthetha inyani kuphela! watsho.
Ekugqibeleni wavuma uJabu: Ewe, ndiwatyile. Ndicela uxolo!
UThembi umnike iglasi ezele liyeza elithile eliphelisa amahlaba. Ndiyathemba ukuba nifunde isifundo Jabu. Andifuni namnye umntwana wam oya kubolelwa yintliziyo kwaye ndiyakhathazeka xa niphosisa.
UJabu ufunde isifundo. Ufunde ukuba ukuxoka kubolisa intliziyo, kanti kunokwenza neentlungu!
