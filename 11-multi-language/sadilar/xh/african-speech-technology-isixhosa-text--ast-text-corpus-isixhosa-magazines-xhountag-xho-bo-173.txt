NABABETHA NGEZIKA TSHIWO
Inkqubo yomthetho kweli lizwe lethu iyandixaka maxa wambi. Eminye imithetho iyasebenza kwabanye abantu ukanti kwabanye ayisebenzi. Ngokomzekelo, abaqhubi beemoto bavunyelwe ukuba babe nesixa esithile sotywala egazini labo -okuhle kukuba Io mthetho unegalelo elithile kukhuseleko ezindleleni. Kodwa ke, kuthekani ngababetha ngezikaTshiwo? Bavunyelwa ukuba basele banxile, ngokwenjenje babang' iingozi ezindleleni - bade babangele neengozi ezimanyumnyezi zeemoto ingakumbi koohola beendlela. Ngokwazi kwam, ekuphela komthetho osebenza kubo kukungaseli esidlangalaleni. Loo mthetho awufane unyanzeliswe kuba ukusela kuyinto eqhelekileyo kuluntu lwethu. Ngokolwam uluvo, abantu ababetha ngezikaTshiwo ezindleleni kufuneka nabo bahlawuliswe baze bavalelwe njengoko kunjalo kubaqhubi zimoto abanxilayo. NguA.Z.M., eLady Frere.

R2OOOO
NGABA SIMKELWE ZIINGQONDO?
Mvanje ndiye emngcwabeni obuseDaveyton ndaza kamva kwafuneka ndinyangwe ngenxa yomothuko. Saxelelwa ukuba akukho mntu uza
kuzila, endaweni yoko kukhaliswe amakhasethi omculo. Amadoda ebeze ku1o mngcwabo ebenxibe iimpahla zexabiso befake nezihombo ezimbejembeje, abasetyhini benxibe iilokhwe ezibonisa umzimba. Bagalele utywala kwibhokisi yomngcwabo xa ibisehliswa, endaweni yokuba kugalelwe umhlaba okanye iintyatyambo njengesiqhelo, adubule ibhokisi yomngcwabo baza emva koko bayitshisa imoto y alowo oswelekileyo. Kwakhona badlalise iimoto emadlakeni, lo mkhwa ndingathi sisimbo sabenzi bobubi. Uluntu lwakowethu luphambene, akukho mntu oluhoyileyo. Amapolisa ayakuthanda ukuya kule mingcwabo ukuze onwabe. Ndiqinisekile ukuba ezi zaphuli-mthetho inezixhobo ezingekho mthethweni yaye ziqhuba iimoto ezibiweyo. Akukho mntu uphilileyo ongatshisa imoto ayithenge ngemali ayisebenzele nzima. Kweli Iizwe lethu amanani olwaphulo-mthetho antingile -- abantu bayalukhalazela kodwa babe beluxhasa ngandlela thile ngokuthenga izinto ezibiweyo. Ndidikiwe kukuphulaphula uJessie Dwarte othi wenza konke okusemandleni akhe ukuphelisa ulwaphulomthetho. Ukuba wenza konke okusemandleni akhe, lonke iSebe
lamaPolisa aseGauteng lifanele lichithwe aze onke aphelelwe ngumsebenzi. Esikufumanayo kwisebe Iamapolisa kukuthetha kakhulu, andimbeki-tyala kwaphela uJessie. Uluntu lufuna ukukhe Iucocwe iingqondo kuba ingathi ziye zisimka - akukho mntu osahlonipha iinasiko nokuxabiseka kwawo. Lifikile ixesha lokuba abazali babuye babafundise abantwana babo ngezinto ezibalulekileyo ebomini ukuze uMzantsi Afrika ube lilizwe ekunokuphilwa kulo.
NguZ.P.N., eBenoni.

ANGABANTU
Hayi indlela ekukhathaza ngayo ukubona amapolisa ebulawa ephangele. Oku akuyomfuneko. Akholo ukuqinisekisa ukuba kukho uxolo nocwangco, esikhusela kubaphangi nababulali abawuthathela ezandleni zabo umthetho. Amatyendyana afuna ukuba ngamapolisa kufuneka aqonde ukuba azombela ingcwaba kuba kulo msebenzi unokusutywa kukufa. Khawucinge nje xa ilizwe Iingenamapolisa - akukho mntu ebenokuziva ekhuselekile. Bekungakho iziphendu nobomi obonwabisayo bungabikho emhlabeni. Amapolisa kufuneka ahlonitshwe noluntu kufuneka lusebenzisane nawo kangangoko kunokwenzeka. Loo minyaka yobumnyama yocalucalulo idlule amapolisa anamhlanje akholo ukukhusela namphi na ummi othobela umthetho.
