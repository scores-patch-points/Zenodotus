I
 GAMA elithi AIDS sisishunqulelo samagama athi (Acquired lmmune
Deficiency Syndrome). Xa
unentsholongwane YeAIDS inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho inokuba
buthathaka.
Kodwa inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho inamandla kunokuba ucinga, iyakwazi ukulwa nentsholongwane yeAIDS. Ukuze uyakhe kwaye uyixhase inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba kubalulekile ukuyiqonda.
lnkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba yinxalenye yomzimba engabonakali njengamanye amalungu anjengentliziyo, isibindi nolusu. lfana nomkhosi wokh useleko lwesizwe. Umkhosi ulwa nalo naluphi na utshaba oluhlaselayo, okanye oluyingozi kwisizwe. Ngokufanayo inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba ilwa neentsholongwane ezizama ukuhlasela imizimba yethu.
Oku komkhosi inamajoni awahlukahlukeneyo alwa emhlabeni, emoyeni okanye elwandle, inkqubo yakho yokuzikhusela komzimba ilwa kwizigaba ngezigaba. Ukuze ilwe, inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba ifuna izixhobo neendlela ezintle
ezifu manekayo.
Ngaphandle kwamayeza alwa neentsholongwane esona sixhobo singcono ongasinika inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho kukutya ukutya okunempilo okunokukwazi ukusebenza kakuhle. Ukutya okunemoilo kunika umzimba wakho izondlo ezifunekayo eziphucula amandta neziluncedo ekuthinteleni isifo.
Naluphi na uhlobo Iwesifo luyenza buthathaka inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho kungako kufuneka kuliwe naso msinya nangokuphumelelayo. Oku kuquka izifo ezidluliselwa ngeendibano zesondo.
Xa unentsholongwane yeAlDS, kubalulekile ukuba ube neendibano zesondo ezikhuselekileyo ukuzikhusela ekuphindeni uhlaselwe kwakhona yintsholongwane yeAIDS kwanezifo ezidluliselwa ngokuba neendibano zesondo.
Kodwa inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba ayisebenzi kuphela emzimbeni. Enye indlela yindlela ophila ngayo, njengokuziyekelela kucinezeleko engqondweni, ukuba neemvakalelo ezilandulayo nezinika ithemba kwanesimo sengqondo ngokomoya, zonke ziba negalelo kwinkqubo yomzimba yokuzikhusela.

Ukuze ube sempilweni nakuba unentsholongwane yeAIDS, kubalulekile ukuphepha ukusoloko ucinezelekile engqondweni kuquka ukucinezeleka ngokwemvakalelo zama ukuba uhlale udlamkile uzinika ithemba usithi ndiza kuphila, kunokuba ube nexhala lokuthi iyeza imini yokufa kwam.
Ukholo lubalulekile kakhulu xa kuthethwa ngokusinda. Kufuneka sizame ukuzomeleza ngokomoya silufune uncedo. Ululeko, ukucamngca, ukuziqinisa zizixhobo ezinamandla zokuzakha ubuqu nokomoleza inkqubo yomzimba yokuzikhusela nangokwasengqondweni.
Nkqu nokuba intsholongwane yeAIDS iyenza buthathaka inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho, unokwenza okuthile ngayo. Nguwe ekufuneka ubone ukuba ukhetha hlobo luni lwendlela yokuphila - indlela yokuphila exhasa neyomeleza inkqubo yokuzikhusela komzimba wakho okanye indlela yokuphila eyenza buthathaka nenceda ukuba intsholongwaye yeAlDS iyitshabalalise, kuze ekugqibeleni amathuba akho okuphila aye ephela.
Xa ndaqala ukuxilongelwa intsholongwane yeAIDS ngo-1993 akukho mntu wandixelela ukuba oku kuza kuthetha ntoni ebomini bam. Kwandithatha iminyaka ukuqokelela inkcazelo ekufuneka ndiyazi ngokuqhubeka emzimbeni wam. Ukuqonda intsholongwane yeAIDS nendlela ebuchaphazela ngayo ubomi bam kundomelezile ukuze ndiphile ndinethemba ndisempilweni.
