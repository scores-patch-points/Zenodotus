Ukungatshotshelani kwale mibutho bekubangelwa kukuba neembono ezahlukeneyo.
I-NSC ibigxekwa kanobom ngumbutho weSports & Recreation ngokungabi nanto iyenzayo ekuphuculeni umgangatho wemidlalo yabantwana basezilokishini nabasezilalini -nakubeni inayo imali yokwenza oko.
I-NSC yona ibifuna ukuqonda kwigosa lezemidlalo likarhulumente waKwaZulu, UPhilemon Goge , ukuba yintoni eyenziwe ngumbutho wakhe kwiminyaka eli-15 edlulileyo ujongene nokuphucula umgangatho wemidlalo.

Abantu abaninzi babengayithandi into eqhubekayo, yaye bayikwaya imidlalo yeNational Development Games eyayiququzelelwa yiNSC Sothern Natal .
Abantu abehlukeneyo ababekwaye le midlalo bathi bafuna iNSC ibanike imali yokuba bayenze ngokwabo le midlalo. IOfisi enkulu yeNSC yayikhaba ngawo omane loo nto.
Saxelelwa ngale ngxaki isigqeba SeNSC neSebe lezeMidlalo. ISebe lezeMidlalo laphendula ngokuxelela amaqela abandakanyekayo ukuba enze iinzame zokusombulula iingxaki zawo.
Loo myalelo awuzange usiwe so yiKwaZulu/ Natal Sports & Recreation engazange iye kwiDevelopment Games ezaqhubekayo nakubeni ingekho.
Ngenxa yale meko, uMphathiswa wezeMidlalo, uSteve Tshwete, wayigxeka kanobom iNSC kukoyiswa kwayo ukwenza umsebenzi wayo ngendlela elanelekileyo elizweni lonke, hayi kwiphondo lakwaZuluNatal kuphela.
Waxelela nabo babeyikwayile imidlalo ukuba ngokwenjenjalo abalungisi nto, koko banciphisa amathuba eembaleki ezahlukeneyo.
Ukungavisisani kwale mibutho akuzuyinceda nganto imidlalo. Amagosa emibutho
afanele asombulule iingxaki zawo aze aqalise ukuzama ukuphucula umgangatho wemidlalo.
Akufanelanga kubekho igosa lombutho wemidlalo elithintela iimbaleki ekubeni nenxaxheba emidlalweni. Nabani na owenza loo nto ubonisa ukuba akayithandi imidlalo, utshilo uMphathiswa uTshwete. Kuthe ekuqaleni kukaAprili , uHarry Naidu , onguSihlalo weNSC eSouthern Natal , noPhilemon Goge wekwaZulu/ Natal Sports & Recreation , bavumelana ngokuba basebenzisane ekusombululeni iingxaki zokuphuculwa komgangatho wemidlalo.
Siyasebenzisana kwiinzame zokuphucula umgangatho wezemidlalo nabantu, kuquka nabasezilalini.
Le ngxaki ayikho kwingingqi yeSouthern Natal kuphela, utshilo uMnu. Naidu .
