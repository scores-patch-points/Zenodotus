iSurviving Rape
Uphengululo
ISurviving Rape isandul' ukupapashwa yiNew Reader Project eThekwini. Le ncwadi ilungcelele lokufundisa abantu ngamalungelo abo. Ibhalwe ngolwimi olulula yaye iluncedo ebantwini abafuna ukuphucula ulwazi lwabo lokufunda. UTyhila Ufunde ukuzisela olu phengululo!

Baninzi abantu abadlwengulwayo eMzantsi-Afrika yonke imihla. Ngenxa yoko kubalulekile ukuba abantu bazi kangangoko benako ngako. Kufuneka sazi amalungelo ethu nemasikwenze xa
sidlwenguliwe. Naku okuza kwenziwa yile ncwadi.

Iinkcukacha ezikwisurviving Rape zibhalwe kakuhle yaye ziluncedo. Unikwa amacebo amaninzi ngomawukwenze xa udlwenguliwe. Kukho noluhlu lwemibutho enokukunceda xa udlwenguliwe.

Ngaba ikho into enokwenziwa ukuphepha ukudlwengulwa? Oko kuchaziwe kule ncwadi,.. Kubalulekile ukwazi indlela yokunciphisa amathuba okudlwengulwa. Kunzima nokuphepha ukudlwengulwa.

Le ncwadi iza kuba luncedo ebantwini abaninzi. Ishicilelwe ngesiNgesi nangesiZulu. Xa ufuna ukuthola iSurviving Rape , unokubhalela kwiThe New Readers Project , Department of Adult and Community Education , University of Natal , Durban , 4014.

Konke ngeJazz !
Ukhe uve ngoKippie Moeketsi noTandie Klaasen ? Zimvumi zeJazz ezaziwayo ezo. Ngoku abakwaViva Books bakubhalela amabali abo ngolwimi olulula. Zombini ezi ncwadi
ziyanceda ekuphuculeni ulwazi lwakho lokufunda.

UKippie Moeketsi wayeyimvumi ephambili yeJazz eMzantsi-Afrika. Wayetsala nzima ebomini yaye wasweleka elihlwempu. Kwakungenzeka njani oko kwimvumi eyayinesiphiwo ngolo hlobo? Incwadi ethi Kippie Moeketsi. The Sad Man of Jazz isibalisela ngobomi bakhe ukususela ekukhuleni kwakhe kuse ekunxileni nasekufeni kwakhe. Ingakuphosi le ncwadi

Ibali likaTandie Klaasen alifani nelikaKippies . Akazange afikelele lula enkcochoyini yaye wayesenempumelelo ngoo-1970. Ngelo xesha wayecula neHarlem Swingsters yaye wayekhenkethe eLondon nomdlalo weqonga othandwa othi King Kong . Ubomi bakhe baguquka - xa wathelwa ngepetroli lulutsha olubini lwaza lwamtshisa. Incwadi ethi Thandie's Blues isibalisela ngamaxesha obunzima ebomini bakhe nendlela owayezisombulula ngayo iingxaki zakhe.

Xa unomdla wokufunda ngobomi beemvumi zeJazz ezazidumile, zifanele zingakuphosi ezi ncwadi. Ukhumbule ukuba ziya kukunceda uphucule ulwazi lwakho lwesiNgesi. Xa uziOdola , bhalela kule dilesi : Viva Books , P.O. Box 28510, Kensington , 2101.

Ukungabikho koxolo ekhaya
Imbi into yokuba lungabikho uxolo phakathi kwabazali ekhaya. Abazali abasoloko bexambulisana phambi kwabantwana abaqondi ukuba
oko kuyabaphazamisa abantwana. Bagqibela bengazi ukuba bafanele bavumelane nowuphi umzali. Oko kubenza babe ngamalolo kuchaphazele nenkqubo yabo esikolweni . Esikolweni abamameli into abayifundiswayo basoloko becinga ngeengxaki zakowabo.

Abazali mabahlale ngoxolo ekhaya xa befuna abantwana babo babe nenkqubela ebomini. Bafanale babonakalise ukuba bayathandana. Sonke siyazi ukuba apho kukho uthando kukho uxolo.

NguV.P. Diko, kukomani

Ndifunda kwisikolo apho abafundi abantsundu baphethwe kakubi. Abafundi babelungu bayabaleka xa bebona abafundi abantsundu ngokungathi babona inyoka.
