ABANTWANA ABANGOONONGOGO  INGXAKI ENTSHA
EMZANTSI-AFRIKA
Kwiphepha lama-20 nelama-21 kolu shicilelo, sikhuphe iifoto ezikhwankqisayo zamantombazana aselula aphila ngokuthengisa imizimba yawo emadodeni azizinhanha embindini weJohannesburg . La mantombazana asemancinane kangangokuba amanye aneminyaka eli-12, kodwa anolwazi olupheleleyo ngobomi bokuhlala ezitratweni , yaye akukho mntu unokuwafundisa into entsha!
Xa ebesichazela izinto ezifunwa ngabaxumi bawo kwindibano yesondo, siye saqonda ukuba sisenzo esimanyumnyezi esingafanelanga samkelwe luluntu esi!
Khawufan'ucinge xa indoda enesidima -ingakumbi etshatileyo - ithenga ukuba nendibano yesondo nomntwana oneminyaka eli-12. Zayaphi iimvakalelo zobuzali nokuziphatha kakuhle? Ngaba kukukhanuka isondo ngokugqithisileyo okunjani oku?
Aba bantwana babeka impilo nokhuseleko lwabo esichengeni ngokuvuma ukuthathwa ngabantu abangabaziyo bahambe nabo ngeemoto . Badla ngokuthathwa basiwe kwiikona ezimnyama, apho bafika basetyenziswe kakubi ngokwesondo kwizitulo zangasemva zeemoto , baze bahlawulwe imali encinane okanye balaxazwe bengafumananga nepeni emdaka besazi ukuba akukho mntu banokucela uncedo kuye.
Njengoko isifo seAIDS sifike ngamand1a, aba bantwana baza kusinwenwiswa okomlilo wedobo. Siyathemba ukuba uMongameli Mandela noKumkani Goodwill Zwelithini baza kulifunda eli nqaku, ukuze bazame ukuyisombulula le ngxaki kumaphulo abo abantwana yaye bawuphelise ngokukhawuleza lo mkhwa mbi. Abantwana bethu baxabiseke gqitha ukuba bangaphila ngokuthengisa ngemizimba yabo. Makwenziwe iinzame zokuwunqanda lo mkhwa.
De kube lixesha elizayo, ndiyathemba ukuba niza kulubona lukufanele ukufundwa olu shicilelo, kwaye lunamanye amanqaku onwabisayo.

NguDAIZER MQHABA (UMHLELI OMKHULU)





10 BONA- AUGUST 1995
OZUZE I-R50 !
INKQATHO KUKHUPHISWANO LWE-SHELL ROAD TO FAME
Ndingumfundi wasepitoli owenza unyaka wesithathu kwizifundo zomculo, yaye bendimenywe nabanye abafundi ukuba siyoncedisa abagwebi kukhuphiswano lweShell road to fame . Siye samangaliswa kukuba singaziswa kubagwebi bolu khuphiswano nakukungahlaliswa kunye nabo. Thina besihleli nabantu abebezobukela. Bebebabini abagwebi, omnye wabo ndiyazi ukuba ungumdanisi walapha. Ndiye ndathi nqa ukuba angathini umntu ongumdanisi abe ngumgwebi kukhuphiswano lomculo. Ndiqaphele nokuba abagwebi abakhange babhale nto tu phantsi xa bekuqhubeka ukhuphiswano ngokungathi bebesazi kwakuqala ukuba ngubani oza kuphumelela. Siye sahlala sabukela izinto ezahlukeneyo ebezisenziwa ngabebengenele ukhuphiswano, kodwa into ebuhlungu kukuba abakhange baphumelele abantu abebefanele baphumelele. Ngabagwebi kuphela abaziyo ukuba babakhethe njani abaphumeleleyo. Kodwa thina siwufundeleyo umculo, asivumelani nezigqibo zabo. Isidanisile indlela oluqhutywe ngayo yaye kubekho inkqatho kwiziphumo. Kwabebefanele baphumelele kodwa abaphumelela, ndithi kubo: Qhubekani nomsebenzi omhle! Ningatyhafi! NguP.S. ePitoli .
