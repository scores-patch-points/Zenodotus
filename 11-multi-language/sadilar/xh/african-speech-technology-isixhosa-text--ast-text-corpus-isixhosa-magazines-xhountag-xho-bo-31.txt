Iyenyuka iBONA
Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto yinto engenakunqandwa njengokufa. Nakubeni kuyinto engathandekiyo, akukho ndlela yimbi esinokuyiphepha ngayo ngaphandle kokuba sizame ukuyamkela. Eyona nto ibhetele ngokunyuka kwexabiso kukuba xa lisenyuka unako ukongeza okuthile kwimveliso yakho ukuze ibe nako ukulifanela elo xabiso - ukanti xa kutshone umntu wakowenu awunako ukuvala isithubo sakhe ngenye into. lxabiso IeBONA ukususela kolu shicilelo liza kunyuka nge-45c, nto Ieyo ithetha ukuba ixabiso elitsha liza kuba yi-R5,95 xa sele kufakwe neVAT . Sele sinexesha sizama ukunqonda ukunyuka kwexabiso laIe magazini . lxabiso layo lagqibela ukunyuka ngoseptemba ka-1998 xa iBONA yanyuka isuka kwi. R4,50 yaba yi-R5,50. Alikhange linyuke ixabiso iayo unyaka wonke nesiqingatha. Kodwa kuthe ngenxa yokunyuka kweendleko zephepha ezokushicilela,  ezeinki nezokuthumelo imagazini  kwiindawo ngeedawo1 kwanyanzeleka ukuba silinyuse ixabiso layo.
Eyona nto isivuyisayo kukuba nangona Iinyukile ixabiso IeBONA, iseza kuhlalo iyenye yezona magazini zinexabiso eliphantsi kweli lizwe. Inkampani yakwaCaxton  Magazines eshicilela iBONA iyaqonda ukuba nangona kufuneko ishicilele imagazini enenzuzo kufuneka ibacingele nabafundi bethu. Eneneni ukunyuka kwexabiso le magazini ephambili njengeBONA nge-45c kusenokubonwa kuphantsi kakhulu nabanye abantu.
Sizame ukuwunyusa umthomo weBONA ngokubo kushicilelo lukaApril siqalise umhlathi wokulungiswa kweenwele oza kubakho rhoqo. umhlothi uyenye yezinto enanithe masizenze xa sasithe sibhaleleni nisixelele enikufunayo. Safumana iimbono ezininzi kodwa sibone ukuba masiqale ngomhlathi wokulungiswa kweenwele njengoko sele kuyinto ethandwayo kwishishini lezobuhle eMzantsi Afrika.
lngcali kunonophelo lweenwele iza kukuchazela konke ofuna ukukwazi ngalo mba ubalulekileyo wokuzenza mhle.
IBONA iza kuqhuboka nokuniphathela amabali anomdla nanemfundiso - ukususela kumabali angeemvumi zeli kuse kwiinkcukacha zemisebenzi ezifunwa kakhulu ngabantu. OIu shicilelo nalo Iukuphothele amabali onomdla. Sikuphathele uRebecca Malope elinganisa iilokhwe ezintle zomtshato, nto leyo ibonisa ukuba sekumbovu itshate le mvumi yomculo wegospile . Sikuphathele nebali elihambis' umzimba leziphumo zempindezelo eyachaphazela umntu ekwakungahIaselwi yeno. Sifanele sixolise ngokuba singabi nalo ibali lemifanekiso - iimvula ebezihlasele ngomandla KwaZulu-Natal zenze kwunzima ukufota iindawo ebeziza kulalelwa phandle zeli bali, kodwa ke Iiza kuba likhona kushicilelo olulandelayo. Nikonwabele ukufunda olu shicilelo kwaye siyo kuzamkela nezimvo zethu ngokunyuka kwexabiso lale magazini .

Ukrexezo alusosisombululo
Ndiyintombazana engazani nandoda kwaye ndiyazidla ngalo nto. Andivumelani namakhosikazi anganyaniseka kubayeni bawo ngenxa yeengxaki abanazo emtshatweni - ukukrexeza soze kusombulule zingxaki.
Amabhinqa kufuneka aziphathe kakuhle abe ngumzekelo omhle kumantombazana asakhulayo. Amabhinqa aselula makaqiqe phambi ngokuba atshate - xa ungonwabanga ugqibela sele usenza izinto ezigwenxa. Masomelele silwele amalungelo ethu sihloniphe imizimba yethu.
nguM.J.T, eLindley

eliza kukonwabisa.

AKULUNGANGA
