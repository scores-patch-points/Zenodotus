Kwiinyanga ezisixhenxe ezidiulileyo IBONA iguquke yaba yimagazini entsha nezinikeleyo ekunyuseni umgangatho wobuntu. Oku kwakusenziwa kukuba iyimagcizini yabafundi abaneenongo, abaneembopheleleko nabathanda ulonwabo. Iziphumo nentsabelo esiyifumeneyo kubantu ngabantu ibonise ngokuphandle ukuba sihamba ekhondweni elililo. Asikuphathelanga imagazini entle nje kuphela nokuye kwabangela safowunelwa ngabafundi nababhengezi abaninzi besincoma, koko linyukile nenani labantu abayithandayo. Ushicilelo lukajanuwari futhengise ngokuthe kraya ku nolukaDisemba.

Akukho mntu ubeyilindele loo nfo yaye iye yancumisa wonk umntu apha kwaBONA, phofu ke iphinde yasinika ucelo-mngeni lokuba siqhubeke nokuvelisela nina bafundi IBONA esemgangathwen i en iyilindeleyo. Siyazi ukuba ukugcina imagazini ikhula kufana nokugcina ukunyuka kwexabiso legolide kwiimalike zehlabafhi. Kodwa siyazi ukuba iincwadi esizifumana zivela kuni bafundi ziza kuqinisekisa ukuba iBONA ihlala iyinfandane yenu, de ifumane nabanye abafundi abatsha.

Siqinisekile ukuba siza kufumana abafundi abatsha ngenxa yecandelo lethu Jokulungisa iinwefe efiza kubakho rhoqo, neliqafe kwiBONA yale nyanga. Eli candelo liza kuya lisanda ngokwanda yaye libe bhetele ngokuba bhetele, yaye liza kuba neenkcukacha ezivela kwiingcali zokulungisa iinwele zeli nezophesheya.

Kwiminyaka ediulileyo kuninzi obekuthethwa nobekubhalwa ngesiko elidala lamaXhosa lolwaluko. Amaphepha-ndaba nomabonakude ebevela neendaba ezihambis umzimba ngolwaluko - maxa wambi abakhwetha benzakele amalungu obudoda angabi nakuba safungiseka.
