Siphiwe: Ndicinga ukuba ufanele ukummangalela. UMnu. Ramsden akazange akuqeqeshe. Akazange azame ukufumana indlela yokusombutula ingxaki yakho ngaphambi kokuba akugxothe.

Siphiwe: Usenokunikwa imbuyekezo kuba anisavani noMnu. Rarnsden.

Inyathelo 2:
Ukusombulula imbambano
Umoletets neshopstewrd yakhe bagqibe ekubeni ufanele amangale. Kufuneka bafumane indawo abanokucela uncedo lokusonjululwa kwembambano.
Umthetho omtsha uchaza yonke imibutho namaqumrhu achophela iimbambano. Kufuneka owona mbutho ukufaneleyo. Oko kuxhomekeka ekubeni:
kugxothwe iqela labasebenzi na okanye kugxothwe umsebenzi omnye,
nakwisizathu sokugxothwa kwakho.

Ukugxothwa komntu omnye kuba engasebenzi kakuhfe okanye ukungaziphathi kakuhle kuchotshelwa yiCommission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) okanye iBargaining or Statutory Council (BCISC).

Ngaba oko kuthetha ntoni? l-CCMA liqumrrhu efitsha elisandui ukusekwa. Amalungu azimeleyo eCCMA achophela yaye asombulule iimbambano. Oku kuthetha ukuba aphulaphula okutshiwo ngumsebenzi nangumqeshi baze benze isigqibo sokuba ubefanele na okanye ubengafanelanga ukugxothwa umsebenzi.

lkho eminye imibutho echophela iimbambano. Ezi ziiBargaining and Statutory Councifs kunye neLabour Court neLabour Appeat Court. lshopstewrd yakho okanye umququzeleli wombutho wabasebenzi uya kukuxelela eyona ufanele ufake isimangalo sakho kuyo.
