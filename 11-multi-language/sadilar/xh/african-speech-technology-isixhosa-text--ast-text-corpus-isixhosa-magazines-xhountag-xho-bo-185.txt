Ngoku ithi ayizikuhlawula kuba umkhwe wam wophula umthetho wendlela. nguS.D..M., eAcornhoek. Ukuba ingozi awasweleka kuyo umkhwe wakho ibangelwe kukungakhathali kwakhe, intlawulo eniyifuna kwithird party ayinakuhlawulwa kuba nguye owabangela loo ngozi. Kodwa ke, ukuba ngenxenye ukwenzakala kwakhe kubangelwe nguye kwaze ngenxenye kwayimpazamo yomnye, ninako ukuyifuna intlazuulo, kodwa umonakalo uya kuhlawulwa ngokobungakanani bempazamo eyenziwe nguye.

Kufuneka ucele igqwetha elikumeleyo likuchazele ngeziphumo zetyala lolwaphulo-mthetho okanye ezophando kuba zingabubungqina bungakhathali bungqina obo bubalulekileyo ukuze nikwazi ukuyifumana intlawulo kwithird party.
