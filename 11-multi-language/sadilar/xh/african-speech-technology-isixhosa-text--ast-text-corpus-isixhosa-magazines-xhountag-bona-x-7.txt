IMBALI

Ubukhoboka eKoloni
Ukwenza uphando kubalulekile xa sifuna ukufumana iinkcukacha ezibhekele phaya
ngembali. Sinokwazi njani ngezinto ezenzeka mandulo ekungekho bantu bazibonayo
okanye izinto ezabhalwa ngazo phantsi? Kule nyanga uTyhila Ufunde ubonisa
abafundi beBanga lesi-5 nelesi-6 indlela yokukwenza oko! Sikwenza oko
ngokuqwalasela ubukhoboka eMzantsi-Afrika.

Uyazi ukuba abantu abaninzi abahlala eMzantsi-Afrika namhlanje banezinyanya ezazingamakhoboka eKoloni ? Abantu abaninzi bayamangaliswa yiloo nto. Amakhoboka ayelandwa anjenge Angola, Malasia naseSri Lanka . Singazifumana njani ezinye iinkcukacha ngale mbali xa yenzeka kwiminyaka engama-300 eyadlulayo?

Ukuqokelela ubungqina
Buncinane ubungqina obufumana xa uphanda ngembali yezinto ezenzeka kudala. Zithathu izizathu zoko:
 izinto ezenzekayo zalityalwa. Ubungqina busenokuba batsha, balahlwa okanye balahleka.
 zazingekho iikhamera okanye iifoto eziya kugcina ubungqina bezinto ezazisenzeka.
 babembalwa abantu ababekwazi ukubhala, ngoko zazimbalwa izinto ezibhalwayo. Le mibhalo yayidla ngokuba nomkhethe kuba wawufumana ukuba izizimvo zombhali wayo.

Oko kwenza kube nzima ukufumana ukuba kwenzeka ntoni kanye. Phofu ke zininzi iindlela onokufumana ngazo ubungqina. Masijonge indlela ababufumana ngayo ubungqina ababhali bembali xa babephanda ngobukhoboka eKoloni .

Umthombo 2:
Iincwadi
Kuphela kweleta ekhoyo le eyabhalwa likhoboka laseKoloni . Ibhalwe ngesiBuginese, ulwimi oluthethwa ngamanye amakhoboka aseEast Indies . Umbhali wale leta uxelela umhlobo wakhe ukuba ubengaphilanga, ngoko ubengenako ukumtyelela. Uhambisa athi onke amakhoboka angamaBuginese makamanyane yaye ancedane kwimeko akuyo ebuhlungu yokuba ngamakhoboka.

Umthombo 2: Uphando Ngemvelaphi
Ii-Archeologist ngabantu abaphanda ngeendawo ezazihlala abantu. Akunakwenzeka ukuba abantu bangashiyi nto ibonisa ukuba bakhe bahlala kuloo ndawo bakuyo. Izinto ezinjengeendlela, iindonga zezakhiwo ezidala nezinto eziyinkunkuma ezasalayo. Oko kunika umkhondo ngabantu ababehlala kuloo ndawo.

Ngo-1991, Ii-Archeologist saseUniversity of Cape Town ezaziphanda ngobukhoboka zaqala ukomba izinto ezashiyeka kwifama ekufuphi naseSomerset West . Igama lale fama yiVergelegen yaye kwakuyekaWitlem van de Stel , uMlawuli weCape Colony ukususela ngo-1699 kuse ku-1707.
Iqela lee-Archaeologist lafumana imali endala, imibhobho emidala kunye nebhola yenkanunu. Ngaphantsi komhlaba kwindawo eyayihlala amakhoboka bafumana namathambo omntu. Bacinga ukuba loo mathambo kwakungawekhoboka elilibhinqa elasweleka lineminyaka emalunga namashumi amahlanu. Amazinyo akhe ayebonisa ukuba ungumntu owayitya kakhulu intlanzi kanti ekutya kancinane ukutya okunesitatshi . Bamthiya igama lokuba nguFlora .

Umsebenzi 1
Ngokusebenzisa yonke le mithombo ingasentla, bhala imbali emfutshane ngobukhoboka. Ngaba le mithombo ikunika iinkcukacha ezaneleyo? Yeyiphi enye indawo onokufumana iinkcukacha kuyo?

Imibhalo yenkundla
Njengoko ufumene kwimithombo yangaphambili, amakhoboka ayephethwe kakubi gqitha. Ayemaninzi amatyala ngenxa yoko. Nangona ikhoboka nomnini walo babenako ukumangala enkundleni, iinkundla zazidla ngokutyekela ngakubanini bamakhoboka.

Ngexesha lamakhoboka kwakubhalwa phantsi okutshiwo kwiinkundla zamatyala. Imibhalo yamatyala amakhoboka nabaphathi bawo agcinwe kwithala leencwadi elilodwa eKapa ekuthiwa yiCape Archives .

Umxholo 20
 Ukufunda ngobuchule
Abantwana abaninzi bayathanda ukukubonisa into abazenzele bona ngokwabo. Amaxesha amaninzi kudla ngokuba yimigca emininzi okanye imibala efakwe ephepheni. Kodwa xa umntwana esenza into, uyafunda nokufunda.
Okufundwa ngumntwana
Umntwana unokufunda izinto ezininzi ngokwenza izinto ezilula azicingele yena. Ukuzoba, ukufaka umbala, nokudlala ngentlama kuya kubenza:
 zikhule izihlunu ezandleni naseminweni yabo. Oko kunokubanceda balungele ukuya
esikolweni apho baya kufunda ukubhala;
 baqonde ukuba mininzi imibala eyahlukeneyo namagama emibala eyahlukeneyo;
 kubafundisa ukuma kwezinto ezahlukeneyo;
 kubenza bazazi zinto ezikufutshane nabo, benze izinto abazibonayo bakwazi nokusebenzisa amandla abo okuyila engqondweni;
 bacinge bakwazi nokwenza izigqibo.



20 BONA XHOSA - August 1995


ABANTWANA ABANCINCI


Izinto Onokuzenza

Ukuzoba Nofufaka Umbala

Udla ngokubazi ubudala bomntwana ngokujonga into ayizobileyo okanye ayifake umbala. Umntwana udla ngokukwazi ukubamba ipensile xa eneminyaka emithathu kodwa angakwazi ukuzoba umfanekiso obonakalayo. Uya kuya efunda xa seneminyaka emine ukuya phezulu.

Bancome abantwana bakho xa bekubonisa izinto abazenzileyo! Oko kuya kubakhuthaza. Nazi zinto ezonwabisayo onokuthi abantwana bakho mabazenze! Sikubonisa nendlela neendawo onokufumana kuzo izinto ezingabizi imali enkulu.

Izinto ekufuneka ube nazo
Iphepha
Iphepha liziindleko. Nanga amacebo okufumana iphepha elibiza imali encinane:

 Sebenzisa iimvulophu ezindala ezivuliweyo

 amakhadibhodi anokutya avuliweyo

 Cela ivenkile okanye iofisi ekufutshane nawe ikuphe amaphepha abangawasebenzisiyo

Ipeyinti

Unokuzenzela eyakho ipeyinti ngokuxuba iflawa namanzi. Ngokugalela amanzi amaninzi, iya kuba manzi ipeyinti yakho.
Zikhethele ukuba ufuna ijiye kangakanani. Ngokugalela into ejika ibala lokutya uya kwenza ipeyinti yakho ibe nombala owufunayo. Unako ukuthenga into yokujika ibala lokutya nakuyiphi na ivenkile okanye isuphamakethi .

Izinto zokuzoba nezokufaka umbala

 Umntwana wakho unokuqala ukupeyinta ngeminwe. Ngokuhamba kwexesha unokusebenzisa izinti neebhrashi .
 iikhrayoni neepensile 

Amacebiso omawukwenze
ukupeyinta ngeminwe

Xa abantwana beqala ukupeyinta , bayathanda ukuyiva nokudlala ngepeyinti . ukupeyinta ngeminwe yeyona ndlela ilula yokuqala ukupeyinta . Kufuneka
Ipeyinti ijiye. Mabathi nxu iminwe yabo epeyintini , bazoze kwiphepha okanye bafake umbala emfanekisweni owuzobileyo.

Imigcana egosogoso nejikelezileyo
Akufunda ukufaka umbala kwimifanekiso umntwana wakho, zama ukuzoba imigcana egosogoso kulo lonke iphepha. Mabafake umbala kwimingxuma.

Imizobo yezandla neenyawo
Abantwaba bakho mabathi nxu izandla zabo epeyintini baze bazibeke ephepheni. Banokwenza izandla ezahlukeneyo besebenzisa izandla neenyawo ezingafaniyo. Bakukwenza oko banokuthelekisa izandla neenyawo, baxele ezinkulu nezincinane.

Unokufundisa umntwana wakho ukuzoba nantoni na athanda ukuyizoba. Banokuchitha iiyure ezininzi bezoba bakuyeke wenze imisebenzi yakho. Khumbula ukuba Ipeyinti iyangcolisa ngoko kufuneka bafake umbala ngaphandle okanye ugqume yonk'into.

Ukudlala Ngentlama
Njengoko ukupeyinta nokuzoba kunokufundisa abantwana izinto ezininzi, kunokubafundisa nokudlala ngentlama. Xa beqala baya kufunda ubuchule bokulawula izandla zabo. Ngokuhamba kwexesha baya kufunda ukuceba nokwenza izigqibo.

Indlela yokwenza intlama yokudlala

Unokuyithenga intlama yokudlala kanti kulula nokuyenza. Sebenzisa le resipi ingezantsi:
 3 iikomityi zeflawa emhlophe
 1 ikomityi yetyuwa
 1 1/2 yekomityi yamanzi
 1 itispuni yeoli

 into yokufaka umbala ekutyeni intlama yokudlala inokugcina abantwana bexakekile iiyure ezininzi. Xa beqala ukunikwa intlama yokudlala badla ngokonwabela ukuyihlaba nokuyibhuqa-bhuqa bengezi nto ngayo. Bayeke bakwenze oko ixesha elithile ngaphambi kokuba ubacebise izinto amabazenze.

Amacebiso ezinto
Abanokuzenza

Indoda
Qinisekisa ukuba unentlama yokudlala eyaneleyo enemibala eyahlukeneyo ukwenza le ndoda. Umzobo uya kukucebisa ngemibala eyahlukeneyo osenokuyisebenzisa. Umntwana wakho makakuncedise ekwenzeni le ndoda.

Inyathelo loku-1
Yenza intlama ibe yisoseji ende enkulu. Yenza iingalo ezimbini neengqakumba ezincinane zezandla. Yongenza iingqakumba ezimbini zeenyawo.
Inyathelo lesi-2
Yenza ingqakumba yentloko. Yenza ingqakumba encinane yempumlo. Yenza amehlo sebenzisa amaso xa unawo uze uzobe umlomo.

Inyathelo lesi-3
Ukuba uyathanda, unokuyenzela ibhanti indoda oyenze ngentlama. Ukuyigqibezela, sika imicwana yentlama engathi ziinwele uze uzincamathisele entloko.
Ngoku uyenzile indoda yentlama. Xa abantwana bakho bebadala unokuthi mabakhe ibhinqa ngokwabo. Jonga ukuba bayakwazi na ukulenza.
