ABANYE abantu bayawoyika bewanyemba amakhanukan'odwa, bambi bayawanyamezela kodwa bambalwa kakhulu abayiqondayo into ayiyo nabawamkelayo njengabantu abafana nabo.
Uninzi kumz'oNtsundu lukubona kusesona sihelegu ukukhanukana ngesondo kwabantu abafanayo ngesini- inene bakubona kusesona siqalekiso. Bathi akumkhwa wakwaNtu koko linyala elibolekwe eNtshona.
La mabala angaka abekwa kuba uninzi lungenalwazi lubhekele phi ngamakhanukan'odwa. Kungeso sizathu amakhanukan'odwa esiba ngamaxhoba enzondo, phofu ezondwa ngabo bawacingela ukuba azizinto ezithe gqwangu lisondo nezikhathaza abantwana.
Ngokomzekelo uMongameli Mugabe wawanyelisa ngelithi azizinto ezabelana ngesondo ezenza amanyundululu, esaleka wathi, "Amakhanukan'odwa mawayenzele eMerika naseYurophu imikhwa yawo emdaka, waza akaluvumela uqhankqalazo lwamalungelo awo kumsitho weencwadi eZimbabwe".
Kodwa ke nokuba ngumkhwa okanye asimkhwa waseAfrika lo, into ebalulekileyo yeyokuba kutshanje amakhanukan'odwa abhinqileyo nangamadoda aya evela ngokuvela elubala enyanzela abantu abafana nam nawe ukuba bathathe ingqalelo.
Ngathi umgaqo-siseko omtsha welizwe sele uyiqwalasele le nto. Unamagatya ekuthenjwayo ukuba aza kunceda ekwenzeni abantu ukuba babahloniphe abanye bangajongi kuphela into abayiyo.
Ngamakhanukan'odwa umgaqo-siseko omtsha uthi, "Nabani na unelungelo elilinganayo ngaphambi komthetho nakukhuseleko lwawo. Akukho namnye umntu oya kucalucalulwa ngqo okanye ngandlel'ithile ngenxa yobuhlanga, isini, ibala, ubomi abukhethayo ngokwesondo, ukugogeka, ukholo, inkolelo, amasiko okanye ilwimi.
Ngenxa yezizathu ezicacileyo akakho owaziyo ukuba mangaphi na amakhanukan'odwa aNtsundu. Ngokutsho kweGay and Lesbian Organization of the Witwatersrand ( GLOW ) neminye imibutho yawo elizweni jikelele, le mibutho yazi kuphela lawo angamalungu ayo.
Le mibutho ithi oku kungenxa yokuba maninzi amakhanukan'odwa asazifihlileyo. Amakhanukanodwa, ingakumbi aNtsundu, aphila obentshontsho ngenxa yokungamkeleki kwawo eluntwini.

Kodwa nokuba bathini na abeendaba nabanye abantu abonyanya amakhanukan'odwa, mna ngokunokwam akusoloko kunkentez' isondo apha engqondweni yam. Enyanisweni (kwaye ndithanda ukuphinda ndigxininise) umahluko phakathi kwam namanye amabhinqa kukuba akukho nto inditsalayo endodeni ngokwasentliziyweni, emzimbeni, engqondweni, emoyoni nakwisondo.
Ndiyatya, ndilale, ndiy'endle, ndibukele umabonakude, ndizikhuphe, ndiye emathekweni nabahlobo bam njengalo naliphi na ibhinqa eliphangelayo elithi liphumle ngempela- veki . Kodwa andikhe ndibuzwe nakanye kudliwano-ndlebe ukuba ndisichitha njani na isithukuthezi (ngokungathi ngaphandle kwamathandabuzo ndisichitha ngesondo), ukuba ndixelenga phi na ukuba ndiyaxelenga (ngokungathi umntu ofana nam akanakuphangela) ukuba ndithanda kutya kuni na (ngokungathi andityi), ukuba luhlobo luni na lwencwadi endilufundayo nemiboniso bhanyabhanya endiyithandayo (ngokungathi ndikhetha ezamanyundululu esondo).
