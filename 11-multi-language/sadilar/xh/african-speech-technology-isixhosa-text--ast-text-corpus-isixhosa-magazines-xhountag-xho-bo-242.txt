UKUKHULELWA KOLUTSHA NEZiFO EZiSULELWA NGESONDO.



L
 inyuke ngokungathethekiyo inani lolutsha olukhulelwayo kule mihla. Nkqu namantombazana aselula aneminyaka eli-12 ubudala afunyaniswa ekhulelwe.
Naxa kunjalo, kufunyaniswe ukuba uninzi lolutsha lukhulelwa lungafuni. Oko kukhokela ekubeni babe baninzi abantu abaqhomfayo neentsana ezilahlwayo.
Oku kubonisa ukuba uninzi lolutsha lukhulelwa lungakucebanga, zibe yimfuneko ke ngoko iiklinikhi zocwangciso-ntsapho.
iiklinikhi zocwangciso-ntsapho zibaluleke kakhulu ebomini bolutsha. Zenzelwe ukunceda ututsha ukuba luyiqoncie indlela olukhulelwa ngayo, indlela olunokuzikhusela ngayo ekukhuselweni zilufundise ngeendibano zesondo ezikhuselekileyo.
Ukukhulelwa kolutsha kunokuthintelwa ngokusebenzisa izithinteli kumitha ezifana neepilisi, isitofu neekhondom. lzithintelikumitha kufuneka zisetyenziswe ngokwemiyalelo ebhaliweyo kuzo okanye ngokukhokelwa ngumongikazi okanye ugqirha ojongene nazo.
Ulutsha oluninzi luyazi ngeeklinikhi zokucwangcisa kodwa aluzisebenzisi kuba abantu abaninzi besithi iipilisi nesitofu ziwenza umzimba womntu ubukeke utyebile, ungenawo umtsalane. Ukanti kuthiwa ikhondom yona ayimnandanga.
Zonke ezi nkolelo aziyonyani. Zisasazwa ngabantu abangakuthandiyo ukusetyenziswa kwezithinteli-kumitha. lzithinteli-kumitha zinokunceda ekuthinteleni uiutsha oluba neendibano zesondo lungakhulelwa Iungacebanga.
Omnye umba obaluiekileyo nochaphazela ulutsha oluninzi zizifo ezisulelwa ngesonto (STDs). Uninzi lolutsha aluzithatheli ngqalelo ezi zifo luthi, soze ndizifumane mna.
Kodwa inyaniso yeyokuba nabani na unokusulelwa sisifo esisulelwa ngesondo kuba usifumana kwiqabane lakho xa ninendibano yesondo.
Uninzi lwezi zifo lunokunyangwa kodwa zinokuba yingozi xa zinganyangwa.
Kukho izifo ezifana neGcushuwa neAIDS. Umahiuko phakathi kwezi zifo zisuieiwa ngesondo kukuba iGcushuwa iyanyangeka kanti iAids ayinyangeki. Elona xesha iGcushuwa ebulala ngalo kuxa ifikelele kwinqanaba eliphezulu Iokunganyangeki sele isonakalisa iiseli zomzimba.
