Xa usebenza kwiFront Office nguwe ojongene nokwamkela iindwendwe. Ungumntu wokuqala odibana neendwendwe xa zifika kwaye nguwe obonisa indlela eziza kuhlala ngayo ehotele. lnto ekufuneka umilisele ingqondo kuyo kukunika abathengi inkonzo engenachaphaza.

Ehotele usebenza noosomashishini beli nabamanye amazwe ngokunjalo nabakhenkethi. Lo msebenzi usenokuba nzima gqitha ngamathuba athile. Xa ufumana izikhalazo kufuneka uzisombulule ngenyameko. Umthengi uyahlawula ngenkonzo esimnika yona ngoko sifanele simanelise.
lreceptionist yehotele ayigqibeli ngokubhalisa abantu abafuna indawo nokuqwalasela izikhalazo zabantu. Mininzi imisebenzi eyenzayo. Umntakwethu mdala kunam - uneminyana engama-21. Abazali bangqongqo kakhulu ekhaya. Njengoko ndiyintombazana kufuneka ndibuye ekhaya ngentsimbi yesithandathu. Xa umntakwethu ebuye emva kwelo xesha akangxoliswa, kodwa mna ndiyangxoliswa, ndibuzwe ukuba Ndivela phi nokuthi Ubungaboni ukuba kusebusuku ngoku?
Xa sisekhaya nomntakwethu, kufuneka ndicoce ikhaya. Yena usebenza xa ethanda. Ndim ophekayo, kanti ndiyangxoliswa
ngabazali xa ndingalicocanga kakuhle ikhaya. Yena akangxoliswa tu.

lbali likaBuyani
Abazali bam balungile. Ndisabaphulaphula abakutshoyo. Akukho undinyanzelayo kubo ngento. Xa umama okanye utata endixelela into ndiyayenza. Kodwa abazali abawaphathi ngokufanayo amakhwenkwe namantombazana. Amantombazana kufuneka abuye ekhaya ngexesha elithile. Amakhwenkwe ayakwazi ukuphendula abazali babo.







Ibali likaNondumiso
U
 Buyani noNondumiso banamabali angaqhelekanga. Amantombazana aqala emancinane ukukhuthazwa ukuba agcine usapho aziqhelise nokuba ngoomama. Afundiswa ukugcina abantwana nokugcina ikhaya. Akothusi ukubona intombazana eselula ikwazi ukugcina abantwana bakowayo abancinane.

Ngaba kunjalo nasemakhwenkweni? Akudli ngakuba njalo! Amakhwenkwe ayakhuthazwa ukuba aqhube kakuhle ezikolweni yaye akagadwa kakhulu ngabazali. Kwakhona akhululekile ukuhamba nabahlobo bawo yaye afunde izinto ezahlukeneyo ngobomi bawo.

Ihlabathi elicalula ngesini

amadoda kufuneka ayosebenza kanti amabhinqa kufuneka agcine iintsapho.

Kodwa ngabazali kuphela abacinga ngolo hlobo. Nokuba ungaya phi na uva abantu bethetha into enye. Nkqu nabaqeshi baqesha amabhinqa ukuba benze imisebenzi ethile kuphela - bathunge iimpahla, bacoce amakhaya bapheke nokutya. Abanye bade bawahlawule imivuzo ephantsi amabhinqa.
