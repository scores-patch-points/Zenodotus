UKhabonile Zondo wozalwa ngasuku olwandulela
iKrismesi ngo-1961 kwilokishi endulo yosePayneville kufuphi naseSprings eMpuma-Randi. Wakhulela eThokozo upho wayesoloko esiya rhoqo ecaweni - utatomkhulu wakhe wayengumfundisi kwaye lonke usapho lwakowabo lukholiwe - xa engenzinto wayechitha ixesha Iakhe edlala ibhola ekhutywayo.
UZondo waqala ukudlala ibhola ekhatywayo xa wayedlalela iThokoza Benfica kwiDefy League, ekwokuyisecond division yeNPSL engasekhoyo. Wayengumdlali wosemva nomfaki-manqaku, uKhabo wakhawulezo wabangumtsalane kwamanye amaqela kwaye ngo-1981 wathengwa yiThokaza Mighty Greens. Wadlala kuyo iisizini ezine phambi kokuba othengwe liqela lefirst division yeNPSL, iVosloruus Stalions.
Wayeka ukudlala  ibhola ngo-1986 waqalu isuphamakethi nesilarha noyise. Esinye isizathu sokuba akhawuleze ayeke yayingumtshato wakhe noMemme saza esi sibini salizwa ngentombi kwangaloo nyaka.
UZondo ongumakhi oqeqeshiweyo owafunda eEdendale Technikon eMgungundlovu, wakhela usapho isupermarket nepomokazi lomzi onamagumbi ali-15 eMabuya Park olwalusele luneentombi ezintlanu.

Ngokuhamba kwexesha uKhabo uqeqeshe iqela elidala lakwaTransnet Senior lnterprovincial Tournament eBhayi, waza ngo-1995 unyaka emva kokuba eyekile kwiSporting Spudes, wakhethwa njengomntu oza kuchonga obadlali beEastern Transvaal
