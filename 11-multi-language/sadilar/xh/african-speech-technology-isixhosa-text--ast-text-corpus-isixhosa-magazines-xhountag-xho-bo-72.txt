Eminye imibandela eza kufumana ingqalelo kwinkqubo yepolitiki yeli qela yidemokhrasi, inkqubela kwamashishini amancinane namakhulu okuya kwenza kubekho amathuba emisebenzi, ukuqeqeshwa kwabantu kwimisebenzi eyahlukeneyo nokumiswa kwamalungelo abasebenzi apho imibutho yabo iya kuba nenxaxheba enkulu.
Okumangalisayo kukuba ngoku iPAC ivumela nee-settlers (abeLungu namaNdiya) ukuba zibe ngamalungu ayo, kanti okumangalisa nangakumbi kukuba lo mbutho usandul ukunyula umlungukazi ovela kwelinye ilizwe ukuba abe ngumququzeleli wesebe layo eChatsworth kwiphondo laKwazulu-Natal.

kweli engekafiki kweli lizwe.
UBeate waqala ukuba nobudlelane nePAC esengumlungelelanisi kaMasipala waseRotterdam apho umsebenzi wakhe yayikukunikela ngemali kwimisebenzi yempilo, ukufundisa abantu nokufundisa abantu abadala eMzantsi-Afrika.
