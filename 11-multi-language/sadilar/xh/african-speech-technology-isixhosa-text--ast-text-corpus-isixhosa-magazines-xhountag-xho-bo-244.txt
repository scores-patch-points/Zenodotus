IPEPILE INYANGA UMQALA OBUHLUNGU
Xa kwithuba elizayo uhlaselwa ngumqala obuhlungu okanye ukhohlokhohlo, zama olu nyango lungaqhelekanga - ired hot cayenne pepper. Idarnbisa iintlungu zomqala, utsho ujane Giltinan oligosa eliphambili kwezonyango eBastry University Natural Health Clinic eSeattle.
Inegalelo ekupheliseni ichiza elibangela iintlungu
nesencamini yemithambo-luvo. Iyazithomalalisa de
imithambo-luvo ikwazi ukwenza imisebenzi yayo.
Umxube nje wekota yetispuni yepepile, incindi
yesiqingatha selamuni kwanecephe letyuwa uyakwazi
ukunyanga.

UKUSELA IIPILISI
Iipilisi nekhapsuylzi zihla lu1a xa uziginya nekomityi
yamanzi umile - yima malunga nomzuzu omnye

okanye ukuba nesicaphucaphu.

ONKE AM.ANENEKAZI AFUNA LKHALSIYAM
Ukuba ulinenekazi mihla le ufuna ikhalsiyam -mhlawumbi oyifumanayoayanele. Ikhalsiyam ikhusela ukukrunkquka kwamathambo, isifo sokuceketheka kwamathambo nesibangela ukwaphuka kwamathambo namahleza, nokukhokhoba kwamanenekazi amadala.

XA UNEBHABHALAZA

UVITHAMINE ULWA NOKUNGAZALI
Uphando lamvanje ludize ukuba uVitamin E ukhuthaza inkanuko yesondo ukwandisa namathuba akho okukhawula. Phofu ke iVithamin E iyawanceda amadoda kwezesondo ukanti ibangela abasetyhini bazale, ibuyisela ukuvukelwa emadodeni, utsho oyingcali ngevithamini, uGqr Early Mindell, umbhali weVitamin Bible.

I
 -Omo Choir Challenge ikhule ngokukhula ukusukela ngo-1 990 xa yayiqaliswa njengokhuphiswano lweekwayala zezikolo zakwaZulu-Natal, kwaye ngoku ifikelela phantse kwizigidi ezithathu zabantu kanti zingama-900 iikwayala ezikhuphisana kuyo.
OIu khuphiswano ludibanisa iikwayala zeengingqi ezintandathu, ekuyiNorthen Transvaal, ikwazulu-Natal, iEastern Transvaal, iNorth Western Transvaal neMpuma Koloni, kwaye kuyaqala kulo nyaka ukuba kubekho nengingqi yaseFree State/Lesotho.
Nyakenye kwakukhuphisana iikwayala ezingama-6500, laza ibhaso lokuqala lazuzwa yiTsomo High School yaseMpuma Koloni. Kwaqhwatyelwa izandla oko kuba le ngingqi yayiqala ukulungenela olu khuphiswano.
Kulo nyaka sigqibe ekubeni sifake ingingqi yaseFree State/Lesotho kuba abeSuthu bayakuthanda ukucula yaye
siyaqonda ukuba iikwayala zale ngingqi ziza kuzimisela gqitha kolu khuphiswano lweOmo Choir Chatlenge, utshilo uHerb Klein, onguMphathi weMveliso yeOmo.
Kubalulekile nokuba sinike ababhexeshi beekwayala ithuba lokuphucula ulwazi lwabo, ngoko yiloo nto iOmo Choir Challenge inika ababhexeshi beekwayala ezingenele olu khuphiswano ithuba lokuqeqeshwa sekusele iinyanga ezimbini ukuba luqalise, uhambise watsho uMnu. Klein.
Ukuqeqeshwa kwababhexeshi beekwayala kuqale ngojuni kulo nyaka. Kumenywa ababhexeshi beekwayala kusetyenziswa iSebe leMfundo ukuba baye kwiindibano zokubaqeqesha ebezisenziwa kwingingqi nganye. injongo yezi ndibano kukunika ababhexeshi ulwazi olufunekayo emculweni weekwayaia nokubafundisa izinto ezifunekayo kolu khuphiswano.

imithetho nemiqathango yokhuphiswano iyaxoxwa nababhexeshi beekwayala. Ababhexeshi abafundiswe kulo nyaka bathe bafumene ulwazi namava kwezi ndibano.
lzikolo zasezilalini zamabanga aphantsi naphakathi ziyangeniswa kolu khuphiswano baze ababhexeshi beekwayala zazo basebenzise ulwazi Iwabo ukuze unyuke umgangatho wokucula kweekwayala zabo.
