U-Alyce efake icici lempumlo ngaloo minyaka wayeselula.
laseZimbabwe lamqesha ukuba abe ngoyena msasazi uselula wenkqubo zabantwana zikanomathotholo.
Ndakhula ngokukhawuleza kunoontanga bam ngenxa yokuba ndandisamkela umvuzo ndisemncinane. Ekupheleni kwayo nayiphi inyanga ndandiba nemali endilingeneyo epokothweni yam kodwa ndandithanda ukuyonga, ndiyisebenzisa kuphela xa kufanelekile, utsho uAlyce .
Ngo-1987, wayefunda iindaba zelizwe kumabonakude waseZimbabwe. Uthe akuzibona ukuba ufikelele encotsheni emsebenzini wakhe ekhaya, uAlyce othanda ukubona izinto wawela imida yaseMzantsi Afrika ngo-1991 ukuze azosebenzela iRadio 702. Waza ngoJanuwari ka-1992 walibhinqa lokuqala elingumsasazi wenkqubo ethi Good Morning South Africa .
Ibingeyiyo into yokuba ndimnyama kuphela, bendiselula, phantse ndineminyaka engama-23 -kwaye ezo zinto zombini zibangele umdla. Ndiyazi ukuba abantu abaninzi bebefuna ukubona ukuba ndiza kuqhuba njani kodwa ndandizithembile. Kaloku ndandinawo amava.
Emva kwenyanga elapho uAlyce uphinde wenza usapho lwakwaChavunduka lwazingca ngokuba libhinqa lokuqala elintsundu ukufunda iindaba kuTV-1 kwaSABC - noomakhwekhwetha abanjengooJane Hicks noAnand Naidoo .
Ehleli esofeni emzini wakhe oseSandton eJohannesburg efunda inoveli entsha, uAlyce Chavunduka uyavuma ukuba ukufunda yeyona ndlela achitha ngayo ixesha xa ephuma emsebenzini.
Ndiyawuthanda umzi wam, ndingumntu olithandayo ikhaya. Andisayi kwimiboniso bhanyabhanya! Ngoku ndihlala ekhaya ndizithengele amakhasethi evidiyo xa ndithanda.
Uthi nangona bekungafani nangoku ubeqala ukubona uMzantsi Afrika.
Ngoko bendithanda indawo ezidlala umculo wasekhaya. Isizathu soko ubundikhumbuza eHarare xa nomsakwethu sasineqela elibizwa ngokuba yiDevotion .
Sasijikeleza edolophini siculela iimvumi ezithile ezinye zazo ziimvumi ezaziwayo emculweni. Bendiqhele ukunxiba icici lempumlo! Ngoko zimbalwa izinto endingazenzanga.
Abantu balindele bakubone ufana nomntu abambona kumabonakude emakhayeni abo. Xa singekho kumabonakude uninzi lwethu lubukeka lwahlukile! Kaloku oyena mntu ungaphantsi kwemake-up .
Bandenza imake-up kumabonakude kangangokuba xa ndifika ekhaya ebusuku kufuneka ndihlambe kwakhona. Ke ngoko andithandi ukuthambisa imake-up xa ndingekho semsebenzini, ngaphandle kokuba ndiza kudibana nomntu obalulekileyo.
Nangona uAlyce enomdla ngokwaneleyo ukumodela iimpahla uyakwazi nokugcina iimpahla zakhe azinxiba kwiTV .
Ezinye ndizinxiba kaninzi ngenxa yokuba ndisonqena ukuya kuthenga izinto ezintsha kwaye andingomntu uthanda ukuhomba. Xa ndithenga amaxesha amaninzi ndithenga izinto zasendlwini andithengi iimpahla zokunxiba - nangona ndithanda ukukhe ndizame iinwele ezifakelweyo ezahlukileyo!
