Kwakukho inkosi esisityebi egama linguThabo eyayineentombi ezintathu, uDudu, uBongi noThembi. Yayiyindoda exakeke kakhulu kwaye emva kokuswelekelwa yinkosikazi wawusenziwa ziintombi zayo umsebenzl wekhaya xa iphume ngomsebenzi.
Umafungwashe wayo, uDudu, wayehoye oodadewabo ababini abancinci kwaye inkosi yayingazi ukuba ngoyena ulivila.
Nangona kunjalo yayisoloko imncoma, impha izipho kwaye imenzela izinto ngaphezu kwabanye kuba yayikholelwa ekubenl ngoyena wenza okusemandleni akhe ukuyanelisa.
Ngubani owayenokuyithela nqa? Kaloku xa ibuya ekhaya yayisoloko ifika inguDudu oyilindileyo, eqhayisa nangendlela asebenze nzima ngayo ngethuba oodadewabo bebelibele kukudlala bengenzi nto.
Nanjengoko uDudu wayengomdala -elulame okwegusha - uyise wayekholelwa kuye kwaye babesithi uBongi noThembi xa bemphikisa abagxolise kanobom uyise.
Ningathini ukuthi lixoki udadewenu? Benimele nibulele ukuba nodade onjengoDudu onikhathalele kangaka. Nganiyintoni ukuba ebengenabubele nabuntu, kwaye engenzi konke okusemandleni akhe ukunikhathalela? watsho ebangxolisa.
Ngenye imini inkosi yathi kuDudu:
Kusebusika ngoku kwaye kufuneka kuvunwe umbona. Nceda uqinisekise ukuba kuyenzeka oko kuba mna ndisahamba.
Ndiza kwenza njalo tata. Uyazi ukuba ungakholosa ngam, watsho uDudu
ebonakalisa ukuthembeka ebusweni.. Uthe akuhamba uyise uDudu wayalela
oodadewabo ukuba bawuvune ngokwabo umbona. Xa ningakhawulezisi andizokunipha ukutya nobabini ngokuhlwa nje! watsho ebagrogrisa.
Ngethuba evuna amantombazana uDudu wayengqengqe emthunzini phantsi komthi, ecinga ngezipho aza kuziphathelwa nguyise akubuya. Ngelo xesha igqwirha lombona -elaliyindoda ebhityileyo enentshebe ende, iindlebe ezinkulu nekepsi emthubi etsolo -lalibabukele.
Lawasizela la mantombazana mabini mancinci labona ukuba malimfundise isifundo uDudu.
Emva kweentsuku ezimbalwa ukubuya kwenkosi, uDudu wayisa eshedini nalapho umbona wawugcinwe khona. Konke oku ndikwenze ndedwa tata. Ndiyathemba ukuba wanelisekile, waphosisa uDudu.
Ndanelisekile ntombam. Ndiyabona ukuba usebenze ngamandla. Yiza, uzokubona isipho endikuphathele sona, yatsho inkosi.
Kodwa bathe ngaphambi kokuba baphume kwakho nto isebezayo emboneni:
Ndiyakuvela Dudu, Ndiyakuvela Uyivile loo nto tata? wabuza uDudu
livakala ilizwi lakhe ukuba uyoyika. Hayi ntombam, andivanga nto.
Mhlawumbi zindlebe zakho. Yiza siye endlwini.
Kodwa wamlandela ndawo yonke uDudu
usebe-sebe kwaye wayengaviwa mntu ngaphandle kwakhe: Ndiyakuvela Dudu, Ndiyakuvela!
Akazange abe nako ukuwunyamezela uDudu waza wagqiba kwelokuba amxelele inyani utata wakhe. lndlela acaphuka ngayo uyise ayithetheki kwaye ukusukela loo mini wayeka ukuba yeyona ntandane kayise.
UDudu wafunda ukuba akulunganga ukuxoka ingakumbi ukuqhayisa ngomsebenzl angawenzanga ngokwakhe.
Ewe, usebe-sebe wasemboneni wamluleka uDudu..

UTommy wayethandana nentombi eyayisebenza eHill . Yayintle yaye ingumntu wabantu. Wayethanda ukuthetha izinto ezilula nayo, wayengazithethi nayo izinto ezibalulekileyo kuthi sobathathu. Yayingakuthandi ukufunda iincwadi, kanti UTommy wayekuthanda ukufunda iincwadi nokuphulaphuta umculo. Le ntombi yazama nje yona ukuba ifane noTommy .

Le ntombazana yayityhilekile yaye sasiyithanda sinoJohnny njengodade. Oko kusenokuvakala kumangalisa, kanti akunjalo. Nkqu noTommy wayengathethi ngokuthandana okunenjongo. Wayesithi kunzima ukufumana intombi emfaneleyo kweli lizwe ngenxa yemeko zelizwe. Ngoko wayonwabile nesithandwa sakhe.

Nathi sazama ukwenjenjalo sinoJohnny , kodwa amalinge ethu awa phantsi. uJohnny wayengafani tu noTommy . Sasingabantu abangafaniyo kangangokuba andiyazi into eyayisenza sivane.

UJonny wayekuthanda ukuncokola yaye engumntu onengxolo. Wayesoloko esenza izinto ezingalunganga. Wayesoloko ezifaka ezingxakini kuze kufuneke simncede sinoTommy . Ebantwini abangamaziyo wayengumntu ongachanekiyo ukuba unjani nongakhathaliyo.

Umama wakhe wayebulewe yindlala. Wayesesikolweni yena ngelo xesha, ngoko wayiva emva kwexesha elide loo nto. Wayezibulala ngendlala kuba efuna ukufundisa unyana wakhe yaye yena wayengayazi loo nto. Emva wachitha ixesha elininzi eyedwa yaye ethe cwaka. Nkqu nathi sinoTommy sasingafikeleli kuye. Wayengangeneki tu. Kuthi WayesenguJohnny esimaziyo qha engonwabanga. Wayetshintshile kumntu wonke. Kodwa akazange ayeke ukuphoxa abantu uJohnny , yaye wayenobuchule bokususa uchuku. Babembalwa abantu ababemqonda, uninzi lumgxeka.

Nobam ubomi babufana nobabo. Ndandilihlwempu, ndifuna intombi eza kuba liqabane lam nezinye izinto
onokuzifumana emfundweni xa ungenguye uMlungu eMzantsi-Afrika.
