Nali ithuba LAKHO lokuza kwiRAMA/BONA WOMENS
CLUB!


UMHLA: ngomGqibelo, 13 September, 1997
INDAWO: Simpa Mbabane HaII
IXESHA: Ngeye-1:30 emva kwemini
ISITHETHI SEMBEKO: Nkszn Moroesi N. Khoza
UMXHOLO: Igunya Labasetyhini


Manenekazi ahlala kufutshane naseMbabane, eSwazini nali ithuba lenu lokudibana nothile oziqonda kakuhle ingxaki abajamelana nazo abasetyhini ukuze bazixhase bona ngokwabo kwezoqoqosho. Le ndibano yeRAMA/BONA ithembisa ngokunik umdla nokufundisayo. isithethi sembeko uMoroesi Khoza ukufanelekele ukuthetha ngomxholo wegunya labasetyhini. Fudula engumLungelelanisi weSizwe kwiUnited Nations lnternational Trade Centre Project on Export Market and Product
Development for Women Entrepreneurs and  Operatives. Yena buqu linenekazi elinegunya elingenakugungqiswa-mntu, ukhe wayipublisher kwanomhleli weemagazini ezimbini, usebenze kanobom njengetitshala esekondari nakwiitheknikon, sithetha nje uyiNqununu yeSwaziland lnstitute of Management and Public Administration (SIMPA). Oyingcali kwezoqoqosho ekhaya uVeronica Mkafi wakwaRAMA uza kube ekho ebonisa ngokupheka kwakhe okuluncuthu. Manenekazi ninokulindela ukutsalwa kwamatikiti awelwa ngumq esandleni kwanamabhaso eemveliso zakwaRAMA. Kuza kube kuphungwa neti.
Ningaphoswa yile ndibano enik umdla, ukuze nifumane indawo yokuhlala ncedani nifike kwangethuba, manenekazi.

UPhomolo Modise 

BEKONWATYIWE kufundwa. Omele iRama uVeronica Mkari ubabonise iiresipi ezimnandi ezinokuphekwa ekhaya ukanti isithethi sembeko uPhomolo Modise ubachazele ngenkcazelo yoqoqosho lwasekhaya nendima abanokuyidlala oomama emizini yabo.
Amanenekazi afunde lukhulu. Uphomolo Modise ucacise ukuba umama ungumntu
obalulekileyo ekhaya nentsika yosapho.
Kuqala kufuneka uzazi. Qonda ibhinqa. Liyinkosikazi, umama, umsakwabo, umhlobo notitshala. Lingoyena mntu ubalulekileyo okufutshane nawe.
Abasetyhini badlala indima ebalulekileyo elusatsheni nakuluntu, kufuneka bazi yonke into malunga noqoqosho lwasekhaya noqoqosho ngokubanzi.
Kufuneka bazi ngeemfuno neendlela zokuxhasa, amaxabiso nokwenza ukhetho
abangenakuzisola ngalo
