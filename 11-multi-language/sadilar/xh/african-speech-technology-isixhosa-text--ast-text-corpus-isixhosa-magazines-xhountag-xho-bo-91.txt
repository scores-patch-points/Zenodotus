SITHI MAZ'ENETHOLE
KWABAKWALANGA
Abafundi bethu KwaLanga basamkele ngezandla ezingenamkhinkqi
kwindibano yamvanje yeRama/BONA
Kubalisa uPRIMPOSE WILLIAMS, kufote uDON BAIN




 NAKUBA indibano yethu KwaLanga iqalwe nguMfundisi uStefane ngokuvula ngomthandazo obukeke ngathi ungummangaliso kuba abantu baye bephokoka xa ebethandaza.
Kuthe emva kokuba uSista Mfenyana amkele iindwendwe uPrimrose Williams obemele iBona, wabulela bonke abaye bawenza umzamo wokuza.
Emva koko uNkszn Khasana wazise isithethi sethu sembeko uNomawethu Mnweba, esichazela ngembali yakhe emfutshane. UNomawethu ngunontlalontle owaziwayo, uchazwe njengomntu oziphulaphula ngenyameko iimfuno zabanye. UNomawethu unikele intetho echukumise wonk'umntu. Naye ngenkqu ungumzali ongatshatanga, uthethe ngeenzima abajamelana nazo abazali abangatshatanga kwanabantwana babo, ingakumbi abantwana abazalwe ngaphandle kweqhina lomtshato.
Uthe: Xa ungumzali ongatshatanga kunzima kwaye uziva ulilolo kufuneka nantoni na uyenze kungekho mntu ukukhuthazayo, okuxhasayo nokunik' ifuthe, futhe elo lidla ngokubakho xa le mbopheleleko yokuba ngumzali usabelana nayo neqabane elinothando.
Nakuba bekho abazali abangatshatanga ngenxa yokufa noqhawulo-mtshato, oomama abangafshatanga nabanfwana babo bajongelwa phantsi





bexhatshazwa luluntu. Ngelishwa asinakuyishicilela yonke intetho yakhe, kodwa kuthe phambi kokuba ehle eqongeni, ephelekwa yintlokoma yokuqhwatywa kwezandla, wanikela la macebiso alandelayo kubazali abangatshatanga.

Okwenzeka ngaphakathi endlwini yakho, enoba ayibukeki intle, kokona kuya kuba nempembelelo -mhlawumbi EYONA mpembelelo -kukugqiba uhlobo lomntu omfuna abe lulo umntwana wakho.

Vuma ukuthi unolona celo-mngeni lukhulu ebomini bakho, zama ukuba ube ngumzali ophumelelayo. Ukuze ulungiselele imeko-bume yempumelelo kubantwana bakho, kufanele wazi ukuthi unako ukuyenza. Ukuphumelela yenye yezinto ekubangela uzithembe.
 Usapho lomzali ongatshatanga lunokuphumelela ukuba onke amalungu alo ayabambisana. Bancede abantwana bazi ukuthi banembopheleleko enkulu.
 Ukho umthombo kumzali ongatshatanga. Ikwcingulo mthombo ozisa impumelelo kulo naluphi na usapho - uThixo.
Menze abe liqabane elikhethekileyo njengomzali ongatshatanga.
Imvakwemini ibingenakuphelela ukuba uVeronica Mkari akakhange abe nokwenza umboniso. Kuba ixesha belixabisekile, ubonise indlela yokwenza iBanana Loaf elula neluncuthu.
Kufike ixesha lokutsalwa kwamatikiti awelwe ngumq'esandleni, abanaloo matikiti bazuze iintlobo ngeentlobo zamabhaso. Kuthe emva kokuvala ngomthandazo kukaNkszn Jutu kwaphungwa iti nemiqhathane.
Inxalenye yesihlwele izonwabisa ngokucula nokuqhwaba izandla kwindibano yeRama/BONA.
