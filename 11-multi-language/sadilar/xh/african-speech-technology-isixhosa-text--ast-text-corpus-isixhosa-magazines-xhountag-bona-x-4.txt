ABAFUNDI ABATSHA 
The concertina .
Khathazile follows her heart
KuTyhila Ufunde wenyanga ephelileyo, sifunde ngendlela uKhathazile Madlala awathandana ngayo noGasa. Ibali laphela xa uyise ezomlanda kuba uGasa
engayihlawuli ilobola. Ngoku funda ukuba kwaqhubeka ntoni eluthandweni labo



Ndayiva iConcertina
Ndandingonwabanga tu ekhaya. Ndandikhumbula ngendlela engathethekieyo uGasa.

Kwathi ngobo busuku ndisalungiselela ukulala, ndeva ukukhala kweconcertina phandle. Ndaqonda ukuba nguGasa lowo. Wathi uza kugoduka.

Ndandisazi ukuba azikho iinkomo ebuhlanti bakuloGasa. Ubuhlanti bakhiwe ngamatye amakhulu yaye bunokuvalela umhlambi weenkomo zomlimi omhlophe. Ndathi kuba ndimthanda, ndandingakhathali nokuba abunankomo.

Ngentsasa elandelayo wafika kwakhona utata. Ngorhatya wafika uGasa neconcertina yakhe.
Ngoko ndanditandwa ekhaya ndiphinde ndiqhweshe ndiye kuloGasa.

Ndandimthanda uGasa, ngoko ndathi ndakubona utata esihla entabeni ezondilanda, ndabaleka ndayozimela emlanjeni.

Ndazala umntwana oyintombazana
Laqhubeka ixesha ngale ndlela de ndakhulelwa. Kwanyanzeleka ukuba abantu bakuloGasa bayoxela umonakalo kutata. Kwakufuneka bahlawule inkomo ngomonakalo owenziwe ngunyana wabo. Andazi ukuba bayifumana phi loo nkomo, kodwa bayihlawula bona kutata.
Isiko lithi lihlazo ukuzala kwentombazana engatshatanga phambi koninazala ngoko
ndagoduka ndayobelekela ekhaya. Ndahlala ekhaya ndisela isihlambezo. Elo yeza laliza kundinceda xa ndizala.
UGasa waya kusebenza eRhawutini kwimigodi yegolide . Wayefuna ukufumana imali yokundilobola.
Ndandingenaye umama, ngoko kwathi kwakufika imini yokubeleka kwam, umakazi wabiza amanye
