N
 GO-1994 ikumkani yekwaito ingene kwiqonga lomculo inenjongo enye: ukuba yeyona mvumi ibalaseleyo kweli enohlobo lwayo olulodwa lomculo.
Loo mnqweno uphantse waphelel emoyeni xa izoyikiso zokumbulala zifikelela kwinqanaba elinganyamezelekiyo emva kokuba kuhlaselwe usapho lwakowabo, hlaselo olo wenzakala kulo umninawa wakhe, uMakhendlas.
Nakuba kukho impikiswano ngendlela acula ngayo, kunzima ukufumana ukuba uArthur Mafokate, obefudula enguMr Soweto nongumdanisi ohlawulwayo angabuphemba njani ubundlobongela ngomculo wakhe.
U-Arthur wacel umngeni kumculo wepop owawukhawuleza uphelelwe kwiminyaka yoo-90, ngokuthi gqi nohlobo lomculo ekuthiwa yi-kwaito kule mihla - umculo womjuxuzo othandwayo ezilokishini noyinxalenye yobomi obuphilwa kule mihla.
Inkoliso yamazwi eengoma zekwaito ayaphindwa-phinclwa kodwa anamazwi anomtsalane
ngamaxesha amnandi, kanti akwanemixholo enento yokwenza ngezentlalo neepolitiki. lkwaito kuthiwa iyelele kwihip-hop yaseMerika.
U-Arthur uthi: lkwaito yeyokonwabisa uluntu. lnika abantu isingqisho saselokishini abasithandayo.

entle, uhlawulwa i-R15 000 eza kucula. Sethengise ngaphezu kwe-150 000 ngecwecwe elithi Kaffir, ntengiso leyo ilingana neyecwecwe leBoom Shaka elithiywe kwangayo, icwecwe lakhe elitsha elithi Die Poppe SaI Dans nalo lithengwa okwamagwinya ashushu.

Q
 HO xa uJerry Sikhosana efaka inqaku xa iOrlando Pirates idlala neChiefs, uyamphoxa unina.
UNksk. Sikhosana akazi ukuba kungani umntwana wakhe wesithathu eqhubeka nokuphelisa imincili aba nayo xa ebona iqela alithandayo iChiefs itywatyusha iOrlando Pirates. Ngenye imini wade wancokola ngokuthi uza kuyeka ukuphekela umntu ododobalisa iqela alithandayo.
Ngenene ke uSikhosana uyayingcwaba iChiefs! Wayiqala kumdlalo wesemi-fayinali yeBobsave Cup apho wafaka amanqaku amathathu iChiefs ibhulelw amasaka ngo-4-1 yipirates. Uphinde eso ngo-Januwari ngokufaka inqaku ephumelele ngalo ipirates nelophule irekhodi entle yechiefs yokungabethwa emidlalweni yePremier Soccer League.
Kodwa ngoku ihlebo lidulile USikhosana unqwenela ukudlalela lona eli qela asoloko elingcwaba.
Ndidlalela ipirates kuba indiqeshile. ngoko kufuneka ndiwenze kakuhle umsebenzi wam. Ndikhe ndifake amanqaku abalulekileyo xa ithuba ndilifumana. Ndifaka amanqaku nakwiqela endilithandayo kuba ndifuna ukuzibonakalisa ebantwini, kuquka noClive Barker. Ndifuna ukubonisa
iKaizer Chiefs ukuba ndingumdlali oyingozi. Ngaloo ndlela ndithi kuyo xa ifuna umdlali waphambili mayicinge ngoJerry. Kudala ndandikunqwenela ukunxiba ijezi yayo egolide namnyama.
Kodwa eli qela alingxamelanga kumthenga 10 mdlali, kwaye yena usathi, Ungaze ubheje ngemali yakho ukuba iza kuphumelela iChiefs xa sidlala nayo.
UJerry Sikhosana waqala ukudlala


Xa ndandiqala ukudlalela ipirates abalandeli bayo babengandithandi. Babesithi andibaniki abanye abadlali ibhola. Phofu ke ingxaki yabo kukuba babengayazi indlela endidlala ngayo. Ngoku bayazi ukuba ndidlala kanjani.
Ndiyavuma ukuba ndinomona wokufaka amanqaku, kodwa ke oko kuyandinceda kanti nabo kuyabanceda. Umqeqeshi wePirates wayeyiqonda indlela endidlala ngayo. Yiloo nto eyayimenza andifake emdlalweni nokuba abalandeli abandithandi. Eyona nto wayeyifuna ngamanqaku, yaye ewafumana.
Umqeqeshi weqela lelizwe, uClive Barker, naye uyamqonda uSikhosana ukuba ungumdlali onjani, yaye umnike ithuba lokubonakalisa isiphiwo sakhe kwiqeta lelizwe. USikhosana wathandwa ngokukhawuleza ngabalancieli kuba nguye owadribula abadlali basemva beZaire waza wathumela ibhola kuMasinga owafaka inqaku. IBafana Bafana yabetha iZaire ngo-1-O kuloo mdlalo weWorld Cup.
Nangona engumdlali obonakala enengcwangu ebaleni, uJerry uthetha ngokupholileyo yaye ungumntu okuthandayo ukuncokola. Ndiyathanda ukuhlala nabahlobo sincokole, utshilo ngobomi bakhe nabahlobo.
Uya kunqandwa ziinkwenkwezi to mdlali bambiza ngokuba ngu-Legs of Thunder. lminqweno yakhe yengomso kukudlalela elinye lamaqela aphambili aseYurophu iminyaka emibini okanye emithathu.
