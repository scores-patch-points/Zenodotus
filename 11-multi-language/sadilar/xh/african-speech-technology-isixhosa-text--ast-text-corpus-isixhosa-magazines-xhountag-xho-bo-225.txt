Mgcine umntwana
Uninzi lukhetha ukubagcina abantwana. Kufuneka uqiniseke ukuba uza kuba nayo imali nenkxaso kusapho lwakowenu, kubahlobo okanye kumfana oncuma naye. Thetha nabo ngalo mbandela-unokumangaliswa yindlela abakunceda ngayo. Xa umfana oncuma naye ephika ukuba unguyise womntwana, unokuvavanywa ukuze kufumaneke inyaniso. Oko kudla ngokwenziwa emva kweenyanga ezintandathu umntwana ezelwe. Buza iklinikhi okanye ugqirha ukuba lunokwenziwa phi uvavanyo. Ukulwa nokufumana imali yesondlo kunzima kunokuba kubonakala. Xa ufuna ukuxhaswa ngemali ufanele uyifune imali yesondlo kuyise womntwana. Kufuneka ummangalele enkundleni xa engafuni ukuyihlawula imali yesondlo. Cela abazali bakho okanye ititshala ikuncede ekwenzeni oko.

Ukukhulisa umntwana ongengowakho
Ambalwa amabhinqa acinga ngokuthatha abantwana babanye abantu abakhulise. Kodwa xa unomntwana ube uqonda ukuba wena, usapho lwakowenu nomfana oncuma naye akanako ukukhathalela umntwana, zimbalwa izinto onokukhetha kuzo. Ukukhuliswa komntwana lolunye usapho kuthetha ukuba umnika usapho olufuna umntwana kodwa lungabafumani. Ukunika umntwana wakho akhuliswe lolunye usapho akuthethi kuthi awumfuni umntwana.
Ukuba umkhathalele umntwana wakho yaye uyaqonda ukuba akunako ukumkhulisa, kungani ungathethi nomongikazi waseklinikhi ngokuqhomfa.

Ukuqhomfa
Uyakhathazeka xa ukhulelwe ungathandi ukukhulelwa. Abanye bade bazame ukuziqhomfa ngokwabo izisu abazikhulelweyo. Bahlambe ngamanzi ashushu, basele izinto zokucoca endlwini, okanye basebenzise iinaliti zokumitha kodwa bangakwazi ukuqhomfa izisu yaye oko kuyingozi.
Amabhinqa amaninzi azama ukuziqhomfela ekhaya izisu. Ukuqhomfa isisu ekhaya kuyingozi gqitha kuba okunye kwenziwa kwiindawo ezingacocekanga.

Wonk'umntu makafunde




ukangaphi usiva ngombhodano okwimfundo nokuba kukho iititshala ezininzi gqitha eMzantsi-Afrika? Kukhangeleka ngathi oko
akunityhafisanga ekunqweneleni ukuba ziitisthala . Kule mihla luninzi ulutsha oluya kwiiyunivesithi neekholeji luyofundela ukuba ziitisthala .

Kule nyanga uTyhila Ufunde uqwalasela imisebenzi eyahlukeneyo oyenzayo xa uyititshala  ukususela ekufundiseni abantwana abaselula kakhulu kuse kwasebekhulile. Siqwalasela nesizathu sokuba abantu banqwenele ukuba ziitisthala nokuthi bathanda ntoni kulo msebenzi.

Ukufundisa abantwana abaselula kakhulu
Bekukade kuyinto enganikwanga ngqalelo ukufundiswa kwabantwana abaselula kakhulu. Kodwa oko kutshintshile kule minyaka. Bayanda
abantu abakuqondayo ukubaluleka ngokufundiswa kwabantwana beselula.

ititshala yabantwana abaselula ibanceda ukubanika ulwazi oluya kubanceda xa sebengena kwisikolo sabantwana abadala. Ukufundisa abantwana abaselula kumnandi kodwa kunzima yaye kuyadinisa.

Abantwana bahlala iiyure ezimbalwa esikolweni badla ngokufunda iiyure ezine. Phofu ke kufuneka kufuneka ukhumbule ukuba abantwana bangumsebenzi. Kufuneka uzinikele emsebenzini wakho. Xa ubonakalisa ukudinwa okanye ungonwabanga baqalisa ukungavani nawe.

Ukufundiswa kwabantwana abaselula akupheleli esikolweni kuphela. Kufuneka ititshala yabo izilungiselele imini elandelayo yakufika ekhaya oko kuquka ukucinga izinto ezintsha oza kubafundisa zona eziya kubonwabisa abantwana.
