IPURE PERFORMANCE
inonophela naluphi na uhlobo olusu ikwanezithako ezizizo zobunzulu-lwazi nezendalo ezikhanyisa zigudise ulusu lakho. Zonke iimveliso ngaphandle kwesicoci zinesithako esitsha nesithandwayo senzululwazi: I-Alpha Hydroxy Acid . IPURE PERFORMANCE ineeasidi zesiqhamo ezikhanywe kwilamuni necucumber , ziluncedo ekwenzeni ulusu lube lutsha ngathi lolosana, lukhanye, ludiz'ukuba sempilweni ngokuzishenxisa msinya iiseli ezonakeleyo ngaphezulu kolusu ukuze kuvele iiseli ezinik' impilo nezintsha. Umphumo: kukukhanya kolusu okugudileyo okuzifunxa lula iimveliso zokunonophela ulusu.

Udidi olulungele wena: i-Oily Skin Range ithintela ulusu olukhanyayo nolunyinyithekayo ngezi ndlela zimbini: inokukhanywe kwigrapefruit nokulungisa iintunja ezivuleke kakhulu, zize izithako zobunzulu-lwazi zilawule isixa seoyile ekhutshwa lulusu. Inkqubo enamanyathelo amathathu iquka iPurifying Face Wash iSoothing Skin Astringent neLight Day Protection eneSun Protection Factor ( SPF ) of 8.

ipure performance ikwaneNormal / Combination Skin Range , eyilelwe abasetyhini abakhathazwa kukunyinyitheka kwempumlo, izidlele nebunzi nabanobuso obuthanda ukoma. Kwabasetyhini abavakalelwa kukuba ulusu lwabo lomile, luqinile lungakhululekanga, kukho iDry Skin Range . Kukho neemveliso ezifanela zonke iintlobo zolusu: iActive Cleansing Mask neGentle Face . Nengoncedo olwengeziweyo, iimveliso ezifumisayo zinezicoci ezibalulekileyo ezinjengoVithamin E onceda ekukhuseleni ulusu kwizinto ezibangela amanya njengokucinezeleka, ukutshaya, imitha egqatsileyo yelanga nongcoliso. Ukuze ube nako konke, kufumane konke

Kubonakala ngathi mihla le kuveliswa intlama yamazinyo. Nganye ibhetele kunelandelayo, ungayikhetha njani? Kulula. Khangela intlama yamazinyo ekunika konke okufunayo.


NAKU OKUFUNAYO.
* Okulwa nePlague . iPlague lukhokho lwentsholongwane eyileka emazinyweni akho mihla le. Kukho isixa sentsholongwane yendalo emilonyeni yethu sandiswa kukutya esikutyayo. (Kungenxa yoko le nto oogqirha besithi masitye ukutya okunempilo kuphela.) iPlague iqokelelana phakathi kwamazinyo neentsini, ibangela isifo seentsini.
Xa ungawabrashi amazinyo akho uya kuphawula indlela ezishwabana ngayo iintsini ethubeni namazinyo akho ayagungquma, ekugqibeleni aphume. Okufunayo yintlama yamazinyo elwa nePlague nekwazi ukuyithintela ingaphindi iyileke.
* Okukunika Umoya Onuka Kamnandi. Xa iintsholongwane zisetyisa ukutya okusemlonyeni zikubangela ube nevumba elinuka kakubi. Eli vumba linokupheliswa ngokubrasha ngentlama yamazinyo enesithako esikubangela ube nomoya onuka kamnandi.
* Okulwa Nemingxuma Emazinyweni. Amazinyo akho agqunywe ngenamel ewakhusela kwiintsholongwane eziwabangela abole. Ukuba intsholongwane iwakrukrutha de igqobhose le namel kofuneka uye kugqirha wamazinyo ukuze ivalwe. Ukuba akukhawulezi wenjenjalo lonke izinyo liya kutshintsha umbala ngenxa yokubola. Intlama yamazinyo kufuneka ibe neflouride ukuze yomeleze le namel , ikuthintele kwakuqala ukuvela kwemingxuma.
*Isicoci. Ukuba unokucinga ngezinto esizifaka emilonyeni yethu unokuqonda ukuba kulula kangakanani ukuba amazinyo ethu aphelelwe bubumhlophe bawo obunhle. Ukuze siwagcime emhlope, emahle kufuneka sisebenzise intlama yamazinyo enceda ekuwagcineni amazinyo ecocekile, emhlophe.


NANTONI NA UYAYIFUMANA.

INTLAMA YAMAZINYO IAQUAFRESH IKWENZA KONKE.

Ubusazi nje ukuthi intlama yamazinyo iaquafresh ikuphela kwenemigca? Kungenxa yokuba le ntlama yamazinyo yakwaAquafresh inoko okufuna kwiintlama zamazinyo. Umgca obomvu ngumlwi onamandla weplague , umgca oluhlaza ukwenza ube nevumba elinuka kamnandi, omhlophe ngumlwi wemingxuma nomcocisisi.

KHUMBULA...

kusasa nangokuhlwa ukuze uwugcine umlomo wakho ucocekile unomoya onuka kamnandi.

WAZALELWA kumaNtla weLondon (kodwa akazelelwanga eMpuma Yurophu ), kumzi ekwakusoloko kuvakala izandi zomculo owawuthandwa ngabazali bakhe kanti emzini kaninakhulu wayesiva umculo wereggae qho ngeempelaveki -inde gqitha imvelaphi kaMichelle emculweni.
Umculo kaGladys Knight , oweThe Ohio Players noweAl Green uxutywe nokaBob Marley , uDennis Brown noSugar Minot - kuze kuxutywe nezithethe ezahlukeneyo zabantu baseLondon zenze umculo oyivulele amathuba ebomini le mvumi iselula. uMichelle uthi esemncinane wayechitha imini ngokuhlala emethini ephulaphule umculo.
UGeneral Levy wayehlala ngaphesheya kwesitrato kanti kwakufutshane kwakuhlala amalungu eMC Juice , umama wayephangela kwinkampani eshicilela amacwecwe kaOmar .
Umculo yinto eyayinditsholozela ndisakhula. Ndandiphulaphula iNucelus , iMtume, IPlanet Rock , The Treacherous Three nePublic Enemy neElectic Ballroom eCamden .
Lolu hlobo lomculo wehip-hop nowesoul awuchola kwizitrato ngezitrato uMichelle awawusebenzisa kwiqela lakhe lentambula lokuqala, iRomor Phus .
Eli qela lalinomculo okhawulezayo kodwa wawumfanele uMichelle kuba wayexakekile efunda kwisikolo sokudlala imidlalo yeqonga nokucula emini.
Iminyaka emihlanu kamva sabonwa isiphiwo sakhe somculo yinkampani eshicilela amacwecwe kaS.W.V. akaOmar nakaJade , waza wavelisa uncuthu lomculo okwicwecwe lakhe elitsha elithi Walk With Pride ngo-1994. Eli cwecwe lakhe lokuqala lineengoma ezininzi ezimnandi eziquka nekwicwecwe lakhe lokuqala elinengoma enye elithi Looking Up .
Eli cwecwe lanyuka kumanqwanqwa omculo de lakwindawo ye-11.
Ukuze ashicilele icwece lakhe lesibini elithi Sweetness , uMichelle wantingela eLos Angeles ukuze adibane nombhali wengoma uNarad Michael Walden (Obhale iingoma zikaWhitney Houston noAretha Franklin ). Umphumo woko kudibana luncuthu lomculo.
Iingoma ezinjengethi Girlfriend , enesingqisho sebhesi nerap, nengoma eyintloko yeli cwecwe ethi Walk With Pride , zibonisa ukuba uMichelle ligqiyazana elikhutheleyo nelizimiseleyo.
Ukususela kwingoma yeSoul ethi Rise Up , nengoma ethi Personality kuse kwethi Freedom nengumculo wokholo eli cwecwe liyayichukumisa intliziyo. Uya kude udinwe kukuzijuxuzela iingoma zibe zisemnandi.
Nakuba uNevada Walden , uDavid James , uSteve Jervier noSimon Climie bequkiwe kule albham yakhe, uMichelle ukungqinile ukuba akalawulwa ngomnye umntu emculweni wakhe kuba ubhale okanye wancediswa ekubhaleni iingoma ezingama-40 ekhulwini kwicwecwe lakhe.
