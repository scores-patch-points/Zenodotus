Iinjongo zeJOKO kukulondoloza nokuphucula ukubaliswa
kweentsomi zeqela izanendaba ababalisi bamabali. Ngokwenjenje liqalisile iphulo lokubalisela abantu amabali. Sekuyiminyaka emithathu ngoku, abakwaJoko bexhasa imibhiyozo yokubaliswa kwamabali, kwanakwiinkqubo koonomathotholo kuquka iRakgolo Mangokoane kwisikhululo sosasazo lwesiPedi. IAfrika inenkcazelo entle ngamabali amandulo, de kube mvanje ibifudula ixhomekeke ekudluliseleni ulwazi nezithethe zayo ngomlomo. Ukutyhubela iminyaka isithethe sokubalisa iintsomi nezaci siye sakokosa ubuntu ezintliziyweni nasezingqondweni zabantu abamnyama. Ukubalisa iintsomi kuye kwalondoloza okwaa kuhlekisa kwama-Afrika. Kodwa kwiminyaka yamvanje ukuxabiseka kobomi, izinto neenkolelo zokhokho kuye kwasithwa bubundlobongela, ungquzulwano kwiipolitiki nobomi basezixekweni. Ukubalisa amabali kujoliswe ekubuyiseleni oku kuxabiseka kulutsha lwanamhlanje nasekubuyiseleni esi sithethe.
Nyakenye abakwaJoko baqalise iphulo loluntu lokubalisa iintsomi. Lusaqhubeka uphando lokufumana iintsomi zakudala nokuze loo mabali ahlale kwizizukulwana ngezizukulwana nokuze angaphelelwa ngenxa yamabali amatsha.

Nkqu nemozulu ibintle xa bekunikezelwa amabhaso eBP/Bona/Soul City Health Care Worker of the Year obelusekapa ngolwesibini ngojuni 3. Abafikelele kumanqam abathandathu kolu khuphiswano ibinguPhilip Whitey Jacobs, uNothemba Dubula, uAgnes Qwabe, uBeauty Kekana, uMoses Mathonsi noGladys Moketshe bonke baphathwe kakuhle kwelo lasekapa. Abanye bebeqala ukukhwela inqwelo-moya, abanye beqala ukuthi cakatha eKapa. Bazibonele ngawabo amehlo ngentaba yetafile, unxweme, ulwandle nokutshisa kwelanga (bekuzolile) bekwenye zeehotele ezintle zaseKapa. Ngamnye kwabafikelele kumanqam uvakalise umbulelo osuka entliziyweni ngebhaso alifumeneyo leHealth Care Worker of the Year nelidlala indima ebalulekileyo ekuqondeni ukubaluleka konompilo eluntwini. Bavakalise ukuba ayingabo bodwa ekuye kwahlonitshwa ukusebenza nzima nokuzinikela kwabo, kuvakaliswe ukuba baliqhayiya kuluntu. Impumelelo yamaphulo abo isekelwe kuthabatho-nxaxheba nenkxaso yoluntu abangamalungu alo. Ixesha abalichithe kwindawo entle, kwanokonwaba okuxhanyulwe ngabafikelele kumanqam kudambise unxunguphalo lwenkqubo yokugweba ebinzima ekugqibeleni. Eli ligqiza ekuye kwadliwan 'indlebe nalo ngabagwebi: owaphumelela ngo-1996 uShadrack Molokoane, kwanabafikelele kumanqam uTheresa Shabalala, uMercia Arendse, uZolela Ngcwabe, uKoba van Wyk noAntoinette Ntuli wakwaSoul City.
