Ukobuza yinto entsha kunonophelo lolusu kule mihla, kodwa kwenza imimangaliso! Ukobuza kukusuka kweeseli ezifileyo eziye zihlale kwiintunja zolusu xa usobuza nezithi zilwenze ulusu lubukeke ludiniwe kwaye lurhabaxa.
Izithambiso zokwebuza zobuso nomzimba zineenkozwana ezimenyezelayo, ezikhuhla amacwecwana olusu zilwenze mpuluswa lubonakale lusempilweni.
Izithambiso zokubuza aziyithathi indawo yezicoci-lusu. Ngoko kubalulekile ukuqhubeka nokusebenzisa isepha yakho oyithandayo yokucoca. Yikhethe kwezakwaPalmolive zona
eziba nononophelo eluswini lwakho. Zama
iPalmolive Green Natural Skin Care Soap ene Extra Cleansing, iGlycerine kunye neeHerbs okanye iPalmolive Pink Natural Skin Care Soap ene Extra Conditioning, Lanolin.
Xa sele ulucocile ulusu lwakho qaba isithambiso sokobuza sakho lusafumile ulusu lwakho. Sifake ngobunono ujikelezisa iminwe. Luhlambulule ngamanzi ugalele isifumisi-lusu.
Sisebenzise kusasa okanye ngokuhlwa isithambiso sokubuza sakho kube kathathu ngeveki xa unolusu olumenyezelayo. Isithambiso sokubuzisa umzimba sona sinokusetyenziswa kangangoko umntu efuna.











IZITOVU
KUZITHEMB A

Onke amabhiqa ayazi ukuba nokuba usandul'ukutshata na okanye seluneminyaka utshatile, isitovu yenye yezinto ezibalulekileyo ofanele ube nazo.


Sisitovu esenza kubekho ubudlelane ekhaya - buza umyeni nabantwana bakho - kaloku ixesha lokutya lixesha lokuba usapho ludibane luxoxe ezidla umzi kwaye lonwabele ukuba ndawonye. Yiyo loo nto isitovu kusesinye sezixhobo ezibalulekileyo ezifanele ukuthengwa, kwaye yiyo loo nto abantu abaninzi bethenga izitovu zakwaDEFY
Akukho mntu ungaziyo ukuba le nkampani ibisoloko ivelisa izixhobo
zasekhitshini nezokuhlamba impahla zodidi oluphambili, ezithembekileyo, nezifaneleyo ukuthengwa ezisebenza ngombane apha eMzantsi-Afrika.
Xa ungena emzini omtsha okanye ufuna isitovu esitsha sodidi oluphambili esinexabiso eliphantsi, izitovu zeDEFY 500mm ezizimelayo zikulungele.
Ezi zitovu zibini zongeza kwezo zakwaDEFY ezikhawulelana neenguqu kwiimfuno nakwimeko yemali yabo bonke abemi baseMzantsi-Afrika kwaye zenziwe ngendlela eyenza kube lula ukuzisebenzisa kwibhodi esasaza umbane yakwaEskom efakelwa kwizigidi ngezigidi zamakhaya eMzantsi-Afrika.
Izitovu zodidi oluphambili iDefy 511 neDEFY 521 Solid Plate zinomtsalane, kulula ukuzigcina zicocekile, zithembekile kwaye zikubhakisa kakuhle.
Izitovu zeDEFY 500mm zakhiwe ngendlela eyenza ukuba zingene kumakhitshi amancinane.
IDEFY511 inokuplagwa kwiplagi eqhelekileyo eseludongeni into ethetha ukuba akukho mfuneko yeendleko zokusidibanisa nombane. Oku kuthetha ukuba unokusitshintshela kolunye udonga xa uthanda.
Ngaphandle kokusebenzisa iipleyiti ezintathu ezomeleleyo nezicoceka lula unokusebenzisa indawo yokubhaka nokugrila.
Ezinye izinto ezenza esi sitovu sinkqenkqeze phambili:
Ngamaqhosha aso ekulula ukufikelela kuwo.
 Indawo onokugcina kuyo iimbiza neepani zakho.
 Iishelfu zeonti ekulula ukuzikhupha, ezingangenwa ngumhlwa, ezingakhawuleziyo ukonakala, ezicoceka lula nezikhuphekayo.
 Iwotshi enemizuzu engama-80 ekunceda ekugcineni amaxesha akho okupheka neqinisekisa ukuba ikeyiki nokutya okuphekayo kuphekeka
kakuhle.
Esinye isitovu esilungele amakhitshi amancinci yiDEFY52I. Siyafana neDEFY 511 qha sona sineepleyiti ezine kwaye sidityaniswa nombane nge- cable.
Zozibini ezi zitovu zikwenza upheke, ubhake yaye u- grile kakuhle.
Izitovu zakwaDEFY zezodidi oluphambili kodwa kunjalo le nkampani ineencutshe ezikumasebe ayo akulo lonke eli ajongene nokuqinisekisa ukuba ezi zitovu zisebenza ngendlela efaneleyo.
Ukuba nesitovu sakwaDEFY kukwenza ungakhathazeki ubomi bakho bonke.
