USIMBA Morri yingcali ekukhaliseni ikatala eAfrika. Abathandi bomculo bamkhumbula ngecwecwe lakhe lokuqala ethi Wasamata.
Wazalelwa kwisithili saseNarok eKenya ungowesizwe samaMasai . Unina owayengumlonjikazi, wayecula edlala ipiyano kwicawa yabo.
USimba uthe: Ndiyamthanda umama, undifundise ukuhlonipha.
USimba ulaze esemncinci ikhondo emakalithabathe: isidanga saseYunivesithi seCultural and Social Anthropology , wasiphumelela kwiYunivesithi iWitwatersrand eJohannesburg .
Ngo-1983, esengumfundi eWits uSimba usungule iqela lakhe
lentambula ekuthiwa ngaMapantsula. AMapantsula aduma ngezoo mini kwezempucuko. Eli qela laqhekeka kamva, uSimba Morri wacula yedwa. Oku kumbangele wacula kulo lonke eli nakwamanye amazwe aseAfrika.
Igalelo lakhe kweli alinakungahoywa xa kuthethwa ngomnyhadala wezenkcubeko, iikhonsathi zokunyusa inkxaso- mali , kwanakwezeepolitiki . USimba uye walundwendwe oluhlonitshiweyo eUmzantsi Cultural Centre Festival ebiseClaremont eThekwini.
Ingoma ethi Unity nethi Celebrating Life ziye zakhethwa zaza zasetyenziswa KwiSABC Election Unit xa bekunyulwa oorhulumente bakamasipala .
U-1995 ube ngowona nyaka uxakekileyo kuSimba Morri kuba ebekhenketha kweli. Ukwaxhase uCrosby , uStills noNash kwiStandard Bank Arena eJohannesburg .
USimba uwubiza umculo wakhe ngokuthi yi-Mashariki Muzik -ngumxube wesingqisho nesimbo esiquka umculo weengcungcu eKenya, umculo isuka kuMbindi weAfrika, iChimurenga eZimbabwe uMbhaqanga nomjuxuzo waseMzantsi-Afrika naseMozambique .
