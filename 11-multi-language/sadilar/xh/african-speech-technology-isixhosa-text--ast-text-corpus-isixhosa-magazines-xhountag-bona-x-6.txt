INYANISO
Ukuthintela ukumitha nokucwangcisa kuthetha into enye...ukuceba usapho lwakho nokuthinteta ukumitha okungalungiselelwanga. Unyango lwale mihla lusiphathele iindlela ezisebenza ngokuyimpumelelo ukucwangcisela iintsapho zethu.  LEO
( July 23 - August 23)
Ziphucule kuba uza kuba nethamsanqa kwiinzame ozenzavo. Xa kukho imfuneko, lungisa indlela obukeka ngayo nendlela oziphethe ngayo. Zimisele nakwintoni na oyenzayo. Akunakunconywa xa ungafaneleki. Malunga nothando nobuhlobo kufanele uthabath inyathelo eliza kubuqinisa ubuhlobo benu.Yiba sisithandwa nomhlobo osenyongweni kwiqabane lakho. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowama-24 nowama-25. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-6 no-7.

VIRGO
( August 24 - September 22)
Lisebenzise kanobom eli xesha lithembisayo apho unokuzenzela okanye uphucule idumasi yeshishini lakho ukuze ufumane inzuzo encumisayo kwimali eza kungena. Ukuze ube nethamsanqa kufuneka ukhawuleze kwinto iyenzayo. Lixesha lokuva umanyano olupheleleyo nesithandwa sakho, ukuba ningabantu abathandana ngenene. Ukuba akunalo iqabane, zifumanele omnye umntu omthandayo. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowama-26 nowama-27. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-8 no-9.

LIBRA
( September 23 - October 22)
Ukuze wenze inkqubela encumisayo milisel'ingqondo kwizinto ezithile. Awubikwa hlaba, unehlombe kodwa awunamonde. Cokisa umsebenzi omnye ngexesha. Zinike ixesha lothando ngokonwabisa isithandwa sakho ngezinto eziyolisayo. Cebani ukuzenza kunye izinto ezinwabisayo. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowoku-1, owe-2, owama-28, owama-29 nowama-30. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-1, u-2 no-3.

SCORPIO
( October 23 - November 22)
Ngoku uza kwamkela, ukhathazwe msinya ngabantu nezinto ezenzeka ekhaya. Kanti uza kuba liqabane eligqibeleleyo kwizalamane ezikhathazekileyo. Ngokuqinisekileyo uza kufumana izisombululo zeengxaki zekhaya. Xa ufuna ukwaziwa, unconywe, yiba nobuhlobo eluntwini. Usenokuzifumanela iqabane elikufaneleyo. Xa utshatile lixesha lokuhlaziya uthando lwenu. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowe-3, owe-4 nowama-31. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-3 no-4.

SAGITTARIUS
( November 23 - December 21)
Yamkela ixesha leziganeko ezonwabisayo eziya kukwenza usoloko udlamkile. Lixesha lokufezeka kweenjongo zakho ezithile, kanti ikwayinyanga yokuzuza izinto yinto, ngoko lisebenzise kakuhle ithamsanqa lakho. Ngokusebenzisa ingqondo ungayizuza intliziyo nomphefumlo wesithandwa sakho. Yenza abantu banxibelelane nengqondo yakho, intsabelo yothando iza kukwanelisa. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowe-5, owe-6 nowe-7. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-5, u-6 no-7.

CAPRICORN
( December 22 - January 20)
Ngandlel ithile iziganeko ziza kuhambisana namacebo nemigudu yakho ngeli xesha, uqinisekile ngokufumana uchatha woncedo kwithamsanqa lakho. Kwimizamo yakho yamvanje unokuyibona inkqubela ungazibhokoxanga. Isithandwa sakho sibonise ububele. Unokuluvuselela uthando ngokusiphathaphatha, usidlalisa. Imihla yethamsanqa: ngowe-8 nowe-9. Amanani ethamsanqa: ngu-8 no-9.

AQUARIUS
( January 21 - February 19)
