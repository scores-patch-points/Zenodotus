Kwathengiswa nesitulo awayesithanda uWhitney, ipiyano yakhe enkulu neembasa ezili-16 zikaBobby Brown.

Uloyiko lokuba iziyobisi ziza kumbulala lukho nakunina kaWhitney, uCissy Houston, ovumileyo kutshanje ukuba ukhe wayidomboza intombi yakhe ukuba iyeke iziyobisi ikhulise uBobbi.
