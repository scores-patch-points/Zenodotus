I-1997 HEALTH CARE WORKER 
IMBASA YOKUBHIYOZELA INKQUBELA YONOOMPILO ABABALASELEYO


Lifikile elo xesha kwakhona, apho wena mfundi uza kucelwa ukuba ukhethe abantu ababalaselayo kumsebenzi wononophelo lwezempilo eluntwini lakho. Uyakukhumbula ukuba owabalasela kunyaka ophelileyo uMasephoko Shadrack Molokoane, ongunompilo kuluntu lwaseMathabatha eNorthern Province. Nangona uShadrack engengonompilo uqeqeshiweyo, wazuza imbasa yeHealth Care Worker of the Year 1996 ngomsebeuzi wakhe wokuzinikela kwabaphethwe sisifo i-asbestosis.


Kumdlalo odumileyo weSOUL CITY omalunga nemeko ekhoyo ngokwenene yempilo eMzantsi Afrika, ababukeli baye bamqhela uSister Bettina noSister Lizzie, abangoonompilo ababini abanenkathalo kwiklinikhi iMasakhane. La manenekazi awanyangi kuphela izigulana ezizokucela uncedo kubo koko basombulula neengxaki zabantu kuba nabo banezifo eziphethe nabanye abantu. Uyakukhumbula ukuba uSister Lizzie ufumanise ukuba unentsholongwane yeHIV kwaye ngenxa yoko ubanika iingcebiso abantu ngend1ela yokumelana nesi sifo ngendlela ezahlukileyo.

Kwakhona, iSoul City iBP neBona babaxhasa ngokupheleleyo oonompilo baseMzaritsi Afrika kwaye bafuna wena uchonge abantu ocinga ukuba bayifanele le mbasa yokusebenzela impilo yoluntu. Awuzokube unika kuphela ophumeleleyo ithuba Iokuwuphucula umsebenzi wakhe ngebhaso lemali azakulifumana kodwa nawe uza kube uzinika ithuba lokuzizuzela i-1000 ngokukhetha Ioo mntu.
Obalaseleyo uzakuba ngumntu owenza umsebenzi eluntwini kwaye kufuneka enze oku:
Afumane isisombululo kwiingxaki zempilo
. Akhuthaze kwimiba ephathelele nezempilo;
  .  Achithe ixesha ephulaphula izinto eziphathelele kwezempilo athethe nangazo kwaye akhuthaze impilo entle eluntwini ngokubanzi;

. Acebise ngendlela abantu abanokuwasebenzisa ngempumelelo amaziko ezempilo akufutshane nabo;

. Abonisane noogqirha nabongikazi kummandla ahlala kuwo;

. Azinikele eku1usebeuzeleni uluntu kwaye abe nobud1elwane obuluqilima noluntu;

. Abe nomdla kuyo yonke imicimbi ephathelele nezempi1o nophuhliso;

. Ahloniphe bonke abantu naye abe ngumntu ohloniphekileyo;

. Athembeke, anyamezele kwaye aqonde.
