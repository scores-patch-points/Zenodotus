Ithabise mme o ntlafatse Senyesemane sa hao. Araba dipotso o ntse o latela matshwao mme o tla fumana lebitso la sebini sa Afrika Borwa dikgeong tse fiifaditsweng.
No 64
HAPA
Dipina tsa lerato la letsatsi la Valentine tse bitsang
RE bitsa bohle ba lerato... O a tseba hore Hlakola ke kgwedi ya lerato, ka hoo, BONA le Revolver Records di na le diphuthelwana tse seng ka tlase ho 50 tsa mmino wa classic le wa reggae wa lerato ho natefisa letsatsi leo.
Reggae Love Songs ke pokeletso ya dipina tse 21 tsa reggae tse kang Mr Loverman ka Shabba Ranks, Baby I Love Your Way ka Big Mountain, I Got You Babe ka UB40 le e nngwe e dipina tseo e sa leng di ratwa naheng ena, Can't Be With You Tonight ya Judy Boucher.
Young, Gifted And Black ke pokeletso ya dipina tsa lerato tse kang Johnny Nash - I Can See Clearly Now, Musical Youth - Pass The Dutchie, Pato Banton - Baby Come Back, Maxi Priest - Close To You le Third World's Now That We've Found Love.
Trojan Reggae ke e nngwe ya dipina tsa reggae tsa mantlha ho tswa Trojan Records, khampane e kgolo lefatsheng e rekisang mmino. Mona o ka lebella dipina tse kang Dennis Brown, Toots And The Maytals, Bob Marley le Jimmy Cliff.
Ka hoo, ithabise, o natefise letsatsi la hao le moratuwa wa hao ka dipina tsena...
