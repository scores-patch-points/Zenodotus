Ditefello tsa botho 2010

Tse hapang maikutlo
MBAB o ile wa ntsha lesedinyana la ditaba le tshehetsang kahlolo ya Lekgotla le Phameng la Dinyewe ya la 16 Tlhakubele 2010 moo mmuso o neng o laelwa ho phethisa ditshwanelo tsa ona tsa molaotheo mabapi le tshebediso ya dipuo tsa semmuso nakong ya dilemo tse pedi tse tlang. MBAB wa boela ya bontsha boikemisetso ho neha mmuso tshehetso ya ona e felletseng ho kenya tshebetsong leano la dipuo tse ngata ntle ho leeme tshebediso ya dipuo tsa semmuso. Ditho di ile tsa bontsha ho thabela qeto ena mme tsa kgotsofalla hore MBAB o ile wa fadimehela qeto eo.
Setho sa MBAB Wilna Liebenberg o tla lo etsa puo ho London Book Fair (mohla la 19-21 Mmesa 2010, Earls Court) lebitsong la MBAB. Puo ka, “South African Translators’ Institute: Unlocking South Africa’s Multilingual Treasure Trove”, o tla fana ka yona ka la 20 Mmesa ka Market Focus Pavilion.
