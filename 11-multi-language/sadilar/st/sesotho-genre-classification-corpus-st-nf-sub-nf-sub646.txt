sotho.2349.baking
Ithute ho baka, o ka kgona ho iphedisa ka hona...
Itshebetse
BONA SESOTHO - March 2007
KE eng seo o ka se etsang haeba o sa fumane mosebetsi, o se na le mangolo a mosebetsi o itseng" "Ithute ho baka!" ao ke mantswe a Patricia Mthombeni eo e sa leng a ruta ho baka sekolong sa Thola Ulwazi Baking School mona Thekong ho tloha ka 2000.
"Ke bo bong ba boiphihlelo ba bohlokwa boo motho a ka ithutang bona. Ha feela o na le motlakase kapa kgase mmoho le setofo, o ka kgona ho iphedisa.
"Hape ke wena ya hlophisang hore na o rata ho etsa bokae, le hore ke tjhelete ya boithabiso kapa ya ho iphedisa.
"E mengata menyetla bathong ba bakelang mahae. Batho ba hloka dikuku tsa manyalo, matsatsi a tswalo, mafu le mekete ya dikapeso - kapa tsa mekete efe kapa efe.
"O tlameha ho bitsa hantle."
E mong wa barutwana ba atlehileng dithutong tsena ke Nonhlanhla Khumalo, ya seng a na le kgwebo ya hae ya bo baka lapeng moo a bakang dikuku tsa manyalo. "Ke qetile dithuto tsa ka tsa ho baka ngwahola mme ka etsa dithuto tsa letsatsi ke ithuta ho etsa dikuku tsa manyalo," ke yena eo.
"Ke ne ke kgone ho etsa tjhelete di tloha feela. Mang kapa mang a ka baka, empa he lerato la ntho eo o e etsang le lona le a hlokahala, athe hape o tlameha le ho tseba ho etsa icing dikukung tsa manyalo.
"Ha ke sa le ngwana, ke ne ke tlwaetse ho utswa resepe ya rakgadi ke bake ha a le siyo.
"Ke ne ke phakise ho hlokomela hore Modimo o ne o mphile talente mosebetsing ona."
Nonhlanhla o dutse a na le diotara. Nakong eo re neng re bua le yena, o ne a bolela ha a na le diotara tsa dikuku tse 50 tsa manyalo tseo a neng a tlameha ho di baka dikgweding tse ka tlase ho tse tharo!
"Ke ntse ke sebeletsa lapeng," ke yena eo, "empa ha ho sebetsehe hantle hobane ke tlameha ho baka nakong eo lelapa le sa sebediseng kitjhene! "Ke lakatsa ho ba le sebaka sa ho sebeletsa."
Dithuto tsena mane Thola Ulwazi di fumaneha hane ka beke hoseng feela dibekeng tse nne. Ka mora ho qeta dithuto tsena, baithuti ba ebe ba se ba kgona ho baka mefuta e mengata ya dikuku jwalo ka scones, muffins, queen cakes, mmoho le "beer box" cakes. (Tsena ke tse sebediswang haholo meketjaneng mme ha o sa qala, o se sebedise pane e kgolo! Kuku e bakwang ho beer box e aletswa ka pampiri, ho kengwe icing e be e kgabiswe).
Di-scones le di-queen cakes o ka di rekisetsa batho ba rekisang.
Kajeno ke se ke kgona ho ruta bana ba ka. [s]

Vanilla Beer Box Cake
Disebediswa
2 dikopi (500ml)
tswekere
240g margarine
1 kgabana (5ml) vanilla essence
4 mahe a maholo
4 dikopi (1 000ml) cake flour
5 dikgabana (25ml) baking powder
1/2 kgabana (2,5ml) letswai
1 kopi (250ml) lebese kapa metsi, kapa tsona ka bobedi.
margarine ya ho tlotsa pane
Mokgwa
1. Kopanyetsa margarine le tswekere sejaneng ho fihlela e ba hlama e bobebe.
2. Tshela vanilla essence le
mahe, o kopanye.
3. Sefela folouru, baking powder le letswai sejaneng se seng.
4. Tshela folouru hanyane hanyane motswakong wa margarine. Tshela lebese kapa metsi, o kopanye. (Hlokomela o se kopanye nako e telele).
5. Tlotsa wax paper hanyane ka mafura mme o e ale ka hara beer box o shebise tulo e mafura hodimo. (Haeba o tla sebedisa oven tray, sebedisa ya boholo ba 36 x 27cm e be botebo ba 5cm o e tlotse ka mafura). Baka ontong ya mofuthu wa 170oC metsotso e 35-45.
6. E tlose ontong. E tlohele e sete metsotso e 5.
7. E behe rakeng ya ho fodisa. E tlohele e bate pele o ka e kgabisa ka Butter Icing.
Butter Icing
Disebediswa
3 dikopi (750ml) icing sugar
150g margarine
1 kgabana (5ml) vanilla essence
phophi ya metsi
Mokgwa
1. Kopanyetsa icing sugar, margarine le vanilla essence sejaneng. Di kopanye hantle.
2. Tshela metsi butle butle ho fihlela icing e ba lebejana. Tlotsa kuku ka yona.
Ditjeho: R18 (Kuku e ka rekiswa ka R70.)
Ho e fepela: E ka sehwa dilae tse 30.
Quick Scones
Disebediswa
2 dikopi (500ml) folouru
1 kgaba (15ml) baking powder
1/2 kgabana (2,5ml) letswai
4 dikgabana (20ml) tswekere (ha o rata)
1 lehe le leholo
3 dikgaba (45ml) oli
1/2 kopi (125ml) buttermilk
1 mothwebe wa lehe
1 kgaba (15ml) lebese
Mokgwa
1. Sefela folouru, baking powder le letswai jekeng o di phete hararo. Tshela tswekere.
2. Kopanyetsa lehe, oli le buttermilk sejaneng se seng.
3. Tshela motswako wa buttermilk folourung mme o phakise ho "seha" ka thipa. (Haeba e hlile e le thata, eketsa lebese).
4. E ale hantle lepolankeng le fafaditsweng folouru e be morumo wa 2cm. E sehe ka scones cutter kapa puturned glass.
5. Di tshele ho baking sheet e seng e tlotsitswe ka mafura. Tlotsa ka lebese le lehe ka hodimo.
6. Baka ontong e seng e le mofuthung wa 200oC metsotso e 10-15 ho fihlela di butswa.
Ditjeho: R5 (Scone ka seng o ka se rekisa R1,20.)
Resepe ena e etsa di-scones tse 9-12.
