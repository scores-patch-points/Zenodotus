sotho.1128.SaskoJuly
o hape meputso e fihlellang ho R400 000!
NA o inehela ka pelo yohle ya hao le moya ha o baka- Na bohle ba kopa o ba ekeletse- Ha ho le jwalo, ke nako ya ho sebedisa bokgoni boo hore o ikgapele meputso e babatsehang e fihlellang ho R400 000 e tsheheditsweng ke ba ha Sasko Flour, Ellerines le BONA.
Ho na le ditlhodisano tse fapaneng tse nang le mapetjo a tsona kgwedi le kgwedi:
n Phupu - puddings
n Phato - muffins (sweet and savoury)
n Loetse - quiches.
Ka moo o ka kenelang
Kgwedi le kgwedi re romelle resepe ya hao eo hara metswako ya hao ho nang le Sasko Flour e dumellanang le lepetjo la kgwedi eo, mme o tlatse setlankana sa ho kenela o se romele ho: BONA/Sasko Bake With Your Soul (July), Box 1127, Rivonia, 2128. O ka kenela ya kgwedi e le nngwe feela kapa wa di kenela kaofela - ho tswa ho wena!
Jwalo ka kgwedi ho tla kgethwa mohlodi wa lepetjo leo. Ba bahlano ho tloha ho Motsheanong, Phupjane, Phupu, Phato le Loetse ba tla mengwa moketeng wa mabenyane ho ya qothisana lehlokwa ka Mphalane mane School of Culinary Art, Randburg, moo ho tlang ho hatjwa meputso e babatsehang athe ba bang ba bane ba tla hapa meputso ya matshediso.
Meputso ya mohlodi
Mohlodi o tla hapa: Lengolo la ho reka ha Ellerines la R60 000; thepa ya kitjheneng e dikarolo di 5 ya Denver; pakana ya disebediswa tse nne (Tedelex kettle, steam iron, double hotplate le 2-slice sandwich-maker); 26-L Defy microwave oven; Defy Gold 4-plate stove; Defy 240-L double-door fridge; Defy Gold 9,2kg twin-tub washing machine; KIC 175-L chest freezer; 8-piece Emerald dinette set; Estia mini kitchen le health griller.
Tse bitsang:R83 600.
Wa pele ya mo latelang: Lengolo la ho reka ha Ellerines la R30 000; 26-L Defy microwave oven; Defy Gold 4-plate stove; LG 240-L combi fridge, le Defy Gold 9,2kg twin-tub washing machine.
Tse bitsang: R42 500.
Wa bobedi: Lengolo la ho reka ha Ellerines la R20 000; Estia mini kitchen le health griller; Fuchs 4-plate stove; LG 240-L combi fridge, le 4-in-1 appliance pack (Tedelex kettle, steam iron, double hotplate, 2-slice sandwich-maker).
Tse bitsang: R28 600
