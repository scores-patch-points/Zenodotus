Mokgwa
1. Qala ka ho futhumetsa onto ka motjeso wa 160oC. Tlotsa baking tray ka mafura. Kopanya disebediswa kaofela ntle ho almonds. Kgoba ho etsa hlama, (mohlomong metsotso e 2 ho ya ho e 3.)
2. Tshela almonds. Bopa hlama dibolwana tsa boholo ba 2.5cm.
3. Di arohanye ka 5cm ka hara baking tray. Di etse bophara ba 6mm ka ho e sidila ka kgalase e tlotsitsweng ka mafura ya inelwa ka hara tswekere.
4. Baka metsotso e 10 kapa ho fihlela di loka.
