Syrupy Orange And Lemon Cake
□ 3 olamula
□ 3 amawolintshi
□ 1 inkomishi (250ml) imajarini
□ 2 3/4 izinkomishi (685ml)
ushukela omhlophe
□ 6 amaqanda ashayiwe
□ 1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) ubisi
□ 3 izinkomishi (750ml) ufulawa i-self-raising esefiwe
□ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) amanzi
1. Gcoba ithini lamakhekhe elingu-24cm diameter. Ndlala i-baking paper.
2. Greytha amakhasi amawolintshi nakalamula ukuze kuphume izipuni ezingu-3 (45ml) zalokhu ngakunye.
3. Khama amawolintshi nolamula ukuze kuphume u-3/4 wenkomishi (185ml) kajusi.
4. Epanini shisa imajarini nezinkomishi ezimbili (500ml) ushukela, 1 isipuni samakhasi kalamula nawewolintshi wehlise umlilo. Kugovuze kuze kuncibilike imajarini. Kuthele endishini.
5. Faka amaqanda, ubisi nofulawa. Kushaye kuze kube bushelelezi. (Qaphela ungakushayi kakhulu)
6. Kuthele ethinini. Kubhake kuhhavini oshisiswe ngezinga elingu-160oC imizuzu engu-75 noma i-skewer uma sifakwe phakathi nendawo ekhekheni siphume singenalutho. Kupholise ethinini.
7. Epanini hlanganisa ndawonye amakhasi asele kalamula nawewolintshi, ujusi, ushukela namanzi. Kubilise kancane ukuyeke kugxabhe imizuzu engu-10. Thela i-syrup eshisayo ekhekheni elipholile. Kupholise imizuzu engu-10 bese ukukhipha ethinini.
Kubiza: R18,78
