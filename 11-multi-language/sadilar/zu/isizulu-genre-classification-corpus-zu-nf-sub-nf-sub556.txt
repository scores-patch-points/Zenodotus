NGABE UNGUBANI?
U-Joaquim Alberto Chissano
U-Joaquim Chissano wazalelwa eMalehice, eGaza Province okwakuyi-Portuguese colony yaseMozambique.
Waba ngumfundi omnyama wokuqala owafunda eLiceu Salazar eLourenço Marques (iMaputo manje) okwakuyiyona kuphela i-high school kuleyo ndawo.
Wenza i-medicine ePortugal, kodwa ngenxa yokuba yisishoshovu kwezepolitiki kwadingeka ukuba abaleke aye eTanzania.
Wamela iFrelimo, umbutho waseMozambique owawulwela inkululeko eMozambique, eParis ngeminyaka yo-1960.
Ngesikhathi elaseMozambique lithola inkululeko ngo-1975, wayesengu-major-general kule nhlangano.
U-Samora Machel owayengumongameli omusha kwelaseMozambique wamqoka njengongqongqoshe wezangaphandle.

I-DISCOVERY
Umkhumbi-mkhathi iDiscovery ukuma-NASA shuttles amathathu asebenza njengamanje njengoba ikanye ne-Endeavour ne-Atlantis. Yaqala ukundiza ngo-1984 kanti yaba ngumkhumbi-mkhathi owazala iHubble Space Telescope.

U-Glenn owayeneminyaka engu-77 wazakhela igama ngokuba ngumuntu omdala kwabake bahamba ngesiphuphutheki beya emkhathini. Uhambo lwayo lwakamumva iluthathe ngo-October nyakenye. I-Discovery isindize ka-33, yachitha izinsuku ezingu-241 emkhathini, yazungeza uMhlaba ka-3 808 yahamba amakhilomitha angu-150-million.

INSIMBI iyi-alloy, okusho ukuthi ixutshwa necwengekile. Ensimbini, isithako esimqoka yi-iron. Kodwa kuba ezinye “izithako,” ezinjenge-manganese, i-tungsten noma i-carbon ezenza ukuba iqine.
Insimbi eyakhiqizwa kuqala ngeyase-East Africa kanti yakhiqizwa ngo-1400BC. Kuqale phakathi kwe-19th Century ukuthi insimbi ibe ngumkhiqizo ongabizi.
Namuhla, ibalwa kulezo zinto ezande kakhulu emhlabeni kanti isemqoka kakhulu uma kwakhiwa amabhilidi, amathuluzi, izimoto nezimpahla zikagesi.
ElaseChina yilona elikhiqiza insimbi kakhulu, cishe lenza u-one-third wensimbi eyenziwa emhlabeni wonke, kulandele iJapan, iRussia neMelika.
UTHI UBUWAZI?
Bonke abantu bakhuluma ngokubaluleka kokulungiswa kwemfucuza kabusha ukuze isebenze kodwa embonini yezinsimbi lokho akukusha. Isineminyaka engaphezulu kuka-150 isebenzisa insimbi engasasebenzi ngoba lokho kushibhile kunokwenza entsha.
