zulu.685.legal aid
UMBONO WETHU: “Ukwakha iSA lapho amalungelo oMthetho-sisekelo wethu ekhuthazwa, ahlonishwe avikelwe abuye agcwaliswe khona ukuqinisekisa ukuthi bonke abantu bathola ubulungiswa.”
I-Legal Aid Board
I-Legal Aid Board of South Africa yinhlangano yezomthetho ezimele eyasungulwa ngaphansi kweLegal Aid Act (Act 22 of 1969) neLegal Aid Amendment Act (Act 20 of 1996). Injongo yayo wukunika abantulayo usizo lwezomthetho ngezindleko zikahulumeni njengoba kubekwe kuMthetho-sisekelo waseSA (Act 108 of 1996), othi bonke abantu mabasizakale ngezobulungiswa.
YINI ESIFUNA UKUYIFEZA?
Konke esikwenzayo kusekelwe kuMthetho-sisekelo. I-Legal Aid Board iyingxenye ebalulekile yoMthetho-sisekelo waseSA edlala indima enkulu ekuqinisekiseni ukuthi siphila emphakathini wentando yeningi ezinzile nezwe elivikela ligcine umthetho.
UBANI OTHOLA USIZO LOMMELI KAHULUMENI?
Sibheka lokhu okulandelayo ukuze uthole usizo lommeli:
– Siqhathanisa umholo wakho nesimo somndeni wakho.
– Sibheka onakho okungadayiswa ukuze kukhokhelwe izindleko zommeli.
I-Legal Aid Board igunyazwe ukusiza:
Izingane; Abesimame; Abangenamhlaba – abasuswe ngenkani emhlabeni wabo, ezindlini nabasebenza emapulazini; Idlanzana labantu – lapho icala elilodwa lingasiza khona abantu abaningi; Bonke abantu abathweswe amacala abangenamandla okuthola ummeli; Iziboshwa – abantu abazohlala ejele kuze kufike isikhathi sokuqulwa kwamacala abo noma asebegwetshiwe abafisa ukuba amacala abo aqulwe kabusha.
SIQINISEKISA KANJANI UKUTHI ABANTU BATHOLA USIZO OLUFANELE?
Izinjongo zethu zokuqinisekisa ukuthi sinika abantu usizo lomthetho olusezingeni eliphezulu zibandakanya:
– Ukunika abampofu nabangenamandla okuzilwela usizo lwezomthetho.
– Ukwakha izingcitha-buchopho ezizoqinisekisa ukuthi sinika abantu usizo olusezingeni eliphezulu.
– Ukuthatha izinyathelo ezisheshayo ekunikeni izingane usizo lomthetho kanye
– Nokusabalalisa usizo lomthetho nokwakha izindlela ezinqala zokusingatha lowo msebenzi.
KUYILUNGELO ELIKUMTHETHO-SISEKELO UKUTHOLA USIZO LWETHU
Ngokomthetho-sisekelo bonke abantu banelungelo lokuba nenkululeko yokungacwaswa nelungelo lokuphila. I-Legal Aid Board yasungulelwa ukuqinisekisa ukuthi le mithetho iyahlonishwa. I-Legal Aid Board ikunika usizo uma ungenamandla okukhokhela ummeli wakho.
Umgomo wethu owokuqinisekisa ukuthi uma uthweswe icala liqulwa ngendlela egculisayo. Unelungelo lokuthula uma uboshwa noma uthweswa icala. Awukwazi ukuphoqwa ukuba wethule ubufakazi obungasetshenziswa ecaleni lakho.
Kufanele uvele enkantolo ngokushesha engakapheli amahora angu-48 uboshiwe noma kuphele usuku inkantolo isebenza emva kokuba ubanjiwe.
Kufanele ubekwe icala noma uchazelwe ngokuboshwa kwakho ngosuku lokuqala uvele enkantolo.
IZINGANE ZINAMALUNGELO
I-Legal Aid Board isiza izingane uma amalungelo azo engahlonishwa. Izingane zinamalungelo akhethekile. Izingane ezineminyaka engaphansi kuka-18 zinelungelo:
– Lokuba negama nobuzwe;
– Lokunakekelwa abazali noma umndeni noma uhulumeni uma zingenabazali;
– Lokuthola ukudla, ikhaya, usizo lwezempilo, lwemfundo nolwezenhlalakahle;
–Lokuvikelwa ekuphathweni kabi, ukunganakwa nokuhlukunyezwa;
– Lokuvikelwa ekwenzeni umsebenzi okungesiwo owezingane;
– Lokungaboshwa, ngaphandle uma kungekho okunye okungenziwa, kanti uma iboshwa ayihlaliswe nezinye izingane;
– Lokuba nommeli ozoyimela uma ivela enkantolo ngamalungelo anjengokutholakala kwesondlo sezingane, ukuvikelwa, imfundo, ukunakekelwa noma amanye amalungelo.
AMALUNGELO ABESIMAME
Abesimame nezingane yibona abathathwa njengabantu abadinga ukuvikelwa kakhulu. Abesilisa nabesimame bangahlukumezeka emakhaya noma bahlukumeze kodwa abesimame yibona abasengozini kakhulu kanti ziyanda izigameko zokuhlukunyezwa kwabo. Yingakho nje abaMmeli bakaHulumeni bebathatha njengabantu abangenamandla okuzilwela.
