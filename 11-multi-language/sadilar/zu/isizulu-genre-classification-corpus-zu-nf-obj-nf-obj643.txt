Indlela yokwenza
1. Shisisa uhhavini ube ngu-160oC. Gcobisa i-oven-proof dish.
2. Sika izibindi uzinonge ngosawoti nophepha. Zibhuqe kufulawa. Epanini thosa ucezu lwesibindi ngalunye zize zibe nsunjwana kodwa zibe hlaza ngaphakathi. Zihlele kahle kwenye indishi elungisiwe.
3. Thosa u-anyanisi nogaligi kuthambe. Kufake ngesipuni phezu kwesibindi. Hlela ukherothi phezulu ulandelise ngamazambane uvuvuzele ngama-herbs. Thela ubisi phezulu. Ngebhulashi gcoba amazambane ngowoyela.
4. Kubhake imizuzu engu-40-60 isibindi sivuthwe namazambane ahlofozele. (Gudluza isivalo emva kwemizuzu engu-30 ukuze amazambane ahlofozele kahle.)[z]
