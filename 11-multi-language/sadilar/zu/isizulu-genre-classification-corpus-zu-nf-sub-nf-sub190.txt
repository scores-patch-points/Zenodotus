Ngo-May 29, 1953, u-Edmund Hillary waseNew Zealand noSherpa Tenzing Norgay waseNepal baba yithimba lokuqala ukufinyelela esicongweni sale ntaba.
Emva kwesikhathi uTenzing wavuma ukuthi uHillary nguye owabeka unyawo lwakhe kuqala kule ntaba.
Bathwebula izithombe bambela phansi amaswidi ambalwa kanye nesiphambano esincane eqhweni ngaphambi kokuba behle.

Konke ngoKHEROTHI
l Ukherothi (Daucus carota) uyi-vegetable eyimpande kanti ngokuvamile usawolintshi noma umhlophe ngombala.
l Ukherothi udleka kalula.
l Ungawudla unjalo, uwuqobe uwufake kumasaladi noma wenze isitshulu ngawo. Uma uthanda ungawubilisa. Ungawusebenzisa futhi emakhekheni nakuma-pudding.
l Okherothi banothiswe ngokudla okwakha umzimba okunjengofayibha, ama-antioxidants nama-beta-carotene nokuyizinto ezikusiza ekuboneni kahle emehlweni.
l Okherothi badabuka e-Afghanistan kanti amacembe nembewu yawo kwaqale kwasetshenziselwa ukwenza izithako zokuthandeka, hhayi isiqu sawo.
l Ngokwe-European Union, ukherothi ungathathwa futhi njenge-fruit. Lokhu kungenxa yokuthi ujamu kakherothi ubalulekile kubantu abangamaPutukezi.

Umlingo we-Microwave
SEKUPHELE iminyaka engu-60 kulo nyaka i-microwave yaba khona futhi yenza izimpilo zabantu ziba lula.
UDkt Percy LeBaron Spencer, nokunguye ongumsunguli we-microwave wazifundisa ngokwakhe ukuba ngunjiniyela esasebenza eRaytheon Corporation ngesikhathi enza i-microwave.
Kwakungesikhathi ayehlola khona ukusebenza kweshubhu le-vacuum entsha lapho athola khona ukuthi ushokoledi ayewufake ephaketheni wawusuncibilikile. Lokhu kwamholela ekutheni ahlole umshini wama-popcorn. Emva kokuba exhume umshini wama-popcorn, wabukisisa ngeso elibanzi ebona ama-popcorn ephekeka aze agcwala phansi endlini ayesebenzela kuyona.
Okunye akwenza ngonyaka ka-1945 no-1946 kwamholela ekwenzeni i-microwave yokuqala eyadayiswa ngo-1947. Yayinesisindo esingu-340kg ingaphezu kuka-1,5m ukuphakama. Yayisetshenziswa kakhulu emakhishini emikhumbi nasema-restaurant.
Phezu kwezinkoleloze ezishiwo nge-microwave, kodwa iqiniso wukuthi i-microwave ingenye yezimpahla zikagesi ezisetshenziswa ekhishini ezisebenza ngokukhulu ukushesha uma zisetshenziswe ngendlela eyiyo - kusho uLouis Bloomfield, onguphrofesa wesayensi eNyuvesi yaseVirginia eMelika.
