Pudding
250g ama-dates
5ml i-bicarbonate of soda
250ml amanzi abilayo
80g imajarini ethambile
130g ushukela onsundu
5ml i-vanilla essence
2 amaqanda
225g iSasko Self-raising Flour esefiwe
50g i-pecan nuts eqotshiwe
Toffee Sauce
130g ushukela onsundu
60g imajarini encibilikisiwe
250ml ukhilimu
1. Endishini, hlanganisa ama-dates ne-bicarbonate of soda. Thela amanzi abilayo, ukuyeke imizuzu engu-20 kuze kuthambe ama-dates.
2. Kwenye indishi, hlanganisa imajarini, ushukela ne-vanilla.
3. Shaya amaqanda, elilodwa ngesikhathi ufake engxubeni, faka isipuni sikafulawa uma ingxube ingashubile.
4. Thela ufulawa osele, i-pecan nuts nengxube yama-date.
5. Faka endishini esetshenziswa kuhhavini engu-20 x 30cm. Bhaka kuhhavini oshisiswe ngo-180oC imizuzu engu-30-40.
6. Usosi: Epanini, hlanganisa ushukela, imajarini nokhilimu. Bilisa kugxabhe imizuzu engu-5.
7. Khipha u-pudding kuhhavini, thela usosi. (Seva nokhilimu noma u-ice-cream.)
