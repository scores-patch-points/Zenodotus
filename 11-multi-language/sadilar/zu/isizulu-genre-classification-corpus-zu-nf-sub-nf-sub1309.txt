I-NILE
IGAMA elithi "Nile" liqhamuka olimini lwesiGreek kanti lichaza ukuthi "amathafa omfula."
Zimbili izingxenye ezinkulu zomfula iNile: i-White Nile, esuka enkabeni ye-East Africa, ne-Blue Nile esuka e-Ethiopia.
I-White Nile isuka kwiLake Victoria eRipon Falls, eduze kweJinga e-Uganda, ingenele iVictoria Nile egobhoza cishe ibanga elingu-500km inqamula iLake Kyoga, ize ifinyelele kwiLake Albert.
Emva kokwehlukana neLake Albert, lowo mfula usuke usuwaziwa ngokuthi yi-Albert Nile.
Uphinde ugelezele eSudan lapho waziwa ngokuthi yi-Bahr al Jebel. Ekungeneleni kwe-Bahr al Jebel neBahr el Ghazal, uba ngubude obungu-720km kanti lowo mfula waziwa ngokuthi yi-Bahr al Abyad, noma yiWhite Nile, ngenxa yobumba oluphuma emanzini awo.
Kusukela lapho, lo mfula ungenela e-Khartoum.
I-Blue Nile noma i-Bahr al Azraq isuka kwi-Lake Tana ezintabeni zase-Ethiopia.
I-Blue Nile ihamba cishe ibanga elingu-1 400km kuya e-Khartoum, nokuyilapho ihlangana khona ne-White Nile bese kuphuma i "Nile".
Umfula kulapho impilo yempucuko yabantu baleyo ndawo ilele khona, ngokunjalo nabantu baseGibhithe bakwazi ukutshala ukolo nezinye izitshalo ukuze bondle izizukulwane ngezizukulwane zesizwe sabo.
