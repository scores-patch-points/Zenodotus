O-‘P’ abane bezezimakethe
IMPUMELELO yebhizinisi lakho izoncika ekutheni ubaqonda kanjani o ‘P’ abane bezezimakethe: PRODUCT, PRICE, PROMOTION, PLACE.
Product. Lona wumkhiqizo owuthengisayo okufanele uwenze udonse amakhasimende. Lokhu ungakwenza ngendlela owupakisha ngayo, ikhwalithi, uchunge nokutholakala kwawo.
Price. Kufanele kucace ukuthi amanani akho uwabeka kuphi, ufuna ukuthola inzuzo engakanani, uzokwenza kanjani ukuba abantu bangayikhaleli imali yabo.
Promotion. Azi ukuthi uzowuthuthukisa kanjani emphakathini umkhiqizo wakho nokuthi ingakanani ibhajethi oyibekele lokhu.
Place. Lokhu akusho indawo odayisela kuyona kuphela, kodwa lapho kukhona amakhasimende akho nokuthi yiziphi izindlela ozozisebenzisa ukuwusabalalisa.
