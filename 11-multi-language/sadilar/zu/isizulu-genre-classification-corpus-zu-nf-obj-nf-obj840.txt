AmaCango Caves atholakala enkwalini cishe esineminyaka engu-4 500-million ubudala. Atholakala ochungeni lwezintaba zaseSwartberg eduze kwedolobha lase-Oudtshoorn eWestern Cape.

Obhejane
Siye sizitshele ukuthi obhejane yizilwane zakuleli - kodwa iqiniso wukuthi bayatholakala nakwezinye izingxenye zomhlaba…
Obhejane baseNdiya
Baphinde baziwe ngokuthi ngamaGreat One-horned Rhinoceros. Lolu hlobo lobhejane lutholakala eNepal nase-Assam kwelaseNdiya.
LwalusePakistan naseBurma kanti seluyatholakala naseShayina.
Lolu hlobo lobhejane baseNdiya lunesikhumba esiwugqinsi esinsundu ngokucwebezelayo. Imilenze yabo ngenhla namahlombe iqumbile kanti futhi baneziboya ezincane emzimbeni.
Usayizi wayo ulingana nalowo weWhite Rhino e-Africa. Oweduna unesisindo esiye sifike ku-3 000kg owensikazi ube ngu-1 500kg.
Unophondo olulodwa elingubude obungu-100cm.
Ama-Javan Rhinoceros
Lolu nguhlobo olungajwayelekile oluyingozi emhlabeni. Ngokwezibalo zika-2002, bangu- 60 kuphela abasalayo eJava (Indonesia) naseVietnam.
Banophondo olulodwa ekubeni abanawo uboya emzimbeni. Isikhumba sabo sibukeka sishwabene. Asebekhulile banesisindo esingu- 1 000kg. Abamaduna kuphela abanophondo.
AmaSumatran Rhinoceros
Lolu nguhlobo lobhejane abancane kunabo bonke nolunoboya obuningi obenza lukwazi ukuphila endaweni efana neyaseBorneo naseSumatra.
Lolu nguhlobo futhi olungajwayelekile - cishe sekusele abangu-300 kuphela.
I-Sumatran rhino esikhulile ingu-130cm ukuphakama, ibe nesisindo esingu-700kg.
Njengaleyo etholakala kwelase-Africa, inezimpondo ezimbili kanti olukhulu ngoluphambili.[z]
