UMA kuziwa ngasekukhetheni okukufanele kokuvikela ukukhulelwa, okuningi kuncika ezidingweni zakho. Lokhu kubandakanya impilo yakho, noma uhlela ukuba nabantwana.
Okunye okubalulekile wukuthi, ngabe usothandweni olwethembekile yini, uthandana nomuntu oyedwa noma baningi oya nabo ocansini.
Phezu kwezinga eliphezulu lobuchwepheshe kwezokwelapha nasekuthuthukisweni kwezindlela zokuvikela ukukhulelwa, amakhondomu kuseyiyona ndlela ekuvikela kwi-HIV/AIDS nakwezinye izifo ezithathelana ngokocansi.
Ochwepheshe beluleka ngokuthi uma ungathembekile othandweni lwakho, kufanele noma kanjani uhlale usebenzisa amakhondomu ngisho ngabe kukhona okokuvikela ukukhulelwa okusebenzisayo.

Ezithinta ezocansi
Esimweni esiphuthumayo
INDLELA ephephile nesebenzayo yokuvikela ukukhulelwa, i-Emergency Contraceptive Pills (ECPs) ingakuvikela ekukhulelweni emva kokuya ocansini olungaphephile.
Ziningi izindlela ezehlukene ezikhona, kodwa zonke zivikela ukukhulelwa ngokumisa noma ngokubambezela iqanda ukuba likhishwe kuma-ovary noma limise iqanda eselivundile ukuba libambelele kwi-uterus.
Ama-ECPs angasetshenziswa ezimweni ezilandelayo:
Uma uye ocansini ungahlelanga futhi ngendlela engaphephile;
Uma waphoqelelwa ukuya ocansini futhi ungazisebenzisi izinto zokukhulelwa;
Kwadabuka ikhondomu ngesikhathi niya ocansini;
Uma uphuthwe amaphilisi amabili noma angaphezulu okuvikela ukukhulelwa;
Uma usungaphezu kwamasonto amabili ekutheni uthole umjovo wakho wokuzivikela.
Ama-ECPs asebenza ngempela lapho ethathwe engakapheli amahora angu-24, kanti angathathwa kuya ezinsukwini ezinhlanu emva kocansi olungavikelekile.
Uma uwasebenzisa ngokushesha, kulapho ekusebenzela kakhulu. Kudingeka ukuba uthathe amaphilisi amabili: elokuqala ngokukhulu ukushesha emva kocansi olungavikelekile, elesibili emva kwamahora angu-12.
(Qaphela: Ama-ECPs akufanele uwasebenzise esikhundleni sento yokuvikela ukukhulelwa. Uma esetshenziswa njalo, amathuba okukhulelwa makhulu kunokuba usebenzisa izinto zokukhulelwa ezijwayelekile.)
Okungagunyaziwe - izindlela zendalo
Indlela ye-rhythm kanye neyokungathululeli ngaphakathi, zombili yizindlela zendalo. Kodwake ayikho kuzona egunyazwa ngochwepheshe.
Indlela ye-rhythm ibandakanya ukugcina amarekhodi okuya ezinsukwini zakho ukuze ugweme isikhathi sokuvunda kowesimame.
Eyokuthululela ngaphandle ibandakanya ukuphuma kowesilisa ngaphambi kokuba achithe kanti ayithembekile ngoba enye imbewu ingangena ngaphakathi kowesimame ngaphambi noma nangemuva kokuchitha.
Ama-Spermicides amakhemikhali abulala imbewu kanti ayizindlela ezehlukene - igwebu, injimbilili, iphilisi elincibilikayo noma ukhilimu - okushuthekwa esithweni sangasese. Kufanele asetshenziswe ne-diaphragm ("okuyinkomishana" efakwa ngaphakathi kowesimame). Ngeke wethembele kulokhu, kunalokho usungasebenzisa nekhondomu futhi.[z]

Ukuvikela ukukhulelwa 'ngokuvala inzalo'
UKUVALA inzalo yindlela ekhona kwabesilisa nabesimame. Noma ngubani oneminyaka engu-18 nangaphezulu osengaba ngumzali angavalwa inzalo ngokwesicelo sakhe.
Ukwelulekwa ngochwepheshe kuyenziwa, kusayinwe nefomu yemvume - kanti zombili izindlela zisebenza ngokungamaphesenti angu-99.
Abesimame: Ukuhlinzwa kwaziwa ngokuthi yi-tubal ligation. AmaFallopian tubes okuyiwo ahamba amaqanda kusuka kuma-ovaries kuya kwi-uterus ayaboshwa. Lokhu kuvimbela amaqanda ukuba angavuthiswa yimbewu yowesilisa.
Abesilisa: Le ndlela ibizwa ngokuthi yi-vasectomy. Amashubhu "embewu" ayasikwa aboshwe. Lokhu kuvimbela imbewu ekungeneni oketshezini olungenayo ngesikhathi niya ocansini. Izinkinga nokwehluleka kwale ndlela yinto engavamile. (Qaphela: Le ndlela ayanele ukuvikeleka okusheshayo ekukhuleliseni - ezinye izindlela zokuvikela ukukhulelwa zigunyazwe ukusebenza ezinyangeni ezintathu emva kwale ndlela.)
