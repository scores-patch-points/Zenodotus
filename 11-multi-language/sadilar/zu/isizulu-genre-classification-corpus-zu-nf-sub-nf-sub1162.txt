Izingozi zemigwaqo
Nginombono engicabanga ukuthi ungenza kunciphe izingozi zomgwaqo kuleli. Kufanele amaphoyisa omgwaqo abe khona kuyona yonke imigwaqo, abe namasosha ukuze awasize.
Kulabo abasebenzisa amatekisi ngithi: uma umshayeli ehamba ngejubane elingavumelekile kufanele nimtshele ehlise ijubane. Ningalokothi ningene etekisini yomshayeli odakiwe.
Abantu abahamba ngezinyawo nabo kufanele baqaphele.

Yilwa nobugebengu
Ngicabanga ukuthi sonke kufanele sizibeke icala uma sekuziwa ngasebugebengwini, kanjalo nabezobulungiswa nohulumeni. Izigebengu akufanele ukuba zithole ibheyili ngoba kubukeka sengathi lokhu yikho okwenza isimo sedlulele. Abantu abathenga izimpahla zokwebiwa yibo abakhuthaza izenzo zobugebengu. Kufanele babike izigebengu emaphoyiseni. Ngaleyo ndlela bangasebenzisana ngokubambisana namaphoyisa ukuze bagweme ubugebengu. Sonke masilweni nobugebengu ukuze sithokozele inkululeko esayilwela sonke.
N Gandela, Flagstaff

Okunye ngemingcwabo…
Iningi labantu likhathazwa yizindleko ezibizayo zemingcwabo - ikakhulu ezidalwa wukudla okudliwa abantu abasuke beze emngcwabeni. Ngabe ukupha abantu ukudla kubalulekile?
Empeleni singasho ukuthi yinto esiphelelwe yisikhathi. Lo mkhuba yinto eyayenziwa kudala lapho abantu babehamba izinsuku eziningi bephuma ezindaweni ezikude - ngezinyawo noma ngezinqola ezazidonswa yizinkomo. Kwakuthi emva komngcwabo bese belala lapho kulelo khaya ngenxa yokukhathala, banikwe nokudla.
Namuhla njengoba sekukhona izimoto, amabhasi, izitimela namabhanoyi, ukuhamba akuseyona into enzima. Ngabe mhlawumbe sesingasho ukuthi ukuba nephathi emva komngcwabo yinto osekumele siyiyeke?
Ngaphandle kwalokho, uma abantu beza emngcwabeni ngoba bezobona ukuthi nhloboni yokudla okuzodliwa - pho ubani obadingayo?
PD, Mdantsane [z]

Ukuphuza
Ukuphuza utshwala emphakathini yinto esithanda ukudlanga.
Okokuqala ukuphuza utshwala kwenza umqondo wakho ukhohlwe kalula. Uvuka ekuseni ungazi ukuthi ufike kanjani ekhaya ebusuku izolo nokuthi ithange lemoto uligcwalise kanjani.
Kubuhlungu ukungakhumbuli ukuthi yini oyenzile. Ngisho ungaba wumuntu othula kanjani, kodwa utshwala buyakushintsha ngesikhathi uphuza ugcine usunomsindo.
Kodwa phezu kwalokho abantu basaqhubeka nokuphuza.
Impendulo yalokhu wukuba ufune usizo lochwepheshe. Uma unenkinga yokuphuza, sekuyisikhathi sokuba uyeke ukuphatha kabi umndeni wakho, kube khona okwenzayo ukuze uzelaphe!
