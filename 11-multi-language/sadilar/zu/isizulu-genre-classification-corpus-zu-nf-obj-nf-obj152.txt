Odinga ukuba ukwenze:
1. Hambela ama-forum noma ama-workshop abeBONA Empowerment Club.
2. Thola imininingwane yebhizinisi lakho nefomu yoku oda emicimbini yethu kulo nyaka.
3. Qala uzitholele amakhasimende, uxhumane nabantu ongabathengisela. Ungadayisa emphakathini, kuma-spaza shop noma ezikoleni.
Qhubeka ufunde uBONA ukuze uthole kabanzi ngebhizinisi lobhulankethi bakwa-Aranda nangama-workshop eBONA Empowerment Club kulo nyaka.
U BONA nabeSAICOM Payphones bazobe bene-empowerment forum ezokwaziwa ngokuthi “Ukuxhumana ongakumela ngabeSAICOM South Africa”. Lapha kuyogxilwa ekufundiseni ngozisungulela ibhizinisi locingo emphakathini wangakini. AbakwaSAICOM bazonikhombisa ukuthi anganisiza kanjani amabhizinisi enu nemindeni yenu nomphakathi imbala.
U-SAICOM uhamba phambili kwezezingcingo zamaGSM kanti uyakudilivela futhi. Le nkampani ikholelwa kubantu kunasekwenzeni inzuzo kanti futhi izibophezele ekusebenzisaneni namakhasimende azimisele ngokuphumelela kwezamabhizinisi.
AMAKHASIMENDE akwaSAICOM kuningi azokuhlomula ngokuzisungulela amabhizinisi. Uhlelo lwalokho lulula kakhulu: awekho amakontileka (contracts), kunezindlela ezilula zokukhokha, ayikho imibandela yokusebenza kwakho kanti kukhona nocingo lukazwelonke elingakusiza.
U-SAICOM ungenye yezinkampani ezidlondlobala ngokushesha eSA kanti usuhlomulise izinkulungwane zabantu bakuleli. Qiniseka ukuthi nawe uzitholela indawo namuhla!
USHUKELA we-Illovo ugcina abantu bakuleli benomdlandla kanti uyatholakala ucolisekile (omhlophe) noma ungacolisekile ) o-brown).
O-brown noma ongacolisekile, uwushukela ngokwendalo. Awugqunyisiwe, awufakwanga izithako zemibala. Lo shukela ulungele ukubhaka nokwenza iziphuzo ezishisayo kanti yiwo osetshenziswa abahluzi botshwala uma benza utshwala besintu KwaZulu-Natal.
Ushukela o-brown ufakwe ezikhwameni ezinhle zikapulasitiki ezitholakala ngo-500g no-1kg.
Uchunge locolisekile lubanzi kakhulu - kusukela ezikhwameni ezincane ezingu-250g kuya kwezinkulu ezingu-12,5kg ezihambisana naleso esijwayelekile esingu-2,5kg. Ezindaweni eziningi, yizo lezi zikhwanyana ze-Illovo ezihamba phambili kubathengi.
