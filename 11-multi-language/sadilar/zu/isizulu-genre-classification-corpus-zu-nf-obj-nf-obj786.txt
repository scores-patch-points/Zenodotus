BONA ZULU – April 2007
NGO-APRIL iBONA Empowerment Club izohlangana edolobheni elinobungani iPort Elizabeth. Sinamalungu athembekile amaningi kuleyo ndawo kanti futhi singajabula ukuba sinibone nonke.
Ingqikithi ka-April ithi, ukuziphilisa ngokudla, ezokuvakasha nokulima. Sidinga ukuzinakekela lezi zimboni ukuze senze iSouth Africa indawo abaningi abazoyivakashela nokuba ibe yindawo enhle abantu abangahlala kuyo.
Sizonikhombisa ukuthi ningaphumelela kanjani kulezi zimboni kanti izikhulumi zethu zosuku zizosinika izeluleko nolwazi oluyusizo ekukhuliseni amabhizinisi nokuba niphumelele ezifisweni zenu.
Sizizwa sihlonipheke kakhulu ukuba noPriscilla Leander njengesikhulumi sethu sosuku kulo mcimbi wale nyanga kanti simbonga kakhulu ngokuba nathi asicobelele ngamakhono akhe.
Sizimisele ukuba sinibone nonke ePort Elizabeth. Nibe nePhasika elihle!
Yenza okubhakayo kunambitheke kamnandi!
IBONA Empowerment Club ibonga kakhulu ukujoyinwa yiSasko Flour njengabaxhasi abakhulu kulo nyaka. Kuningi esesikufundile kuma-workshop kanti futhi kuningi esisakubhekile ngamasu namacebo akwaSasko Flour.
Njengoba iPhasika selisemnyango, ayikho into engafudumeza ikhaya lakho njengokufreshi okubhakile nokuzokwanelisa amakhasimende akho.
Imikhiqizo yakwaSasko Flour ikwenza kube lula ngaphezu kwakho konke. Umndeni wakho wonke uzokuthokozela ukukusiza ngesikhathi wenza okumnandi kwePhasika nawo ukuthokozele ukukudla futhi.
Uma wenza umsebenzi wokuziphilisa ngokudla, iPhasika yisona sikhathi esihle kakhulu sokukhombisa amakhono akho wenze amakhasimende akho azizwe ebalulekile. ISasko Flour yiyo efanele noma yini oyibhakayo.
Ufulawa wakwaSasko wenziwe ngokolweni wakuleli naphesheya ocolisekile ukuqinisekisa ukuthi ubhaka okuphambili. Ngisho ngabe ubhaka ama-scones noma eyehla esiphundu i-chocolate cake, ungayethemba iSasko Flour ukuze okubhakayo kunambitheke kamnandi.
USasko Flour usaqhubeka nokusilethela ikhwalithi ephambili njengoba usuwenzile kule minyaka engaphezu kwengu-70.
Uthi ubuwazi ukuthi isiphehli sikafulawa ukugunyazwa ngabe-HACCP e-Africa, yiSasko Flour Mill ePE? Lokhu kusho ukuthi iSasko Flour iyahlangabezana nezidingo zezinga eliphezulu lasemhlabeni ngekhwalithi nangokuphepha kokudla. Kungakhoke sikuthokozela ukuba nomcimbi wale nyanga edolobheni elinobungani iPort Elizabeth.
Ungakhohlwa ukubukela uhlelo lodumo, lweSasko Flour oluthi Mara And Friends ngoLwesithathu ngo-6 ntambama kuSABC 2. Hlanganyela noMara Louw nezivakashi zakhe ngesikhathi bekhuluma futhi bethokozela okumnandi okubhakwe ngendlela yakwaSasko Flour.
