Beef Burger Patties
Patties
□ 1 ucezu lwesinkwa esimhlophe esicwiliswe ku-1/4 inkomishi (60ml) ubisi
□ 1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
□ 2 izigaxa ugaligi ogayiwe
□ 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-parsley eyonyisiwe
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
□ 500g i-mince
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela wokuthosa
□ 2 u-anyanisi osikwe izingcezu
Izithako zokwenza
□ 6 ama-hamburger rolls asikwe ohhafu
□ u-lettuce
□ 2 utamatisi osikwe izingcezu
□ 1/4 u-cucumber osikwe izingcezu
□ i-tomato sauce noma i-mayonnaise yokunonga
1. Patties. Endishini hlanganisa ubisi, u-anyanisi, ugaligi, i-parsley, u-curry ne-mince. Kwenze ama-patties ayisithupha ukufake efrijini ukuze kuqine kahle.
2. Epanini thosa ama-patties imizuzu engu-4-5 ohlangothini ngalunye.
3. Kulo lelo pani thosa u-anyanisi uze uthambe.
4. Ukwenza ama-burger - beka u-lettuce, utamatisi no-cucumber kwi-roll ngayinye phezulu, i-pattie no-anyanisi ukunonge nge-tomato sauce noma i-mayonnaise.
5. Kuseve nama-chips noma okuhlofozelayo.

Fried Chicken
□ 3 izipuni (45ml) i-self-raising flour
□ 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-Aromat
□ 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-chicken spice
□ 1kg izingcezu zenkukhu ezigxajisiwe
□ 2 amaqanda ashayiwe
□ uwoyela wokuthosa
1. Esikhwameni sepulasitiki hlanganisa ufulawa, i-Aromat ne-chicken spice.
2. Faka inkukhu esikhwameni, uyixukuze ukuze igcobeke yonke. Cwilisa ucezu ngalunye lwenkukhu eqandeni bese uphinde ulubhuqe ngofulawa.
3. Epanini thosa izingcezu zenkukhu zize zibe nsunjwana.

u-curry powder
□ 1 1/2 amaqhuzu e-beef stock encibilikiswe ezinkomishini ezingu-2 (500ml) amanzi abilayo
□ 3 ukherothi osikwe izingcezu
□ 6 amazambane aqotshiwe asikwa ohhafu
□ 1 isipuni (15ml)
i-cornflour
□ 2-3 izipuni amanzi abandayo
□ 3 ohhafu bolofu bezinkwa ezimhlophe noma 1 1/2 olofu abamhlophe.
1. Epanini thosa inyama ize ibe nsunjwana uyisuse epanini uthose u-anyanisi uze uthambe. Faka utamatisi no-curry powder ukuthose imizuzu emihlanu ulokhu ukugovuza. Buyisela inyama epanini.
2. Bondela phakathi i-stock esishisayo, ukumbozisise kugxabhe emlilweni ihora.
3. Bondela phakathi ukherothi namazambane kuthambe ukuyeke kugxabhe imizuzu engu-30-50 noma inyama ize ithambe.
4. Kujeke hlanganisa i-cornflour namanzi ukugovuzele epanini kushube i-gravy.
5. Gubha isinkwa phakathi ufake isishebo phezulu ubeke okwesinkwa okukhiphe esinkweni. Kuseve ngokushesha.

□ 6 i-dried curry leaves
