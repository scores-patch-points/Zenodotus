"Lesi ngesinye sezibonelo esikhomba ngokusobala ukuthi abantu abasebenzisa umgwaqo kufanele bakwazi ukubekezelelana," kusho uMdu Mkhize oyiphoyisa lezindaba zomphakathi. "Akekho umuntu ongekho engozini emgwaqweni."
UMargaret Mbatha ngelinye ilanga wayethi uyajika emgwaqweni wathola ukuthi kukhona imoto eyayivimbile phakathi nomgwaqo. Emva kwalokho washaya i-hooter. "Ngiyacabanga ukuthi lokho kwamcasula umnini waleyo moto ngoba wavele wahlehlisa imoto yakhe ngesivinini washayisa eyami. Emva kwalokho wakhuluma ememeza esho amazwi ayinhlamba, wasuka wahamba."
Abashayeli abahlaza abangayigcini imithetho yomgwaqo baya ngokwanda kanti ubabona ngokuhamba ngesikhulu isivinini, bashayele ezinye izimoto ama-headlights, bancike ngemumva eduze kwezinye izimoto kanti nolimi lwabo aluluhle - banokweyisa.
Abezokugcinwa komthetho bathi ukuhluthuka kwabashayeli abaningi kudalwa wukubambezeleka kwi-traffic - abanye bakhiphela ukucasuka kwabo emgwaqweni.
Onguchwepheshe wezengqondo uGideon Mchunu uthe: "Abantu abashayelayo banomkhuba wokukhiphela ukucasuka kwabo kwabanye ngoba bengenandaba bengazani. Umuntu okuthukayo emgwaqweni ungathola umhlola ukuthi lapho eya khona ephathini mhlawumbe, ngeke abe yileyo nto."
"Izinto ezifana nalezi ungazigwema ngokuba ukwazi ukuziqaphela bese wenza konke okusemandleni ukuba zingenzeki," kusho uReggie Ntombela, ongumqeqeshi wezokushayela.
Abashayeli abajoja abanye ngemumva
"Uma ubona imoto iza ngamandla emva kwakho kufanele wehlise ijubane ukuze uvule isikhala phakathi kwakho nemoto engaphambi kwakho," kusho uNtombela.
"Lokhu kusiza ekutheni uma kwenzeka kudingeka ukuba ume ngokushesha nokuthi lona okuxine ngemumva ehlise ijubane kuze kufike ithuba lokuba umdedele.
Abantu abacusheleza ezinye izimoto
"Kufanele futhi uhlale ubaqaphele abantu abathanda ukuhamba becusheleza ezinye izimoto. Esimweni esinjena, kufanele wehlise ijubane," kusho uNtombela.
Kufanele njalo ngama-second angu-3 kuya kwakungu-10 ulokhu ubheka izibuko zakho ukuze ubheke isimo esiseduze kwakho.
Uma ushintsha ama-lanes sebenzisa i-indicator yakho ukuze nabanye abashayeli babone ukuthi uzimisele ngani kusenesikhathi. "Ukucusheleza ezinye izimoto akusizi ngalutho, kunalokho kungadala izingozi.
"Uma ngiya emsebenzini ngihamba ngo-70 km/h ngifike kahle futhi - hhayi njengomuntu obelokhu ecusheleza ezinye izimoto ephula imithetho yomgwaqo," kusho uNtombela.
Ukubhekana ngeziqu zamehlo
Kufanele wehlise umoya ungaqophisani nalowo muntu. Ukwenza isibonelo salokhu, uma omunye umshayeli enyusa ijubane ngesikhathi uthi uyamdlula vele ungabe usaqhubeka nalokho wehlise umoya.
"Kugweme njalo ukubhekana ngeziqu zamehlo. Lokhu akusizi ngoba mhlawumbe uzothola ukuthi ucasulwa umuntu odakiwe noma odle izidakamizwa," kusho uSibonelo Hlatshwayo onguchwepheshe wezengqondo.
