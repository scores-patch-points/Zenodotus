Ukutshuza olwandle
Ukutshuza kungumdlalo odlalwa olwandle kanti owudlalayo ugibela ebhodini athwalwe yigagasi elimkhiphela ngaphandle olwandle. Abatshuzi babuye batshuze beguqe ngamadolo noma balale ngezisu ebhodini elifishane, bagwedle izikebhe, basebenzise ne-surf skis (uhlobo lwesikebhe).
Ukutshuza kungumdlalo owaqalwa esiqhingini saseHawaii ngeminyaka yo-1700 uqalwa nguLt James King owabhala ngawo emibhalweni ayeyibhalela uKapteni uJames Cook.
Ukutshuza kubalulekile emasikweni aseHawaii. Amakhosi ayeqhakambisa ubuholi bawo ngokutshuza!
Iningi lamabhodi okutshuza asetshenziswa esikhathini samanje enziwe nge-polyurethane foam, kanti anokhuni ngaphezulu ukuze aqine kahle. Okuthofozelayo okufakwa kuwona kuqala kukhavwa ngendwangu ye-fibreglass egcotshwa nge-polyester resin. Lelo nalelo bhodi lipendwa kahle ukuze libe bushelelezi licwebezele. Emabhodini enziwe kamumva kusetshenziswa i-epoxy eqinile nelula kune-fibreglass.
Ezintweni ezisetshenziswa ekutshuzeni kukhona ibhodi, intambo (eboshelwa eqakaleni kumtshuzi ukuze azimelele ebhodini), i-wax eyenza ukuba izinyawo zomtshuzi zingasheleli, namaphiko afakwa ngaphansi ebhodini ukuze ligijime kakhulu libambelele egagasini.
