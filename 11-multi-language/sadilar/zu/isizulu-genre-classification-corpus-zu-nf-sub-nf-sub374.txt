NGICABANGA ukuthi mhlawumbe sekuyisikhathi sokuba uhulumeni ashintshe eminye imithetho ngoba sidinga kakhulu ukuvikeleka ezigebengwini.
Kubukeka sengathi bambalwa abantu abawahloniphayo amaphoyisa, nomthetho-sisekelo nohulumeni.
Ake nicabange ngemidlwembe nemigulukudu yezigebengu ehlukumeza umphakathi wethu, engasabi ukutshela eyabahlukumeza yaboshwa ukuthi izobuya izoziphindiselela - lokhu ikusho phambi kwamaphoyisa.
Kufanele kube nemithetho eyisipesheli eqinile ebhekiswe kulabo ababulala amaphoyisa.
Okuxakayo wukuthi abantu bahlale bekhala ngamaphoyisa njalo kodwa akukho noyedwa okhuluma ngobugebengu.
Ngineqiniso ukuthi asilwelanga inkululeko ukuze izimpilo zethu zidicilelwe phansi - amasela nababulali.
N Zifo, Kuilsrivier

KUNGANI kudingeke ukuba iziguli zime ngaphandle zilinde ukuvulwa komtholampilo? Lokhu kusanda kwenzeka kimina. Ngisho nasekuseni ngezikhathi zasehlobo, kuyenzeka kubande - cabangake ukuthi kunzima kangakanani ebusika! Abantu balinda kusukela ekuseni ngovivi - abantu abadala nomama nezingane ezigulayo…
Ngeke yini abahlengikazi basemtholampilo bahlele nonogada ukuba avule amasango ukuze abagulayo bazokuma ngaphakathi lapho kungconywana khona ukubanda?
Enye into engicabanga ukuthi kufanele ilungiswe, yilantshi nesikhathi setiye. Siyazi ukuthi nabahlengikazi bayadinga ukubamba umoya ngalezi zikhathi, kodwa kungani bonke baye kanyekanye ngesikhathi esisodwa bashiye iziguli zilindile?
ZM, Illovo
