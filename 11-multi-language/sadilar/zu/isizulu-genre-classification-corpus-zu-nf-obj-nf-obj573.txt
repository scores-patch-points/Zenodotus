CAJUN STICKY RIBS

IZELULEKO ZOKUBRAYA
■ Ngaphambi kokuqala ukubraya, kuhle ugcobe ucingo ozobrayela kulona ngowoyela wokupheka. Lokhu kuzosiza ekuvikeleni inyama inganamatheli.
■ Ngaphambi kokubraya sika u-anyanisi uwukhuhle kwi-grill ukuze inyama inambitheke kamnandi.
■ Amalangabi adalwa amanoni enyama aconsela emlilweni. Uma lokhu kwenzeka kwenza ukuba inyama ishe ibe mnyama inganambitheki kahle. Ukuze ukugweme, yiba ne-spray bottle yamanzi abandayo, ukuze udambise amalangabi lapho evela.
■ Uma uhlanza okokubraya, kungakuhle usebenzise ibhulashi locingo ngesikhathi isashisa.[z]

BEER BRAAI BREAD
* 1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) i-wholewheat flour
* 2 1/2 izinkomishi (625ml) i-self-raising flour
* 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) usawoti
* 3 izipuni (45ml) imajarini
* 3/4 inkomishi (185ml) ubhiya
* 3/4 inkomishi (185ml) ubisi nowoyela wokubhrasha ulofu
* 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ushizi ogreythiwe iCheddar
* 4 i-spring onions esikwe izingcezu (ngokuthanda kwakho)
1. Hlanganisa ofulawa nosawoti. Khuhlela phakathi imajarini.
2. Enza imbobo phakathi nendawo, ufake ubhiya nobisi wenze inhlama.
3. Hlanganisa inhlama uyibumbe ibe ngu-loaf. Brasha ngowoyela ugoqe nge-foil.
4. Kubraye imizuzu engu-15. Vula i-foil uvuvuzele ngoshizi no-anyanisi. Kuyeke imizuzu engu-5 ushizi uze uncibilike.

* 4 izipuni (60ml) i-castor sugar
* 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-cinnamon

3. Kuseve kushisa no-ice-cream.
