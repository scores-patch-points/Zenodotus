zulu.617.petrol
NGESIKHATHI ngiqala ukushayela ngeminyaka yo-1970 u-petrol wawubiza u-9 cent ilitha. Kusukela ngaleso sikhathi sesande kakhulu isibalo sezimoto emgwaqweni kanti akunasiqiniseko sokuthi usazotholakala yini u-petrol eminyakeni ezayo. Ohulumeni emhlabeni wonkana sebebonile ukuthi inkulu imali abangayenza ngabashayeli.
Isimo sibukeka sisazoba muncu kanti akufanele sizithembise ukuthi inani lika-petrol liyoke lehle. Esingakwenza wukuzama ukuba siwusebenzise ngokuwonga. U-petrol osethangini lakho wuphathise okwemali esebhange usebenzise ilitha ngayinye ngokuyonga.
Indlela elula nesebenzayo ekongeni u-petrol wukungayisebenzisi kakhulu imoto yakho.
Kodwa lokho akusho ukuthi ngeke usakwazi ukuzikhipha ngeSonto ngemoto yakho. Zithokozise ngemoto yakho kodwa qaphela ukuthi yimalini edleka ngesikhathi ushayela ube nesiqiniseko sokuthi uthola okukahle ngemali yakho.
Zihambele ngezinyawo uma uvakashela umngani wakho oseduze.
Uma kungenzeka sebenzisani imoto eyodwa nabangani noma nabantu osebenza nabo ukuze nihlanganyele emalini ka-petrol. Ngelinye isonto ningasebenzisa imoto yakho nisebenzise eyomngani ngesonto elilandelayo uma niya emsebenzini.
Ngaleyo ndlela uzokonga imali ka-petrol, yokusevisa imoto, yokuguga kwamasondo nezinsimbi zayo.
Uma ilitha ka-petrol ibiza R5,80 imoto yakho ihamba u-10 kilometres ngelitha eyodwa, usebenzisa cishe u-60c ku-petrol kuphela ngalelo nalelo kilometre olihambayo.
Ngo-40-kilometre owuhamba uya emsebenzini ukhokha u-R24 nsuku zonke, noma u-R480 ngenyanga.
Ngokujoyina umngani wakho inyanga izophela uno-R240 owonge ngo-petrol kuphela - kanti nenani lemoto yakho lizothi xaxa uma usuyidayisa ngoba izobe ihambe amakhilomitha ambalwa.
Njengoba usuzame ukwehlisa izindlela ozihamba kungenasidingo, uthini ngalezo zikhathi okudingeka ukuba uhambe ngazo ngemoto" Ungawonga kanjani u-petrol"
Izeluleko...
Imoto yakho ayihlale isesimweni esihle. I-air filter engcolile noma ama-spark plugs aphelile kungenza imoto iwudle kakhulu u-petrol.
Qiniseka ukuthi amasondo emoto anomoya ofanele. Uma engenamoya imoto iwudla kakhulu u-petrol kanti asheshe aphele.
Bheka ukuthi amasondo emoto yakho aphela ngokulinganayo yini wenze i-wheel alignment uma engapheli ngokulinganayo. Lokho kuzokongela imali enhle ku-petrol nasemasondweni.
Bheka ukuthi u-petrol udleka kangakanani. Ngaleyo ndlela uzokwazi ukubona uma kunenkinga uyilungise.
Ungayidumisi imoto bese uhlala kuyona ngoba uthi uyayifudumeza. Yidumise uyiyeke imizuzwana embalwa ukuze uwoyela ukhuphuke ufike kuzo zonke izindawo enjinini bese ushayela ngokuyincenga igeji layo lokushisa lize likhombise ukuthi injini isishisa ngokufanelekile.
Kugweme ukuhamba ume. Uma i-robot oya kulona libomvu ungayi kulona usundiza ngoba kuzodingeka ukuba usebenzise ama-brakes ukuze ume - lokho kumosha u-petrol. Phakamisa unyawo lwakho lwangakwesokudla kancane ulwehlise ngokuluncenga uye erobhothini kahle ulinde ukuba likuvulele.
Izindlela ozihambayo zihlele ngendlela osebenzisa ngayo u-petrol. Uhambo olude emgwaqweni onguthela wayeka ludla u-petrol omncane kunohambo olufushane oluhamba phakathi kwezakhiwo.
Ungawunyatheli ngamawala u-petrol. Wunyathele kahle uwuyeke uma indawo yehlela.
Khuphula amagiya ngokushesha ingaze iphelelwe amandla injini. Uma amandla enjini ehla endaweni ekhuphukayo, faka igiya eliphansi unyathele u-petrol ngendlela obuwunyathele ngayo kunokuthi uwunyathele kakhulu.
Shayela uvale amafasitela.
Uma unemoto eyiveni engamboziwe, yimboze ngoseyili. Uma ungenawo useyili, shayela imoto yakho uyivule indawo engemuva uma uthatha uhambo olude ngoba uma ivaliwe ibamba umoya.
Ungazifaki izinto okuthiwa zonga u-petrol ngoba azisebenzi! Ungawuhlanganisi u-petrol wakho nezinto esizibona zikhangisiwe - abakhiqizi bawo bachitha imali eningi bewuthuthukisa ukuze usebenze kahle.
Abanye bethu bathanda ukushayela bagijime kanti sikhona isikhathi nendawo yokukwenza lokho. Ukugijima kukufaka ezindlekweni zika-petrol nezamathikithi enhlawulo kanti kuyingozi.[z]
