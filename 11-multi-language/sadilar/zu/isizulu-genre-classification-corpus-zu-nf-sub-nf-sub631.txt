ABE-MAGEU NUMBER 1 BETHULELA ABESIMAME BAKULELI ISIGQOKO!
NGO-AUGUST mhlaka 9 wuSuku lukaZwelonke labesimame eSA. Kulo nyaka sigubha iminyaka engu-51 abesiMame baMashela iNkululeko nokuyinto eyenzeka ngomhlaka 9 August 1956 ePitoli. Ekukhumbuleni igalelo lalaba besimame ababeka izimpilo zabo engcupheni yejele, ukuboshelwa emizini yabo nokuvalwa imilomo, abeMageu Number 1 bahalalisela bonke abesimame kule nyanga.
“Wathint’ abafazi, wathint’ imbokodo,” - lesi yisona siqubulo sethu sale nyanga.
Emcimbini weBONA Empowerment Club ngo-August, abeMageu Number 1 bazokube behlonipha abesimame abakhombisa inqubekela phambili kusukela ngo-1956 abakwazile futhi ukuba basebenzise amathuba akhona eSouth Africa eNtsha.
Lawa mathuba abandakanya amabhizinisi, ezikaqedisizungu nezobuciko.
NGOMHLAKA 9 August izwe lonke lizobe lizinikele entuthukweni yabantu besimame kuwo wonke amazinga omphakathi. Gubha ukuthuthuka kwakho ngenkomishi yetiye nalezi zibiliboco zokumnandi okunoshokoleti. Khumbula ukuthi iSasko Flour yenziwa ngokolo ocolisekile wekhwalithi ephambili wakuleli nakwamanye amazwe.
1. Hlanganisa ushokoleti, imajarini, i-vanilla essence nezinhlayiya zekhofi esosipanini ukuncibilikise kancane emlilweni omncane. Kugudluze emlilweni ukubeke eceleni ukuze kuphole. 2. Shaya amaqanda noshukela kuze kushube bese ukufaka phakathi engxubeni kashokoleti esipholile. 3. Kufake phakathi kufulawa osefiwe namakinati. Thela ingxube kwi-baking pan egcotshiwe engu-27x17cm. 4. Kubhake ngezinga elingu-180°C imizuzu engu-25-30, ingxube ize iqine emaceleni kodwa ithambe phakathi nendawo. Kupholise ukusike izingcezu.
