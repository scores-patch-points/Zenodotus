ukhilimu ophehliwe namakinati aqotshiwe okuhlobisa
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini hlanganisa irayisi, ubisi noshukela. Kubilise ulokhu ukugovuza. Yehlisa umlilo ukumboze kugxabhe imizuzu engu-25-30 irayisi lize lithambe kumunceke lonke uketshezi. (Faka amanzi amancane uma kunesidingo.)
2. Kugudluze emlilweni ubondele phakathi okwamaqanda okuphuzi. Kubeke eceleni kuphole.
3. I-Sauce. Kujeke hlanganisa i-cornflour nesipuni esisodwa esinguhhafu wamanzi ukubeke eceleni.
4. Faka amanzi asele epanini. Yekela ushukela uncibilike. Kubilise kugxabhe imizuzu emihlanu. (Ungakugovuzi) Faka ujamu nengxube ye-cornflour. Ilokhu ukugovuza kuze kushube
i-sauce.
5. Faka ukhilimu kwi-rice pudding ebandayo. Kuphake ezindishini eziyisithupha ze-pudding kuthi phezulu uthele i-sauce ngesipuni. Kubandise ihora ngaphambi kokukuseva. Kufake ukhilimu phezulu. Kuvuvuzele ngamakinati ukuseve.
