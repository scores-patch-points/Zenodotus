Insimbi!
INSIMBI iyi-alloy, okusho ukuthi yenziwe ngama-substances amabili nangaphezulu. Isithako esimqoka kakhulu ensimbini yi-iron. Kodwa yizithako ezinjenge-manganese, tungsten noma i-carbon ezenza ukuba insimbi iqine.
Insimbi eyakhiqizwa kuqala yayiliqhamukisa e-East Africa kanti yakhiqizwa ngo- 1400BC. Kwaqala phakathi kwe-19th Century ukuthi insimbi ibe ngumkhiqizo ongabizi.
Namuhla insimbi ingumkhiqizo ojwayeleke kakhulu emhlabeni kanti isetshenziswa emabhilidini, emathuluzini, ezimotweni nasezimpahleni zikagesi.
ElaseChina lihamba phambili ekukhiqizweni kwensimbi njengoba likhiqiza u-one-third wensimbi ekhiqizwa emhlabeni wonke, kulandele iJapan, Russia neMelika.
