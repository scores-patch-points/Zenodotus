NGONWABU
u Unwabu luyinzalo yezibankwa. Igama lesiNgisi unwabu olubizwa ngalo elithi "chameleon" lisuselwe kwelesiGreek elisho "Ibhubesi lomhlaba", (earth lion).
u Unwabu lwande e-Africa kanti ubude balo bungu-2,8cm kuye ku-68cm.
u Izinzwani zalo ezinhlanu zihlangana zibe mbili noma zibe ntathu kanti unyawo ngalunye lubukeka sengathi yipheya lezindlawu. Lokho kwenza ukuba lubambelele kalula emagatsheni. u Uzwane ngalunye lunozipho olubukhali olulusiza uma luhamba esihlahleni.
u Izimanzi zibeka amaqanda emva kwamasonto amathathu kuya kwayisithupha zimithi. Uma amaqanda eselungele ukubekwa, unwabu lumba umgodi ojule ngo-10cm kuya ku-30cm. Uma seluwumbile lubeka amaqanda alo emgodini. Emva kwalokho liyawugqiba luyishiye leyo ndawo.
u Kokunye unwabu lubeka amaqanda angu-100 ngesikhathi.
AMEHLO... Izinkophe ezingezansi zihlangene nezingenhla kanti kusala isikhala esincane esanele ukuthi libone kahle. Unwabu luyakwazi ukunyakazisa amehlo alo abone izinto ezimbili ezihlukene ngesikhathi esisodwa.
ULIMI... Ulimi lonwabu lude (kokunye luba lude ludlule umzimba). Ngaphambili lunenjimbilili okuyiyona edoba okuzodliwa. Indawo engaphambili ingumsipha oyibhola. Uma unwabu selubambe elikufunayo, ulimi lubuyela emlomeni bese luhlafuna ukudla kwalo. Unwabu oluncane luyakwazi ukudla iqhwagi noma isinambuzane esikhulu.
IMIBALA YONWABU... Unwabu ludume ngokushintshashintsha umbala walo. Umbala uchaza isimo salo ngokomzimba nangokomoya kanti yiwona obalulekile ekuxhumaneni kwalezi zilwane. Unwabu alukwazi ukushintsha umbala walo ufane nendawo olukuyona njengoba abaningi becabanga!

Isiphepho siba wumoya onamandla oxhumana nefu elibizwa nge-cumulonimbus nendawo engaphandle emhlabeni.

Umlingo we-Microwave
SEKUPHELE iminyaka engu-60 kulo nyaka i-microwave yaba khona futhi yenza izimpilo zabantu zibe lula.
UDkt Percy LeBaron Spencer, nokunguye ongumsunguli we-microwave wazifundisa ngokwakhe ukuba ngunjiniyela esasebenza eRaytheon Corporation ngesikhathi enza i-microwave.
Kwakungesikhathi ayehlola khona ukusebenza kweshubhu le-vacuum entsha lapho athola khona ukuthi ushokoledi ayewufake ephaketheni wawusuncibilikile. Lokhu kwamholela ekutheni ahlole umshini wama-popcorn. Emva kokuba exhume umshini wama-popcorn, wabukisisa ngeso elibanzi ebona ama-popcorn ephekeka aze agcwala phansi endlini ayesebenzela kuyona.
Okunye akwenza ngonyaka ka-1945 no-1946 kwamholela ekwenzeni i-microwave yokuqala eyadayiswa ngo-1947. Yayinesisindo esingu-340kg ingaphezu kuka-1,5m ukuphakama. Yayisetshenziswa kakhulu emakhishini emikhumbi nasema-restaurant.
Phezu kwezinkoleloze ezishiwo nge-microwave, kodwa iqiniso wukuthi i-microwave ingenye yezimpahla zikagesi ezisetshenziswa ekhishini ezisebenza ngokukhulu ukushesha uma zisetshenziswe ngendlela eyiyo - kusho uLouis Bloomfield, onguphrofesa wesayensi eNyuvesi yaseVirginia eMelika.
Ulwazi silwethulelwe ngabe-Whirlpool. Vakashela ku: www.whirlpool.co.za uma ufuna imininingwane ngezitolo zabo, izeluleko namaresiphi.[z]
