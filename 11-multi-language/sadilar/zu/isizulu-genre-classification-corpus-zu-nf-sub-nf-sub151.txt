Teen vs youth
E-SA sekufana nosiko ukukhuluma nge-"youth" njengento ehlukile kubantu abasha - kodwa kokubili kubhekana nezinkinga ezifanayo.
I-"youth" ikuchaza kokubili njengokukhula - phakathi kwesikhathi sobusha nobudala - kanjalo nokukhula nangendlela yosiko.
Ngendlela yosiko, "i-youth" ibiza imali eshisiwe, njengoba eminingi imikhiqizo eyenziwayo, yenzelwa ukuba ithengwe yiyo.
