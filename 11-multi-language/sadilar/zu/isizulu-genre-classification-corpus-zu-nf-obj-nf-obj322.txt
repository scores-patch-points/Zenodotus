OKUMNANDI
UMSOMBULUKO
HOT TEXAN CHICKEN WITH FRIED RICE
Chicken
* 2 izingcezu zenkukhu
* 1/4 inkomishi (60ml) uwoyela
* usawoti nophepha
* uwoyela
* Texan Spice Rub
* 1 isipuni (15ml) ama-peppercorns
* 1 isipuni (15ml) i-whole coriander
* 1 isipuni (15ml) i-Aromat
* 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-turmeric
* 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-ground ginger
* 2 upelepele obomvu okhishwe izinhlamvu waqotshwa
Fried Rice
* 2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
* 1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
* 3 amaqanda ashayiwe
* 2 izinkomishi (500ml) irayisi eliphekiwe
* 1 inkomishi (250ml) ama-frozen peas
1. Inkukhu. Bilisa imizuzu engu-15. Kugudluze ukuyeke kuphole ukukhuhle ngowoyela ukunonge.
2. Texan Spice Rub. Epanini hlanganisa ama-peppercorns, i-coriander, i-Aromat, i-turmeric, i-ginger nopelepele ukushise (ngaphandle kukawoyela) kuze kuhlangane. Kugudluze emlilweni ukuyeke kuphole ukufake esikhwameni sepulasitiki ukugaye ngengaphansi lebhodlela le-spice noma i-pestle ne-mortar.
3. Khuhla i-spice enkukhwini ukulungise ngayizolo ukuyeke ubusuku bonke.
4. Epanini shisa uwoyela uthose inkukhu kuphekeke. Kubeke eceleni ukugcine kufudumele.
5. Kulo lelo pani, shisa uwoyela uthose u-anyanisi uze uthambe. Faka amaqanda ukugovuze umzuzu owodwa. Faka irayisi no-peas uqhubeke ukushise. Kuseve nenkukhu efudumele.
Kwanela ababili
ULWESIBILI
BOEREWORS SUPPER
* 2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-pasta emfushane (macaroni, screws, shells)
* 1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
* 750g ama-boerewors
* 1 uphepha obomvu noma o-green okhishwe izinhlamvu waqotshwa
* 4 ukherothi ocwecwiwe wenziwa imicu
* 4 amazambane acwecwiwe aqotshwa
* 2 u-anyanisi osikwe izingcezu
* 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) u-curry powder
* 2 utamatisi oqotshiwe
* 1 inkomishi (250ml) i-chicken stock
* usawoti nophepha (ngokuthanda kwakho)
1. Bilisa i-pasta emanzini anosawoti ize ithambe. Icwenge uyibeke eceleni.
2. Epanini shisa uwoyela uthose ama-boerewors aze aphekeke. Kukhiphe epanini ukusike izingcezu ezingu-2cm. Kubeke eceleni.
3. Kulo lelo pani, thosa uphepha, ukherothi, amazambane no-anyanisi - u-anyanisi uze uthambe.
4. Govuzela phakathi u-curry powder ukuthose umzuzu. Yehlisa umlilo ufake utamatisi. Kugxabhise kushube usosi. Faka i-stock ukupheke kuthambe ama-veg.
5. Phindisela ama-boerewors epanini, ukugxabhise, ukunonge. Hlanganisa i-pasta nama-boerewors, ukuseve ne-green salad enoshizi ogreythiwe iCheddar.
Kwanela abangu-2
ULWESITHATHU
QUICK AND EASY THAI CHICKEN
* 1 x 410g ithini le-coconut milk
* 1 isipuni (15ml) i-curry paste ebomvu noma e-green
* 8-10 izifuba zenkukhu eziqotshiwe
* 1 u-anyanisi osikwe izingcezu
* 1 inkomishi (250ml) u-green beans oqotshiwe
* 2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-basmati, jasmine noma irayisi elijwayelekile
* 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) i-desiccated coconut
1. Epanini bilisa i-coconut milk ukubondele kwi-curry paste.
2. Faka inkukhu no-anyanisi ukugxabhise imizuzu eyishumi inkukhu ize iphekeke.
3. Faka u-green beans ukugxabhise eminye imizuzu embalwa. (Ubhontshisi kufanele uklamuzele.)
4. Pheka irayisi ulandele imithetho esephaketheni ufake usawoti.
5. Epanini (elingenawoyela), shisa i-desiccated coconut ize ibe nsunjwana. Kubondele erayisini. Phaka ingxube yenkukhu erayisini ngokwahlukanakwezindishi.
Kwanela abane
ULWESINE
BUTTERNUT AND PILCHARD CASSEROLE
Lentil Rice
1 inkomishi (250ml) irayisi eli-brown noma elimhlophe
* 1 inkomishi (250ml) i-brown lentils
* 1 ilitha yamanzi
* 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) usawoti
* Pilchard Casserole
* uwoyela wokuthosa
* 2 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
* 2 izigaxa zikagaligi ogayiwe
* 1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
* 5 ama-baby marrows aqotshiwe
* 1 i-butternut esikwe izigaxa
* 2 amazambane acwecwiwe aqotshwa
* 1 x 410g ithini likatamatisi
* usawoti nophepha
* 1x 410g inhlanzi esethinini esikwe izigaxa
* 1 isixha se-dhania leaves eqotshiwe
1. Lentil Rice. Epanini hlanganisa zonke izithako. Kumboze kugxabhe cishe imizuzu engu-20 irayisi nama-lentils kuze kuthambe namanzi aze amunceke.
2. Pilchard Casserole. Epanini shisa uwoyela uthose u-anyanisi nogaligi kuze kuthambe. Bondela ku-curry powder.
3. Faka ama-marrows, i-butternut namazambane. Kumboze kugxabhe.
4. Faka utamatisi, ukunonge, ukumboze kugxabhe kuze kuthambe amazambane ne-butternut. Bondela phakathi inhlanzi ukushise.
5. Faka i-dhania kwi-casserole ngaphambi kokukuphaka kwiLentil Rice.
