Beef In Stout
Beef
□ 1kg i-beef cube
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) ufulawa
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
□ 2 u-anyanisi ocwecwiwe
□ 1 indukwana ye-celery eqotshiwe
□ 2 izinkomishi (500ml) stouté
□ 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) ushukela
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) i-tomato purée
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) iWorcestershire sauce
Amadombolo
□ 100g i-self-raising flour
□ 50g imajarini
□ 2-3 izipuni (30-45ml) amanzi abandayo
1. I-Beef. Faka inyama nofulawa kupulasitiki ukushukuze kunindeke inyama. Epanini thosa inyama, uyikhiphe uthose u-anyanisi.
2. Faka i-celery yenyama, i-stout, ushukela, i-tomato purée neWorcestershire sauce. Kuthele kwi-casserole dish. Kubhake ngo-160oC ihora, inyama ize ithambe. Faka amadombolo.
3. Amadombolo. Endishini khuhlela imajarini kufulawa kufane nezimvuthu zesinkwa.
4. Faka amanzi ukwenza inhlama. Kubumbe amadombolo angu-10 ukubeke kwi-casserole.
5. Buyisela i-casserole kuhhavini, ungambozi imizuzu engu-20.

Beef Pot With Cheese Dumplings
Beef
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
□ 2 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
□ 750g i-stewing beef
□ 5 ama-teaspoon (25ml) ufulawa
□ 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) ushukela
□ 1 x 340ml ithini iCoca-Cola
Amadombolo
□ 1 inkomishi (250ml) i-self-raising flour
□ 1/4 inkomishi (60ml) imajarini encibilikisiwe
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) ushizi ogreythiwe
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) amanzi
□ usawoti nophepha
1. I-Beef. Epanini thosa u-anyanisi uwukhiphe bese uthosa i-beef ufake ufulawa ukugovuze.
2. Faka ushukela ulokhu uthela iCoca-Cola, uze ufane no-anyanisi ophekiwe.
3. Kubeke kwi-casserole dish ukumboze ukupheke ngo-160oC ihora noma inyama ize ithambe. Faka amadombolo.
4. Amadombolo. Endishini hlanganisa izithako. Zehlukanise amaqoqwana angu-8 uwabumbe abe amabholana.
5. Beka amadombolo phezu kwe-casserole ufake uketshezi oluningi. Kubuyisele kuhhavini ungakumbozile imizuzu engu-20-30.
