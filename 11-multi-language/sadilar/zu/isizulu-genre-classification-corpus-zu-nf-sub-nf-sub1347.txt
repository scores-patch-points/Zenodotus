WUSUKU oluhle kuGerry Rantseli - angacwecwa i-apula alidle. Uma usuku lwakhe luluhle ngokucwecwa i-apula, ngabe uma lulubi luba njani" "Nginenhlanhla ngoba azisekho izinsuku ezingangiphathi kahle," kusho yena emamatheka.
"Ngike ngahlushwa wukulunywa okwakwenza ngihluleke nawukuya emsebenzini."
U-Gerry wayeneminyaka eko-20 esanda kuzakhela igama kwiTV ngesikhathi eqala ukuhlushwa wukulunywa. Kwathatha iminyaka ukuthi akuqonde kahle okwakumphethe waze wezwa ngodokotela ukuthi umuntu oyedwa kwabayisithupha ohlushwa yisifo esibizwa nge-irritable bowel syndrome, noma i-IBS - wayengomunye walabo bantu.
I-IBS ibalwa kulezo zifo eziba yimbangela yokuthi abantu balove emsebenzini kodwa ngokocwaningo, abantu abathathu kwabane abane-IBS abakaze bathole ukwelashwa.
Abanye bathi i-IBS yi- "spastic colon," igama elisho ukufinyela komsipha - kanti kuningi ongakwenza ukuze usizakale.
Ebantwini abanjengoGerry, le nkinga ingaxazululeka ngokwenza izinto ezinjengokucwecwa i-apula ngoba lokho kusiza ngokususa i-fibre eyenza ukuba ulunywe.
Gwema ukuhlinzwa!
"Iziguli zivame ukushaywa wuvalo uma zilunywa zicabange ukuthi zinomdlavuza - kokunye isiguli sicabange ukuthi sinesifo senhliziyo uma kwenzeka kuqaqamba isifuba nemilenze," kusho uDokotela uKeith Pettengell, onguchwepheshe wezifo ezithinta amathumbu eThekwini.
"Odokotela bavame ukuhlola isiguli ukuze bathole ukuthi aziphethwe yilezo zifo. Kodwa uma kutholakale ukuthi isiguli sine-IBS, singazelapha ngaphandle kokuhlinzwa."
Kodwa ngeshwa, akusibo bonke odokotela abakwaziyo lokho. Njengoba izinhlungu zike zedlulele esiswini, kwesingezansi nakwezinye izindawo, odokotela bavele bathi isiguli asihlinzwe kungadingekile.
"Yisifo engisibiza ngeWandering Scarred Patient Syndrome lesi," kusho uDokotela uPettengell. "Ocwaningweni olwenziwa ezigulini ezingu-145 ezine-IBS, ziyingxenye yokuthathu okwadingeka ukuba zikhishwe i-appendix kanti abesimame kwadingeka ukuba bakhishwe isibeletho. Kodwa lokho akwenzanga mehluko enkingeni ezazinayo."
"Ebantwini abayikhulu abahambela ama-workshop eNovartis IBS engangingusihlalo wawo ngonyaka odlule, bahlanu kuphela abasizwa wukuhlinzwa," kusho uGerry.
Abanye baba namahloni okukhuluma ngezimpawu zalesi sifo - uhudo, ukuzingcolisela nokuqunjelwa ukwenza isibonelo - kangangoba bagcina bengayi kodokotela baye isimo sesisibi kakhulu.
"Bahlale befuna ukuba sendaweni enendlu yangasese," kusho uDokotela uPetengell. "Abacabangi ukusuka emakhaya."
Ukulwa ne-IBS
U-Gerry Rantseli akafunanga ukuba izimpawu zalesi sifo zize zibe mandla kakhulu. Washeshe waphuthuma kudokotela ukuze athole izimpendulo ayezidinga.
"I-IBS yangivula amehlo," kusho yena. "Ngihlale ngifuna ukuba izinto zenzeke ngokushesha - ngangingenasikhathi se-IBS kodwa futhi ngangingafuni ukuphila ngemithi yokuyelapha.
"Ngachitha isikhathi ngifunda ngami - ngathola nokuthi esingezansi sami sigqilazwa wukukhandleka kwengqondo yami. Ngathola nokuthi lokho kukhandleka kwengqondo kuzoxabana nokudla ukudla okungenamsoco."
Kwaphela isikhathi esingaphezu konyaka uGerry ezama ukudambisa izimpawu zalesi sifo ngokushintsha indlela ayephila ngayo.
Akubanga lula ukuyijwayela indlela okwakumele adle ngayo ngoba wayevame ukudla irayisi elimhlophe, amazambane nobhatata, isitshulu, isiteki, inkukhu, nenhlanzi engazwani nesinkwa, utamatisi nezithelo.
Ukuzimisela kukaGerry kwamsiza ngoba wagwema ukwelashwa ngendlela engafanele.
NgokukaDokotela uPettengell, i-IBS yisifo esingalawuleka ngokushintsha indlela odla ngayo (bheka kwi-panel) ungazigqilazi uma uzivocavoca, wenze izinto ezinjengokuhamba noma i-yoga. Njengoba kuke kwenzeke ukuba uphinde uhlaselwe yi-IBS ikakhulu uma ingqondo ikhandlekile, kungenzeka kudingeke ukuba welashwe isikhashana.
"Amaphilisi ayasiza" kusho uDokotela uPettengell, "kodwa awaselaphi lesi sifo. Asiza uma uxineke kakhulu."
Kukhona izindaba ezimnandi kwabangu-15 percent abane-IBS. Kungenzeka ukuthi baphathwe yilesi sifo ngenxa yamagciwane angena esiswini akhandla umgudu wokudla.
"NgokukaDokotela uPettengell, "lolu hlobo lwe-IBS lungelapheka isiguli singaphinde sihlushwe yilona."
