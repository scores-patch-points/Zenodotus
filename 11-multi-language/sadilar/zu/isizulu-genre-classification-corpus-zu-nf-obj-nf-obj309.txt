NGENZA izifundo zami ze-diploma ye-resource management. Nginesifiso sokucobelelana nabanye abacabanga ukuzofunda esikhungweni semfundo esifana nalesi ngonyaka ozayo.
Okokuqala wukuba bagxile ezifundweni zabo. Lokhu kungaba lula kakhulu uma ukhetha abangani abanezinhloso ezifana nezakho.
Yibo abazokukhuthaza lapho uzwa unokwenqena futhi bakusize uma umsebenzi usumningi.
Okubalulekile ngaphezu kwalokhu, wukuphasa izifundo zakho. Ungakwazi futhi ukuthola isikhathi sokuzithokozisa, kodwa ungalibali ukuthi usenyuvesi ngenxa yokuzokwakha ikusasa lakho eliqhakazile.
Ngakhoke gxila ezifundweni zakho - ngoba yizo ezizokwakhela ikusasa elihle.
Enyuvesi akufani nasesikoleni esijwayelekile - nguwe okufanele uzilawule ngokwenhlonipho, usebenzise noma yiliphi ithuba elingakuphumelelisa.
Okwesibili, kufanele uzithathele amanothi ngokwakho, uwafunde emva kwanoma yikuphi ukwethulwa kwezifundo. Lokhu kuzokwehlisela umthwalo kuthi lapho kufika izivivinyo zifike usuzilindele.
Okwesithathu - kubalulekile ukukwazi ukulawula isikhathi sakho. Lokhu kusho ukuzibekela isikhathi esanele sokufunda kanjalo nesokuzithokozisa.
MJ, Masia

Hloniphani othisha bethu
OTHISHA ababengifundisa ngiyohlale ngiziqhenya kakhulu ngabo. Ngiye ngiphatheke kabi uma ngizwa abazali abaningi besola othisha uma izingane zabo zingaqhubi kahle esikoleni.
Abazali kufanele bakhumbule ukuthi - isibonelo esihle abasikhombisayo emakhaya, yiso esingakhuthaza nezingane zabo ukuba ziqhube kahle esikoleni.
Uma ingane yakho ingaqhubi kahle esikoleni, kufanele uzibuze ukuthi wena uyikhuthaza kangakanani ukuba ibambelele ezifundweni zayo.
Othisha basiza ekwakheni ikusasa lethu, ngakhoke badinga ukuhlonishwa. Abazali kufanele bayeke ukugxeka othisha kodwa bagxile ezibophweni zabo zokufundisa izingane inhlonipho.
Beauty Jojozi, Mount Frere

Umnyango wezemfundo wasethembisa imfundo yamahhala - kungakho ngamangala ngesikhathi ngicelwa ukuba ngikhokhe imali yesikole neyokuthenga izincwadi.
Lapho ngihlala khona awekho amabhasi athutha izingane zesikole uma ziya noma sezibuya esikoleni. Ngesikhathi ngifunda eGoli, lolu sizo lwalutholakala mahhala.
Kuyacaca ukuthi uhulumeni awuzinakile nakancane izindawo ezisemaphandleni.
N. Makhi, Viedgesville

Kungani ningabi namahloni ezenzo zenu? Abantu abadala kufanele bayekwe bathokozele imali yabo yempesheni ngokuthula ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa.
Masiyekeni ukulahla abantu abadala!
Eunice Mapanga, e-mail

Kunzima ukufuna umsebenzi
Ngokwezibalo ezisemthethweni, cishe bangu-7 million abantu abangaqashiwe kuleli.
Lokhu yikho okuholela ekutheni abantu abaningi babulawe yindlala.
Into abaningi abangayicabangi wukuthi: ukufuna umsebenzi, wumsebenzi onzima futhi ongakhokheli. Kunalokho, kudla wena imali.
Okokuqala, kudingeka uthenge amaphephandaba ukuze ubheke umsebenzi ngaphansi kwengosi ye-classifieds.
Okwesibili, kufanele ube ne-CV eyenziwe kahle nokuyinto ebiza imali - kanjalo futhi noma usuyithumela nge-fax lapho kunomsebenzi khona.
Uzodinga futhi ama-photocopies e-ID yakho nokunye okubalulekile.
Uma ube nenhlanhla yokubizelwa i-interview, kuzodingeka imali yokufinyelela kuleyo nkampani - uma uzohamba ngezinto zokuthutha.
Kulabo abafuna umsebenzi, engingakusho wukuthi: lezi yizikhathi ezinzima, kodwa ngeke kugcine zona.
Ungalilahli ithemba, izinto zizolunga.
XC, Phase 5
