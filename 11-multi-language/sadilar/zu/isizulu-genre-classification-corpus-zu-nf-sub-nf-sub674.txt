Uma kubekhona ukulwa ngenxa yobudedengu, loyo olimele noma olahlekelwe kufanele athole amademeshe nokuyinto ekhishwa ngumuntu owonile.
Ukuze kutholakale amademeshe, umuntu okunguye ofake icala lokuthola amademeshe (i-plaintiff) kumele akhiphe ubufakazi bokuthi owalimaza (kulokhu oyi-defendant) wayebudedengu. Ukwenza lokhu, kumele kube khona ubufakazi obukhombisayo ukuthi kwabakhona ubudedengu.
Kungumsebenzi wanoma yimuphi umuntu ukunakekela omunye. Ukwenza isibonelo, umuntu opaka imoto yakhe endaweni ephakeme, kungumsebenzi wakhe ukunakekela abanye ngokuba agweme imoto yakhe ingehli ilimaze impahla.
Kulokhu kufanele umnini wemoto noma umshayeli aqikelele ukuthi amabhuleki ayasebenza.
Kulolu daba lwakho kukhona izinto okufanele zibhekwe, (njengesenzo noma inqubo yokuphawula lapho umuntu ethatha izinqumo ezisusela kokwenzeka.)
