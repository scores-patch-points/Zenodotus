Siza edolobheni langakini kulo nyaka
UBONA nabaxhasi bawo be-2005/2006 BONA Empowerment Club bazokwenza omkhulu umehluko empilweni yakho.
Kulo nyaka sizokuba nemibuthano nama-workshop kulo lonke elaseSouth Africa ezindaweni ezifana naseMthatha, Witbank, Mafikeng, Nelspruit naseWelkom. Amalungu eBONA Empowerment Club eThekwini, Bloemfontein, eGoli, ePitoli nasePort Elizabeth nawo azokwazi ukuhambela le mibuthano nama-workshop emadolobheni awo.
Siyophinde sibe nama-workshop ayogxila kakhulu ekufuyweni kwezinkukhu nasekutshaleni ama-vegetable, ukuthwetshulwa kwezithombe, ibhizinisi lokudla (catering), ukubhasobha izingane, ukuziqalela i-spaza shop sakho nokunye okuyonihlaba umxhwele. Ama-workshop abeBake and Cook for Profit azoqhubeka zinyanga zonke.
Imiklomelo eyaziwa ngokuthi yiMageu Number 1 Awards yenzelwe ukukhuthaza ithuthukise amabhizinisi amancane ngaphansi kweSMME. Ihambisana nemali engu-R2 000 kubo bonke abangu-12 abafinyelele kumafayineli. Owokuqala, owesibili nowesithathu kwabafinyelele kumafayineli bayothola R15 000, R7 000 noR4 000 ngokulandelana kwabo.
Ngaphezu kwalokho, abawinile abathathu ukuphuma phambili bayothola umklomelo oqoshiwe kanye nezitifiketi ezifreymiwe. Bonke abayongenela lolu hlelo bayothola izitifiketi ezibhaliwe.
Abafinyelele kumafayineli zinyanga zonke bayovezwa kuBONA kusukela ngoJanuary 2006.

UNGAZIPHILISA KANJANI EMBONINI YOKUDLA?
I-ILLOVO yethenjwe yizizukulwane zabantu bakuleli ekukhiqizeni oshukela nama-syrups acolisekile. Abe-Illovo bebambisene nabakwaBONA babephambili ohlelweni lokuthuthukisa umphakathi iBake and Cook for Profit ekuqaleni konyaka odlule nokuyinto eyaba nomphumela omuhle wempumelelo. Inhloso yabo kwakungukusiza umphakathi ukwazi ukuzisungulela amabhizinisi embonini yokudla nokuyinto abangaphumelelanga kuyona kuphela, kodwa futhi basiza abaningi ezweni lonkana ukuba bakwazi ukuzisungulela amabhizinisi abo. Ezinye zalezi zindaba zempumelelo zizokhishwa kuBONA ekuhambeni konyaka ukuze kukhuthazeke abaningi bathole ithuba lokuzakhela imali ngokudla.
Bheka imikhiqizo ye-Illovo eluhlaza nemhlophe ube nesiqiniseko sokuthi ikhona ohlwini lwezimpahla zakho ozozithenga zekhwalithi ephambili.
I-ILLOVO isebenzisa ushukela ocolisekile wekhwalithi ephambili.
