Sonke sinecala
OMUNYE wodokotela bengqondo wathi: "Uma umuntu eqala ehlangana nobugebengu, uyabuzonda, kodwa uma kunokuxhumana nabo, uyabenza agcine ngokubuthanda."
Izinga lobugebengu ezweni lethu liphezulu ngokumangalisayo - okubuhlungu wukuthi ubugebengu , yilobo obuhambisana nodlame.
Mina ngokwami ngibona ukuthi: "Sonke sinecala - uma ngithi 'sonke' ngisho uhulumeni, amaphoyisa nomphakathi wonke wezwe lethu.
Uhulumeni unecala ngoba kubukeka ungazimisele nakancane ukwenza okuthile ukuqeda ubugebengu.
Iningi lezikhulu zamaphoyisa ezweni lethu liyazinikela ekutheni libambisane nabombutho weSAPS. Benza konke okusemandleni abo phezu kwezingqinamba ababhekene nazo.
Kodwa futhi kukhona labo abasebenzisana nezigebengu ngokuba bantshontshe amadokodo, badayise ngezibhamu nokunye okuningi okubhebhethekisa isimo sobugebengu emphakathini wethu.
Amalungu omphakathi akhuthaza ubugebengu ngokuthenga izimpahla ezintshontshiwe, angazibiki izenzo zobugebengu ezikhulwini zamaphoyisa.
Masilweni nobugebengu ngokudalula izigebengu emphakathini wethu.
Mphikeleni Mahlangu, Phase 5
Masikhulume ngocansi
Abazali bethu benza konke okusemandleni abo ukusifundisa ukwehlukanisa okuhle nokubi. Kodwa futhi basenamahloni okuxoxisana nathi ngezocansi.
Uma uke wakhuluma ngegama "ucansi", kubona usuke ususho enkulu inhlamba.
Kodwa noma kuphi sihlale sizwa abanye abazali bekhuthaza ukukhuluma ngokukhululeka ngezocansi. Kungani bona besaba"
Bazibandakanya nalokhu lapho sesisezinkingeni..
M Moya, Port Elizabeth
Kubo bonke abantu bakuleli

Nganginesifiso sokuqhuba izifundo zami, kodwa ngeshwa umndeni wami wawungenayo imali. Angizange ngililahle ithemba, ngasebenza kanzima. Namuhla sengingusomabhizinisi wesimame onenkulu impumelelo.
Ngisho nangesikhathi abangani bami bengigcona bezama ukungidukisa, ngaqhubeka. Ibhizinisi lami ladlondlobala lisuka ekwenzeni amabhengela obuhlalu kuya ekwenzeni amakhethenisi, izindwangu zetafula nezikhwama.
Manje senginenkampani yami yokwakha.
Yekani ukulokhu nikhala bantu bakithi - vukani!
AZ, Dibeng

Yekani ukuxhaphaza izimoto zikahulumeni
Njalo ngihlale ngibona bexhaphaza izimoto zikahulumeni. Kukhona okudingeka ukuba kwenziwe ngalokhu.
Kujwayelekile ukubona abasebenzi bakahulumeni besebenzisa izimoto zikahulumeni bezisebenzisela izidingo zabo. Lezi zimoto zenzelwe ukusiza umphakathi, kodwa amaphoyisa azisebenzisela izidingo zawo kuphela njengokuya emaphathini nokuyothenga izidingo zemindeni yawo.
Kudingeka kube khona indlela yokujezisa laba bantu. Nathi njengomphakathi, masikubike ukuxhashazwa kwezimoto zikahulumeni. Lena yimali yethu yentela abayixhaphazayo.
Papikie, Koringpunt

(1) Engqondweni yakho zitshele ukuthi uzophumelela ukufeza amaphupho ozibekele wona. Zibekele imigomo yokuphila ngokwamaphupho akho.

Ngokucabanga nje kuphela, uyakwazi ukubona ukuthi yonke into ilula ukufezeka.
