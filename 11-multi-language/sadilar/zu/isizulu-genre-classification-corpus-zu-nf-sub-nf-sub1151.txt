NgoFebruary 2007, iBurj Dubai yadlula iSears Tower njengebhilidi elinama-floors kunawo wonke emhlabeni.
Okunye okungase kulandele wukuba iBurj Dubai ibe yibhilidi elide kunawo wonke ngokusuka phansi emhlabeni kuye kufinyelele kuma-antenna alo. Okwamanje kuseyiSears Tower yaseChicago engu-527m ukuphakama.
Emva kokwedlula iSears Tower okulandelayo ezimisele ngako wukuba yisakhiwo eside kunazo zonke emhlabeni ngokwedlula iCN Tower yaseToronto eCanada nokuyiyo eseyinde njengamanje njengoba ingu-553m ukuphakama.
Leli bhilidi kulindeleke ukuba liphothulwe futhi kube sekuhlala abantu kulona ngo-2009.
Ngaphakathi kunemihlobiso kaGiorgio Arman - kanti ihhotela i-Armani Hotel (okungelokuqala lalolu hlobo) lizothatha indawo yama-floors angezansi angu-37.
Ama-floors kusuka ku-45 kuya ku-108 azokuba nama-private apartment kuma-floors angu-64 - athengwa wonke engakapheli amahora ayisishiyagalombili eqalile ukudayiswa.
Amahhovisi azogcwalisa ama-floors amanye.
Kuleli bhilidi kuzokuba khona futhi ikheshi elinesivinini kunawo wonke emhlabeni elikhuphuka lehle ibanga elingu-18m ngomzuzwana. Kuzokuba khona namasitebhisi azihambelayo angu-56.
Akukaziwa ukuthi iyothi lapho isiphelile iBurj Dubai ibe ngubude obungakanani nakuba kukholakala ukuthi ingaba ngaphezu kuka-800m ukuphakama.

AMA-CD okuqala enziwa efekthri yakwaPhillips eduze kwaseHanover eGermany ngomhlaka 17 August 1982. Kwathi ngeminyaka yo-1990, ahlahla indlela yokwenziwa kwamaCD-ROM namaDVD.
AbakwaPhillips, okuyibo abasungula amaCD bebambisene nabakwaSony, bacabangela ukuthi asengaphezu kuka-200 billion amaCD asethengisiwe.
AmaCD okuqala ashicilelwa kwaba ngawe-albhamu ethi The Visitors yeqembu lomculo we-pop laseSweden i-Abba.
Empeleni abakwaPhillips babezimisele ngokudonsa abathandi bomculo we-classic bekholwa wukuthi bazokuthanda ukuzwakala komsindo oyikhwalithi bawathokozele namaCD abizayo.
Babeqinisile ngoba kumaCD kuzwakala umsindo omnandi owawukade ufihlekile kumarekhodi angamapuleti.
“Ngesikhathi umdlali wopiyane waseChile u-Claudio Arrau eqopha enye yamaCD okuqala ngaphansi kwePolygram kwatholakala ukuthi wayelokhu eshaya izandla enyakazisa izinyawo ngesikhathi edlala.
