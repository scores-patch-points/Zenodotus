BONA ZULU - May 2007
ULEBO MASHILE usiza ekufakeni ugqozi ekuthuthukiseni izinkondlo zakuleli nokuyinto edingeka kakhulu. Le ntokazi eneminyaka engu-28 isizakhele elikhulu igama njengembongi emakilabhini e-jazz, nakumafestiveli ezobuciko ezweni lonkana.
ULebo waqala ukuzibonakalisa ikakhulu ngo-2005 ngesikhathi izinkondlo zakhe zimakhela udumo zimgqamisa ekulweleni amalungelo abesimame nokulingana ngokobulili.
Wayengomunye wezintokazi ezazisebenzisa amazwi njengesikhali - ezifana noThandiswa Mazwai noSimphiwe Dana emculweni kanye noNtsiki Mazwai noNapo Masheane ekubhalweni kwezinkondlo.
Kodwa kwathi ngesikhathi uSABC 2 enika uLebo uchunge lohlelo lwakhe lwamasonto onke, lokho kwamlethela udumo.
Wayekholelwa ekutheni aphume aye phakathi kwabantu axoxe izindaba zabo ngendlela okwakwenziwa ngayo e-Africa amakhulu ngamakhulu eminyaka, kodwa lokho ekwenza nethimba labamakhamera enzela isizukulwane sanamuhla.
Kungakapholi maseko iL'Attitude yase ibukwa abalandeli abangaphezu kwezigidi ezimbili.
Wathatha ikhamera waya elokishini laseKhayelitsha ngenhloso yokuyoqopha ngemizwa yomama namadodakazi ngokudlwengulwa kwezingane.
Wayekhona futhi kulezo zinkumbi zabantu ezazimemeza ngaphandle kwenkantolo ngesikhathi kuqhubeka icala lokudlwengula likaJacob Zuma.
"Kuyigxathu elikhulu lamasiko ukuba abesimame bakwazi ukukhuluma ngezinto ezinjena. Imibono ngaye naloyo owayembeke icala yayishube kakhulu."
Esinye isiqephu sasimayelana nemboni yomculo we-kwaito - ekutheni kungani yenza abanye abaculi babe osozigidi kodwa abanye ibashiye behlupheka.
Ukufakana imibuzo nabaphambili kule ndima abanjengo-Arthur noDon Laka kwamsiza uLebo ukuba azi ukuthi izinkondlo zakhe naye angazidlondlobalisa kanjani zibe wumsebenzi omkhulu.
"Amagama amakhulu embonini yethu yomculo, awalabo abanomqondo okhaliphile kwezamabhizinisi," kusho yena.
"Basheshe bazilawulele ukuthengiswa komculo wabo basungule nezinkampani zabo. Angisiye umculi, kodwa nami futhi nginabalandeli abafanayo phakathi kwalabo abayizigidi ababukela iMTV neChannel 0.
"Ithuba lokugqama kwiTV angilisebenziselanga ukuthuthukisa izinkondlo zami kodwa ngakwazi ukuthi ngokukhulu ukushesha ngethule incwadi yami yokuqala ngikhiphe neCD yami yokuqala."
Izinkondlo zikaLebo zisebenzisa isigqi se-hip-hop ukukhombisa indlela abuka ngayo impilo emgwaqweni, emalokishini nasezindaweni ezisemaphandleni.
ULebo uthi ayiswelakali into angabhala ngayo, njengoba umphakathi wethu uzabalaza nsuku zonke ngenxa ye-HIV/AIDS, ukuhlukunyezwa kwabesimame nezingane, ukucwasana ngokobulili nenkohlakalo.
Ngokufunwa ngapha nangapha, lokho kumenza akwazi ukuhlela kahle umsebenzi wakhe. "Ngibhala kakhulu lapho ngihleli ezikhumulweni zezindiza ngilindele amabhanoyi," kusho yena.
"Ngiyakugwema ukuya emaphathini ngoba kudla isikhathi esibalulekile sokubhala. Ngiwuthanda kakhulu umsebenzi wami.."[z]

BONA ZULU - May 2007
INKINGA: Ngisebenzela inkampani enkulu yezokuxhumana. Ezinyangeni ezintathu ezedlule ngakhushulelwa esikhundleni esisha kwathi ezinyangeni ezimbili ezalandela ngehliswa. Inkampani yangitshela ukuthi yenza iphutha ngokungikhuphulela esikhundleni.
Ngabatshela ukuthi ukukhushulwa kwami kwakuha-mbisana nokwenyuka komholo okwangenza ngazifaka ezikweletini ezifana nokuthenga ifenisha entsha.
Inkampani yaphoqelela ukuthi ngangazi lesi sikhundla sasingangifa-nele.
Ukukhuluma iqiniso, ngangikwazi lokho njengoba yasho inkampani, kodwa ngasho ukuthi abanye babasebenzi ababili abadala enkampanini basho ukuthi indlela ezosetshenziswa izokwenziwa ingabi nzima kimina.
Sengiyinhlekisa enkampanini njengoba sengilahlekelwe ukukhushulelwa kwami imali kanti futhi sekungumqansa ukukhokha ama-akhawunti ami.
