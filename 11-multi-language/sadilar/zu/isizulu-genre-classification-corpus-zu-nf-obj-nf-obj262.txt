Okumele ukwazi...
nge-cheetah
lIgama elithi "cheetah" lisuselwe kwelesiHindu elisho "okunamabala."
lI-cheetah iyinzalo yamakati azingela ngokusebenzisa ijubane kunokunyonyoba noma ukuzingela eyimihlambi.
lIgijima ngaphezulu kwazo zonke izilwane kanti ibanga elingu-457m ingaligijima ngesivinini esingu-100km ngehora.
lInekhanda elincane elinamehlo aphezulu. Amabala amnyama asuka eceleni ebusweni aye ekhaleni avikela amehlo elangeni ayisize uma izingela noma ibuka kude.
lIsisindo se-cheetah esindala singu-40 kuya ku-65kg. Eyeduna inkudlwana kuneyensikazi kanti inekhanda elikhulu kodwa akulula ukuhlukanisa eyeduna neyensikazi ngokuyibuka nje kuphela.
lUboya be-cheetah buphuzi bunamabala amnyama.
lIyona kuphela engakwazi ukuzifihla kahle izidladla zayo. Zihlala zibonakala kanti yizona ezimelela ngazo uma igijima.
lI-cheetah yisilwane esilimala kalula. Kulolu hlobo lwamakati, iyona kuphela ekwazi ukujwayela indawo entsha.
UTHI UBUWAZI" Ama-cheetah andonda uma edonsa umoya kodwa awabhongi. Amabhubesi, izingwe nama-jaguar ayabhonga andonde uma ekhipha umoya.

UNGAKABI khona umjovo osetshenziswa odokotela uma behlinza iziguli, kwakudingeka ukuba basheshe lapho bezihlinza. Babeshesha ngoba isiguli sasihlinzwa sizwa sisezinhlungwini!
UDokotela uRobert Liston waseLondon wayengomunye wodokotela ababesheshisa uma behlinza iziguli. Kodwa nalokho kuyabulala...
Ekupheleni kweminyaka yo-1800, uListon wabulala abantu abathathu. Babephila emva kokuhlinzwa kodwa bagcine beshonile ngenxa yokuphathwa yizilonda ezingapholi.
UListon wake wanquma iminwe yomsizi wakhe ngephutha. Lowo msizi wakhe naye washona ngenxa yezilonda ezazingapholi. Lo dokotela wabuye wanquma ngephutha i-coat kazakwabo owayebukela ngesikhathi kuhlinzwa - wafa ngenxa yokushaywa wuvalo!

YINTOKAZI enemilenze emide, izinwele ezinde nephimbo elihoshozelayo eligqamile. Lowo nguTina Turner ingqalabutho kwezeRock.

Eseneminyaka engu-16, wathuthela eSt. Louis, eMissouri wazakhela igama ngomdlandla ayeba nawo uma ecula ne-Ike And Tina Turner Revue ngeminyaka yo-1960 no-1970.
Wayenza ezibukwayo esteji ngaleso sikhathi.
Imicu abaduma ngayo efana nethi A Fool In Love, River Deep, Mountain High and I Want to Take You Higher yaba yimbangela yokuthi izingoma zabo zidlalwe kakhulu emisakazweni emhlabeni wonkana.

Ngo-1984, wakhipha i-albhamu ethi Private Dancer. Yaba yimpumelelo enkulu yaqinisekisa ukuthi uTina akasophinde ahlupheke.
NgoDecember 2005, uTina Turner wathola iKennedy Center Honors emva kokwenza ezibukwayo eJohn F. Kennedy Center For The Performing Arts eWashington, DC. Ngokuthola lowo mklomelo wajoyina abaculi abanjengo-Aretha Franklin, Michael Jackson, Ray Charles, Little Richard noChuck Berry.
