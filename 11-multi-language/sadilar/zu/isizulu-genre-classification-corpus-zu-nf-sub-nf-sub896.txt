Masilwe nokuhlukumeza!
Maningi amangcwaba ezingane ezabulawa abahlukumezi. Izwe lethu alenzanga mizamo yokusiza zona kanye nezinkulungwane zabantu abadlwengulwayo, abanganakiwe abahlukunyezwa wubugebengu.
Le nkinga ikhona nakwamanye amazwe kodwa eSouth Africa imandla kakhulu.
Masiba-mbisane ukuze izwe lethu liphephe ezinganeni zethu.
Isaac Jacobs, Beacon Valley
Olunye uhlobo lokuhlu-kumeza
Abesimame baseSA mabapha-phame! Sekukhona olunye uhlobo lokuhlukumeza olunyonyobela imiphakathi yethu.
Siyazi ukuthi akufanele sivumele abesilisa basiphathise okwezibi kodwa okunye okubi kakhulu yilokhu engikubiza “ngokuhlukumezana kwabesimame bebodwa”.
Kukhona omama abadala abaneminyaka eko-50 abazwana nokukhuluma kabi ngabesimame abashadile abasebancane!
Abakwenzayo ngikufanisa nokwenziwa abesilisa abahlukumeza abesimame. Singalindela kanjani ukuhlonishwa abesilisa uma thina singahloniphani?
NN, Butterworth
Zimiseleni

AS, Mhluzi
Ikusasa eliqhakazile
Njengabantu baseSA, esikucabangayo nesikwenzayo kwakha ikusasa lezwe. Sinawo amandla okuba lusizo ezweni sibambe iqhaza ekuxazululeni izinkinga zaseSA.
Impilo yethu mayilawulwe yizenzo zethu ezinhle sisebenze kanzima ukuze izwe lethu libe sesimweni esihle.
C. Sepeko, Jouberton

Kuqala ekhaya…
Iseluleko esiqondiswe kubazali
KUNESISHO solimi lwesi-Ashanti esithi: “Ukonakala kwesizwe kuqala emakhaya.”
Uma sekukhulunyiwe kwenziwa nemizamo yokulwa nodlame lwansuku zonke kanye nobugebengu obushaqisayo obenzeka eSouth Africa, kufanele sihlale sibheke lesi sisho.
Ngumndeni okufanele uzame ukwakha izimilo, abazali babe yisibonelo esihle ngaso sonke isikhathi.
Emndenini izingane kumele zifunde ngokuxhumana, ngokuba nozwelo, ngothando nangenhlonipho.
Yilezi zinto okufanele sizame ukuzifundisa izingane zethu, sithi sizifundisa nathi sibe yizibonelo ezinhle kuzona ngokwenza okufanayo.
Esikoleni, i-syllabus kufanele ibe nohlu lwezinto ezimqoka ezikumthetho-sisekelo wethu kubuye kufundiswe izingane ngocansi nangokuphathwa kwemali.
Izingane zethu asizifundise ukuthi akukho okwedlula ukuba zifunde ukuziphatha kahle!
Brian Mahlangu, Tshwane
