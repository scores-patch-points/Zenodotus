PANDA
I-panda (Ailuropoda melanoleuca noma "ibhele elinezidladla ezimnyama nezimhlophe ezifuze ezekati") yibhele elitholakala maphakathi neChina naseningizimu yaleli lizwe.
Kulula ukuyibona ngoba inezibhadu ezimnyama emehlweni, ezindlebeni nasemzimbeni. I-panda isengozini yokushabalala. Angu-1 600 ahlala endle kanti kuthiwa angu-188 ayehlala endaweni ecashile ekupheleni kuka-2005, kulawo angu-20 atholakala ngaphandle kwelaseChina. Kodwa ngokwemibiko kuthiwa isibalo sama-panda ahlala endle siyakhula.
I-panda ikhonze ukudla uqalo kanti iyaludla noju, amaqanda, inhlanzi nama-yams.
Ubude bama-panda amadala bungu-1,5m kanti isisindo saweduna singaba ngu-115kg, izinsikazi zona zivame ukungabi nesisindo esikhulu.
I-panda inomsila omncane ubude bawo obungu-15cm.
Ama-panda angahlala ezivalele isikhathi esingangeminyaka engu-20-30.
Uma ubulale i-panda wawugwetshwa ngokubulawa eChina nokuyinto eyaphela ngo-1997 emva kokuthathwa kwesinqumo sokuthi abenze lokhu mabagqunywe ejele iminyaka engu-20. I-panda yeduna ayibi nasandla ekukhulisweni kwewundlu. Iwundlu lizalwa linombala o-pink, lingenaboya futhi lingaboni. Lincela ka-14 ngosuku kube yimizuzu engu-30 ngesikhathi. Eyensikazi iyahamba iyozifunela ukudla ishiye iwundlu lodwa.
Emva kwesonto elilodwa kuya kwamabili izelwe, isikhumba sayo siyashintsha sibe-grey lapho kuzogcina kumile khona uboya obumnyama.
Iwundlu liqala ukuzama ukuhamba emva kwezinsuku ezingu-75-90.
Amawundlu akwazi ukudla uqalo emva kwezinyanga eziyisithupha ezelwe. Ama-panda asekhulile angaba nesisindo esingu-45kg emva konyaka kanti ahlala no 'mama' wawo izinyanga ezingu-18 kuya onyakeni.

Iyini... iYOGA?
I-Yoga iyindlela yakudala eyadabuka eNdiya yokwelapha umphefumulo. I-Yoga yaqala phakathi kuka-1500 no-1200 BC.
Namuhla i-yoga ithathwa njengendlela yokugcina umzimba nomphefumulo kusesimweni esihle.
Ngaphandle kwelaseNdiya, i-yoga yeyanyaniswa nokwenziwa kwe-asanas - izindlela zokumisa umzimba zeHatha Yoga.
I-Hatha Yoga yasungulwa nguYogi Swatmarama kwelaseNdiya nge-15th Century. Igxile kakhulu kwi-shatkarma, esho ukuhlanzwa komzimba okuholela ekuhlanzweni kwengqondo namandla.
Nge-yoga kuhloswe ukuba kukhululwe umhlaba ekuhluphekeni nasezinkingeni zokuzalwa nezokufa.

NGO-1660, uLouis XIV owayeyinkosi yaseFrance wathatha isinqumo sokwakha isigodlo kude nesiphithiphithi saseParis. Wasakha eVersailles kanti kwathi emva kweminyaka wabe esesakhe saba yisigodlo esikhulu kakhulu e-Europe.
Ngo-1678 inkantolo nohulumeni waseFrance kwathuthela eVersailles. Imbangela yalokho kwakungukuthi uLouis wayefuna ukulawula yonke into.
Wabuye wathi iziphathimandla ezazinezikhundla mazichithe isikhathi esithile njalo ngonyaka eVersailles ngoba efuna ukuzinqunda amandla okuphatha.
I-Hall Of Mirrors iyona ndawo egqame kakhulu kulesi sigodlo. Iku-first floor kanti inezibuko ezinkulu ezingu-357. Imidwebo esophahleni iveza uLouis njengombusi wamaRoma.
Emagcekeni aseVersailles kukhona ingadi enkulu enamachibi nama-fountain ahlale ephuma amanzi.
Kwakudingeka imali enhle ukuze isigodlo saseVersailles sihlale sisihle. Kuthiwa ukusinakekela kubandakanya nokunakekelwa kwabasebenzi namalungu asesigodlweni kwakudla u-25 percent wemali yaseFrance.
Horrible rules
If you'd lived at the Palace Of Versailles there were a whole lot of strict rules of behaviour to follow. Some examples...
v People who wanted to speak to the king could not knock on his door. Instead, using the left pinkie finger, they had to gently scratch on the door, until they were granted permission to enter. As a result, many courtiers grew that fingernail longer than the others.
v A lady never held hands or linked arms with a gentleman. Besides being in bad taste, this practice would have been impossible because a woman's hooped skirts were so wide. Instead, she was to place her hand on top of the gentleman's bent arm as they strolled through the gardens and chambers of Versailles.
v When a gentleman sat down, he slid his left foot in front of the other, placed his hands on the sides of the chair and gently lowered himself into the chair. There was a very practical reason for this - if a gentleman sat too fast, his tight trousers might split.
