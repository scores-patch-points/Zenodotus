ISELULEKO #1: Hamba ibanga elifushane
Isinyathelo ongasithatha wukuhamba ibanga elifishane. "Sungula i-lift club noma nihlanganyele emotweni eyodwa nozakwenu abahlala ngandawonye," kusho uSARITA WALLER waseSea Point.
"Uzokwazi ukonga uphethiloli ukwazi nokufika ngesikhathi ehhovisi."
Ngaleyo ndlela nizokwazi futhi ukunciphisa isibalo sezimoto emgwaqweni - kunciphe nokuguga kwamasondo akho.
USarita uthi kufanele ukuhlele kahle ukuhamba kwakho uma uzokuya kothenga. Lokhu konga kuzokwehlisa izindleko zegrosa nemoto yakho ihlale isikhathi eside.
ISELULEKO #2: Izinto zokuthutha umphakathi
Uma ungakwazi ukuba nisungule i-lift club, cabangani eyokusebenzisa izinto zokuthutha zomphakathi - kusho u-OSMAN MAKHAMBA waseWellington noBUSISIWE MAHLANGU waseMhluzi.
Uzozuza ngendlela efanayo njengakwi-lift club ukwazi futhi nokufika ngesikhathi emsebenzini.
Asisho ukuthi akufanele ushayele imoto yakho uyithokozele, kodwa kufanele unciphise ukuyisebenzisa kwakho ngosuku - kuzothi lapho usuyisebenzisa, uyithokozele.
ISELULEKO #3: Yinakekele
Cishe bonke abafundi bethu abasibhalela babalula okubalulekile ngokugcina imoto yakho isesimweni esikahle - siyabonga!
I-air filter noma ama-spark plugs agugile angenza ukuba uphethiloli wakho udleke kakhulu kuye kufike ku-20%.
Umoya emasondweni kufanele ubhekwe kakhulu. Kufanele uwafuthe acishe ukugcwala ngendlela egunyazwe abenzi bawo.
Amasondo angenawo umoya owanele awadali ukudleka kukaphethiloli kuphela kodwa adleka ngokushesha futhi, kanti angakudalela ingozi.
ISELULEKO #4: Khumbula iqanda
Indlela oshayela ngayo ibaluleke kakhulu. I-accelerator yakho yihloniphe uyinyathele sengathi ngaphansi kwayo kuneqanda elibolile elingafa noma kunini. Yinyathele kancane kuthi lapho ufuna ukwehlisa ijubane uyidedele kancane usekude.
"Hamba ngesivinini esikahle," kusho uYAKOOB CASOOJEE waseNigel.
"Umshayeli oshayela kahle, ibhuleki lakhe ulisebenzisa ngokufanele. Zama ukugcina imoto yakho isegiyeni eliphezulu. Ngale ndlela unika injini ithuba lokusebenza kahle ngisho uyireva kancane."
Iseluleko sikaYakoob sihle kakhulu, kodwa ungayivumeli imoto izabalaze emagiyeni aphansi kakhulu.
Imoto esesimweni esikahle ivame ukuba phakathi kuka- 2 500 no-4 000rpm.
Zama ukuyigcina ikulesi simo ngokuba ibe semagiyeni afanele zikhathi zonke.
ISELULEKO #5:I-Aircon evuliwe (ngesinye isikhathi)
Kukaningi umbuzo othi yini engcono ekudleni uphethiloli ekushayeleni uvule i-aircon noma uyivalile
Impendulo ithi, ukuze wonge uphethiloli indlela eyiyo wukushayela uvule i-aircon uvale amafasitela.
I-aircon ithatha amandla amaningi enjinini, nokusho ukuthi kukhona uphethiloli osebenzayo kulokho kodwa hhayi omningi.
Ukushayela kancane kwi-traffic kungakusiza uma i-aircon uyivalile, kodwa uma uhamba ngejubane emgwaqweni ovulekile, uyonga uma uvule i-aircon.
Okubalulekile wukuthi kufanele uyiphathe kahle imoto yakho uyisebenzise ngobuhlakani.
Ukushayela kwi-traffic akusiyo into encane, ngakhoke zama ukusebenzisa omunye umzila ukuze ufike lapho uya khona.[z]

Ngesikhathi sokushicilela, ochwepheshe bezomnotho bexwayisa ngokuthi maduze sizokhokha R10 ngelitha kaphethiloli.
UDavid Shapiro ophuma e-Sasfin Frankel Pollak Securities wexwayisa ngokuthi iSA isazobhekana nefu elimnyama.
Ekhuluma emcimbini owawuseThekwini ngoFebruary wathi umnotho womhlaba uyehla kanjalo nowoyela namanani okudla ayakhula.
Kusekukhulu ukwenyuka kwamanani okudla njengoba nawommbila ekhule ngokuphindiwe eminyakeni eyishumi eyedlule. Abakhiqizi bokudla sebebheke ukusebenzisa i-bio-fuels njengesisombululo ekukhiqizeni ukudla.
"Ukungazinzi kwesimo sezombusazwe, ubugebengu, inkohlakalo, amandla ezokumbiwa phansi eSA - kwenza abavakashi babe manqika ekutshaleni izimali," kusho yena .
Abanini bezimoto bazokhipha imali engu-R1-billion ngenyanga ngenxa yokwenyuka kukaphethiloli, amabhizinisi akhiphe R670-million kuthi ngezidingo zasendlini kube ngu-R330-million ngenyanga kusho uTony Twine oyisazi sezomnotho enkampanini eseGoli i-Econometrix.
Ekhuluma nabeSunday Tribune uTwine wathi amabhizinisi azokhipha imali encane ngamaholo nokuyinto ezobeka umnotho kwenkulu ingcindezi.
Ebuzwa ngabeSunday Tribune ngoFebruary 24 ukuthi kuzothatha isikhathi esingakanani ngaphambi kokuba uphethiloli ufike ku-R10 ilitha, uMuzi Mkhize wabeDepartment Of Minerals And Energy wathi: "ISA yona yamukela inani eselibekiwe, engingakusho wukuthi sisazokhipha imali eningi ngophethiloli."
