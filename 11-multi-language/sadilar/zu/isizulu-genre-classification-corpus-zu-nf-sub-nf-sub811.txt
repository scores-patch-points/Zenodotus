Okwamanje kusekahle
INTO engahlanganisa isizwe samasiko ehlukene, nesizwe esihlukaniswe wukubandlululana - wukubuyisana.
Ukubuyisana yindlela engcono yokuletha ukuzwana nokuthula phakathi kwezinhlanga ezehlukene.
Ngaphezu kwalokho, ukuzwana kwezinhlanga yinto ebalulekile kunoma yiliphi izwe ikakhulu ekulivuseleleni.
Uhulumeni wethu kufanele siwuncome kakhulu ngokukwazi ukuba ukwenze lokhu.
Kufanele futhi situse nabantu bakuleli abafana nawe nami ngokuba negalelo kulokho. Masiqhubeke sizibophezelele ekubuyisaneni nasekukhohlweni okwedlule kanjalo nasekuxolelaneni.

Mateu Monama, Kwathema

Abazali kufanele babe qotho ekukhuliseni izingane zabo!
ABAZALI abashayela izingane zabo imithetho eqinile, basethubeni elihle lokukhulisa izingane ezingazibandakanyi nezidakamizwa. Ngokwami ngibona sengathi izingane ezinobudlelwane obuhle nabazali bazo yizo eziziphatha kahle.
Lokhu kusho ukuthi, ukubheka izingane zakho zibuka iTV, nokushaya umthetho ngamaCD okufanele ziwadlale nokwazi ukuthi ziqhuba kanjani esikoleni, ngezinye zezinto ezinomphumela omuhle ekukhuliseni ingane kahle.
Kufanele futhi ubeke imithetho yokuthi yini ezingayenza ekuphumeni kwesikole nezingeke ziyenze, kanjalo nangezimpelasonto. Kufanele nidle i-supper ndawonye njengomndeni njalo ebusuku bese futhi uzihlukanisela imisebenzi yasendlini.
Lena iyona ndlela yokunqanda izingane zakho zingabi yimidlwembe.
Rhulani Wa Ka Chauke, Malamulele
