Inomswakama
NgoJanuary 2005 ngathenga indlu kwi-estate agent eyaziwayo. Emva kokuba isibhaliswe ngegama lami, ngathola ukuthi inomswakama ongapheli. Amanzi ayazigelezela ezindongeni agcwale phansi.
Ngabiza inkampani ehlola umswakama ezindlini yangitshela ukuthi sekuneminyaka le ndlu inenkinga yalo mswakama. Owayeyihlola wangikhombisa okwenziwa owayengumniniyo ezama ukugqiba lowo mswakama. Wathi unesiqiniseko sokuthi i-estate agent yangifihlela ngamabomu ukuthi inomswakama.
Kwathi uma ngibikela umakhelwane ngokwenzekile, wahleka wathi naye ubenolwazi ngenkinga yale ndlu kanti akekho umuntu ongazi ngayo.
Ukuyilungisa kuzodla imali engaphezulu kuka-R20 000.
Ngokwami, owangidayisela indlu ne-estate agent abathembekanga kimina ngokungifihlela kwabo ngale nkinga.
Kuyiphutha ukuthi kube yimina engizokhokha izindleko zokulungiswa kwayo. Yini engingayenza"
JW, Joburg

Kuzodingeka bakhokhele nendawo ozobe uqube kuyona okwesikhashana ngesikhathi besalungisa indlu uma kungukuthi ngeke bakwazi ukuyilungisa uhleli kuyona.
Uma usubale imali ezodingeka ngokulungiswa kwayo, babhalele incwadi ebaphoqa ukuba bakhokhe. Kufanele bakhokhe leyo mali zingakapheli izinsuku ezintathu kanti uma bengakhokhi, encwadini ozoyibhala akucace ukuthi bazothola isamanisi.
Uma i-estate agent nowayengumnini muzi bengafuni ukukhokha, kuzodingeka ukuba uthole ummeli ozoludlulisela phambili udaba lwakho.
