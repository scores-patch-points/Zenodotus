GINGER CRINKLES
Isikhathi Sokukulungisa: 30 imizuzu
Isikhathi Sokukubhaka: 8 imizuzu
Kuphuma: 48 amabhisikidi
* 180ml (3/4 inkomishi) ibhotela noma imajarini
* 200g ushukela
* 5ml (1 i-teaspoon) i-baking powder
* 5ml (1 i-teaspoon) i-bicarbonate of soda
* 10ml (2 ama-teaspoon) i-ground ginger
* 2,5ml (1/2 i-teaspoon) i-ground cinnamon
* 2,5ml (1/2 i-teaspoon) i-ground cloves
* ibinzana likasawoti
* 1 iqanda
* 60ml (1/4 inkomishi) i-honey noma i-golden syrup
* 675ml (2 1/2 izinkomishi) ufulawa
* 250ml (1 inkomishi) ushukela
* Okokukuninda:
* 60ml (1/4 inkomishi) i-crystallised ginger eqotshiwe
1. Shisisa uhhavini ngo-200°C. Endishini shaya ibhotela nge-electric mixer imizuzwana engu-30. Faka ushukela, i-baking powder, i-bicarbonate of soda, ginger, cinnamon, cloves nosawoti. Kushaye kuze kuhlangane, ukhothuluze emaceleni endishi.
2. Shayela phakathi iqanda ne-syrup. Shayela phakathi ufulawa nge-mixer. Sebenzisa isipuni sokhuni.
3. Ebhodini elibhuqwe ngofulawa, bumba inhlama ibe ngamabholana angu-3cm. Gingqa amabhola kushukela, uwabeke ahlukane ngo-6cm kuma-baking trays angagcotshiwe. Sebenzisa imfoloko ukucindezele kancane phezulu ufake i-crystallised ginger.
4. Kubhake imizuzu engu-8 - 10 kuze kube kuyasetha emaceleni kuklamuzele ngenhla.
5. Kupholise kwi-baking tray umzuzu. Kukhiphele kwi-wire rack ukuyeke kuphole.
kubiza: R15
Enye indlela: Esikhundleni se-crystallised ginger hlanganisa isipuni (15ml) i-ginger egreythiwe nenkomishi (250ml) ushukela enhlameni ye-roll ngaphambi kokukubhaka.
Ukubhaka ozokubeka: Bhaka amakhekhe uwapholise, wenze abe ama-layer wendlale amaphepha ane-wax phakathi nendawo. Wafake esitsheni esivalwayo uwagcine izinsuku ezingu-3 noma uwa-freezer izinyanga ezingu-3.
I-Ginger

EYEHLA ESIPHUNDU
GINGER & FRUIT FRIDGE SLICES
Isikhathi Sokukulungisa: 30 imizuzu
Isikhathi Sokusetha: 60 imizuzu
Kuphuma: 60 izingcezu
* 2 x 200g amaphakethe amaTennis Biscuits
* 250g ibhotela noma imajarini
* 100g ushukela
* 500g ama-raisins, ama-sultanas anama-currants
* 100g i-glacé cherries eqotshiwe
* 15ml (1 isipuni) ikhasi likalamula eligreythiwe
* 30ml (2 izipuni) i-crystallised ginger eqotshiwe
* 100g i-dessicated coconut
* 2 amaqanda amakhulu ashayiwe
* i-dessicated coconut yokunonga
1. Ndlala i-baking paper kwiSwiss roll tin (elishonayo noma eliyisikwele) umboze nge-layer yamaTennis Biscuits unqamule lapho kudingeka khona.
2. Endishini, gaya amabhisikidi asele.
3. Epanini ncibilikisa ibhotela ufake uhhafu wamabhisikidi agayiwe nezithako ezisele - ngaphandle kwamaqanda no-coconut. Pheka imizuzu engu-10. Kugudluze emlilweni.
4. Bondela phakathi amaqanda ukushaye ngokushesha. Kuhlanganise. Kubuyisele emlilweni omncane imizuzu engu-5.
5. Shutheka phakathi amabhisikidi agayiwe. Kugudluze emlilweni ukuvuvuzele ngo-coconut. Kufake efrijini kusethe ngaphambi kokukusika.

GINGER ALMOND BARS
Isikhathi Sokukulungisa: 20 imizuzu
Isikhathi Sokukubhaka: 45 imizuzu
Kuphuma: 24
* 250ml (1 inkomishi) imajarini
* 125ml (1/2 inkomishi) ushukela
* 10ml (2 ama-teaspoon) i-almond essence
* 500ml (2 izinkomishi) ufulawa
* 200g ama-raisins acwiliswe ku-250ml (1 inkomishi) itiye elimnyama elishisayo elicwengiwe, imizuzu engu-10.
* 30ml (2 izipuni) i-crystallised ginger eqotshiwe
* 2 okwamaqanda okumhlophe
* 125ml (1/2 inkomishi) i-icing sugar
* 125ml (1/2 inkomishi) i-almond evengiwe
1. Shisisa uhhavini ngo-180°C. Endishini, hlanganisa imajarini noshukela. Faka 1 i-teaspoon (1/2) i-almond essence. Faka ufulawa, hlanganisa kube yizimvuthu.
2. Kucindezele endishini esetshenziswa kuhhavini engu-30 x 25 x 5cm. Bhaka imizuzu engu-25.
3. Epanini, fudumeza ama-raisins ne-ginger.
4. Endishini, shaya okweqanda okumhlophe. Faka i-icing sugar kancane kancane ushaye, faka 1 isipuni ngesikhathi, faka i-almond essence esele. Shayisisa kuze kube namabhamuza.
5. Ndlala ingxube yama-raisin phezu kwe-crust. Ndlala i-meringue ngaphezulu, vuvuzela ama-almonds.
6. Khuphula izinga lokushisa libe ngu-200˚C. Bhaka imizuzu engu-15. Kupholise ngaphambi kokukuqoba.
Izindleko zokukwenza: R25

MASENZE IPULANI
Ipulani yebhizinisi lakho iyibalazwe eliya empumelelweni yakho. Ungakayidwebi ipulani yebhizinisi lakho, kusemqoka ukuzibuza imibuzo emihlanu:
1 KUNGANI? Kungani ufuna ukuvula leli bhizinisi?
Cishe u-80% wabantu uphendula ngokuthi, “Ngifuna imali”. Ngeshwa, ucwaningo lukhomba ukuthi amabhizinisi angaphezulu kuka-80% avulwe ngenhloso “yokwakha imali” awaphumeleli.
Umbuzo othi kungani uma kukhulunywa ngokuvulwa kwebhizinisi kufanele uphendulwe ngokuthi “Ngifuna ukwakhela umndeni wami impilo engcono” noma “Ngifuna ukwenza umahluko emphakathini”. Ngaleyo ndlela isizathu sokuvula kwakho ibhizinisi kuzoba esiphathekayo kanti ukuphumelela kwalo kusho ukuthi uzoba nemali ethe xaxa ebhange.
Khumbula: uma ibhizinisi lakho ulivule wazi ukuthi ulivulelani, uzophumelela noma kanjani!
2 INI? Yimiphi imikhiqizo ozoyenza? Ibhizinisi lakho lizohluka ngani kwamanye?
Uma sewazi ukuthi yini ofuna ukuyenza, phendula ushaye ngamafuphi.
3 UBANI? Ubani ozosisiza?
Uma wazi ukuthi yini ofuna ukuyenza, bheka ukuthi kule yonto ofuna ukuyenza uhlulwa yini ufune umuntu ozokusiza. Makube ngumuntu ozomethemba umchazele ukuthi yini ofuna akwenzele yona.
4 NINI? Ngizolivula nini?
Zibekele umnqamula-juqu kukho konke okumele kwenziwe ngaphambi kokuvula ibhizinisi, njengokuthenga isitokwe sakho uqinisekise ukuthi siyadilivwa, uvule i-account yasebhange eseceleni ozofaka kuyona imali yakho wenze nokunye.
5KANJANI? Ngizoliqhuba kanjani?
Lapha sihlanganisa konke ndawonye: abantu abazokusiza abanekhono elifanele ibhizinisi kanye nezindlela ezizosetshenziswa ukuqinisekisa ukuthi umkhiqizo noma umsebenzi wenu ngoweqophelo eliphezulu.
Uzophumelela noma kanjani uma uhlolisisa le mibuzo zikhathi zonke kukukhanyele ukuthi okwenzayo ukwenzelani.
Ngamanye amazwi, ipulani yebhizinisi lakho kumele uyihlole njalo uyiphucule uma ibhizinisi likhula.
Lluwellyn Diedericks
