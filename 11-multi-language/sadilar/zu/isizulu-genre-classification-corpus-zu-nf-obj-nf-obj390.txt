Uhambo lukaHenry Cele lokugcina
UHenry Cele kwadingeka aphile impilo yamaqhawe amabili - kodwa wazikhethela umthwalo wokuphila impilo yokuba yisibonelo esihle kubantu abaningi
IZINYONI ezazindiza nokuxokozela kwezinkawu kwakungamduduzi uRambo inja uHenry Cele ayithola elokishini laKwaMashu – uRambo angaduduzwa wukubona iqhawe lakhe eladuma ngelikaShaka.
“U-Henry wadabuka uma ebona esinye isidalwa esasingenabani,” kusho uJenny Hollander, umfelokazi kaHenry.
U-Henry wadalulela abakwaBONA ngoMarch 2006 ukuthi kwamphatha kabi ukulahlwa kwakhe abazali bakhe esemncane kanti waphawula kanjena, “kwahlwa emini ngangawuzwa nomoya owawuvunguza.”
Ugogo wakhe wayesebenzela umndeni wamaNdiya ehlala noHenry endlini eyayakhelwe ngaphansi kweyalo mndeni incane kangangoba uHenry wayengakwazi ngisho nokuma aqonde uma engaphakathi kuyona.
Ugogo washona uHenry eneminyaka engu-12 kanti ikhono lakhe lokudlala ibhola kanjalo nobude bakhe kwamsiza wakwazi ukuthola indawo yokukhosela ehostela labesilisa elalikuSomtseu Road eThekwini.
Watshela uJenny ukuthi esehlala lapho wayelala ngaphansi kwebhentshi amadoda ayehlala nawo emupha ukudla amnike namaphepha okubhala i-homework.

“Wayeneminyaka eyisishiyagalombili ngesikhathi ehlangana nowesimame owambuza ukuthi, ‘Ngabe uyangazi?’
“Wathi kwavele kwathi makamphendule ngokuthi, ‘Yebo, ungumama wami’.
“Wamthatha waya naye lapho ayehlala khona nomyeni wakhe nendodakazi yabo. Kodwa wanyamalala esontweni elalandela lelo,” kusho uJenny.
U-Jenny wakubona ukubaluleka kokuba anikeze le ndoda ekhule kanzima uthando. U-Henry ubekuthanda ukudla abekuphekelwa ngumkakhe.
Kwakuthi uma ezohamba uJenny, ashiye enze isiqiniseko sokuthi efrijini kunokudla amphekele kona.
Ngesikhathi uHenry eseNorth Africa eyodlala efilimini ethi The Citadel ngo-2001 wacela umkakhe ukuba amthumelele izimpahla zokugqoka. U-Jenny wafaka nempuphu namakhekhe ebhokisini.

Umsindo

Umshado kaHenry noMavis wawungahambi kahle ngesikhathi behlangana noJenny.
