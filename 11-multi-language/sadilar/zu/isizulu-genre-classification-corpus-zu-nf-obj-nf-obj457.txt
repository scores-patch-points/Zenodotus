Beef And Peach Stew
Izithako
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
1kg inkomo eqotshiwe
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) ufulawa
1 x 410g ithini izingcezu zepetshisi, ucwenge ujusi uwubeke
1 inkomishi (250ml) iqhuzu lesobho lenkomo
1 uphepha oluhlaza oqotshiwe
2 izinduku ze-celery eqotshiwe
3 ukherothi oqotshwe imisweswe
1 isipuni (15ml) u-vinegar
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) usawoti
2 izipuni (30ml) iWorcester sauce
Indlela yokwenza
1. Shisa uwoyela epanini. Thosa inyama no-anyanisi.
2. Sefa ufulawa. Faka ujusi wamapetshisi nesobho.
3. Faka ezinye izithako ngaphandle kwamapetshisi. Pheka u-1 1/2 hour.
4. Faka amapetshisi. Fudumeza useve nerayisi noma isitambu.

Izithako

2 izipuni (30ml) i-tomato purée
1 u-brinjal ocwecwe waqotshwa
usawoti no-pepper wokunonga
I-Topping
1 inkomishi (250ml) amasi
2 amaqanda ashayiwe
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) i-self-raising flour
1 inkomishi (250ml) ushizi iCheddar egreythiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. I-Mince. Ncibilikisa i-margarine epanini. Thosa u-anyanisi ufake ne-mince.
2. Faka ufulawa, isobho ne-purée. Faka u-brinjal ukunonge. Pheka imizuzu engu-10 ukufake endishini esetshenziswa kuhhavini.
3. I-Topping. Hlanganisa zonke izithako ukuthele engxubeni ye-mince. Bhaka kuhhavini ongu-180oC imizuzu engu-30 kuze kusethe i-topping.
Kubiza: R32,15

Scone-dough Meat Roll
Izithako
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
1 u-anyanisi ocwecwe waqotshwa
600g i-mince
2 ama-teaspoon iWorcester sauce
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) iqhuzu lesobho le-chilli beef
1 uphepha oluhlaza oqotshiwe
2 ukherothi ogreythiwe
Inhlama
2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-cake flour
2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-baking powder
1/2 i-teaspoon (2ml) usawoti
3/4 inkomishi (180ml) ubisi
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) uwoyela
ubisi lokucwebezelisa
Indlela yokwenza
1. Inyama. Shisa uwoyela epanini. Thosa u-anyanisi ne-mince.
2. Faka iWorcester sauce, isobho, uphepha oluhlaza nokherothi ukupheke imizuzu engu-10.
3. Inhlama. Sefa izithako ezomile endishini ufake ubisi nowoyela.
4. Ndlala inhlama endaweni evuvuzelwe ngofulawa. Beka ingxube ye-mince ngaphezulu.
5. Kugoqe kume njengeSwiss roll. Brasha indawo engaphezulu ngobisi.
6. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-200oC imizuzu engu-30.

Savoury Sausages
Izithako
1 isipuni (15ml) i-cornflour
2 izipuni (30ml) ushukela
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) i-tomato sauce
1 x 410g ithini lamapetshisi
8-12 amasosiji
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
1 uphepha obomvu oqotshiwe
1 uphepha oluhlaza oqotshiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Kujeke bonda i-cornflour noshukela one-tomato sauce.
2. Enkomishini cwenga uketshezi lwamapetshisi ulugcwalisele ngamanzi kube yinkomishi. Faka engxubeni ye-tomato sauce.
3. Epanini bilisa amasosiji emanzini.
4. Hlela ama-veg kwi-ovenproof dish. Beka amasosiji namapetshisi, ngaphezulu uthele usosi.
5. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-200oC imizuzu engu-15.
