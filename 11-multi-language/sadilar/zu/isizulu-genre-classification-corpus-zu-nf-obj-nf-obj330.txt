UNGAZE ubone ngesibazi esingenhla kweso lakwesobunxele ukuthi uVincent “The Rock” Vuma ongushampeni weSA junior middleweight wenza msebenzi muni njengoba abaningi baye bacabange ukuthi wumkhangisi. Useke wakucabanga ukuba abe ngumkhangisi, “kodwa emva kwesikhathi mhlawumbe emva kokuthatha umhlalaphansi.”
Ukubukeka kahle yinto esemndenini wakhe. Umfowabo u-Amos ngumkhangisi wengqephu esihlale simbona kwiTV.
Ukube kwakungesiyo inxa ka-Amos, uVincent ngabe akashayi isibhakela.
“Ngesikhathi sisuka ekhaya eHlanganani eLimpopo siya eGoli siyofuna impilo engcono, kwakungumfowethu omncane owayengondla ngisashaya isibhakela ngaphansi kweligi yabangama-amisha ngisazicija,” kusho kuchaza yena.
Esasebenza njengonogada, u-Amos nguye owayekhokhela izindleko zezikweleti. Lokhu kukhombisa ngokusobala ukuthi lezi zelamani zisondelene kangakanani.
“Umama wethu wedlula emhlabeni ngineminyaka emithathu, umfowethu omncane enowodwa,” kusho uVincent, “ngakhokhe saba ndawonye kusukela ebuncaneni.
“Sasihambe sihlala sisuka komunye umndeni siye komunye. Ubaba wethu wayenamakhosikazi amaningi nezingane ezingaphezu kuka-19, ngakhoke sasingeke sihlale naye.”
Ukubukeka kwakhe kudida kakhulu kanti ubunzima ahlangabezana nabo esakhula yikho okwakhiqiza umshayi-sibhakela oqinile.
Ngesikhathi sokushicilela, lo shampeni ogama lakhe lesiShangane elithi Matimva lichaza “amandla,” wayesewine izimpi ezingu-17 kwezingu-19 kanti ezingu-14 waziwina ngama-knockouts.
Isivinini aphebeza ngaso umuntu alwa naye eduze kwakhe, yiso esiyichaza kahle indaba yokubukeka kahle kwakhe.
“Uma ngithola isikhala, angichithi sikhathi,” kusho yena. “Ngishayela ukubulala ukuze ngigudluze engilwa naye ngokushesha,” kuchaza yena.
Uthi umuntu onzima asake alwa naye nguWilliam Gare. Sebalwa kathathu bebanga isicoco kanti uVuma waziwina zonke lezo zimpi - nakuba kunjalo uyazi kufanele amqaphele.
“Uma uGare engiphonsela inselelo futhi, ngiyomnika elinye ithuba kodwa kulokhu ngiyoqiniseka ukuthi ngimenza athathe umhlalaphansi esibhakeleni.”
UVuma uchitha isikhathi sakhe ezilungiselela impi elandelayo ngisho ngabe engakasayini inkontileka yanoma iyiphi impi.
Usuku lwakhe luqala ngo-6 ekuseni ngokugijima - nokuyibanga cishe elingu-17.
ISonto yilona langa lo shampeni aphumula ngalo.
Unkosikazi wakhe uMavis, ngumsiki wengqephu. Wenza izingubo zemishado nezeminye imicimbi eyisipesheli.
“Uma enama-oda amaningi, kuye kudingeke ukuba ngimsize, mhlawumbe ngoku-ayina izimpahla aseziqedile noma ngokunquma okotini abalengayo.”
Wengeze ngokuthi uMavis usemsize kakhulu emsebenzini wakhe. “Ukuba khona kwakhe empilweni yami kungisiza ekutheni ngibe ngumshayi-sibhakela ozihloniphayo.”
Uphinde amphekele nokudla okuyikho - irayisi nofishi uma ezilungiselela impi ngoba kudingeka ukuba agcine isisindo sakhe esifanele.
“Nakuba ngisaziqeqesha kanzima ngisho ngingenazo izimpi, kodwa ngisakuthanda ukudla kwami okufana nephalishi, i-mopane worms nesipinashi.
Lezi zithandani zaqala ukuthandana ngesikhathi zisengabafundi base-high school eLimpopo Province kanti kumanje banendodakazi eneminyaka eyisikhombisa.
Umqeqeshi uNick Durandt noDingaan Thobela babe nesandla ekusizeni uVuma kulo msebenzi wakhe wesibhakela ngokumbeka ethubeni lokuthola ukuqeqesheka okuyikho.
Into angasoze ayilibala ngomsebenzi wakhe wesibhakela, wukungcwekisana noDingaan ngesikhathi iRose Of Soweto isewushampeni womhlaba.
“NguDingaan owangikhuthaza ukuba ngibe ngushampeni,” kusho uVuma. “Waze wangithatha wahamba nami eya phesheya eyovikela isicoco sakhe somhlaba. Ngafunda okuningi kuyena kanti ngiwabonga futhi namathuba anginika wona.”[z]
