Egg Roll
Izithako
1 iphakethe (500g) amasosiji engulube
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) izimvuthu zesinkwa
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ushizi ogreythiwe
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
usawoti nophepha
1 i-roll esilungisiwe ye-puff pastry
6 amaqanda abilisiwe ahlukaniswa phakathi
iqanda elishayiwe lokucwebezelisa
Indlela yokwenza
1. Ebhodini khamela inyama engaphakathi kumasosiji uyikhiphe ezikhumbeni.
(Zilahle izikhumba)
2. Endishini hlanganisa inyama yamasosiji nezimvuthu zesinkwa, ushizi no-anyanisi.
3. Ebhodini elibhuqwe ngofulawa, ndlala i-pastry uyivuvuzele ngenyama yamasosiji.
4. Beka amaqanda eceleni kwe-pastry, ugoqe i-pastry bese uyibrasha ngeqanda.
5. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-200oC imizuzu engu-20-30 i-pastry ize ibe nsunjwana.
Kubiza: R28,14

Egg Curry
Izithako
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
3 u-anyanisi osikwe izingcezu
3 utamatisi oqotshiwe
1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
1 i-chicken stock cube encibilikiswe ku-1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) amanzi abilayo
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ama-raisins
1 isipuni (15ml) i-corn flour
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) amasi
8 amaqanda abilisiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini thosa u-anyanisi, utamatisi no-curry powder imizuzu engu-5-10 ulokhu ukugovuza.
2. Bondela phakathi umhluzu wenkukhu nama-raisins. Kumboze ukugxabhise imizuzu emihlanu.
3. Kujeke hlanganisa i-corn flour namasi ukwenza i-paste. Bondela phakathi i-gravy ukubilise ulokhu ukugovuza. Kugxabhise imizuzu emibili.
4. Sika amaqanda phakathi nendawo ngobude bawo, ufake i-gravy. Govuza ukugxabhise omunye umzuzu noma emibili.
5. Ngesipuni faka ukhari ophuthwini olushisayo noma i-Instema, ukuvuvuzele ngokhukhunathi bese ukuseva nezingcezu zikatamatisi noma zikaphayinaphu. Ungabeka futhi eceleni i-chutney engesipuni eduze kwepuleti ngalinye uma uthanda.
Kubiza: R12,78
Kwanela abangu-8
Easy Egg Pie
Izithako
2 amaphakethe e-puff pastry esilungisiwe
6 amaqanda
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) ubisi
6 izingcezu zikapholoni eziqotshiwe
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
2 amazambane acwecwiwe aphekwa asikwa izikwedlana
1 inkomishi 250ml) ama-mixed vegetables aphekiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Ebhodini elibhuqwe ngofulawa, ndlala iphakethe elilodwa le-pastry. Kubeke endaweni ye-pie dish engu-23cm. (I-roll esalayo yiyo ozokwenza ngayo indawo engaphezulu.)
2. Endishini hlanganisa ndawonye amaqanda nobisi, ubeke eceleni cishe okunganela isipuni (15ml) ozocwebezelisa ngakho i-pastry. Faka upholoni, u-anyanisi, amazambane nama-mixed vegetables. Beka i-filling esinqeni se-pastry.
3. Ebhodini elibhuqwe ngofulawa, ndlala iphakethe lesibili le-pastry. Manzisa emachophweni ngamanzi amancane, umboze i-filling ngokugobela amachopho ngaphansi. Cindezela amachopho ngemfoloko. (Hlobisa ngaphezulu noma yingayiphi i-pastry scrap.) Kucwebezelise bese ukubhobosa izimbobo ngemfoloko.
4. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-200oC cishe imizuzu engu-30. (Uma i-pastry isheshe iba nsunjwana ngaphambi kwalokhu, yehlisa umlilo kodwa ungawugudluzi uphayi kuhhavini kuze kuhlangane i-filling.)
Kubiza: R17,89

Egg Kedgeree
Izithako
1 inkomishi (250ml) ubisi
1/2 iphakethe (300g) i-frozen haddock, ekhishwe amathambo yebulwa isikhumba
usawoti nophepha omnyama omahhadla
250g irayisi eliphekiwe
1/2 i-teaspoon (2,5ml) i-turmeric noma isinongo serayisi
1 iqhuzwana le-chicken stock
4 izinkomishi (l ilitha) amanzi
1 isipuni (15ml) i-parsley eqotshiwe noma i-dhania
3 amaqanda abilisiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Faka ubisi epanini lokuthosa, ufake i-haddock. Kubilise, wehlise umlilo kugxabhe i-haddock ize iphekeke. (Izokuba mhlophe phakathi nendawo bese kuba lula ukuyivenga uma isiphekekile.) Icwenge, uyinonge uyibeke eceleni.
2. Beka irayisi, i-turmeric ne-stock cube epanini. Faka amanzi ukubilise. Yehlisa umlilo, ukuyeke kugxabhe irayisi lize liphekeke (10-15 imizuzu), ufake amanzi amaningi uma kudingeka.
3. Cwenga irayisi, ubondele phakathi i-parsley ne-haddock, uhlele kahle amaqanda phezulu. Kuseve ngokushesha.
Kubiza: R35,90
Kwanela abangu-6

Curried Tortilla
Izithako
6 amaqanda
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) amasi
1/2 i-teaspoon (2,5ml) usawoti
2 izipuni (30ml) imajarini noma uwoyela
2 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
2-3 utamatisi oqotshiwe
3 ama-teaspoon (15ml) u-curry powder
4 izinkomishi (1 ilitha) eqotshiwe i-morogo, imifino, iklabishi noma isipinashi
3 izinkomishi (750ml) isitambu esiphekiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Endishini hlanganisa amaqanda, amasi nosawoti ukushayisise.
2. Epanini ncibilikisa imajarini uthose u-anyanisi uze uthambe, ubondele phakathi utamatisi no-curry powder. Kuthose imizuzu emihlanu.
Faka okuhlaza nesitambu, ukuthose kancane imizuzu elishumi ulokhu ukugovuza.
3. Thela ingxube yeqanda kuleyo esepanini. Kupheke ngaphandle kokukugovuza kuze kuhlangane.
4. Sika i-tortilla uyenze izingcezu. Kuseve ngokushesha nezingcezu zesinkwa ezigcotshwe ibhotela noma nesaladi.
Kubiza: R14,90
Kwanela abangu-6
Egg And Corn Fritters
Izithako
5 amaqanda ahlukanisiwe
2 x 410g amathini e-whole corn kernels acwengiwe
1 inkomishi (250ml) amasi
1 inkomishi (250ml) impuphu
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ufulawa i-self raising
1 isipuni (15ml) i-spicy chicken soup powder
usawoti wokunonga
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) uwoyela
Indlela yokwenza
1. Beka okweqanda okuphuzi, i-corn namasi endishini. Usebenzisa isipuni sokhuni, kushayisise. Faka impuphu, ufulawa, isobho eliyimpuphu nosawoti.
2. Kwenye indishi, shaya okweqanda okumhlophe kuze kuhlangane. Kufake kahle engxubeni yama-corn.
3. Epanini elinesinqe esiqinile, shisa uwoyela bese ufaka ingxube ngesipuni, uyicindezele kancane ngengemumva lesipuni ngayinye, ngayinye. Ipheke imizuzu engu-2-3 esayidini ngalinye ize ibe nsunjwana ngombala.
Kubiza: R18,95
