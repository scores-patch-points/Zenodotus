Zifudumeze ngo-
curry
Ungenza u-curry owehla esiphundu ngenyama noma ingekho. Unomsoco, kulula ukuwenza kanti awuthathi isikhathi esiningi
BONA ZULU – June 2007
Chicken Curry-pot Malay Style
Izithako
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) i-red lentils
3 izinkomishi (750ml) amanzi abilayo
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) imajarini
1 u-anyanisi
1 u-green pepper
2 izigaxa ugaligi
3cm ophisi u-ginger ogreythiwe
1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
1 upelepele okhishwe izinhlamvu waqotshwa
1,5kg ophisi benkukhu
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-turmeric
usawoti ne-freshly ground black pepper
i-dhania yokuhlobisa
Indlela yokwenza
1. Cwilisa i-lentils emanzini imizuzu engu-30.
2. Epanini shisa imajarini uthose u-anyanisi, u-green pepper, ugaligi, u-ginger, u-curry powder nopelepele imizuzu embalwa. Faka ophisi benkukhu ukuthose kube nsundu.
3. Faka i-turmeric, isinongo, i-lentils namanzi.
4. Kufake endishini esetshenziswa kuhhavini. Kumboze ukubhake ngezinga elingu-180oC amahora angu-1-1 1/2 bese ukuhlobisa nge-dhania.
Kwanela abangu-6
Kubiza: R45,90
Mutton Curry
Izithako
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1kg umqala wesiklabhu oqotshiwe
2 u-anyanisi
2 izigaxa ugaligi ogxotshiwe
1 x 410g ithini utamatisi oqotshiwe
2 izipuni (30ml) u-curry powder
2 amaqhuzu esobho i-mutton stock encibilikiswe ku-2 izinkomishi (500ml) amanzi abilayo
1 inkomishi (250ml) ukherothi ocwecwe waqotshwa
1 inkomishi (250ml) iklabishi eliqotshiwe
6 amazambane acwecwe aqotshwa
1 isipuni (15ml) i-cornflour
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini thosa inyama ize ibe nsunjwana. Yisuse epanini uyibeke eceleni. Thosa u-anyanisi nogaligi kuze kucishe kuthambe. Faka utamatisi no-curry powder. Thosa imizuzu engu-5 ugovuze. Phindisela inyama epanini.
2. Thela isobho ukumboze. Pheka ihora elilodwa wehlise umlilo.
3. Faka ukherothi, iklabishi namazambane. Mboza upheke imizuzu engu-30-50 kuze kuvuthwe.
4. Kujeke, hlanganisa i-cornflour no-2-3 ama-teaspoon amanzi abandayo. Thela ebhodweni ukupheke imizuzu engu-2-3 ukuze kushube i-gravy.
Kwanela abangu-6
Kubiza: R53,10
Curried Pot-roasted Roll Of Brisket
Izithako
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) usawoti
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-freshly ground black pepper
2 izipuni (30ml) i-cake flour
2 izipuni (30ml) u-curry powder
1,5kg i-rolled brisket
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
1 u-anyanisi
1 utamatisi oqotshiwe
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) amanzi
1 iqhuzu lesobho lenkomo
2 ukherothi oqotshwe ophisi abangu-2cm
2 amazambane aqotshiwe
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) u-green beans
2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-mixed herbs
Indlela yokwenza
1. Endishini hlanganisa usawoti, u-pepper, ufulawa no-curry powder bese ukugcoba enyameni.
2. Shisisa uwoyela kwi-casserole uthose inyama.
3. Faka u-anyanisi notamatisi ukuthose imizuzu embalwa. Faka amanzi nesobho. Kumboze ukupheke isikhathi esingu-1 1/2 hour.
4. Faka ama-veg nama-herbs ukupheke imizuzu engu-25 kuze kuvuthwe.
Kwanela abangu-6

Fruity Mince Pasta Curry
Izithako
750g i-minced meat
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1 isigaxa ugaligi ogxotshiwe
1 u-anyanisi
2 izipuni (30ml) u-curry powder
1 iphakethe i-oxtail soup
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) amanzi
1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ama-raisins
3 izipuni (45ml) i-chutney
2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-pasta
1 isipuni (15ml) i-dhania eqotshiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini, shisisa uwoyela uthose inyama. Susa epanini ubeke eceleni.
2. Thosa ugaligi, u-anyanisi no-curry powder. Faka isobho eliyimpuphu uthele amanzi kancane kancane. Faka ama-raisins ne-chutney.
3. Phindisela inyama epanini. Pheka imizuzu engu-20-30 kuze kushube.
4. Pheka i-pasta emanzini abilayo imizuzu engu-10-15. Cwenga ufake kwi-mince, ukufudumeze bese ukuvuvuzela nge-dhania.
Kwanela abangu-6
Kubiza: R35,90
Curried Carrots
Izithako
1 1/4 izinkomishi (310ml) ushukela
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) u-vinegar, 2 izinkomishi (500ml) amanzi
1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) usawoti
1kg ukherothi ocwecwe waqotshwa
0,5kg u-anyanisi
1 x 410g ithini lezingcezu zikaphayinaphu
2 izipuni (30ml) i-custard powder
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini, hlanganisa ushukela, u-vinegar, amanzi, u-curry powder nosawoti bese ukubilisa.
2. Faka ukherothi, u-anyanisi nophayinaphu, ukupheke kuze kuthambe ukherothi.
3. Kujeke, hlanganisa u-custard namanzi, ukufake epanini ulokhu ukugovuza. Kugudluze emlilweni uma sekushubile.
4. Kupholise ukufake kojeke abavalekayo.
Kwanela abangu-6
Kubiza: R20,23
Curried Neck Of Lamb With Dumplings
Izithako
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
750g umqala wenyama yemvu oqotshiwe
1 u-anyanisi
2 izipuni (30ml) u-curry powder
2 izinkomishi (500ml) iqhuzu lesobho le-mutton
2 izipuni (30ml) u-vinegar
2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) usawoti
i-ground black pepper yokunonga
2 i-celery eqotshwe ophisi
3 ukherothi oqotshwe izindilinga
1 inkomishi (250ml) u-peas oqandisiwe
Amadombolo:
Izithako
1 inkomishi (250ml) ufulawa
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) u-baking powder
1/2 i-teaspoon (2,5ml) usawoti
60g imajarini
amanzi abandayo okuxova
Indlela yokwenza
1. Epanini, shisa uwoyela uthose inyama.
2. Faka u-anyanisi no-curry powder bese ukuthosa. Faka isobho, u-vinegar, usawoti no-pepper.
3. Kumboze ukupheke ihora.
4. Amadombolo. Endishini, hlanganisa ufulawa, u-baking powder nosawoti.
5. Faka imajarini uxove ingxube ifane nezimvuthu zesinkwa. Thela amanzi ukuze kwakheke inhlama.
6. Faka ama-veg, ukhe inhlama ngesipuni uyifake epanini. Kumboze ukupheke imizuzu engu-30. (Ungasivuli isivalo ngesikhathi usakuphekile!)
