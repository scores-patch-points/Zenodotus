IZINHLOKO-DOLOBHA zomhlaba
EZINHLOKO-DOLOBHA zasemazweni amaningi kulapho uhulumeni walelo zwe kuvame ukuba uzinze khona. Kodwa kokunye izinto azilula ngalolo hlobo…
 Bolivia: I-Sucre kuseyinhloko-dolobha yomthetho sisekelo, kodwa uhulumeni wasezingeni likazwelonke kade wayishiya leyo ndawo wathuthela eLa Paz.
 Côte d’Ivoire: I-Yamoussoukro yayilungiselwe ukuba ibe yinhloko-dolobha kazwelonke ngo-1983, kodwa amahhovisi kahulumeni nawamanxusa asese-Abidjan.
 Malaysia: I-Kuala Lumpur yinhloko-dolobha yomthetho-sisekelo kodwa isizinda sokuphathwa kwesifunda sathuthelwa ePutrajaya eqhele ngamakhilomitha angu-30 ngeminyaka yo-1990. Iphalamende lilokhu liseKuala Lumpur.
 The Netherlands: I-Amsterdam yinhloko-dolobha kazwelonke yomthetho-sisekelo nakuba uhulumeni,inkantolo ephakeme nezigodlo zendlovukazi konke kuse-Hague.
 South Africa: Inhloko-dolobha yokuphatha isePitoli, eyokushaywa komthetho iseCape Town kuthi eyokugcinwa komthetho ibe seBloemfontein – kusukela ekusungulweni kwe-Union of South Africa ngo-1910.
Kuyenzeka idolobha lakhiwe ngenhloso yokulenza inhloko-dolobha njengeBrasilia eBrazil neCanberra e-Australia.
Bheka ukuthi ungaziqondanisa yini lezi zinhloko-dolobha namazwe azo (sikwenzele eyokuqala)…

Izinyoni kudingeka zindize ukuze zithole ukudla nokuba ziphunyuke ezitheni zazo. Izinyoni ezizalelayo azikwazi ukuthwala amaqanda asindayo emizimbeni yazo ngoba isisindo singazivimbela ukuba zindize.

I-cell imanzi
NGABE i-cellphone yakho ingene emanzini? Nayi indlela yokuyisindisa…
1 Yikhiphe ngokushesha emanzini. Nakuba kukhona amakhava, amanzi kungawathatha imizuzwana engu-20 ukungena ngaphakathi kwi-cellphone - ngakhokhe shesha!
2 Khipha ibhethri. Lokhu kubalulekile kakhulu. Ugesi namanzi akuhlangani. Kuningi okungavikeleka ngalokhu.
3 Khipha iSIM card. Okunye okubhalwe ekhadini lakho kungasinda. Kwabanye abantu lena yiyona into ebalulekile kune-cell uqobo.
4 Omisa ucingo lwakho. Sebenzisa ithawula noma i-paper towel ukukhipha amanzi amaningi.
5 Iyeke yome kahle. Ngeke ukwazi ukuba ukhiphe wonke amanzi ngokuyesula, ngakhoke yiyeke izomele ngokwendalo.
6 Linda. Lena yingxenye enzima - ukushiya i-cell yakho yodwa kukhishwe ibhethri neSIM card ube uyibeke endaweni lapho izokoma khona. Uma likhona ithuba lokuthi ingase iphinde isebenze, kungadingeka ukuba yome kahle kuqala. Lokho kusho cishe ukuyilinda izinsuku ezingafinyelela kwezine.
7 Ihlole. Qiniseka ukuthi yonke into ihlanzekile futhi yomile bese ubuyisela ibhethri ukuze ubone ukuthi iyasebenza yini.
Uma ucingo lwakho lungasebenzi, zama ukuli-charger ngaphandle kokulisebenzisa nebhethri. Thenga nebhethri entsha.
Uma konke lokhu kungakusebenzeli, luthathe uluhambise kumkhandi owaziwayo - mhlawumbe kuyena ungasizakala.
QAPHELA: Ngeke ube namathuba amaningi okuhlenga i-cell yakho uma ingene emanzini olwandle. Kodwa emva kokukhipha ibhethri ungazama ukuyisula ngamanzi afreshi ukuze kuphume usawoti ngaphambi kokuba uyomise ngendlela esiyibeke ngenhla.
ISEXWAYISO: Ungayishisi ngoba ibhethri ingaqhuma. Ungalokothi futhi uthi uyayomisa ngokuyifaka kwi-microwave ngoba mhlawumbe lokho kungabulala ne-microwave.

Kungani abesilisa babe nezingono?
Ngesikhathi samasonto okuqala ayisithupha kuya kwayisishiyagalombili, umbungu ungakhula ube yintombazane noma umfana.
Uma ungakhuthazwanga ama-hormones esibelethweni, kuthi lapho uba yintombazane kuphele izitho zokuba ngowesilisa.
Izingono zidaleka ebuncaneni nokuyinto eba noma kubani.
Ngoba zibalulekile kwabesimame asebekhulile, ziyaqhubeka zikhule kubona.
Izingono kumuntu wesilisa kuyenzeka zibekhona ekukhuleni kanti kulula ukuba umzimba uzigcine kunokuba ziphele ngesikhathi umfana ekhula.
Lokhu kuyenzeka kuye kufinyelele ezingeni lokuqala kwamabele amancane.[z]
