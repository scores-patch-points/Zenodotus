Ukwakha amathambo
Nika ingane yakho i-calcium
QINISEKA ukuthi ingane yakho idla ukudla okunothe nge-calcium eningi ngoba yenza amathambo akhule. Cishe u-90% wokuqina nobukhulu bamathambo ethu wenzeka ebunganeni. I-calcium futhi ibalulekile ekwenzeni amazinyo aqine abe nempilo.
Izingane zeminyaka ephakathi kuka-1 no-3 zidinga cishe izinga le-calcium engu-350mg ngosuku. Ngezinkomishi nezipuni singakubala kanjani lokhu? Nakhu ukudla nezikali - ongakunika ingane yakho ukuqinisekisa ukuthi ithola i-calcium eyanele...
I-Broccoli
Akuzona zonke izingane eziyithandayo, kodwa ukuyifakela yona ekudleni kungasiza. Cishe u-32mg wezingcezu ezingu-2-3 ze-broccoli.
Ushizi
Ushizi uthandwa yizingane zonke, ngakhoke kungakuhle ingane yakho uyisikele izikwedlana zikashizi uyifakele epuletini ukuze kube lula kuyona ukuwudla.
Cishe u-200mg ngo-30g kashizi iCheddar.
Amawolintshi
Ayikho into emnandi njengojusi wewolintshi osashukela. Unika ingane yakho umdlandla. Cishe u-75mg we-calcium nge-fruit.
Ubisi
Ubisi olushube ngokhilimu luhle kakhulu ezinganeni. Cishe u-300mg we-calcium ngo-250ml.
Inhlanzi usayidinsi osethinini
Iyanambitheka futhi ine-calcium. Nakuba zingeziningi izingane eziyithandayo, kodwa kungakuhle ingane yakho uyijwayeze isencane. Cishe u-400mg we-calcium ngo-100g wenhlanzi usayidinsi.
I-Yoghurt
Izingane eziningi ziyayithanda futhi iqukethe i-calcium. Cishe u-300mg ngo-200ml.

3 IZINDLELA ZOKWENZA
UMSEBENZI UBE LULA
1. Zivocavoce.
Zivocavoce ngaso sonke isikhathi sokukhulelwa. Ukuzivocavoca kungasiza ingane yakho ibeletheke kalula kwehle nesikhathi sokubeletha. Buza udokotela wakho noma umhlengikazi wasemtholampilo ngendlela ephephile yokuzivocavoca ongayilandela.
2. Zivocavoce enyongeni.
Ngaphandle kokuzivocavoca, ama-exercise enyonga owenza phansi ngesikhathi ukhulelwe angasiza ngokwehlisa ubude besigaba sesibili sokusikwa.
3. Ima uqonde.
Ziningi izindlela ongazivocavoca ngazo, kodwa ukuma uqonde kungehlisa izinhlungu nobude besikhathi sokusikwa.
