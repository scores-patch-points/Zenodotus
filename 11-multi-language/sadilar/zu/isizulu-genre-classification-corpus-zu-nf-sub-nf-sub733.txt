Abe-Mageu Number 1: Bakusiza umakethe ibhizinisi lakho
UKUMAKETHWA nokukhangiswa kwemikhiqizo kudlala indawo ebalulekile ebhizinisini. Ezezimakethe zibandakanya ukukhangisa nokudayisa izimpahla, imikhiqizo noma imisebenzi yomphakathi.
Konke okuthinta ibhizinisi lakho kufanele kusebenzisane ukuze lisimame. Lokhu kubandakanya:
•Izimpahla, imikhiqizo nemisebenzi yomphakathi: linhloboni, ngabe abantu bayafuna ukuthenga, likhangiswe kanjani kanye nekhwalithi layo.
•Inani lezimpahla, imikhiqizo nosizo lomphakathi: amanani aphezulu, aphansi noma akahle?
•Indawo odayisela noma osebenzela kuyo: unabantu abaningi abadlula khona noma ithule? Abantu bangakuthola kalula?
•Ungalikhangisa kanjani: ungena umuzi ngomuzi: utshela abantu ngalo, ama-poster, izikhangiso, amapheshana achaza ngalo nokunye.
•Impatho yamakhasimende: unobungani, umqondo wamabhizinisi? Awukwazi ukubekezela, ungumuntu oluhlaza owenza abantu bahambe?
Funda amakhasi kaBONA ezeluleko uthole imininingwane ngalokhu kanti emasontweni ambalwa azolandela iMageu Number 1 izokusiza ukwazi ukuzisebenzela ngokwakho uphathe ibhizinisi elinempumelelo.

INHLANZI iGlenryck Pilchards inika abantu bakuleli ukudla okunomsoco. Kukhona enotamatisi nenopelepele.
•I-Glenryck Pilchards isuke isiphekiwe ethinini, isidinga nje ukuba uyishisise. Noma yikuphi ukudla ungakupheki ngokweqile uma usufake inhlanzi.
•I-Glenryck Pilchards iyaphekeka ethinini emva kokuvalwa kwalo. Ngenxa yokuthi awukho umoya (umoya uqeda izithako zomsoco), umsoco uyagcineka ekudleni nasoketshezini, ngakhoke kufanele usebenzise usosi kumaresiphi enhlanzi.
•Ungawasusi amathambo asenhlanzini iGlenryck Pilchards. Athambile, ahlafuneka kahle kanti ane-calcium.
•Inhlanzi esethinini ayinazo izithako ezigqunyisiwe. Ithini ngokwalo yilo eligqumisa ukudla.
•Amagciwane okudla okusethinini aqedwa ngokushiswa, kanti kuvikelekile ekuboleni ngokuvaleka kwethini.
•Inhlanzi esemathinini ingahlala isikhathi esingangeminyaka emithathu uma ibekwe endaweni epholile neyomile.
•Uma ithini lingenakulimala futhi lingagqwalile, lingaqumbile, okuphakathi kusaphephile ukuthi kungadliwa.[z]
