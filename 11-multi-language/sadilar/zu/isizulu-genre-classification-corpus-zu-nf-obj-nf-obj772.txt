Mother’s Day Fruity Muesli
■ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) i-oats
■ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) i-apple juice
■ 6-8 ama-grapes asikwe ohhafu
■ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ama-cornflakes
■ ama-raisins ambalwa
■ 1 i-apula elisikwe izingcezu ezincane
■ seva ngobisi noma i-yogurt
1. Endishini hlanganisa i-oats ne-apple juice ukuyeke imizuzu engu-30.
2. Faka ama-grapes, cornflakes, raisins ne-apula ukuhlanganisise. Kuseve nobisi noma i-yogurt.
Kubiza: R8,60
Kwanela abangu-2

Vienna And Egg Breakfast
■ 2 ama-Vienna sausage
■ uwoyela wokufraya
■ 1 utamatisi
■ usawoti nophepha
■ 2 amaqanda ashayiwe
■ 2 izipuni (30ml) ubisi
■ 1 isipuni (15ml) imajarini
■ 1/4 inkomishi (65ml) ushizi ogreythiwe iCheddar
■ i-parsley yokuhlobisa eqotshiwe
1. Kwi-baking tray thosa usike amasosiji phakathi nendawo angahlukani uwagobe abe yindilinga uwabrashe ngowoyela.
2. Sika phezulu utamatisi uwunonge ubuyisele okusikile. Bhrasha utamatisi ngowoyela uwubeke kulo lelo tray.
3. Bhaka kuhhavini ongu-180oC imizuzu engu-5-10 noma utamatisi uze uthambe namaVienna ashe kahle.
4. Endishini hlanganisa amaqanda, ubisi nemajarini. Kushise kwi-microwave imizuzu engu-1-2 ukugudluze ukushaye njalo ngo-20 seconds. Okunye ongakwenza wukushisa uwoyela omncane uxove ingxube amaqanda aze avuthwe.
5. Faka utamatisi namaVienna, i-scrambled egg, uvuvuzele ngoshizi ne-parsley. Kuseve ngokushesha.
Kubiza: R8,90
Kwanela 1
Iseluleko: Ungasebenzisa ama-frankfurter sausage uma uthanda.

Sticky Chicken And Ribs
■ uwoyela wokufraya
■ 1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
■ 1 ugaligi ogayiwe
■ 2 izipuni (30ml) i-tomato sauce
■ 2 izipuni (30ml) i-barbecue sauce
■ 3 izipuni (45ml) i-syrup
■ 2/3 inkomishi (170ml) i-chicken stock
■ 1 isipuni (15ml) uviniga
■ 6 amathanga enkukhu
■ 1 isixha sama-ribs asikwe izingcezu
1. Thosa u-anyanisi nogaligi. Faka i-tomato sauce, barbecue sauce, syrup, chicken stock noviniga. Kugxabhise imizuzu engu-15-20.
2. Faka inyama kwi-roasting pan, uthele usosi ngaphezulu. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-180oC imizuzu engu-45-60.
3. Kuseve nosaladi neFrench bread.
Kubiza: R45,25

■ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) i-tomato sauce

■ 1 inkomishi (250ml) ushizi ogreythiwe iCheddar

Easy Fruit Trifle
■ 1 iphakethe lika-jelly
■ 1 inkomishi (250ml) i-fresh noma i-tinned fruit esikwe izingcezu ezincane
■ 1 iSwiss roll
■ 4 izipuni (60ml) i-apple juice
■ 2 izipuni (30ml) u-custard oyimpuphu
■ 2 1/2 izinkomishi (625ml) ubisi
■ 2 izipuni (30ml) ushukela
■ 1 inkomishi (250ml) u-cream
■ 2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-caster sugar
■ ushukela wokuhlobisa
1. Endishini enza u-jelly ngenkomishi eyodwa yamanzi ashisayo noma abandayo.
2. Faka i-fruit endishini yengilazi ufake u-jelly.
3. Usebenzisa imimmese eyehlukene, sika iSwiss roll uyihlele kahle phezu kwe-fruit, uyichele nge-apple juice.
4. Kujeke hlanganisa u-custard nobisi oluncane ukwenza inhlama ebushelelezi. Epanini bilisa ubisi olusele. Faka u-custard noshukela ulokhu ukugovuza. Uma sekushubile kugudluze ukuyeke kuphole.
5. Endishini shaya u-cream ne-caster sugar. Thela u-custard kwiSwiss roll ufake ngesipuni phezulu, uvuvuzele ngoshukela.
Kubiza: R38,55
