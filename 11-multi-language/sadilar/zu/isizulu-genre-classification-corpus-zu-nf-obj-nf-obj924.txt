Rosemary-stuffed Roast Chicken
3 ama-teaspoon (45ml) uwoyela
5 izingcezu zikabhekeni eziqotshiwe
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
3 izingcezu zesinkwa ezicwecwe zenziwa izikwedlana
3 izipuni (45ml) i-parsley eqotshiwe
1 isipuni (15ml) i-rosemary eqotshiwe
1 iqanda
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) ubisi
1 iphakethe lesobho lenkukhu eliyimpuphu
1,5kg inkukhu
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela wokwengeza
1. Epanini thosa ubhekeni no-anyanisi kuze kuthambe. Thosa nesinkwa, i-parsley ne-rosemary. Kubeke endishini yokuhlanganisa.
2. Usebenzisa imfoloko, bondela phakathi iqanda, ubisi nohhafu wesobho eliyimpuphu.
3. Kugxishe ngaphakathi enkukhwini. Endishini hlanganisa isobho eliyimpuphu elisele nowoyela wokwengeza. Kugcobe ngaphezulu enkukhwini.
4. Kuroste kuhhavini oshisiswe ngezinga elingu-180oC amahora angu-1-1 1/4 kuze kuphekeke.

Sunday Lamb Shanks
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
2 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
2 ukherothi osikwe imicu
2 izindukwana ze-celery eziqotshiwe
2 izigaxa zikagaligi ezigayiwe
1,5kg amanqina emvu
2 x 410g amathini katamatisi
2 izipuni (30ml) i-tomato sauce
2 izipuni (30ml) ushukela
1 isipuni (15ml) uviniga
usawoti nophepha omnyama omahhadla
1. Ebhodweni lensimbi (ongalibeka phezulu esitofini nongalisebenzisa kuhhavini), thosa u-anyanisi, ukherothi, i-celery nogaligi kuze kuthambe. Kugudluze ukubeke eceleni.
2. Thosa inyama ize ibe nsunjwana. (Ngeza omunye uwoyela uma kunesidingo.) Ngeza ingxube ka-anyanisi, utamatisi, i-tomato sauce, ushukela noviniga. Kumboze ukubhake kuhhavini ongu-180oC imizuzu engu-30.
3. Yehlisa umlilo ube ngu-160oC, ukubhake esinye isikhathi esingamahora angu-1 1/2-2. Kunonge ukuseve nerayisi noma nophuthu.

Beef Roast
1,5kg i-topside noma i-beef roast
usawoti nophepha
2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-mustard
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela wokwengeza
1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
125g ama-mushrooms asikwe izingcezu
1 iphakethe le-brown onion soup powder
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) amanzi
1. Ebhodini, khuhla inyama ngosawoti, uphepha ne-mustard. Kufake epanini lokurosta ukuvuvuzele ngowoyela. Kupheke kuhhavini onezinga lokushisa elingu-180oC amahora angu-1 1/2.
2. Epanini shisa amanye amafutha, uthose u-anyanisi uze uthambe. Faka i-mushroom uthose imizuzu emibili.
3. Faka isobho eliyimpuphu ukuyeke kugxabhe imizuzu emihlanu.
4. Kuthele ngaphezulu, ukupheke eminye imizuzu engu-30.
