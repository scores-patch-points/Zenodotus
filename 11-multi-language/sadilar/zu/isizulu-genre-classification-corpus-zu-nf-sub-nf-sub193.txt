Ukumelana nokuzila
Siyabonga ngodaba enasiphathela lona ngoDecember 2008 oluthi Ukumelana Nosizo Lokuzila. Uma kufika uDecember bonke abantu bangena egiyeni lomoya wamaholidi ngoba besuke bezimisele ngokuthokozela isikhathi sikaKhisimusi, kodwa ngeshwa akuvamile ukuba njalo kithina sonke.
Kwabaningi uDecember yisikhathi sokukhathazeka okukhulu. Lolu daba lwenu lwabasiza kakhulu labo ababebhekene nesikhathi sokulila. Lwabaqinisa idolo ukuba bakwazi ukubhekana nalesi simo ngesikhathi samaholidi, lapho amazinga okulahlekelwa ephezulu.
Kulabo abalahlekelwa abathandiweyo babo, ngibona kungakuhle babe nebhokisi abazoligcina balifake izinto zikamufi (memory box). Bangafaka kulo izithombe zakhe, izincwadi zonishiyile, izincwadi noma yini lowo muntu akade eyithanda - kuthi lapho nizwa nimkhumbule nilivule nixoxe ngokuzonikhumbuza yena.
Siyethemba leli bhokisi lesikhumbuzo lingasiza kuleso sikhathi sokuzila. Ngaphezu kwalokho, akulahlwa mbeleko ngakufelwa - impilo kudingeka ukuba iqhubeke sazi ukuthi abasishiyile bayohlale benathi ngokomoya ngisho nasemva kokufa.
E Conradie, Pretoria
