Umdidiyeli yena uthumela lo mbiko kwi-network yakhe ngendlela yeSMS.
Amaphoyisa nawo ayaziswa ngalokhu. Olunye usizo olutholakalayo lubandakanya inkinobho yesimo esiphuthumayo se-eblockwatch ekwenza ukuba ukwazi ukushayela inombolo obathinta kuyona ngqo kanjalo nabangani bakho abane bathole amaSMS abatshela ukuthi udinga usizo.
