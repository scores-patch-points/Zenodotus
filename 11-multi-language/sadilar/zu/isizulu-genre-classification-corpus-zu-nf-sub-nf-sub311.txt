zulu.1675.asthma
BONA - August 2007
Kungcono ukuvikela kunokwelapha
indaba nguGRACE KHUZWAYO
Ukungaphefumuli kahle kuyethusa ngezinye izikhathi..
AMAPHAPHU abantu abane-asthma awezwani nezinto eziningi ezitholakala emoyeni. Lokho kuzwela kwawo kuvala imigudu yokuphefumula kungaphefumuleki kahle.
Uma umuntu ongenayo i-asthma engena egumbini elinezintuli akabi nankinga kodwa uma kungena umuntu onalesi sifo kulelo gumbi angagula.
Amazinga okugadla kwalesi sifo ayashiyana, kwabanye asibi mandla kakhulu kanti kwabanye siba mandla. Abanye abantu abanaso, kungenzeka bangazweli kakhulu uma behlangana nezinto ezingasiqubula kanti abanye bangazwela.
Kuningi okungenza umuntu ahlaselwe yi-asthma.
Kulezo zinto singabala: isiqedaphunga, uboya bezilwane ezifuywa ekhaya, (noma ukuba seduze kwaleso silwane), intuthu kagwayi noma yomlilo, izitshalo, izimbali, izimpaphe, izintuli, ukushintsha kwesimo sezulu, ukuzivocavoca ngokweqile, i-stress nezinye izifo.
Izimpawu ze-asthma
Udokotela wakho kuphela ongabona uma uphethwe yilesi sifo kodwa bheka lezi zimpawu ezilandelayo:
ukuphefumulela phezulu
ukukhwehlela, ikakhulu ebusuku
ukunqamuka komoya
ukucinana kwesifuba
ukukhathala uma uzivocavoca.
Ukuzivikela ekufeni
Ucwaningo lukhomba ukuthi elaseSouth Africa lilele isihlanu emhlabeni ngokwezinga lokufa kwabantu ababulawa yi-asthma.
Okubuhlungu kakhulu wukuthi lokho kufa kwabantu bekungagwemeka uma umuntu ezoba nolwazi ngobungozi balesi sifo asebenzise nemithi efanele.
Kuningi okuyimbangela yokuthi abadala nabancane bagcine bebulawe yi-asthma. Kufa kakhulu abantu abampofu, labo abahlala emijondolo.
Lokho kudalwa wukungabi nalwazi olugcwele nge-asthma nangezimpawu zayo nokungakwazi ukuthola usizo lokwelashwa.
Ezinye izinto eziba nomthelela kulokhu yintuthu ephuma ezitofini zikapharafini nokuminyana kwabantu okusabalalisa amagciwane kalula. (Abantu abane-asthma basengozini kakhulu uma bephethwe wumkhuhlane.)
Okunye okuba nomthelela kulesi sifo wukungaqeqesheki kahle kwabasebenzi bezempilo abahluleka wukubona izimpawu zalesi sifo - umuntu one-asthma kuye kuthiwe unomkhuhlane odalwa amakhaza noma i-bronchitis.
Abantu abane-asthma bavame ukungayidli kahle imithi.
Ukuthola usizo
Nakuba kudingeka ukuba izibhedlela zikahulumeni nezikhungo zezempilo zibe nolwazi lokuhlola umuntu onalesi sifo athole nemithi yokwelashwa, lokho akuvamile ukwenzeka.
"Amaphampu e-asthma awavamile ukutholakala ngenxa yokushoda kwezimali. Abantu abasemaphandleni abakutholi ukwelashwa okufanele," kusho uSharon Kling ongudokotela wezingane eTygerberg Children's Hospital nongusihlalo we-Allergy Society Of South Africa (ALLSA).
"Amakhambi e-asthma asohlwini lwemithi esemqoka eSA kanti umphakathi kumele ukulwele ukwelashwa ngendlela efanele."
Ukuzivikela
Abantu abane-asthma bangaphila impilo ejwayelekile uma begwema izinto ezenza bahlaselwe yilesi sifo basebenzise nemithi yabo ngendlela efanele.
Abantu abane-asthma kufanele baqaphele babheke izimpawu zalesi sifo. Kubalulekile ukuzazi izinto ezenza ukuba uhlaselwe yilesi sifo ukuze uzigweme.
Uma usutholile ukuthi yini eyenza sikuhlasele, gwema izinto ezikudalela sona. Ukuhlala uphilile kukuvikela ezifweni eziningi...
Phuza amanzi kakhulu udle izithelo nama-veg afreshi.
Zivocavoce kodwa ungazikhandli kakhulu. (Qiniseka ukuthi umzimba wakho uyafudumala ubuye uphole kahle emva kokuzivocavoca.)
Ungahlali nabantu abanomkhuhlane noma imfiva.
Hlanzeka, geza izandla ukuze uzivikele emagciwaneni.
Ikhaya lakho malingabi nezintuli, hlanza u-carpet nemibhede usule phansi njalo.
Ukwelashwa
Uma sekutholakele ukuthi unalesi sifo uyatshelwa ukuthi yini engakusiza uma sikuhlasela. Ozokwelashwa ngako kuzoya ngokuthi isifo simandla kangakanani kuwena. Okungakusiza okusetshenziswa kakhulu nokusebenzayo ekwelapheni i-asthma yiphampu (inhaler), kodwa ungelashwa nangemithi ephuzwayo noma welashwe nge-inhaler nemithi ephuzwayo.
Kusemqoka ukuthi abanalesi sifo basebenzise imithi yabo ngendlela abayalwe ngayo baqiniseke ukuthi abazelapha ngako kuhlale kuseduze.
Zimbili izindlela zokwelashwa kwabane-asthma: ukuzivikela nokuyidambisa.
Ukuzivikela kwehlisa izinga lokwakheka kwezilonda esifubeni kungabi lula ukuthi i-asthma ikuhlasele. Sebenzisa okuzokuvikela nsuku zonke ukuze kusebenze kahle.
Okokudambisa lesi sifo kuthathwa njengosizo lokuqala ekwelapheni izimpawu noma ukuhlaselwa yilesi sifo. Kubuye kusize ngokuvula imigudu yomoya ohlaselwe yilesi sifo aphefumule kalula. [z]

Okuyiqiniso nge-asthma
I-asthma yisifo esijwayelekile esiphatha oyedwa ezinganeni ezilishumi nakubantu abadala.
I-asthma esinye sezifo eziyingozi ezinganeni kanti yiso esihamba phambili ekwenzeni izingane zilove ezikoleni.
Iningi labantu lenza iphutha ngokucabanga ukuthi i-asthma isifo samakhaza.
Izingane ezihlala emakhaya anabazali ababhemayo, zisengcupheni yokuphathwa yi-asthma.
Omama ababhemayo lapho bekhulelwe, bazibeka engcupheni yokuthola abantwana abanalesi sifo.
I-asthma yisifo okukholakala ukuthi silufuzo.
I-asthma ayelapheki kodwa iyalawuleka ngokusetshenziswa kwemithi efanele.
Ezinye izingane ezine-asthma kungenzeka zibe nezimpawu ezimbalwa nezigigaba ezimbalwa zokuhlaseleka ngesikhathi zikhula, ekubeni ezinye ziba nalesi sifo impilo yazo yonke.
