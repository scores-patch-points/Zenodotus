Zulu.2960.stadiums
Isikhathi sidliwe yinja njengoba kulungiswa izinkundla zebhola eSouth Africa ukuze zibe sezingeni lomhlaba
ENYE YEZINSELELO elaseSA ebelibhekene nayo ekulungiseleleni imidlalo yeWorld Cup ka-2010 bekungeyokwakha izinkundla okuyodlalelwa kuzona. Nakuba izwe lethu lizigqaja ngokuba nezinkundla zeqophelo eliphezulu, ubuncane bazo kanye nezindawo ezakhiwe kuzona kwenza ukuba zingakulungeli ukusingatha lo mqhudelwano.
Ngaphandle kwenhloso yabagqugquzeli balo mqhudelwano yokwakha izinkundla eziningi okuyodlalelwa kuzona, bafuna nokuthi leli lizwe libe nezinkundla eziseqophelweni eliphezulu eziyosiza nesizukulwane esizayo.
Kodwa umsebenzi wokwakha izinkundla ezinhlanu ezintsha eCape Town, eThekwini, ePort Elizabeth, ePolokwane naseNelspruit unezingqinamba.
Kuthiwa uhlelo lokukhishwa kwamathenda aluhambanga kahle kanti kunendida mayelana nodaba lwamabhajethi, ukuya enkantolo kwezakhamizi ezingaphathekile kahle, ukuteleka kwabasebenzi, ukungaqinisekiswa kwezokuphepha lapho kwakhiwa khona, ukushoda kwezimpahla zokwakha nabasebenzi abafundele ukwakha.
Izindawo ezibe nenkinga kakhulu yiTheku neCape Town kodwa ithimba leFIFA elaliholwa nguJerome Valcke ongunobhala wayo lawuncoma umsebenzi osuwenziwe ezinkundleni.
“Ngigculiseke kakhulu ngamalungiselelo,” kusho yena.
Okwamanje, kubukeka sengathi yiCape Town kuphela enenkinga yokuhlangabezana nesikhathi esimiswe yiFIFA sokuba kushaye u-October 2009 izinkundla seziphelile – kodwa noma kunjalo liyosalela emumva ngezinyanga ezimbalwa.
Ake sibheke ukuthi ukwakhiwa kwezinkundla kuhamba kanjani…
