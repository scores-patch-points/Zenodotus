‘Abesimame banganqoba’
Kubukhazikhazi bomcimbi woSuku lwabesiMame wokwethulwa kweMotora U9 phone entsha enombala o-pink no-purple, isikhulumi esiqavile kwakunguJenna Clifford waseSA onebhizinisi lobucwebe ophethe umkhankaso weDream Big okhuthaza abesimame ukuba bazibambele mathupha ezintweni ezibalulekile ezithinta impilo yabo.
Yilezi zifundo azidlulisela kwabesimame nokuyizifundo azithole njengowesimame onebhizinisi:
•Phatha ibhizinisi lakho ngokuthembeka nawe uzithembe.
•Okwenzayo kwenze ngothando, ube ngowesimame othanda ibhizinisi lakhe.
•Ungancintisani nabesilisa ebhizinisini ngoba ngeke uphumelele.
•Funda izincwadi ezifanele ukuze uthole ulwazi oludingayo.
•Zinikele kokwenzayo.
•Ungenzi izinto eziningi – yenza okukodwa ngesikhathi.
•Qikelela ungenzi amaphutha.
•Hleleka.
•Zazi izinjongo zakho uzibekele isikhathi ukuze zifezeke.
•Vuselela ukuzithemba kwakho ngokungagxili ezintweni ezikuhlulayo.
•Sabalalisa umqondo wakho ufunde ukuhlalela ethembeni.
•Hlala nabantu abacabanga njengawe ukuze uhlale ukhuthazekile usebenzise nama-tape aqoshiwe akhuthazayo.
■ Neliswa Mzimba
URosie Motene uthi akayifuni nakancane into ezoma phakathi kwakhe nendlela yempumelelo. “Yisikhathi sokuba abesimame basizane,” kuqhuba yena. Lapha ukanye nabasebenzi bakwaBONA uBusisiwe Motlhasedi noNeliswa Mzimba.

Hamba kahle,
Isaac Hayes
SITHANDA ukuzwakalisa ukudabuka kwethu ngokushiywa emhlabeni ngu-Isaac Hayes ngomhlaka 10 August kulo nyaka. Igalelo lakhe emculweni we-soul ne-funk lingelinganiswe. Amanye ama-albhamu akhe aba nedumela yiHot Buttered Soul (1969) neBlack Moses (1971). Enye into eyamakhela udumo kakhulu yingoma yakhe ayeyidlala efilimini ethi Shaft. UHayes wathola i-Academy Award emkhakheni weBest Original Song ngeShaft, nokwamenza uMmelika omnyama wokuqala ukuwina i-Oscar hhayi emkhakheni wezokudlala kumafilimu. UHayes waphinde wabonakala kumafilimu nasemidlalweni eminingi yeTV. Yiphimbo lakhe elalizwakala kowayedlala indawo ka “Chef” kuSouth Park.
