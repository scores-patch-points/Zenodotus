Okwenzekile:
Amagobolondo amaqanda anokubizwa ngokuthi yi-calcium carbonate. Yilokho okuwaqinisayo.
U-vinegar uyi-acid ebizwa nge-acetic acid.
Uma i-calcium carbonate (igobolondo) ne-acetic acid (u- vinegar) kuhlangana, kwakheka ama-chemical akhipha i-carbon dioxide (i-gas).
Kungenzeka ukuthi uwabonile amabhamuza ngesikhathi ufaka iqanda ku-vinegar - lawo mabhamuza ayi-carbon dioxide.
Ama-chemical akheka kuze kusebenze yonke i-carbon eseqandeni - lokho kuthatha usuku.
Uma ukhipha iqanda ku-vinegar lisuke lithambile ngoba kuphuma yonke i-carbon eqandeni ibe yilawo mabhamuza amancane.
Zama lokhu...
Beka lelo qanda etafuleni usuku lonke, lithinte. Seliqinile futhi!
I-calcium esale egobolondweni ithathe i-carbon ebikwi-carbon dioxide esemoyeni esiwuphefumulayo.
