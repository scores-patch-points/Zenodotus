NAKUBA bawina iWorld Cup ngo-2006, kodwa izinto bezingabahambeli kahle ikakhulu emva kokuthatha umhlalaphansi kwabadlali abaningi.
Phambili leli qembu ligabe ngo ‘nkonko’ olungu-Luca Toni.
Ngeshwa lehlulwa kwi-2008 European Championships kuma-quarterfinals.
I-Italy ingamelana nanoma yiliphi izwe kanti kulo mqhudelwano weConfederations Cup ingenye yamaqembu abhekiwe.
Umdlalo waleli qembu neBrazil eLoftus Versfeld ngoJune 21 ngomunye oyoba yingqophamlando yalo mqhudelwano.
■ OKUFANELE ABHEKWE: Andrea Pirlo (AC Milan, Italy)

Lo mdlali oneminyaka engu-29 uthathwa njengochwepheshe wama-free-kick kanti useshaye amagoli amahle ewashaya ekude.
