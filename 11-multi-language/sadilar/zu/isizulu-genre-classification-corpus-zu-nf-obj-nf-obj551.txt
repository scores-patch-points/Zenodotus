zulu.2405.frozen
Ama-veg aqandisiwe
uwasebenzisa ngobuhlakani. Zama lezi
Creamy Beef Ragout
Izithako
2 ama-teaspoon (10ml) i-spice se-steak and chops
750g isiteki sesitshulu
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) iqhuzu lesobho lenkomo
2 utamatisi osuswe isikhumba waqotshwa
2 ugaligi ogxotshiwe
1 inkomishi (250ml) ukherothi
1 inkomishi (250ml) u-peas
1 isipuni (15ml) i-corn flour, ehlanganiswe namanzi amancane
1 inkomishi (250ml) ubisi
1 inkomishi (250ml) i-shell noodles, ephekiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Bhuqa inyama ebhodini eline-spice. Beka eceleni imizuzu engu-30. Qoba isiteki. Epanini, shisa uwoyela uthose inyama.
2. Faka isobho, utamatisi nogaligi. Mboza upheke inyama ize ithambe.
3. Faka ukherothi no-peas. Pheka imizuzu engu-3. Shubisa i-gravy nge-corn flour. Pheka imizuzu engu-3. Faka ubisi nama-noodles. Fudumeza kungabili.
Kubiza: R45,80
Kwanela abangu-4-6
Country Herb Casserole
Izithako
3 izipuni (45ml) ufulawa
1/4 i-teaspoon (1ml) i-white pepper
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) usawoti
1kg i-stewing lamb noma i-mutton
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) ugaligi ogxotshiwe
4 amazambane acwecwe aqotshwa
6 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
1/2 u-green pepper oqotshiwe
2 izinkomishi (500ml) iqhuzu lesobho lenkukhu
2 izipuni (30ml) i-syrup
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-dried mixed herbs
usawoti wokunonga
2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-frozen mixed vegetables
Indlela yokwenza
1. Endishini, nonga ufulawa ngo-pepper nosawoti. Mboza inyama kahle ngale ngxube.
2. Shisa uwoyela epanini. Thosa inyama nogaligi. Susa inyama epanini uyibeke kwi-casserole dish. Faka amazambane, u-anyanisi no-green pepper.
3. Faka ufulawa osele epanini. Govuza. Faka isobho. Gxabhisa ugovuze. Faka i-syrup, ama-herbs nosawoti.
4. Thela ingxube enyameni nasemazambaneni. Mboza upheke kuhhavini oshisiswe ngo-180oC u-1 1/2 hour.
5. Faka i-mixed veg kwi-casserole. Mboza ubhake imizuzu engu-10. Seva ngokushesha.
Kubiza: R47,80

Neck Of Lamb With Herb Dumplings
Izithako
Isitshulu
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
1-1 1/2 umqala wemvu noma i-mutton
1 u-anyanisi ocwecwe waqotshwa
2 izinkomishi (500ml) isobho lenkukhu elifudunyeziwe
1 inkomishi (250ml) u-peas oqandisiwe
1 inkomishi (250ml) u-green beans oqandisiwe
3 amazambane acwecwe aqotshwa
1/2 i-teaspoon (2ml) i-white pepper
usawoti wokunonga
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-mixed dried herbs
1 isipuni (15ml) i-corn flour ehlanganiswe namanzi
Amadombolo
ibinzana likasawoti
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-mixed herbs
2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-self-raising flour
2 izipuni (30ml) i-margarine
3/4 inkomishi (185ml) ubisi
Indlela yokwenza
1. Isitshulu. Shisa amafutha epanini. Thosa inyama. Faka u-anyanisi uthose umzuzu owodwa. Faka isobho. Mboza upheke u-1 1/2 hours.
2. Faka ama-vegetables, u-pepper, usawoti nama-herbs. Shubisa isitshulu nge-corn flour.
3. Amadombolo. Endishini, hlanganisa usawoti, ama-herbs nofulawa. Faka i-margarine ngeminwe. Faka ubisi kube yinhlama eqinile.
4. Ebhodini elivuvuzelwe ngofulawa, ndlala inhlama ibe lugqinsi olungu-1cm. Qoba noma uyigoqe ime ngendlela oyithandayo.
5. Beka amadombolo phezu kwenyama. Mboza upheke imizuzu engu-15-20. Seva ngokushesha.
Kubiza: R43,90
Kwanela abangu-4-6
Masala Vegetable And Bean Stew
Izithako
1 inkomishi (250ml) i-soya beans (ecwiliswe ubusuku bonke)
1/4 inkomishi (60ml) uwoyela
2 u-anyanisi ocwecwe waqotshwa
2 izigaxa ugaligi ogxotshiwe
1 isipuni (15ml) u-curry powder
2 izinkomishi (500ml) i-frozen mixed vegetables
2 izinkomishi (500ml) iqhuzu lesobho lama-vegetables
1 x 410g ithini likatamatisi oqotshiwe
1 isipuni (15ml) ushukela
usawoti no-pepper wokunonga
Indlela yokwenza
1. Pheka i-soya beans emanzini abilayo isikhathi esingangehora. Cwenga ubeke eceleni.
2. Kwelinye ipani, shisa uwoyela. Faka u-anyanisi nogaligi. Thosa kuze kuthambe. Faka u-curry powder nama-veg. Hlanganisa kahle.
3. Faka isobho, utamatisi, ubhontshisi noshukela. Nonga. Mboza upheke imizuzu engu-35.
Kubiza: R20,75
Kwanela abangu-4-6
Quick Spicy Vegetable Cabbage
Izithako
1 isipuni (15ml) uwoyela
1 u-anyanisi ocwecwe waqotshwa
1 isigaxa ugaligi oqotshiwe
1 u-green pepper oqotshiwe
1 iqhuzu i-chilli beef
1/2 iklabishi eliqotshiwe
1 inkomishi (250ml) ama-vegetables aqandisiwe
Indlela yokwenza
1. Shisa uwoyela epanini. Thosa u-anyanisi, ugaligi no-green pepper. Faka iqhuzu lesobho. Govuza kuze kuncibilike.
2. Faka iklabishi uthose. Faka ama-veg. Thosa imizuzu engu-5.
3. Seva nerayisi noma nophuthu.
Kubiza: R8,10
Kwanela abangu-4-6
Maize Meal Slices With Roasted Vegetables
Izithako
Maize Meal Slices
1 ilitha ubisi
1 1/2 izinkomishi (375ml) impuphu
1 iqhuzu lesobho lenkukhu
1 iqanda elishayiwe
2 izipuni (30ml) i-margarine
Ama-vegetables
2 izipuni (30ml) uwoyela
1kg iphakethe ama-veg aqandisiwe
2 u-anyanisi ocwecwe waqotshwa
usawoti no-pepper wokunonga
1 i-teaspoon (5ml) u-curry powder
1 isipuni (15ml) i-syrup
Indlela yokwenza
1. Thela ubisi epanini ugxabhise. Thela impuphu nesobho. Yehlisa umlilo. Pheka kuze kuvuthwe. Faka iqanda ne-margarine.
2. Ndlala upulasitiki ethinini likalofu. Thela engxubeni. Goqa ngopulasitiki. Mboza ngendwangu ukupholise.
3. Ama-vegetables. Brasha ama-veg ngowoyela. Nonga ngosawoti, u-pepper no-curry powder. Consisela nge-syrup. Rosta kuhhavini oshisiswe ngo-180oC imizuzu engu-15.
4. Qoba izingcezu ze-maize meal uma ulofu usuqinile. Beka ama-veg arostiwe ocezwini ngalunye.
Kubiza: R23,30
