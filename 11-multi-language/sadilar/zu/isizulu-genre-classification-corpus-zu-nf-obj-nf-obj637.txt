Broccoli Salad
Isaladi
* 2 izigaxa i-broccoli, ehleshulwe ophisi abancane yafakwa emanzini abilayo imizuzwana engu-2-3. Cwenga uyihlambulule ngamanzi abandayo
* 1 x 125g iphakethe ubhekeni oqotshwe wathoswa
* 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ama-raisins
* 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) isinkwa esidala esiyizingcezu ezincane ezithosiwe esibizwa ngokuthi ama-croutons (kunamasuphamakethe adayisa ama-croutons kodwa ayabiza)
I-Dressing
* 2 izipuni (30ml) u-vinegar
* 2 izipuni (30ml) ushukela
* 1 inkomishi (250ml)
  i-mayonnaise
1. Faka i-broccoli, ubhekeni, ama-raisins nesinkwa endishini, hlanganisa.
2. I-Dressing. Ncibilikisa ushukela no-vinegar epanini elibekwe esitofini esingashisi kakhulu. Susa emlilweni, faka i-mayonnaise. Thela esaladini ngaphambi kokuseva.

Isaladi

I-Dressing
* 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) uwoyela
* 1/4 inkomishi (60ml) ushukela

Ukugeza u-lettuce
namahlamvu esaladi
Kugezisise. Komise nge-tea towel. Kubeke esitsheni esinesivalo noma kupulasitiki. Beka uphisi we-paper towel esinqeni sesitsha noma kupulasitiki ukuze kuphume umswakama ongadingeki. Faka efrijini ihora elilodwa ngaphambi kokuseva ukuze kuklamuzele kahle.
I-iceberg lettuce
■ Bamba u-lettuce ngesandla, ushaye ngesiqu sawo phezu kwekhabethe, donsa isiqu ngaphakathi kuwona. Wuhlambulule emanzini ahambayo uvuzise amanzi uwubhekise phansi.
■ Ungakasevi, hlephula u-lettuce (ungawuqobi) ube ophisi. Ukuwuqoba kwenza ube nsundu emachosheni.
■ Ungawubeki ngaphandle isikhathi esingaphezulu kwemizuzu engu-15 ngaphambi kokuseva.
Ukuhlanganisa isaladi
Hlanganisa u-lettuce nama-veg aluhlaza ne-dressing useve ngokushesha noma ukubeke endishini yesaladi uthele i-dressing sekusetafuleni. Isaladi iba manzi uma ufake i-dressing kakhulu.

Fancy Chef’s Salad
Isaladi
* 1 u-lettuce ogezwe wahleshulwa
* 1/2 u-cucumber oqotshiwe
* 10 i-cherry tomatoes, engohhafu
* 1 ukherothi ogreythiwe
* 1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
* 3 amaqanda abiliswe aqotshwa
* 1 ucezu ushizi iCheddar eqotshiwe
* 5 ubhekeni othoswe waqotshwa
* isinkwa esingamaqhuzu (croutons)
I-Dressing
* 3 izipuni (45ml) uwoyela
* 2 izipuni (30ml) u-vinegar
* 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) i-mustard
* usawoti no-pepper wokunonga
1. Endishini, hlanganisa u-lettuce, u-cucumber notamatisi.
2. Beka ukherothi ngaphezulu, ubeke u-anyanisi namaqanda. Vuvuzela ushizi, isinkwa nobhekeni.
3. I-Dressing. Kujeke, hlanganisa uwoyela, u-vinegar ne-mustard. Nonga ukuvuvuzele kusaladi.
