BONA ZULU – March 2007
UFikiswa usebe nelikhulu igalelo ezinhlanganweni eziningi ezithuthukisa umphakathi njengosonhlalakahle owaba nguRegional Director for Pietermaritzburg Social Welfare and Development njengoba manje eyi-Head of the KZN Department of Works. Umnyango wakhe usewakhe kakhulu ukuze kube yihhovisi elinentuthuko kakhulu ngaphansi kwezemiSebenzi yoMphakathi.
Kusithokozisa kakhulu njengoba ezokube esinika isikhathi sakhe, esicobelela nangamakhono nolwazi - ikakhulu abangamalungu e-BONA Empowerment Club. UBONA uzimisele ngokuba unibone nonke eThekwini.
NGO-MARCH i-BONA Empowerment Club izobe ihlangene eThekwini. Isiqubulo sale nyanga, ukuthunga, izimboni zokuthunga, ama-design, ifeshini yase-Africa, izimpahla zakuleli eziyiswa kwamanye amazwe kanjalo nomsebenzi wezandla.
Izindaba ezinhle kubo bonke abasiki bengqephu bakuleli wukuthi umsebenzi wakuleli udingeka kakhulu emazweni angaphandle.
Ngisho ngabe ibhizinisi lakho usanda kulisungula, ziningi izindlela ongenza ngazo umehluko, sizokukhombisa lezo zindlela kulo mcimbi wale nyanga.
I-BONA Empowerment Club isiza amalungu ayo ukuba athole izindlela zokucobelelana ngamakhono awo nabantu bakuleli nabasemhlabeni jikelele.
Sifuna ukukukhuthaza ukuze uphumelele.
U-BONA uzizwa uhloniphekile ngokwamukela uDkt Fikiswa Beatrice Madlopha, ongumphathi woMnyango wezeMisebenzi eKZN njengesikhulumi sosuku kulo mcimbi waseThekwini.
BAKE YOUR BEST WITH SASKO FLOUR
NJENGABAXHASI abakhulu bama-workshop e-2007 BONA Bake and Cook for Profit, uSasko Flour usiphathele usiko oseludonse iminyaka engu-70 ekubeni sebeqalile ukucobelelana ngolwazi namalungu uwasiza hhayi ekubeni ababhaki abaphambili nje kuphela, kodwa futhi abe ngosomabhizinisi abaphambili besimame.
Wonke umuntu uyakuthanda okumnandi okubhakiwe, ngisho ngabe amakhasimende akho noma abomndeni wakho - ngakhoke thembela kwiSasko Flour ukuze ukhombise ukuthi konke kwenzeka kalula kanjani. I-Sasko Flour ikhukhumala ngokukhulu ukushesha kanti yenza amaresiphi akho anambitheke kamnandi.
Hlanganyela nathi kuma-workshop eBONA Bake and Cook for Profit ukuze uthole ithuba lokufunda okuningi odinga ukukwazi ngeSasko Flour.
Ufulawa wakwaSasko wenziwe waba ngowekhwalithi eliphambili kuleli kanjalo nokolo osezingeni eliphezulu wakwamanye amazwe.
Bukela uhlelo olusha lweSasko Flour kumabonakude lwezihlabani kuSABC 2 njalo ngoLwesithathu ebusuku nokuluhlelo oluqala ngo-6 ntambama. Lolu hlelo lwaziwa ngokuthi yiMara And Friends kanti lwethulwa yisihlabani somdlalo uMuvhango uMara Louw.
Hlanganyela noMara nezihambeli zakhe njengoba bezobe bekhuluma ngesikhathi bethokozela okwehla esiphundu okubhakwe ngeSasko Flour.
