Ngesonto eledlule wawina umklomelo wokuqala ngomsiko wakhe wento yokwembatha yabesimame eyenziwe nge-mohair embukisweni wefeshini ePort Elizabeth.
USimpiwe ukholwa wukuthi ukuwina kwakhe sekumbeke endleleni ebange empumelelweni ebange empumelelweni yokufezeka kwephupho lami lokusungula isikhungo sokuqeqesha abantu abangasebenzi endaweni yangakubo eJansenville e-Eastern Cape nokuba azisungulele ilebuli yakhe yezimpahla.
UJansenville kulapho kutyholakala khona i-mohair kuleli lizwe. Ngaphandle kokukhula nomhlambi wezimbuzi nokuzwa umkhulu wakhe ehwebelana nabanye abadayisi nokuyilapho kwakugcina khona ukubona inqubo yalokhu.
“Angazi ukuthi yini abebeyenza ngalokhu emnva kokuba sibadayisele,” kusho yena. Emva kokuzama imisebenzana embalwa, kwakuya yilapho lapho ujmkhuklu ayephinde apcabange indaba ye-mohair futhi - kulokhu ecabanga ngendlela yefeshini. wathola ukuthi i-mohair ihanjiswa emazweni afana no-Europe naseNtshnalanga nokuyilapho ifike idayiswe khona kwenziwe ngayo amasudi abesilisa nabesimame.
“Ngathola ukuthi nginalo ikhona lokuthunga nokuzenzela ezami izinto zokugqoka. Abanye balibona ithalente lami, ngaqal;a ngabathungela. Ngazibona ukuthi ngingaliqala ibhiZinis lami elincane kodwa ngaleso sikhathi ngangisathunga ntgesandla,” kucha uSimpiwe.
Ngezinye izikhathi wayekwazi ukuba awuboleke umshini wokuthunga.
Ugogo wakhe walibona ithalente lakhe, wathi maenze umsebenzi wokuba ngumsiki wengqephu. wamthengela umshini wakhe wokuqala, kwathi lapho uSimpiwe eseneminyaka engu-28 wafaka isicelo sokwenza izifundo zokusikwa kwengqephu e-NMMU ePort Elizabeth.
izikhathi zazinzima kodwa umkhuklu wakhe wakwazi ukumkhokhela ezinye zezifundo zakhe ngosizo lomsebenzi wakhe wokulima, kanti nabeVolkswagen Community Trust kukhona abakuphonsa esivivaneni.
Kulokhu sibonga amamaki akhe aphezulu nokwenza ukuba abeMohair South Africa babe ngabaxhasi bakhe abakhulu. Kungakho ebelokhu egxile ekusebenziseni i-mohair emikhiqizweni yakhe.

“IMohair wuboya bendalo, akudingeki nokuba uze ubulale ukuze ubuthole. Ungabuthaka nanoma yiluuphi uhlobo lwendwangu ngoba buhlala isikhathi ezide,” kusho yena.
Yini elandelayo? “Unyaka wami wesithathu enyuvesi uzobandakanya uchunge lwe-mohair oluzobiza kakhulu ngenxa yokubiza kwendwangu. Ngizobe ngisikela aabantu beqophelo eliphezulu ngikwazxi futhi nokuxhumana nabantu abakhulu,” kusho yena.

EMINYAKENI engu-15 edlule uSimpiwe Gwebusha wayengumfana efunda emaphandleni. Ntambama wayehlala elandela umkhulu wakhe epulazini lakhe eliseKaroo okwakufuywe kulona izimbuzi ze-Angora ukuze kutholakale uboya bazo be-mohair obumhlophe.
Ngesonto eledlule wawina umklomelo wokuqala ngokuthunga kwakhe izimpahla zabesimame ze-mohair embukisweni owawusePort Elizabeth.
USimpiwe ukholwa wukuthi ukuwina kwakhe kumbeke endleleni yokufeza amaphupho akhe okuvula isikhungo sokuqeqesha abangasebenzi kubo eJansenville, e-Eastern Cape enze nemithungo ezobizwa ngaye.
USimpiwe ukhumbula ukuthi nakuba akhula nomhlambi wezimbuzi, wayegcina ngokubukela umkhulu wakhe edayisela ababefika kubo bezozithenga.
“Ngangingakaze ngicabange ukuthi yini ababeyenza nge-mohair uma sesibadayisele,” kusho yena.
Waze wacabanga ngayo emva kokuba esezame imisebenzi eminingi – wacabanga ukuyisebenzisa kwezefeshini. Wezwa ukuthi ifayibha ithunyelwa e-Europe nasemazweni aseMpumalanga kwenziwe ngayo amasudi amadoda nawabesimame.
“Ngathola ukuthi nginekhono lokushintsha nokuthunga izingubo zami,” kuchaza uSimpiwe. “Abanye balibona ikhono lami nabo ngabathungela. Nganginephupho lokuvula ibhizinisi lami kodwa ngaleso sikhathi ngangithunga ngesandla.”
Ugogo wakhe walibona ithalente lakhe wathi akenze umsebenzi wokuthunga. Wamthengela umshini wokuthunga kanti wayeneminyaka engu-28 ngesikhathi enza izifundo zokuthunga eNMMU ePE.
“I-mohair yifayibha yemvelo kanti akukho okubulalayo nokucekela phansi ukuze uyithole. Ithambile kanti ikahle uma uyihlanganisa nezinye izindwangu ngoba ayisheshi ukuguga,” kusho uSimphiwe.
Ngabe uzothungani manje? “Okuningi okwenziwe nge-mohair, kuzobiza ngoba indwangu iyabiza. Lowo msebenzi mukhulu kodwa ngizowenza.”
