Owokuzenzela
Iningi labathengi lithatha imishwalensi eliyinikwa yizitolo ngoba ingekho elingakhetha kuyona. Kuhle ukuba nomshwalensi kodwa akudingeki kuze kube owesitolo sakho.
Uma uthenga ngesikweleti izimpahla kuba ezesitolo uze uqede ukuzikhokhela.
Cabanga ngenhlekelele okungenzeka ikwehlele - umuzi wakho ungasha, kuntshontshwe izimpahla zakho noma uphelelwe wumsebenzi.
Uma ungenamshwalensi, kuzodingeka ukuba uthole imali yokukhokha isikweleti sakho.
Bheka ongakwenza ngaphambi kokwamukela umshwalensi owunikwa yisitolo uma uthenga ngesikweleti...
lCabanga: Kungenzeka ukuthi usunawo umshwalensi ongakukhokhela isikweleti sakho. Abanye abantu banemishwalensi efakwa kwi-pension fund yabo. Uma ukhokhela i-bond umnini muzi uba nomshwalensi walowo muzi. Uma uveze ubufakazi esitolo bokuthi unawo, isitolo akufanele sikuphoqe ukuba uthathe omunye.
lHlanganani: Uma bebaningi abasebenzayo ekhaya, nicabanga nokuthenga ngesikweleti kodwa ningenawo umshwalensi, hlanganani emshwalensini owodwa. Uma nihlangana nikhokhele umshwalensi ningonga imali enhle. Khumbula ukwazisa umshwalensi wakho ukuthi uhlala nobani ukuze ugweme ingxabano engaba khona uma sekudingeka ukuba umshwalensi ukhokhe.

Usizo
"Ngeshwa, asikwazi ukushayela imishwalensi imithetho ngoba lokho kungumsebenzi wabalawula ezezimali," kusho u-Astrid Ludin, ongomunye wabagqugquzeli beNational Credit Bill okungenzeka iqale ukusebenza ngo-April ngonyaka ozayo okuhloswe ngayo ukuba kusizwe abenza izivumelwano zokuthenga ngesikweleti. "Sinazo izeluleko ezingasiza kule nkinga."
Okusemqoka wukuthi lo mthetho omusha uzofaka izitolo, omashonisa, amabhange nabanye ingcindezi yokuba "bangaboleki abantu imali ngokungabacabangeli." Okusho ukuthi ungakwazi ukusisula isivumelwano esifana nesikaNtombentsha uma kungukuthi wawungasiqondi ukuthi sithini. Okwamanje qaphela ukhumbule ukuthi uNtombentsha akayedwa kule nkinga yokudlala izitolo zefenisha. E-Vereeniging uSibonelo Magwaza wathenga amakhabethe amathathu ayebiza u-R2 840.

UNtombentsha washayeka kabili
UNtombentsha washaywa yizindleko zomshwalensi - waphinda washaywa yinzalo.
lIzivumelwano eziluthayo: UNtombentsha wayezokhokha inzalo encane ukube wayazi ukuthi ubolekwa imali akanikwa isivumelwano sokukhokha nokuyinto esiyibiza nge-hire purchase.
Inzalo ye-hire purchase ilawulwa yi-Usury Act. Okusho ukuthi ezikweletini ezingaphansi kuka-R10 000 inzalo akufanele ibe ngaphezulu kuka-20 percent - uNtombentsha wakhokha u-32,99 percent. Ingaphezulu kuka-17 percent ezikweletini ezingaphezulu kuka-R10 000.
Kufanele ukhokhe i-deposit uma uthenga nge-hire purchase nokuyinto engenza abathengi bakhethe ukubolekwa imali - ngaphandle kokucabanga ngomthelela inzalo ezoba nawo ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.
lInzalo enkulu: Pho kwenzeka kanjani ukuba uNtombentsha agcine esekhokha inzalo engu-32,99 percent, eyeve ngo-150 percent kwesemthethweni engu-20 percent" "Lokho kudalwa wukuthi izitolo zilawulwa yimithetho elawula ababolekisa ngemali kodwa ebavikelayo abathengi," kusho uSiva Naidoo womnyango wezomnotho nezokuvakasha eKZN. "Kodwa zingakwazi ukufaka isicelo sokuba zikhishwe kwi-Usury Act zibize inzalo enkulu."
lAmaqhinga okudayisa:
UNtombentsha wayezovikeleka ukube wayeboleke imali endaweni ebhaliswe kwiMicro Finance Regulatory Council.
NgokweMFRC amaphepha esivumelwano kumele abhalwe ngolimi umuntu aluthandayo. UNtombentsha uzwa kahle uma ukhuluma naye ngesiXhosa kanti konke kwakuzomcacela kahle ukuba kwakubhalwe ngalolu limi.
I-HP yayingeke imsize ngalutho. Lokho kungenxa yokuthi izivumelwano zeHP zisayinwa esitolo othenga kusona ngokusho kukaSiva Naidoo. Isikhathi sokucabangisisa ngesivumelwano esikunika igunya lokusisula zingakapheli izinsuku ezinhlanu usinikwa kwi-timeshare, uma uthenga umuzi, nasezivumelwaneni ozisayina ungekho esitolo.[z]
