SONKE siyazi ukuthi imingcwabo idla imali eshisiwe esikhathini samanje ngoba labo abashiywa yizihlobo zabo basuke befuna ukuzingcwaba ngesizotha.
Kodwa akufanele kube njalo njengoba uPaseka Hlatshwayo ongusomabhizinisi osafufusa esanda kukufakazela lokho.
UPaseka ukholwa wukuthi akufanele kuye ngokuthi umuntu unemali engakanani ukuze angcwabe kahle ngaleyo ndlela waqhamuka necebo.
"Ngiyazi ukuthi sisuke sifuna ukubangcwaba kahle abathandiweyo bethu kanti ngikholwa wukuthi kusuke kudlalwa ngabantu uma kuzoya ngokuthi banemali engakanani ukuze bangcwabe kahle," kusho yena.
"Okhokho bethu babeqikelela ukuthi bazingcwaba ngesizotha izihlobo zabo bengenandaba nokuthi banamalini yokuzingcwaba."
Konke lokhu kwaqala emva kokuba uPaseka ebukele uhlelo lweTV olwalumayelana nokushoda kwezindawo zokungcwaba wathola ukuthi okwakubekwa njengesixazululo kuloludaba - ukushisa izidumbu noma kungcwatshwe abantu phezu kwabanye - kwakushayisana namasiko ase-Africa.
Wazitshela ukuthi singaba khona isisombululo kulolu daba wathatha isinqumo sokululungisa.
UPaseka wenza ucwaningo ngamasiko ase-Africa amandulo, ikakhulu indlela okwakungcwatshwa ngayo abasebukhosini.
Wathola ukuthi abaholi babengcwatshwa beqosheme.
Babengcwatshwa ngale ndlela ngoba kwakunenkolelo yokuthi umoya wabo awufi kanti nabantu bazalwa beqosheme.
"Ngathola ukuthi ukungcwaba abantu ngale ndlela kubanika isithunzi esibafaneleyo," kusho yena.
"Umuntu ongasekho wayezongcwatshwa ngendlela okwakungcwatshwa ngayo abasebukhosini nami ngaqhamuka nendlela efana naleyo."

Ingcwaba leLebone lithatha indawo engaphansi kuka-66% uma uliqhathanisa nengcwaba elijwayelekile.
"Sabambisana nabeNational City Parks abasisiza ngokuqinisekisa ukuthi bonke omasipala bayakwazi ukumba lolu hlobo lwamangcwaba ethu," kuqhuba uPaseka.
"Abangcwabi bayaqeqeshwa ukuze bakwazi ukungcwaba ngeLebone kanti kulula," kusho yena. Ekuqeqeshweni basizwa yiWits University.

ISELULEKO SOFEZELA:
Kufanele kukudumaze lokhu. Kuyenzeka esimweni esinjengalesi ukuthi umqashwa abone kufanele ukuba akhushulelwe kwesinye isikhundla noma kukhethwe yena esikhundleni sokuba kuqashwe omunye umuntu.
Ngeke ukhushulelwe kwesinye isikhundla uma kungekho sigatshana esivumelwaneni sokuqashwa kwakho lapho umqashi ethembisa khona ukuthi uyokukhuphulela kwesinye isikhundla uma kunesidingo.
Khumbula ukuthi ibhizinisi elomqashi kanti unegunya lokuqasha umuntu amthandayo.
Kodwa lokho akusho ukuthi kufanele ayishaye indiva imigomo ye-Labour Relations Act ethi kufanele alandele imithetho efanele uma eqasha.
Umqashi kufanele enze okufanele uma eqasha - uma kungenjalo, usuke ephula imithetho ye-Labour Relations Act.
Umthetho uthi umqashi kufanele aqinisekise ukuthi uqasha umuntu ozowenza kahle umsebenzi.
Kufanele aqhathanise ikhono lomuntu ofake isicelo somsebenzi nelomuntu amqashile.
Kuvame ukuba kusuke umsindo mayelana nesinqumo esithathwa ngumqashi ngoba lowo nalowo ofake isicelo somsebenzi ubona kufanele ukuba kuqashwe yena. Kuleso naleso sicelo kufanele kubhekwe obonakala engawenza kahle umsebenzi.
Ngeke kube lula ukuthi sikunike owethu umbono ngoba asinayo imininingwane yalowo othole umsebenzi futhi asazi ukuthi umqashi umqashe phezu kwaliphi iphuzu.
