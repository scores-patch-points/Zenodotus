YIMPAMA yesandla nezithende zethu okungenazinwele. Izilwane zinoboya, thina sinezinwele kanti eminyakeni edlule imizimba yethu yayinoboya obuningi. Babusifudumeza ebusika busipholisa ehlobo. Kodwa kuthe ngokuhamba kwesikhathi imizimba yethu yafunda ukuzipholisa ngendlela engcono - ngokujuluka.
Ake ucabange nje ukuthi kwakuzosiphatha kanjani ukujuluka sibe sinoboya obuningi emzimbeni - kwakuzoba sengathi umuntu ugqoke ijezi elimanzi! Uboya emzimbeni bancipha. Kodwa ekhanda sinezinwele ezisivikela elangeni. Yingakho nje zingazange ziphele.
Izinwele zisuka eziqwini zoboya ezincane esikhumbeni ezibizwa ngama-follicles. Izinwele zinama-cells alekene njengama-tiles ophahla lwendlu. Ngesikhathi izinwele zikhula ama-cells ayasuka esikhumbeni afe - kuhle lokho ngoba ukuba abengafi, ukugunda izinwele bekuzoba yinto ebuhlungu kakhulu!
Uboya emzimbeni wethu bukhula bube okokuthathu kwe-millimetre ngosuku. Kodwa abukhuli buqhubeke. Buyama isikhathi esingangezinyanga eziyisithupha. Yingakho nje uboya emzimbeni wethu bubufishane kunasekhanda.
