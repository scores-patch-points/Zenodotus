aphuma phambili!
Iyasha, yonga imali ikunika umdlandla ungayenza ngesikhathi esincane ngamaqanda. Ungayidla noma kunini osukwini (ngisho nasebusuku imbala!). Amaresiphi nezindlela zokuhlela ngabeGemini Food Promotions, izithombe nguKim Thunder

Indlela yokubilisa amaqanda
■ Sebenzisa amaqanda angekho efrijini ukuze angaqhumi. Ngaphambi kokulibilisa iqanda, lizungezise entendeni yesandla sakho ukuze okuphuzi kwalo kube phakathi nendawo ngisho selibilile.
■ Amanzi epanini awadlule amaqanda. Sebenzisa isipuni ufake amaqanda ngokucophelela ukuze angafi. Yehlisa umlilo uyeke amanzi agxabhe kahle.
■ Faka i-teaspoon kaviniga emanzini ukuze amaqanda kube lula ukuwahluba.
■ Uma ufuna iqanda elithambile, bilisa imizuzu engu-3-5 - eliqinile imizuzu engu-6-8.
■ Emva kokuwabilisa, afake emanzini abandayo okwesikhashana. Lokhu kwenza okuphuzi kungashintshi umbala.
■ Uma usika amaqanda abilile aqinile, faka ummbese emanzini abandayo kuqala ukuqinisekisa ukuthi okuphuzi akuvuthuki.
Boiled Eggs In Cheese Sauce
□ 8 amaqanda
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) imajarini
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) i-cornflour
□ 3 izinkomishi (750ml) ubisi
□ 1/2 inkomishi (125ml) ushizi ogreythiwe
□ 1 i-sprig parsley eqotshiwe
□ usawoti nophepha
□ 1 inkomishi (250ml) izimvuthu zesinkwa
1. Epanini bilisa amaqanda emanzini imizuzu engu-6-8 bese uwabeka eceleni.
2. Ncibilikisa imajarini ufake i-cornflour ukubonde kuhlangane. Faka ubisi ulokhu ukugovuza ukuze kube bushelelezi, kubile kuze kushube.
3. Bondela phakathi ushizi, i-parsley, usawoti nophepha.
4. Sika amaqanda abe ngohhafu. Ahlele kwi-ovenproof dish, uthele i-sauce, phezulu kube yizimvuthu zesinkwa.
5. Kubhake kuhhavini ongu-180oC kuze kube nsunjwana (8-10 imizuzu). Kuseve nothosi.

Fried Eggs
■ Ukuze uthose iqanda ngempumelelo, qiniseka ukuthi epanini kukhona imajarini noma uwoyela owanele ukuvimbela ukubambelela epanini.
■ Ngokucophelela, shaya amaqanda uwafake esosweni, ufake elilodwa ngesikhathi bese ulifaka kuwoyela oshisayo.
■ Nciphisa umlilo, uthose iqanda bese uligudluza nge-egg-lifter. Ukwenza okwehlukile, thatha ucezu lwesinkwa esimhlophe wenze imbobo phakathi nendawo. Thosa isinkwa sibe nsunjwana bese usiphendula. Phula iqanda ulifake kule mbobo yesinkwa bese upheka kuze kuqine.

Mexican Omelette
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) imajarini
□ 1 u-anyanisi oqotshiwe
□ 2 utamatisi osikwe izikwedlana
□ 1 i-teaspoon (5ml) ushukela
□ 1 isipuni (15ml) i-sweet chilli sauce
□ 4 amaqanda ashayiwe
□ 4 ama-teaspoon (20ml) ubisi
□ 1/2 i-teaspoon (2,5ml) usawoti
□ 2 izipuni (30ml) imajarini
□ 1/4 inkomishi (65ml) ushizi ogreythiwe
1. Epanini shisa imajarini, uthose u-anyanisi uze uthambe. Faka utamatisi, ushukela ne-chilli sauce. Kuyeke kugxabhe imizuzu engu-5 bese ukugudluza emlilweni.
2. Kujeke hlanganisa amaqanda, ubisi nosawoti.
3. Epanini ncibilikisa imajarini.
4. Kuthele engxubeni yeqanda ukuyeke kuze kuthambe ngaphezulu.
5. Faka ingxube ye-chilli uma i-omelette isisondele ngasekulungeni. Ivuvuzele ngoshizi uyigoqe bese uyidla ngokushesha.
Kubiza: R13,98
