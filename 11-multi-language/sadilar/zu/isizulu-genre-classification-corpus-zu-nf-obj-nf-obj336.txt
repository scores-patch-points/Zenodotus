IBHULOHO elinamakhebuli anezinsika - eyodwa noma amaningi - elide lalolu hlobo emhlabeni yiTatara bridge eliseJaphani.
Linemizila emine yezimoto nomunye owengeziwe wamabhayisikili, izithuthuthu nabantu abahamba ngezinyawo kanti ubude bawo bungu-1 480m.
Leli ngelinye lamabhuloho aluchunge ahlanganisa iziqhingi zaseHonshu naseShikoku, kuye kunqamule eSeto Inland Sea eJaphani.
Lavulwa ngomhlaka 1 May 1999, kanti laqale lahlelelwa ukuba libe ngelidlula izinto ezilengiswayo ngo-1973.
Ngo1989 lashintshwa lasebenza ngamakhebuli.
Izinsika zensimbi ezilisekele zingu-220m ukuphakama kanti zimise okuka-Y. Ukwakhiwa kwaleli bhuloho kwathatha isikhathi esingaphansi kweminyaka eyisithupha kanti akuzange kube khona izigameko zezingozi ngaleso sikhathi.
IBHULOHO elide kunawo wonke emhlabeni nalo liseJaphani. Leli bhuloho yi-Akashi-Kaikyo, noma iPearl Bridge, elinqamula i-Akashi Strait, lixhumanise i-Maiko eKobe ne-Iwaya eziqhingini zase-Awaji. Kwaqale kwahlongozwa ukuba kube yibhuloho elinojantshi wesitimela nomgwaqo kodwa kwathi lapho seliqala ukwakhiwa ngo-April 1986, kwaphoqeleka ukuba kube ngelinomgwaqo kuphela libe nemizila eyisithupha. Leli bhuloho laqala ukusebenza ngo-April 5, 1998.
Leli bhuloho linezingxenye ezintathu. Ephakathi nendawo ingu-1 991m, kanti ezinye ezimbili zingu-960m iyinye. Leli bhuloho lingu-3 911m ubude kanti lingakwazi ukumelana nemimoya engu-286km/h, ukuzamazama komhlaba nemisinga enamandla.

OWLS
lOwls are nocturnal, meaning they hunt at night. They’re found in most areas of the world and there are two kinds – the typical owl and the barn owl.
lOwls cannot move their eyes – they must turn their entire head to look around.
lOwls can’t see up close very well, but can see far away very well, especially in low light.
lMany owls can also hunt by sound in total darkness. Their muffled wings and dull feathers allow them to fly practically silent and unseen.
lThe smallest owls include the pygmy owls, some of which are only 13cm long with a 32cm wingspan.
lThe largest owls are the eagle owls, which are more than 70cm long with a wingspan of just over 2m.
lIn many parts of the world, owls have been associated with death and misfortune, likely due to their nocturnal activity and common screeching call. However, owls have also been associated with wisdom and prosperity as a result of frequently being companion birds for goddesses.
