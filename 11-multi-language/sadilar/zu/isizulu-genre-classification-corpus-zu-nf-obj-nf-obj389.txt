INGOSI
QUmndeni wami ukhonze iYorkshire pudding. Kodwa uma ngizama ukuyenza, iba manzi ithambe ingakhukhumali kahle. Yini engingayenzi ngendlela efanele?
AKunezinto ezimbalwa okumele uzenze ukuze uthole umphumela ogculisayo uma wenza iYorkshire pudding.
Okokuqala, inhlama kufanele ikhukhumale kahle. Hlanganisa inhlama kusasele okungenani amahora amabili ngaphambi kokuyisebenzisa uyifake efrijini. Uma isilungele ukusetshenziswa, yikhiphe efrijini, uthele isipuni samanzi abanda kakhulu enhlameni uyishaye.
Yiba nesiqiniseko sokuthi uwoyela osethinini lokubhaka ushisa kakhulu. (Kufanele kukhuphuke intuthu.) Sebenzisa ithini lokubhaka hhayi indishi eyingilazi – ithini lizongenisa ukushisa kwi-pudding ngendlela efanele.
Bhaka ezingeni lokushisa eliphezulu ungasivuli isivalo ingakapheli imizuzu engu-20 ingxube ibhakiwe!
QAma-chips ami amanziswa amafutha uma ngiwathosa. Ngingenza njani ukuze ahlofozele?
ACwilisa amazambane emanzini abanda kakhulu okungenani ingxenye yehora ngaphambi kokuthosa. Wakhiphe emanzini uwasule. Thosa imizuzu emihlanu. Wakhiphe emlilweni uwabeke imizuzu embalwa, khuphula umlilo uphinde uthose. Ama-chips akho azoba yilokho okufunayo.
QUma ngithenge i-celery, ngigcina ngilahle ingxenye yayo ngoba iyathamba ibe njimbilili emva kwezinsuku ezimbalwa ngiyibeke efrijini. Ngingayigcina kanjani?
AIgoqe nge-aluminium foil uyifake efrijini. Izoba freshi isikhathi eside.
Uma i-celery yakho ithambile, yibeke epanini elinamanzi aneqhwa eliningi ihora elilodwa – izoba freshi. [z]
