Ihlathululo

Imvumo yimvumo etloliweko enikelwa abangahle babe babelethi aba-adopthako uku-adoptha umntwana. Imvumo inganikelwa babelethi, umondli womntwana namkha mntwana. Umntwana angaphikisa imvumo yoku-adoptha nayikuthi uneminyaka eli-10 namkha ngaphezulu. Kungaqalwa iinjamiso zomunye umthetho, umbelethi womntwana unelungelo lokubuyisela emva imvumo enjalo amalanga ama-60 ngemva kokunikela ngayo. Ikhotho yabantwana angeze yenza omunye umlayo woku-adoptha ngaphambi kokuphela kwesikhathi samalanga ama-60.



Ubujamo okungafunwa ngabo umlayo woku-adoptha

ebujameni lapho ababelethi bomntwana bahlongakele begodu kungakakhonjwa umondli womntwana.

umbelethi akakghoni ukunikela imvumo -


ngebanga lokugula ngokwegqondo;
olahle umntwana begodu nokungaziwako bona ukuphi; namkha
utlhorise umntwana ngokomzimba, emmoyeni namkha ngokomseme, umphethe kumbi; namkha wavumela bona abethwe, aphathwe kumbi, namkha atlhoriswe;
ubange namkha urhelebhe ukuletha ukuvuswa kwemizwa khona kuzakuyiwa emsemeni nomntwana, ukuthumba namkha ukusetjenziselwa zomseme komntwana namkha ukulaya umntwana ukwenza izenzo zokungaziphathi kuhle;
ubambe imvumo yakhe ngokungazwakaliko;
ngomntwana obelethelwe ngaphandle komtjhado ubhalelwe ukuvuma ukobana ungubaba womntwana namkha ngaphandle kwebanga elizwakalako ubhalelwe kukwenza imisebenzi yokuba ngubaba emntwaneni obelethelwe ngaphandle komtjhado namkha owenziwa etjhebiswaneni lokuthadana kweenhlobo nonina;
nguyise lomntwana owabelethelwa ngaphandle komtjhado begodu yena  wafunyanwa anomlandu wokukata namkha wokubetha unina lomntwana;
namkha ngemva kokubuza kwekhotho yabantwana kulandela isolo eyenziwe ngunina lomntwana, yathola  bona ukate namkha ubethe unina lomntwana:

Kuye ngokuthi iphenyo elinjalo elizakubumba ukufunywana umlandu emlandwini wobulelesi namkha ukutlhorisa;

namkha umntwana owabelethwa ngaphandle komtjhado ubhalelwe kuphendula, kungakapheli amalanga ali-14, esazisweni anikelwe sona njengombana kuhlosiwe ngenzasi.





Inothisi yemvumo ye-adopshini

Nayikuthi mbelethi munye kuphela onikele ngemvumo, begodu omunye umbelethi akatholakali ukunikela ngemvumo, ukomitjhinara uzakufakazela  imvumo enjalo begodu anikele ngesaziso komunye umbelethi kungakapheli amalanga ali-14. Isaziso sazisa umbelethi bona imvumo inikelwe begodu imnikela ithuba


lokunikela ngeyakhe imvumo namkha lokwala nayo;

namkha anikele ngamabanga wokobana kungani imvumo yakhe kufanele ifunyanwe begodu kungeze umlayo wanikelwa ngaphandle kwayo

namkha wenze isibawo somlayo woku-adoptha nayikuthi ngubaba wemvelo begodu umntwana wabelethelwa ngaphandle komtjhado.


Nayikuthi unina lomntwana owabelethelwa ngaphandle komtjhado uyalivuma itjhuguluko lemininingwana yamabeletho womntwana, ubaba lomntwana kufanele aziswe. Kufanele enze amatjhuguluko afaneleko kungakapheli amalanga ali-14 wokufumana isaziso.

Nayikuthi unina lomntwana akalivumi itjhuguluko lemininingwana yokutloliswa kwamabeletho womntwana, umuntu ofuna ukuvuma bona unguyise lomntwana lowo kufanele enze isibawo ekhotho yabantwana efaneleko bona afumane umlayo wesifungo oqinisekisa bona nguye uyise lomntwana begodu ifuneko yokuvuma ebuya kunina ayithlogeki.

Isaziso esibuya kuyise wemvelo obelethelwe ngaphandle komtjhado ngiyo yodwa into efunekako nayikuthi


uyise uvumile, ngomtlolo, ukuba nguyise lomntwana; begodu nayikuthi ufake imininingwana yakhe ekutlolisweni kwamabeletho womntwana begodu uqinisekisa bona imininingwana ilungile ngazo zoke iinkhathi;

namkha unina lomntwana, ngesinye nesinye isikhathi; namkha unina lomntwana, ngesikhathi sokunikela imvumo,


aqinisekise, ngomtlolo, bona uyise lomntwana uvume ukuba ngubaba lomntwana;

begodu wenze ukwaziwa kwakhe nalapho atholalakala khona kwaziwe;



namkha isisebenzi sezehlalakuhle siletha umbiko oqinisekisa bona ngubani uyise nokobana ukuphi kukomitjhinara ofakazele  imvumo kanina; namkha ekhotho yabantwana lapho isibawo soku-adoptha senziwe khona. Lombiko kufanele ulethwe kungakapheli amalanga ama-60 ngemva kobana unina anikele ngemvumo, namkha kwesinye nesinye isikhathi ngaphambi kobana ikhotho yabantwana inikele ngomlayo woku-adoptha.





Umbelethi onikele ngemvumo kufanele azise ukomitjhinara ngemva kokufakazelwa kwemvumo yakhe.



Nawufuna ilwazi elinabileko, yiya ku-website yomNyango wezoBulungiswa nokuThuthukiswa komThethosisekelo.



Top

Amagadango okufanele alandelwe

Ukunikela imvumo:

Nayikuthi umbelethi namkha umondli womntwana, zalisa iForomo 12, Imvumo yombelethi namkha umondli yokuqintela uku-adopthwa komntwana ; begodu nayikuthi umntwana, zalisa iForomo 13, Imvumo yomntwana yokuvumela uku-adopthwa (Form 13, Consent by child to adoption.

Imvumo kufanele itlolwe phasi. Nayikuthi inikelwa eSewula Afrika, umuntu namkha abantu abanikela imvumo kufanele bayitlikitle phambi kwakakomitjhinara wezehlalakuhle yabantwana, ozakufakazela imvumo.

Imvumo enikelwa ngaphandle kweSewula Afrika kufanele itlikitlwe beyifakazelwe phambi komzenda namkha imitjhini yeSewula Afrika, namkha ijaji, umarhastrada, u-justice of the peace namkha isiphathiswa somphakathi senarha ethintekako.

Nanyana imvumo kufanele ibe namagama wababelethi abahlongozwako, ikhotho yabantwana ingamukela imvumo yababelethi bomntwana namkha umondli enganamagama wababelethi aba-adopthako nayikuthi iyaneliswa bona kusekarekweni ehle yomntwana bona yenziwe njalo.




Ukubuyisela emva imVumo eTloliweko



Ukubuyisela emva imVumo eTloliweko kufanele kwenziwe ngomtlolo. Nayikuthi umbelethi namkha umondli womntwana, zalisa iForomo 12A, Ukubuyiselwa emva kwemvumo yoku-adoptha yombelethi namkha umondli womntwana  phambi komunye nomunye ukomitjhinara kungakapheli amalanga ama-60 ngemva kokunikela isaziso leso.

Nayikuthi umntwana, zalisa iForomo 13A phambi komunye nomunye ukomitjhinara ngaphambi kobana umlayo woku-adoptha wenziwe yikhotho yabantwana.

Nakungenzeka imvumo ibuyiselwe emva kwesinye isiyingi ngaphandle kwesiyingi okwakhutjhwa kiso umlayo namkha lapho isibawo soku-adoptha sizakulalelwa khona, ukomitjhinara khonokho


uzakwazisa ikhotho efaneleko nesisebenzi sezehlalakuhle  bona kukiyiphi ikhotho lapho kuzakulalelwa khona uku-adoptha;
namkha uzakwazisa ukomitjhinara ofakazele imvumo  bona kukiyiphi ikhotho lapho isibawo soku-adoptha sizakulalelwa khona.




Ukomitjhinara khonokho uzakufanisa ikhotho lapho isibawo soku-adoptha sizakulalelwa khona abe azise ikhotho nesisebenzi sezehlalakuhle ngokubuyiselwa emva.

Amazinga wokusebenza nesikhathi

Isikhathi esibekelwe ukusetjenzwa kwesibawo soku-adoptha siyame ekuraraneni kwendaba.

Ngaphambi kobana ukomitjhinara afakazele imvumo, kufanele azise umuntu onikela ngemvumo


ngemiphumela yezomthetho yoku-adoptha;
bonyana, ebujameni bemvumo yombelethi, umbelethi othintekako angabuyisela emva imvumo ngomtlolo eForomoni 12A phambi kwakakomitjhinara omunye nomunye kwesinye nesinye isikhathi kungakapheli amalanga ama-60 ngemva kokunikela imvumo enjalo;
bona, ebujameni bemvumo yomntwana, umntwana othintekako angabuyisela emva imvumo ngomtlolo eForomini 13A phambi komunye nomunye ukomitjhinara kwesinye nesinye isikhathi ngaphambi kobana umlayo woku-adoptha wenziwe yikhotho yabantwana;
begodu umuntu othintekako  angabakhona lokha isibawo soku-adoptha sitjhejwako ngaphandle kwalokha ikhotho, ngokuqunta kwayo, imnikela imvumo yokubakhona ngombana inombono wokobana ukubakhona kwakhe kuzakuba sekarekweni yomntwana.
