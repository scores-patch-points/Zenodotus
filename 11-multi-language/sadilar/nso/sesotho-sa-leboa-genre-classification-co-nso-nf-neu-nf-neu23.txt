PREFACE







Section 32(1) of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa Act 108 of 1996 (the Constitution), provides that everyone has the right of access to records or/and information held by the state and any information held by another person and that is required for the exercise or protection of any rights. This section affirms the fundamental right of access to information and seeks to promote a culture of transparency and accountability in the private and public sector.



Section 32(2) of the Constitution provides for the enactment of a national legislation to give effect to this fundamental right. The Promotion of Access to



Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA) is the national legislation contemplated in section 32(2) of the Constitution.



Section 10 of PAIA provides that the South African Human Rights Commission must compile simple and easily comprehensible guidelines on how to use the Promotion of Access to Information Act.



The Commission is proud to present this; the first publication of the Guide in compliance with its mandate in terms section 10 of PAIA. The narrative part of the Guide is published in all 11 official languages beginning with English and thereafter arranged in alphabetical order. While every effort has been made to ensure the accuracy of the information and data in the Guide, mobility of officials and change of addresses by public bodies may well have affected the currency of some of the information.



Due to the fact that some private bodies (private bodies other than public companies in terms of section 20 of the Companies Act) have been temporarily exempted from publishing section 51 manuals we have not included the contact details of the private bodies in this first publication of the Guide. An electronic version of the Guide will be kept on the website of the South African Human Rights Commission, www.sahrc.org.za and will be updated monthly to keep the public abreast of developments and changes regarding the contact details of information officers of public bodies and general information on PAIA.



The Commission is committed to monitoring, promoting and ensuring the implementation of the right of access to information. PAIA is a legislative milestone in the light of the pre 1994 political environment of secrecy and human rights violations that characterised the system of government. The unique horizontal application of the Bill of Rights in the Constitution makes South Africa’s freedom of information legislation unique in the world, in that PAIA is the only freedom of information legislation that permits access to records held by private bodies. We commend the drafters of PAIA for this futuristic unique feature of PAIA especially in the light of the considerable power that multinational companies wield in the global arena today, particularly the negative impact of some of their activities on fundamental human rights in some regions - especially the third world regions.



PAIA was significantly assented to during the first decade of democracy in South Africa. In recognition of this fact and in line with the national ten year celebration of democracy, the South African Human Rights Commission hosted the Second International Conference of Information Commissioners in Cape Town on 2 and 3 February 2004 which was attended by information commissioners from more that 25 countries from across the world. We were proud to share our experiences with old and

FOREWORD







It is with joy to present to the nation the first edition of the Section 10 Guide in terms of the Promotion of Access to Information Act No 2 of 2000 (PAIA). This Guide is the first on the African continent and the access to information right, as provided in our Constitution, is the only one in the world that applies to both public and private bodies. This Guide has also been translated into all the 11 official languages of the Republic of South Africa for easy access by all the people of our beautiful country.



To move from a deeply inculcated culture of secrecy and bureaucracy to a culture of transparency and accountability is a mammoth challenge. The



prejudice against responsive and open governance is certainly not confined to the previous order. The current hostile and ignorant responses received by the Commission in respect of PAIA are proof of this observation.



It is of critical importance that the citizens be informed about PAIA and how the right of access to information can work for their benefit. Participation in democratic processes can only be effective if it is informed participation. Many of the tragedies in South African history could have been prevented had there been an access to information regime in operation. It is however important that PAIA reaches far beyond the traditional political civil rights and that it adds a new dimension to public debate on every day issues that citizens have to face.



Public and private bodies must understand that their responsibilities under PAIA are not intended to be a costly burden but an essential mechanism to ensure good governance and the transformation of our society. The right, as well as the other entrenched rights should not only be approached in an adversarial manner but rather used as a vehicle to change our society and an opportunity to deal with the vestiges of apartheid.



PAIA is central to the transformation of our society. The rule of law and the democratic constitutional state will perish if there is not open and accountable government. The importance and magnitude of the Commission’s constitutional role to monitor and report annually on the realization of socio- economic rights is matched by its obligation under PAIA.



I would be failing in my duty if I don’t express my special thanks to the PAIA Unit for the countless hours they have given towards the compilation of this Guide. You have through this Guide contributed towards healing the wounds of the past and enhancing our new democracy. We will rejoice if ordinary citizens of our country use this Guide and thereby give more meaning to their freedoms for which they have fought so hard.



Yours sincerely









Dr Leon Wessels



Commissioner responsible for the Promotion of Access to Information Act South African Human Rights Commission

1.	KE  KA  LEBAKA  LA  ENG  TUMELELO  GO  TSHEDIMOŠO ?



MATSENO



Karolo 32(1)(a) ya Molaotheo wa Molao wa Repabliki ya Afrika Borwa, wa 108 wa 1996 (mo o bitšwa "Molaotheo") o fana ka gore motho yo mongwe le yo mongwe o na le tokelo ya tumelelo ya tshedimošo efe goba efe yeo e swerwego ke naga le tshedimošo efe goba efe yeo e swerwego ke motho yo mongwe yeo e nyakegago go tirišo goba tšhireletšo ya ditokelo dife goba dife.



Molaotheo 



Molaotheo o bolela gore Afrika Borwa ke naga ya go ipuša gape ya demokrasi yeo e hlomilwego godimo ga tšwetšopele ya ditokelo tša batho le tshepetšo yeo e nago le maikarabelo gape yeo e tšwelelago nyanyeng bjalo ka tše dingwe tša mehola ya yona. Tshepetšo ya mmušo wa Afrika Borwa pele ga 27 Aporele 1994 magareng ga tše dingwe, e tšweletše ka setšo sa sephiri gape seo se se nago maikarabelo bobedi go ditho tša setšhaba le tša poraebete tšeo di ilego tša lebiša go tšhomišompe ya maatla le ditokelo tša batho. Ke ka lebaka le gore, Molaokakanywa wa Ditokelo ka gare ga Molaotheo, o fana ka tokelo ya tumelelo ya tshedimošo yeo e swerwego ke naga, le tshedimošo efe goba efe yeo e swerwego ke motho yo mongwe yeo e nyakegago go tirišo goba tšhireletšo ya ditokelo dife goba dife. Karolo 32(2) ya Molaotheo e fana gape ka tirišo ya peomolao ya bosetšhaba yeo e tla fanago ka tirišo ya toka ye, ka go hlompha, go šireletša, go hlatloša le go phethagatša toka ye. 



Hlatlošo ya Tumelelo go Molao wa Tshedimošo 



Hlatlošo ya Tumelelo go Molao wa Tshedimošo, wa 2 wa 2000 (mo o bitšwa "PAIA", goba "Molao" ka go šielana) ke peomolao ya bosetšhaba yeo e dirišetšwago go fana ka tirišo ya tokelo ya molaotheo ya tumelelo ya tshedimošo. PAIA e thomilwe go dirišwa ka la 9 Matšhe 2001, ka ntle le dikarolo 10, 14, 15 le 51, tšeo di thomilego go dirišwa ka la 15 Febereware 2002. 



Melawana 



Go ya ka Karolo 92 ya PAIA, Tona ka tsebišo ka gare ga Kuranta ya Mmušo, a ka dira melawana magareng ya ye mengwe malebana le taba efe goba efe yeo e nyakegago goba e dumelelwago ke PAIA gore e laetšwe, taba efe goba efe yeo e amanago le ditšhelete tšeo di lefelwago le tsebišo yeo e nyakwago ke Molao.

3.	KA  MOO  O  KA  DIRAGO  KGOPELO  YA  TUMELELO  GO  TSHEDIMOŠO



Kgopelo ya tumelelo go rekoto e dirwa ka go tlatša foromo ya kgopelo yeo e bitšwago Foromo A ya ditho tša setšhaba le Foromo C ya ditho tša poraebete. Diforomo tša kgopelo di swanetše di tlatšwe gomme di romelwe go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba goba hlogo ya setho sa poraebete woo tumelelo ya rekoto e hwetšwago go yena. Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo ya setho sa setšhaba goba hlogo wa setho sa poraebete e swanetše e fetole go kgopelo mo matšatšing a 30. Tsela ya kgopelo 



Mokgopedi o na le tokelo ya tumelelo go rekoto ya setho sa setšhaba goba setho sa poraebete gomme o swanetšwe a fiwe tumelelo go rekoto ya setho sa setšhaba goba sa poraebete ge e ba mokgopedi o latela dinyakwa tša mokgwatshepetšo tše di latelago:

Gore tumelelo go rekoto yeo e kgopetšwego ga e ganetšwe go ya ka mabakatheo afe goba afe a kganetšo ka tlase ga PAIA. 



Ka moo o tlatšago foromo ya kgopelo ka gona - ditho tša setšhaba le tša poraebete 



Kgopelo ya tumelelo go rekoto ya setho sa setšhaba le sa poraebete e dirwa, ka go tlatša foromo yeo e bitšwago foromo ya kgopelo. Kgopelo e swanetšwe e dirwe go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba goba hlogo ya setho sa poraebete go aterese ya gagwe, nomoro ya gagwe ya fekese goba aterese ya gagwe ya e-meile.

Ge e ba sekgoba ka gare ga foromo ya kgopelo ga se gona, mokgopedi a ka tšwela pele mo lehlakoreng la ka thoko gomme a kgomaretša go foromo ya kgopelo, mokgopedi o swanetše a saene dikgomaretšwa ka moka ga tšona;

Thušo yeo e hwetšagalago go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba 



Ge mokgopedi a tsebiša mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba gore o rata go dira kgopelo ya tumelelo go rekoto ya setho seo sa setšhaba goba sa setho se sengwe sa setšhaba, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a fane ka thušo ya mohuta woo, ntle le tefišo, ka ge go hlokagala, go kgontšha mokgopedi go tlatša foromo yeo e laeditšwego. 



Moo kgopelo ya tumelelo e sa sepelelanego le dineo tša PAIA, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a ka se ganetše kgopelo ka mabaka ka go se sepelelane ga mohuta woo, ka ntle le ge mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a tsebišitše mokgopedi ka go mo ngwalela ka ga maikamišetšo a go ganetša kgopelo, gotee le mabaka a maikemišetšo a go ganetša kgopelo gape le gore, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo goba mohlankedi wa mmušo o tla fana ka thušo go mokgopedi gore kgopelo e dirwe ka foromo ye bjalo, moo go ka se sa bago le mabakatheo a kganetšo. 



Mo go kgonegago, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a fe gape mokgopedi tshedimošo yeo e kgopelwago (yeo tumelelo e ka fiwago goba ya ganetšwa) ka ga thušo efe goba efe yeo e swerwego ke setho sa setšhaba, gape yeo e lego malebana le kgopelo.

Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a fe mokgopedi sebaka sa go ka kgonthišiša goba go lokiša kgopelo, gore e sepelelane le dinyakwa tša foromo ya dikgopelo. 

Ge e ba ka morago ga gore mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a amogela kgopelo ya tumelelo, go lemogwa gore kgopelo e swanetšwe go ba e diretšwe setho se sengwe sa setšhaba, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a fane ka thušo yeo e hlokegago go kgontšha motho gore a dire kgopelo go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba sa maleba; goba o swanetše a fetišetše kgopelo yeo go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa maleba, go tla ya le gore ke e fe yeo e ka dirwago ntle le matsapa. 



Ge e ba kgopelo ya tumelelo e dirilwe go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba gomme rekoto ga e maloka goba ga e ka tlase ga taolo ya setho seo, eupša ka tlase ga taolo ya setho se sengwe sa setšhaba; goba moo taba ya rekoto yeo e kgopetšwego e batamelana le mešomo ya setho se sengwe sa setšhaba, mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše gore mo matšatšing a 14 ka morago ga go amogela kgopelo, a e romele go mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa maleba wa setho seo se sengwe sa setšhaba. Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a tsebiše mokgopedi ka se ka go mo ngwalela. 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a fetole kgopelo ya tumelelo mo matšatšing a 30, ka morago ga go amogela kgopelo. Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a ka oketša sebaka sa matšatši a 30, ka matšatši a mangwe a 30, ge e ba o file mokgopedi tsebišo ya koketšo ka go mo ngwalela. Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a ka oketša fela nako ye gatee, gape ka tlase ga mabaka ao a itšego, a go swana le a; kgopelo e le ya direkoto tše ntši, hlokego ya go boledišana le dikarolo tša setho sa setšhaba goba setho se sengwe sa setšhaba, goba mokgopedi a file tumelelo ya koketšo ka go ngwala. 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo o swanetše a tšee sephetho sa gore o fana ka tumelelo go kgopelo, gomme o swanetše a fe tsebišo go mokgopedi ka ga sephetho se. 

Ge e ba tumelelo go kgopelo e a fiwa, mokgopedi o swanetše a fiwe tsebišo yeo e laetšago tefelo ya tumelelo (ge e ba e a nyakega) yeo e swanetšwego gore e lefelwe, le tsela yeo tumelelo e tla fiwago ka yona. E na le tshedimošo ya matlotlo, kgwebo, saenthifiki goba theknikhale, gomme kutollo ka morago ga moo e ka rata go hlola tshenyo go dikgahlego tša kgwebo goba tša matlotlo tša Naga go ba setho sa setšhaba; 



E na le tshedimošo, yeo ge e ka utollwago, e ka bea setho sa setšhaba maemong a mabe ka kontraka goba ditherišano tše dingwe, goba ya senya phadišano ya setho sa setšhaba ka go tša kgwebo; 



Ke lenaneo la khomphuthara ge le hlalošwa ka gare ga Molao woo o Kopolotšwego ka tokelo, wa 98 wa 1978, leo e lego la Naga goba setho sa setšhaba , ka ntle le ge le hlokega go fa tumelelo go ya ka PAIA. 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo a ka se ganetše kgopelo ya tumelelo ya rekoto ya setho seo ge e ba rekoto e na le tshedimošo:



Yeo e šetšego e le gona setšhabeng; 



Ka ga goba e le ya setho sa setšhaba, ka ntle le setho sa setšhaba seo kgopelo e dirwago go sona, gomme setho se sa setšhaba se file tumelelo ka go ngwala gore kutollo e fiwe mokgopedi; 



Ka ga dipoelo tša setšweletšwa sefe goba sefe goba teko ya tikologo goba nyakišišo ye nngwe (ka ntle le teko ya pele goba dinyakišišo tšeo di dirilwego go tšweletša mekgwa ya go leka) yeo e dirilwego ke goba e direletšwego setho sa setšhaba, gomme kutollo ka morago ga moo e ka utollago polokego ye kgolo ya setšhaba goba kotsi ya tikologo. 



Tšhireletšo ya molao ya tshedimošo ya nyakišišo ya leloko la boraro, le tšhireletšo ya tshedimošo ya setho sa setšhaba 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba a ka ganetša kgopelo ya tumelelo ya rekoto ya setho seo, ge e ba rekoto e na le tshedimošo ka ga nyakišišo yeo e dirilwego goba yeo e sa tlogo dirwa, legatong la leloko la boraro gomme ge e ba e tlo utollwa e ka: 



Bea leloko la boraro, motho yo a dirago nyakišišo goba yo a tlogo dira nyakišišo legatong la leloko la boraro, goba tabakgolo ya nyakišišo, kotsing ye kgolo. 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba a ka ganetša kgopelo ya tumelelo ya rekoto ya setho seo, ge e ba rekoto e na le tshedimošo ka ga nyakišišo yeo e dirilwego goba e tlogo dirwa legatong la setho sa setšhaba gomme ge eba e tlo utollwa e ka:

Tshepetšo ya ditho tša setšhaba

Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba a ka ganetša tumelelo go rekoto ya setho seo, ge e ba kutollo e ka šitiša tshepetšo ya tiragatšo ka go setho sa setšhaba goba magareng ga ditho tša setšhaba, ka go fokotša therišano yeo e bulegilego ya leano, keletšo, pego goba tšhišinyo; goba mokgwa wa boikopantšho, therišano goba ponagatšo; goba ge e ba kutollo ya rekoto e ka, ge re bolelela pele kutollo ya pholisi goba pholisi yeo e laeditšwego e ka šitiša katlego ya pholisi yeo. 



Mohlankedi wa tshedimošo wa setho sa setšhaba a ka ganetša kgopelo ya tumelelo go rekoto ya setho seo sa setšhaba, ge:
