# Wiktionary (English edition, all languages) — attribution and licence

The dictionary here is a **derivative of English Wiktionary** (https://en.wiktionary.org), built from the Wikimedia dump
`enwiktionary-latest-pages-articles.xml.bz2` of **2026-10-01**.

- **Licence:** text from Wiktionary is available under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** licence
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This derivative is offered under the **same licence**.
- **Attribution:** Wiktionary contributors. The history of every page, and so its authors, is at
  `https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/<word>?action=history`.
- **What was changed:** wikitext was read structurally; templates were collapsed to tokens (not expanded), glosses and
  translation pairs kept, etymology, pronunciation, inflection tables, quotations and categories left out. Senses whose gloss is
  a template the reader could not expand are kept as flagged gaps. See `manifest.json` for the reader and counts.
- Anyone redistributing the pack must keep this notice and the licence.
