# colonial-studies audit sheet (60 documents)

Every document in `21-anti-colonial/colonial-studies` carries the label **perspective: colonized peoples**, applied to the folder as a block. This sheet samples 60 of its 1025 English documents at random (seed 2026) so the real mix can be measured. My guesses are withheld.

For each, from the title, author and opening line, mark who is speaking:

- **C**: a colonized or enslaved person, or someone writing from their standpoint (their own account, or an anti-colonial argument by one of them);
- **I**: the imperial, settler or slaveholding side (an officer, administrator, planter, colonist, missionary-of-empire, or fiction from that side);
- **M**: mixed, or about colonialism without a clear standpoint (a treatise, a study, a document, a compilation);
- **?**: cannot tell.
