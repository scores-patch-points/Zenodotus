=== Surah 104: Al-Humaza (The Traducer) ===
Revelation: Meccan

[1]
  AR: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ وَيْلٌۭ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍۢ لُّمَزَةٍ
  EN: Woe to every scorner and mocker

[2]
  AR: ٱلَّذِى جَمَعَ مَالًۭا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ
  EN: Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it.

[3]
  AR: يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخْلَدَهُۥ
  EN: He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

[4]
  AR: كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ
  EN: No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher.

[5]
  AR: وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ
  EN: And what can make you know what is the Crusher?

[6]
  AR: نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ
  EN: It is the fire of Allah, [eternally] fueled,

[7]
  AR: ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ
  EN: Which mounts directed at the hearts.

[8]
  AR: إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌۭ
  EN: Indeed, Hellfire will be closed down upon them

[9]
  AR: فِى عَمَدٍۢ مُّمَدَّدَةٍۭ
  EN: In extended columns.
