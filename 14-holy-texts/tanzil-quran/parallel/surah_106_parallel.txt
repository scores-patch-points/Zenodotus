=== Surah 106: Quraish (Quraysh) ===
Revelation: Meccan

[1]
  AR: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيْشٍ
  EN: For the accustomed security of the Quraysh -

[2]
  AR: إِۦلَٰفِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيْفِ
  EN: Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer -

[3]
  AR: فَلْيَعْبُدُوا۟ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ
  EN: Let them worship the Lord of this House,

[4]
  AR: ٱلَّذِىٓ أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٍۢ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍۭ
  EN: Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear.
