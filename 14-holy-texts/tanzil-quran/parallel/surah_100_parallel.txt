=== Surah 100: Al-Aadiyaat (The Chargers) ===
Revelation: Meccan

[1]
  AR: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ وَٱلْعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبْحًۭا
  EN: By the racers, panting,

[2]
  AR: فَٱلْمُورِيَٰتِ قَدْحًۭا
  EN: And the producers of sparks [when] striking

[3]
  AR: فَٱلْمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبْحًۭا
  EN: And the chargers at dawn,

[4]
  AR: فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِۦ نَقْعًۭا
  EN: Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust,

[5]
  AR: فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِۦ جَمْعًا
  EN: Arriving thereby in the center collectively,

[6]
  AR: إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٌۭ
  EN: Indeed mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful.

[7]
  AR: وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌۭ
  EN: And indeed, he is to that a witness.

[8]
  AR: وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ
  EN: And indeed he is, in love of wealth, intense.

[9]
  AR: ۞ أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ إِذَا بُعْثِرَ مَا فِى ٱلْقُبُورِ
  EN: But does he not know that when the contents of the graves are scattered

[10]
  AR: وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِى ٱلصُّدُورِ
  EN: And that within the breasts is obtained,

[11]
  AR: إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ لَّخَبِيرٌۢ
  EN: Indeed, their Lord with them, that Day, is [fully] Acquainted.
