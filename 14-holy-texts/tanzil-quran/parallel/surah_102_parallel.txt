=== Surah 102: At-Takaathur (Competition) ===
Revelation: Meccan

[1]
  AR: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ
  EN: Competition in [worldly] increase diverts you

[2]
  AR: حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ
  EN: Until you visit the graveyards.

[3]
  AR: كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
  EN: No! You are going to know.

[4]
  AR: ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
  EN: Then no! You are going to know.

[5]
  AR: كَلَّا لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ
  EN: No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty...

[6]
  AR: لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ
  EN: You will surely see the Hellfire.

[7]
  AR: ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ
  EN: Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty.

[8]
  AR: ثُمَّ لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ
  EN: Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.
