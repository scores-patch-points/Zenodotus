=== Surah 108: Al-Kawthar (Abundance) ===
Revelation: Meccan

[1]
  AR: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ إِنَّآ أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ ٱلْكَوْثَرَ
  EN: Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar.

[2]
  AR: فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ
  EN: So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone].

[3]
  AR: إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ
  EN: Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.
