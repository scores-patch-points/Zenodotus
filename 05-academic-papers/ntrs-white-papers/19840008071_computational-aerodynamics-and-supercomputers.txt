16. Abstract

        This paper reviews some of the progress in computational aerodynamics
   over the last decade and describes what remains to be done in the years
   ahead. The discussion includes a description of the Numerical Aerodynamic
   Simulation Program objectives, computational goals, and implementation
   plans.
