CONCLUDING REMARKS

The different types of aerodynamics and bifurcations that can occur under
steady-state boundary conditions have been reviewed. Bifurcations were found to
involve a loss of Fréchet differentiability. The impact of bifurcations on testing
that involves unsteady aerodynamics was reviewed by means of examples of both forced
and free oscillatory motions. The following summarizes our main observations.
